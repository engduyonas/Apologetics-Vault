# Jesus & The Holy Spirit: The True Muhammad/Ahmad!

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)

  * 4 min read


The latest attempt by internet Muslim dawagandists to prove that Muhammad is mentioned in the Holy Bible is to now appeal to a 9th century translation of the LXX version of the Hebrew Bible into Arabic by an Assyrian “Nestorian” Christian scribe named Hunayn Ibn Ishaq. According to these polemicists, Hunayn rendered the Greek term for glory, _doxe_ , as _hmd_ , the Arabic word for praise and from which we get the names Muhammad, Ahmad, etc. This same translator rendered _ta doxa_ as _al-mahmudatu_ , and translated _endoxos_ as _mahmud_. These dawagandists take Hunayn’s translation as proof that Jesus prophesied Muhammad by name!

In light of this desperate attempt of finding their false prophet in the Holy Scriptures, I am now going to turn the tables by showing them that this actually confirms what I wrote in some previous articles that the Muhammad of the Quran is actually Jesus, and that the Holy Spirit is none other than Ahmad. See the links below for further proof.

Seeing that the Muslim polemicists want to argue that the Greek term _doxe_ and its related forms refer to the Arabic word _hamd_ , from which we get Muhammad/Ahmad, this means that it is actually the Holy Spirit who is the real Muhammad/Ahmad! 

We should therefore translate the verse in this manner, “for the Spirit who is Muhammad/Ahmad is with you and rests upon you.”

But it gets a whole lot worse for these Muhammadans.

The Holy Spirit is said to be another Paraclete,

“And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Paraclete (_allon parakleton_), to be with you for all time, the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it neither sees him nor knows him; but you will know him, for he will dwell with you and will be in you.” John 14:16-17 [_MOUNCE_](<https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%2014%3A15-17&version=MOUNCE>)

With Jesus being the other Paraclete who is now with the Father:

“My little children, I am writing these things to you so that you may not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have _an advocate with the Father (parakleton… pros ton patera)_ , Jesus Christ the righteous one.” 1 John 2:1 Mounce

“But the rulers of this world have not understood it; if they had, they would not have crucified our **glorious** Lord (_ton kyrion tes_** _doxes_**).” 1 Corinthians 2:8 [_NLT_](<https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Corinthians%202%3A7-9&version=NLT>)

“My brothers, stop holding the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ, **the Glory** (_ton kyrion hemon 'Iesou Christou,_**_tes doxes_**), with partiality.” James 2:1 [_WPNT_](<https://studybible.info/WPNT/James%202:1>)

Here’s another translation:

“My brothers, as you believe in our Lord Jesus Christ, **who is the Glory** , pay no servile regard to people.” [_Moffatt_](<https://studybible.info/Moffatt/James%202:1>)

Once again, if we apply the logic of these Muslims to the aforementioned texts than this means Jesus is also Muhammad/Ahmad!

This leaves us with Jesus and the Holy Spirit both being Muhammad and Ahmad, since both of them are described as glorious and are both said to be the Paraclete.
