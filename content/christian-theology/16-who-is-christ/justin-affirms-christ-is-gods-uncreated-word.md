# Justin Affirms: Christ is God’s Uncreated Word

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 30, 2025
  * 7 min read


Updated: Oct 30, 2025

In this short post I will cite from St. Justin’s discussion with Trypho the Jew in order to silence the heretics who claim that this blessed martyr taught that Christ is an inferior creature, a secondary god, whom the Father brought into existence. The quotes from Justin prove that he explicitly taught that Christ was already existing before all creatures, and therefore was not a created being. This holy martyr proclaims that the Person of Jesus is God’s Rational Power who sprung forth from within God himself prior to the creation of any created thing. All emphasis will be mine. 

  


Chapter 61. Wisdom is begotten of the Father, as fire from fire

  


**Justin:** I shall give you another testimony, my friends, from the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**,****_that God begot BEFORE ALL CREATURES a Beginning, [who was] a certain rational power [proceeding] FROM HIMSELF_****, who is called by the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**, now the Glory of the Lord, now the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, again Wisdom, again an Angel, then**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and then Lord and**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) ; and on another occasion **He calls Himself Captain, when He appeared in**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)****[**_form_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06137b.htm>)**to Joshua the son of Nave (Nun)****_._** For He can be called by all those names, since He ministers to the Father’s will, and since He was begotten of the Father by an act of will; just as we see happening among ourselves: **for when we give out some word, we beget the word; yet not by abscission, so as to lessen the word [which remains] in us, when we give it out** : and just as we see also happening in the case of a fire, which is not lessened when it has kindled [another], but remains the same; and that which has been kindled by it likewise appears to exist by itself, not diminishing that from which it was kindled. **The Word of Wisdom, who is Himself this God begotten of the Father of all things** , and Word, and Wisdom, and Power, and the Glory of the Begetter, **will bear evidence to me,****_when He speaks by Solomon the following_** :

_If I shall declare to you what happens daily, I shall call to mind events from everlasting, and review them. The Lord made me the beginning of His ways for His works. From everlasting He established me in the beginning, before He had made the earth, and before He had made the deeps, before the springs of the waters had issued forth, before the mountains had been established. Before all the hills He begets me. God made the country, and the_[ __desert__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04749a.htm>) _, and the highest inhabited places under the sky. When He made ready the heavens, I was along with Him, and when He set up His throne on the winds: when He made the high clouds strong, and the springs of the deep safe, when He made the foundations of the earth, I was with Him arranging. I was that in which He rejoiced; daily and at all times I delighted in His countenance, because He delighted in the finishing of the habitable world, and delighted in the sons of men. Now, therefore, O son, hear me. Blessed is the man who shall listen to me, and the mortal who shall keep my ways, watching daily at my doors, observing the posts of my ingoings. For my outgoings are the outgoings of life, and [my] will has been prepared by the Lord. But they who_[ __sin__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _against me, trespass against their own_[ __souls__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) _; and they who_[ __hate__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) _me_[ __love__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) _death._

Chapter 62. The words  _Let Us make man_ agree with the testimony of Proverbs

**Justin:** And the same sentiment was expressed, my friends, by the word of God [written] by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , when it indicated to us, **with regard to Him whom it has pointed out, that God speaks in the**[** _creation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm>)**of**[** _man_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**** with the very same design, in the following words:

_‘Let Us make man after our image and likeness. And let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the heaven, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over all the creeping things that creep on the earth. And God created man: after the image of God did He create him; male and female created He them. And God blessed them, and said, Increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and have power over it.’_

And that you may not change the [force of the] words just quoted, **and repeat what your teachers assert** — either that God said to Himself, ‘Let Us make,’ just as we, when about to do something, oftentimes say to ourselves, ‘Let us make;’ or that God spoke to the elements, to wit, the earth and other similar substances of which we [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) man was formed, ‘Let Us make,’— I shall quote again the words narrated by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) himself, **from which we can indisputably learn that [God] conversed with some one who was numerically distinct from Himself,****_and also a rational Being_**. These are the words: ‘ _And God said, Behold,_[__Adam__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) _has become as one of us, to_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _good and_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _._ ‘ [ _Genesis 3:22_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen003.htm#verse22>) In saying, therefore, ‘as one of us,’ [[**_Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)**] has declared that [there is a certain] number of**[** _persons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>)**associated with one another, and that they are at least two**. For I would not say that **the**[** _dogma_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05089a.htm>)**of that**[** _heresy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**which is said to be among you is**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**, or that the teachers of it can prove that [God] spoke to**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , or that the [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) frame was the workmanship of [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). ** _But this Offspring, which was_**[** _truly_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** _BROUGHT FORTH FROM THE FATHER, WAS WITH the Father BEFORE ALL CREATURES, and the Father COMMUNED WITH HIM_** ; even as the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) by Solomon has made clear, **that He whom Solomon calls Wisdom, was begotten as a Beginning**** _BEFORE ALL HIS CREATURES_****and as Offspring by**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , who has also declared this same thing in the revelation made by Joshua the son of Nave (Nun). Listen, therefore, to the following from the book of Joshua, that what I say may become manifest to you; it is this:

  


_‘And it came to pass, when Joshua was near_[ __Jericho__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08339a.htm>) _, he lifted up his eyes, and sees a man standing over against him. And Joshua approached to Him, and said, Are you for us, or for our adversaries? And He said to him, I am Captain of the Lord’s host: now have I come. And Joshua fell on his face on the ground, and said to Him, Lord, what do You command Your servant? And the Lord’s Captain says to Joshua, Loose the shoes off your feet; for the place whereon you stand is_[ __holy__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) _ground. And_[ __Jericho__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08339a.htm>) _was shut up and fortified, and no one went out of it. And the Lord said to Joshua, Behold, I give into your hand_[ __Jericho__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08339a.htm>) _, and its king, [and] its mighty men.’_(_Dialogue with Trypho the Jew_ , [**_Chapters 55-68_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/01285.htm>))

Chapter 100. In what sense Christ is [called] Jacob, and Israel, and Son of Man

**Justin:** Then what follows—’But You, the praise of [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) , inhabit the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) place’— declared that He is to do something worthy of praise and wonderment, being about to rise again from the dead on the third day after the crucifixion; and this He has obtained from the Father. For I have showed already that [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) is called both Jacob and [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) ; and I have [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) that it is not in the blessing of Joseph and Judah alone that what relates to Him was proclaimed mysteriously, but also in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) it is written that He said: ‘All things are delivered unto me by My Father.’ and, ‘No man [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the Father but the Son; nor the Son but the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and they to whom the Son will reveal Him.’ [ _Matthew 11:27_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat011.htm#verse27>) Accordingly He revealed to us all that we have perceived by His [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) out of the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , **so that we**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**Him to be the first-begotten of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**,****_and to be BEFORE ALL CREATURES_** ; likewise to be the Son of the patriarchs, since He assumed flesh by the Virgin of their [ _family_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>) , and submitted to become a man without comeliness, dishonoured, and subject to suffering. Hence, also, among His words He said, when He was discoursing about His future sufferings: ‘The [ _Son of man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) must suffer many things, and be rejected by the [ _Pharisees_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11789b.htm>) and [ _Scribes_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13634a.htm>) , and be crucified, and on the third day rise again.’ [ _Matthew 16:21_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat016.htm#verse21>) He said then that He was the [ _Son of man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) , ** _either because of His birth by the Virgin, WHO WAS, as I said, OF THE FAMILY OF DAVID and Jacob, and Isaac, and_**[** _Abraham_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) ; or because Adam was the father both of Himself and of those who have been first enumerated from whom Mary derives her descent. For we [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that the fathers of [ _women_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) are the fathers likewise of those children whom their daughters bear. For [Christ] called one of His [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) — previously [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) by the name of Simon — Peter; since he recognised Him to be Christ the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , by the revelation of His Father: **and since we find it recorded in the memoirs of His**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**that He is the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, and since we call Him the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, we have understood**** _that He PROCEEDED BEFORE ALL CREATURES FROM THE FATHER_****by His power and will (for He is addressed in the writings of the**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**in one way or another as Wisdom, and the Day, and the East, and a Sword, and a Stone, and a Rod, and Jacob, and**[** _Israel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>)**); and that He became man by the Virgin** , in order that the disobedience which proceeded from the serpent might receive its destruction in the same manner in which it derived its origin. For Eve, who was a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) and undefiled, having conceived the word of the serpent, brought forth disobedience and death. But the Virgin Mary received [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) and [ _joy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) , when the [ _angel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) Gabriel announced the good tidings to her that the [ _Spirit of the Lord_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) would come upon her, and the power of the Highest would overshadow her: wherefore also the Holy Thing begotten of her is the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) ; and she replied, ‘Be it unto me according to your word.’ [ _Luke 1:38_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/luk001.htm#verse38>) And by her has He been born, to whom we have [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) so many Scriptures refer, and by whom God destroys both the serpent and those [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) and men who are like him; but works deliverance from death to those who repent of their [ _wickedness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) and [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) upon Him. ([**_Chapters 89-108_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916052717/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/01287.htm>))
