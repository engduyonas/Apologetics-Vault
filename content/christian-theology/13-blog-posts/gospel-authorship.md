# GOSPEL AUTHORSHIP

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 20 min read


In this post I share some of the earliest surviving evidence for the authorship of the Gospel. In one of the references, I quote Irenaeus who was a disciple of Polycarp, who himself was a disciple of the Apostles such as the Apostle John. This is significant since it provides an unbroken historical chain that goes back to the very eyewitnesses of the Lord Jesus Christ. All emphasis will be mine.

  


[**_The Muratorian Fragment c. 170 AD_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/http://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/muratorian-metzger.html>)

  


The Muratorian Fragment is the oldest known list of New Testament books. It was discovered by Ludovico Antonio Muratori in a manuscript in the Ambrosian Library in Milan, and published by him in 1740. * It is called a fragment because the beginning of it is missing. Although the manuscript in which it appears was copied during the seventh century, **the list itself is dated to about 170 because its author refers to the episcopate of Pius I of Rome (died 157) as recent**. He mentions only two epistles of John, without describing them. The Apocalypse of Peter is mentioned as a book which “some of us will not allow to be read in church.” A very helpful and detailed discussion of this document is to be found in Bruce Metzger’s The Canon of the New Testament (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1987), pp. 191-201. Below is Metzger’s English translation of a critically amended text of the Fragment, from Appendix IV of the same book (pp. 305-7). I include Metzger’s footnotes, with their original enumeration, and add some supplementary footnotes of my own. —M.D.M.

1 The meaning may be that Mark arranged the material of his Gospel in the order indicated by Peter, who was participant in the events narrated.

  


2 The reading of the Fragment, quasi ut uris studiosum, ‘as so to speak, one zealous for (or, learned in) the law,’ has been variously interpreted and/or emended. For example, Routh took iuris as translating του δικαιου, i.e. Luke was studious of righteousness; Buchanan replaced ut iuris with adiutorem, ‘assistant’; Bartlet supposed that the translator read νοσου as νομου (Luke was ‘a student of disease’); Zahn replaced ut iuris with itineris, thereby referring to Luke’s readiness to accompany Paul on his journeys; Lietzmann conjectured litteris, i.e. Luke was well versed as an author. Harnack (Sitzungsberichte der königlich Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften [1903], p. 213) and Ehrhardt (op. cit.), who retain iuris studiosus of the Fragment, have pointed out that in technical language of Roman law this could refer to an assesor or legal expert who served on the staff of a Roman official. Although this title was current prior to the time of Justinian’s Digest (published in 533) and so was available to the translator of the Fragment, it is anybody’s guess what Greek phrase it represented—assuming, of course, that the Canon was drawn up originally in Greek (unfortunately no help is provided in David Magie, De Romanorum iuris publici sacrisque vocabulis sollemnibus in Graecum sermonem conversis [Leipzig, 1905]).

**Irenaeus c. 180 AD**

  


[** _Chapter 1_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103301.htm>) The apostles did not commence to preach the Gospel, or to place anything on record, until they were endowed with the gifts and power of the Holy Spirit. They preached one God alone, maker of heaven and earth…

1\. We have learned from none others the plan of our [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) , than from those through whom the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) has come down to us, which they did at one time proclaim in public, and, at a later period, by the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , handed down to us in the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , to be the ground and pillar of our [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). For it is unlawful to assert that they preached before they possessed perfect [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , as some do even venture to say, boasting themselves as improvers of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). For, after our Lord rose from the dead, [the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)] were invested with power from on high when the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) came down [upon them], were filled from all [His gifts], and had perfect [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) : they departed to the ends of the earth, preaching the glad tidings of the [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) things [sent] from God to us, and proclaiming the peace of heaven to [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , who indeed do all equally and individually possess the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **Matthew also issued a written**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**among the Hebrews in their own dialect** , while Peter and [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) were preaching at [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) , and laying the foundations of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). After their departure, Mark, **the**[** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**and interpreter of Peter, did also hand down to us in writing what had been preached by Peter**. Luke also, the companion of [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , **recorded in a book the**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**preached by him**. Afterwards, John, the [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of the Lord, **who also had leaned upon His breast** , did himself publish a [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) during his residence at [ _Ephesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) in Asia. (Against Heresies, Book 3)

  


**Tertullian c. 207 AD**

  


Chapter 2. St. Luke’s Gospel, Selected by Marcion as His Authority, and Mutilated by Him. The Other Gospels Equally Authoritative. Marcion’s Terms of Discussion, However, Accepted, and Grappled with on the Footing of St. Luke’s Gospel Alone.

You have now our answer to the  _Antitheses_ compendiously indicated by us. I pass on to give a [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) of the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) — not, to be sure, of Jewry, but of [ _Pontus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12234c.htm>) — having become meanwhile adulterated; and this shall indicate the order by which we proceed. We lay it down as our first position, that the evangelical Testament has [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) for its authors, to whom was assigned by the Lord Himself this office of publishing the gospel. Since, however, there are apostolic men also, they are yet not alone, but appear with [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) and after [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) ; because the preaching of [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) might be open to the suspicion of an affectation of [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) , if there did not accompany it the authority of the masters, which means that of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , for it was that which made the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) their masters. **Of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, therefore, John and Matthew first instil**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**into us; while of apostolic men, Luke and Mark renew it afterwards. These all start with the same principles of the**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**, so far as relates to the one only God the Creator and His Christ, how that He was born of the Virgin, and came to fulfil the law and the**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**.** Never mind if there does occur some variation in the order of their narratives, provided that there be agreement in the essential matter of the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , in which there is disagreement with [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>). [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , on the other hand, you must [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , ascribes no author to his [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , as if it could not be allowed him to affix a title to that from which it was no crime (in his eyes) to subvert the very body. And here I might now make a stand, and contend that a work ought not to be recognised, which holds not its head erect, which exhibits no consistency, which gives no promise of credibility from the fullness of its title and the just profession of its author. But we prefer to join issue on every point; nor shall we leave unnoticed what may fairly be understood to be on our side.

  


Now, of the authors whom we possess, [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) seems to have singled out Luke for his mutilating process. Luke, however, was not an [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , but only an apostolic man; not a master, but a [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , and so inferior to a master — at least as far subsequent to him as the apostle whom he followed (and that, no [ _doubt_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) , was [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ) was subsequent to the others; so that, had [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) even published his [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) in the name of [ _St. Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) himself, the single authority of the document, destitute of all support from preceding authorities, would not be a sufficient basis for our [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). There would be still wanted that [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) which [ _St. Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) found in [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , to which he yielded his belief, and with which he so earnestly wished his own to agree, that he actually on that account went up to Jerusalem to [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) and consult the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , lest he should run, or had been running in vain; [ _Galatians 2:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal002.htm#verse2>) in other words, that the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) which he had learned, and the gospel which he was preaching, might be in accordance with theirs. Then, at last, having conferred with the (primitive) authors, and having agreed with them touching the rule of [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , they joined their hands in fellowship, and divided their labours thenceforth in the office of preaching the gospel, so that they were to go to the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , and [ _St. Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) to the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) and the [ _Gentiles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>). Inasmuch, therefore, as the enlightener of St. Luke himself desired the authority of his predecessors for both his own [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) and preaching, how much more may not I require for Luke’s [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) that which was necessary for the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of his master…

  


Chapter 5. By the Rule of Antiquity, the Catholic Gospels are Found to Be True, Including the Real St. Luke’s. Marcion’s Only a Mutilated Edition. The Heretic’s Weakness and Inconsistency in Ignoring the Other Gospels.

  


On the whole, then, if that is evidently more [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) which is earlier, if that is earlier which is from the very beginning, if that is from the beginning which has the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) for its authors, then it will certainly be quite as evident, that that comes down from the apostles, which has been kept as a sacred deposit in the churches of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). Let us see what milk the Corinthians drank from [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ; to what rule  _of_[ __faith__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) the Galatians were brought for correction; what the Philippians, the Thessalonians, the Ephesians read  _by it_ ; what utterance also the Romans give, so very near (to the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)), to whom Peter and [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) conjointly bequeathed the gospel even sealed with their own blood. We have also  _St._ John’s foster churches. For although [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) rejects his Apocalypse, the order of the [ _bishops_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) (thereof), when traced up to their origin, will yet rest on John as their author. In the same manner is recognised the excellent source of the other churches. I say, therefore, that in them (and not simply such of them as were founded by [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , but in all those which are united with them in the fellowship of the [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)  _of the gospel of Christ_ ) that [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of Luke which we are defending with all our might has stood its ground from its very first publication; whereas [ _Marcion’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) is not [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to most people, and to none whatever is it [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) without being at the same time condemned. It too, of course, has its churches, but specially its own — as late as they are spurious; and should you want to [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) their original, you will more easily discover [ _apostasy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01624b.htm>) in it than apostolicity, with [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) forsooth as their founder, or some one of [ _Marcion’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) swarm. Even wasps make combs; so also these Marcionites make churches.

  


**The same authority of the**[** _apostolic churches_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01634a.htm>)**will afford evidence to the other**[** _Gospels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**also, which we possess equally through their means, and according to their usage — I mean the**[** _Gospels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**of John and Matthew — while that which Mark published may be affirmed to be Peter’s whose interpreter Mark was. For even Luke’s form of the**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**men usually ascribe to**[** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**. And it may well seem that the works which**[** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**publish belong to their masters. Well, then,**[**_Marcion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>)**ought to be called to a strict account concerning these (other**[** _Gospels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**) also, for having omitted them, and insisted in preference on Luke; as if they, too, had not had free course in the**[** _churches_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, as well as Luke’s**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**, from the beginning. Nay, it is even more credible that they**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**from the very beginning; for, being the work of**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, they were prior, and coeval in origin with the churches themselves**. But how comes it to pass, if the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) published nothing, that their [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) were more forward in such a work; for they could not have been [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , without any instruction from their masters? If, then, it be evident that these ([_Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)) also were current in the [ _churches_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , why did not [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) touch them — either to amend them if they were adulterated, or to acknowledge them if they were uncorrupt? For it is but natural that they who were perverting the gospel, should be more solicitous about the perversion of those things whose authority they [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to be more generally received. Even the false [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) (were so called) on this very account, because they imitated the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) by means of their falsification. In  _as_ far, then, as he might have amended what there was to amend, if found corrupt, in  _so_ far did he firmly imply that all was free from corruption which he did not think required amendment. In short, he simply amended what he thought was corrupt; though, indeed, not even this [ _justly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) , because it was not really corrupt. For if the ([_Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)) of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) have come down to us in their integrity, while Luke’s, which is received among us, so far accords with their rule as to be on a par with them in permanency of reception in the [ _churches_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , it clearly follows that Luke’s [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) also has come down to us in like integrity until the [ _sacrilegious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13321a.htm>) treatment of [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>). In short, when [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) laid hands on it, it then became diverse and hostile to the [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). I will therefore advise his followers, that they either change these [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , however late to do so, into a conformity with their own, whereby they may seem to be in agreement with the apostolic writings (for they are daily retouching their work, as daily they are convicted by us); or else that they blush for their master, who stands self-condemned either way — when once he hands on the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of the gospel [ _conscience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>) smitten, or again subverts it by shameless tampering. Such are the summary arguments which we use, when we take up arms against [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) for the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) of the gospel, maintaining both that order of periods, which rules that a late date is the mark of forgers, and that authority of churches which lends support to the tradition of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) ; because [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) must needs precede the [ _forgery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06135b.htm>) , and proceed straight from those by whom it has been handed on. (Against Marcion, [_Book IV_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/03124.htm>))

  


**Origen c. 245** **AD**

  


“Concerning **the four Gospels** **which alone are uncontroverted in the Church of God under heaven** , I have learned by tradition that the Gospel according to Matthew, **who was at one time a publican and afterwards an Apostle of Jesus Christ** , was written first and that he composed it in the Hebrew tongue and published it for the converts from Judaism. The second written was that according to Mark, **who wrote it according to the instruction of Peter, who, in his General Epistle, acknowledged him as a son** , saying, ‘The church that is in Babylon, elect together with you, salutes you and so does Mark my son.’ And third, was that according to Luke, **the Gospel commended by Paul,** which he composed for the converts from the Gentiles. **Last of all, that according to John** “. (Commentary on Matthew, [_Book I_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/101601.htm>) Fragment)

  


**Eusebius c. 324**

  


Eusebius in his church history mentioned Papias, who was a disciple of the Apostles and the Bishop of Hierapolis (even though Eusebius himself questions that he was). Therefore, Papias is another eyewitness to the authorship of the Gospels.

Chapter 36. Ignatius and His Epistles.

  


1\. At that time [** _Polycarp_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**, a**[** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , was a man of eminence in Asia, having been entrusted with the [ _episcopate_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>) of the [ _church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) of [ _Smyrna_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14060b.htm>) **by those who had seen and heard the**[** _Lord_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>).

  


2\. **And at the same time**[** _Papias_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>)**,**[**_bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of the parish of Hierapolis, became well**[** _known_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**, as did also**[** _Ignatius_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07644a.htm>)**, who was chosen**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of**[** _Antioch_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01570a.htm>)**, second in succession to Peter** , and whose fame is still celebrated by a great many…

Chapter 39. The Writings of Papias.

  


1\. There are extant five books of [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) , **which bear the title Expositions of Oracles of the Lord.**[**_Irenæus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)**makes mention of these as the only works written by him** , in the following words: These things are attested by [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) , **an ancient man who was A HEARER OF JOHN and a companion of**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>) , in his fourth book. For five books have been written by him. These are the words of [ _Irenæus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>).

  


2\. But [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) himself in the preface to his discourses by no means declares that he was himself a hearer and eye-[_witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , **but he shows by the words which he uses that he received the doctrines of the**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**from those who were their friends**.

  


3\. He says: But I shall not hesitate also to put down for you along with my interpretations whatsoever things I have at any time learned carefully from the elders and carefully remembered, guaranteeing their [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). For I did not, like the multitude, take pleasure in those that speak much, but in those that teach the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) ; not in those that relate strange commandments, but in those that deliver the commandments given by the [ _Lord_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) to [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , and springing from the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) itself.

  


4\. If, then, any one came, who had been a follower of the elders, **I questioned him in regard to the words of the elders — what**[** _Andrew_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01471a.htm>)**or what Peter said, or what was said by Philip, or by**[** _Thomas_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14658b.htm>)**, or by James, or by John, or by Matthew, or by any other of the**[** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**of the**[** _Lord_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, and what things Aristion and the**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**John, the**[** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**of the**[** _Lord_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, say. For I did not think that what was to be gotten from the books would profit me as much as what came from the living and abiding voice**.

  


5\. It is worth while observing here that the name John is twice enumerated by him. The first one he mentions in connection with Peter and James and Matthew and the rest of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , clearly meaning the [ _evangelist_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) ; **but the other John he mentions after an interval, and places him among others outside of the number of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, putting Aristion before him, and he distinctly calls him a**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>).

  


6\. **This shows that the statement of those is**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**, who say that there were two**[** _persons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>)**in Asia that bore the same name, and that there were two tombs in**[** _Ephesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>)**, each of which, even to the present day, is called John’s**. It is important to notice this. **For it is probable that it was the second, if one is not willing to admit that it was the first that saw the Revelation, which is ascribed by name to John**.

  


7\. And [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) , of whom we are now speaking, confesses that he received the words of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) from those that followed them, **but says that he was himself a hearer of Aristion and the**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**John. At least he mentions them frequently by name, and gives their traditions in his writings**. These things, we hope, have not been uselessly adduced by us.

  


8\. But it is fitting to subjoin to the words of [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) which have been quoted, other passages from his works in which he relates some other wonderful events which he claims to have received from tradition.

  


9\. That Philip the [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) dwelt at Hierapolis with his daughters has been already stated. But it must be noted here that [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) , their contemporary, says that he heard a wonderful tale **from the daughters of Philip. For he relates that in his time one rose from the dead**. And he tells another wonderful story of Justus, surnamed Barsabbas: that he drank a deadly poison, and yet, by the [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of the [ _Lord_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , suffered no harm.

  


10\. The Book of Acts records that the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) after the ascension of the Saviour, put forward this Justus, together with [ _Matthias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10066a.htm>) , and [ _prayed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) that one might be chosen in place of the traitor Judas, to fill up their number. The account is as follows: And they put forward two, Joseph, called Barsabbas, who was surnamed Justus, and [ _Matthias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10066a.htm>) ; and they [ _prayed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) and said. [ _Acts 1:23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/act001.htm#verse23>)

  


11\. The same writer gives also other accounts **which he says came to him through unwritten tradition** , certain strange [ _parables_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm>) and teachings of the [ _Saviour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and some other more mythical things.

  


12\. To these belong his statement **that there will be a period of some thousand years after the resurrection of the dead, and that the kingdom of Christ will be set up in material form on this very earth**. I suppose he got these ideas through a misunderstanding of the [ _apostolic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) accounts, not perceiving that the things said by them were spoken [ _mystically_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10663b.htm>) in figures.

  


13\. **For he appears to have been of very limited understanding** , as one can see from his discourses. **But it was due to him that so many of the Church Fathers after him adopted a like opinion, urging in their own support the antiquity of the man; as for instance**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)**and any one else that may have proclaimed similar views**.

  


14\. [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) gives also in his own work other accounts of the words of the [ _Lord_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) on the authority of Aristion who was mentioned above, and traditions as handed down by the [ _presbyter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) John; to which we refer those who are fond of learning. But now we must add to the words of his which we have already quoted the tradition which he gives in regard to Mark, the author of the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>).

  


15\. This also the [ _presbyter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) said: **Mark, having become the interpreter of Peter, wrote down accurately, though not in order, whatsoever he remembered of the things said or done by**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). For he neither heard the [ _Lord_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) nor followed him, but afterward, as I said, **he followed Peter, who adapted his teaching to the needs of his hearers** , but with no intention of giving a connected account of the [ _Lord’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) discourses, **so that Mark committed no**[** _error_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>)**while he thus wrote some things as he remembered them**. For he was careful of one thing, **not to omit any of the things which he had heard, and not to state any of them**[** _falsely_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>). These things are related by [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) concerning Mark.

  


16\. But concerning Matthew he writes as follows: **So then Matthew wrote the oracles in the Hebrew language, and every one interpreted them as he was able**. And the same writer uses testimonies from the first Epistle of John and from that of Peter likewise. And he relates another story of a [ _woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) , who was accused of many [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) before the [ _Lord_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , which is contained in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to the Hebrews. These things we have thought it necessary to observe in addition to what has been already stated. (Church History, [**_Book III_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250910174257/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250103.htm>))
