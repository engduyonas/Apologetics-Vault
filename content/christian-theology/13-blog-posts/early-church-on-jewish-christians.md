# EARLY CHURCH ON JEWISH CHRISTIANS

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 2, 2025
  * 23 min read


In this post I will cite from some of the church fathers and apologists of the first four hundred years in relation to their view of Torah keeping, especially in regards to Jewish Christians who continued to observe the Mosaic Law. All emphasis mine.

  


**IGNATIUS**

  


Chapter 8. Caution against false doctrines

  


Be not deceived with [** _strange doctrines_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , nor with old fables, which are unprofitable. **For if we still live according to the**[** _Jewish law_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>) , we acknowledge that we have not received [** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>). For the divinest [** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) lived according to [** _Christ Jesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). On this account also they were [** _persecuted_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) , being inspired by His [** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) to fully convince the unbelieving that there is [** _one God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10499a.htm>) , who has manifested Himself by [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) His [** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , **who is His**[** _eternal Word_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**, not proceeding forth from silence, and who in all things pleased Him that sent Him**.

  


Chapter 9. Let us live with Christ

  


If, therefore, those who were brought up in the ancient order of things have come to the possession of a new [** _hope_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07465b.htm>) , **no longer observing the**[** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**, but living in the observance of the**[** _Lord’s Day_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14335a.htm>) , on which also our life has sprung up again by Him and by His death — whom some deny, by which [** _mystery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) we have obtained [** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , and therefore endure, that we may be found the [** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , our only Master — how shall we be able to live apart from Him, **whose**[** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**the**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**themselves in the**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**did wait for Him as their Teacher?** And therefore He whom they rightly waited for, having come, [** _raised them from the dead_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12792a.htm>). [** _Matthew 27:52_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat027.htm#verse52>)

  


Chapter 10. Beware of Judaizing

  


Let us not, therefore, be insensible to His kindness. **For were He to reward us according to our**[** _works_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01115a.htm>)**, we should cease to be**. Therefore, having become His [** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , **let us learn to live according to the principles of**[** _Christianity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>). For whosoever is called by any other name besides this, is not of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Lay aside, therefore, the [** _evil_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) , the old, the sour leaven, and be changed into the new leaven, which is [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). Be salted in Him, lest any one among you should be corrupted, since by your savour you shall be convicted. **It is absurd to profess**[** _Christ Jesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, and to**[** _Judaize_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08537a.htm>)**. For**[** _Christianity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>)**did not embrace**[** _Judaism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**, but**[** _Judaism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)****[**_Christianity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) , that so every tongue which [** _believes_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) might be gathered together to [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

  


Chapter 11. I write these things to warn you

  


These things [I address to you], my beloved, not that I [** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) any of you to be in such a state; [addicted to the error of Judaizing] but, as less than any of you, I desire to guard you beforehand, that you fall not upon the hooks of [** _vain doctrine_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , but that you attain to full assurance in regard to the birth, and [** _passion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11527b.htm>) , and [** _resurrection_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) which took place in the time of the government of [** _Pontius Pilate_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12083c.htm>) , being truly and certainly accomplished by [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , who is our [** _hope_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07465b.htm>) , [** _1 Timothy 1:1_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti001.htm#verse1>) from which may no one of you ever be turned aside. ([**__Epistle to the Magnesians__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0105.htm>))

  


Chapter 5. Pray for me

  


My brethren, I am greatly enlarged in loving you; and rejoicing exceedingly [over you], I seek to secure your safety. Yet it is not I, but [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , for whose sake being bound I [** _fear_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) the more, inasmuch as I am not yet perfect. But your [** _prayer_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) to God shall make me perfect, that I may attain to that portion which through mercy has been allotted me, while I flee to the [** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) as to the flesh of [** _Jesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and to the [** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) as to the [** _presbytery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>) of the [** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). **And let us also**[** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>)**the**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**, because they too have proclaimed the**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**, and placed their hope in Him, and waited for Him; in whom also believing, they were saved, through union to**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, being**[** _holy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>)**men, worthy of**[** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>)**and admiration, having had**[** _witness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>)**borne to them by**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, and being reckoned along with [us] in the**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**of the common hope**.

  


Chapter 6. Do not accept Judaism

  


**But if any one preach the**[** _Jewish law_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>)**unto you, listen not to him**. For it is better to hearken to [** _Christian_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) doctrine from a man who has been [** _circumcised_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) , than to [** _Judaism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) from one uncircumcised. But if either of such [** _persons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) do not speak concerning [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , they are in my judgment but as monuments and sepulchres of the dead, upon which are written only the names of men. Flee therefore the [** _wicked_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) devices and snares of the prince of this world, lest at any time being conquered by his artifices, grow weak in your [** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>). But be all joined together with an undivided heart. And I thank my God that I have a good [** _conscience_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>) in respect to you, and that no one has it in his power to boast, either privately or publicly, that I have burdened any one either in much or in little. And I wish for all among whom I have spoken, that they may not possess that for a testimony against them. ([**__Epistle to the Philadelphians__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0108.htm>))

  


**JUSTIN MARTYR**

  


Chapter 45. Those who were righteous before and under the law shall be saved by Christ

  


**Trypho:** If I seem to interrupt these matters, which you say must be investigated, yet the question which I mean to put is urgent. Allow me first.

  


**Justin:** Ask whatever you please, as it occurs to you; and I shall endeavour, after questions and answers, to resume and complete the discourse.

  


**Trypho:** Tell me, then, shall those who lived according to the law given by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , live in the same manner with Jacob, [ _Enoch_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07218a.htm>) , and [ _Noah_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11088a.htm>) , in the resurrection of the dead, or not?

  


**Justin:** When I quoted, sir, the words spoken by Ezekiel, that ‘even if [ _Noah_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11088a.htm>) and Daniel and Jacob were to beg sons and daughters, the request would not be granted them,’ but that each one, that is to say, shall be saved by his own righteousness, I said also, that those who regulated their lives by the [ _law of Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>) would in like manner be saved. For what in the [ _law of Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>) is naturally good, and [ _pious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>) , and righteous, and has been prescribed to be done by those who [ _obey_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) it; and what was appointed to be performed by reason of the hardness of the people’s hearts; was similarly recorded, and done also by those who were under the law. Since those who did that which is universally, naturally, and [ _eternally_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) good are pleasing to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **they shall be saved through this Christ in the resurrection equally with those righteous men who were before them** , namely [ _Noah_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11088a.htm>) , and [ _Enoch_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07218a.htm>) , and Jacob, and whoever else there be, **along with those who have**[** _known_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**this Christ,**[**_Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, who was before the morning star and the moon, and submitted to become incarnate, and be born of this virgin of the**[** _family_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)**of David** , in order that, by this dispensation, the serpent that [ _sinned_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) from the beginning, and the [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) like him, may be destroyed, and that death may be contemned, and for ever quit, **at the second coming of the Christ Himself** , those who [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in Him and live acceptably — and be no more: when some are sent to be punished unceasingly into judgment and condemnation of fire; but others shall exist in freedom from suffering, from corruption, and from grief, and in [ _immortality_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>).

  


Chapter 46. Trypho asks whether a man who keeps the law even now will be saved. Justin proves that it contributes nothing to righteousness

  


**Trypho:** But if some, even now, wish to live in the observance of the institutions given by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , and yet [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in this Jesus who was crucified, recognising Him to be the Christ of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and that it is given to Him to be absolute Judge of all, and that His is the everlasting kingdom, can they also be saved?

  


**Justin:** Let us consider that also together, whether one may now observe all the Mosaic institutions.

  


**Trypho:** No. For we [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that, as you said, it is not possible either anywhere to [ _sacrifice_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) the lamb of the passover, or to offer the goats ordered for the fast; or, in short, [to present] all the other offerings.

  


**Justin:** Tell [me] then yourself, I [ _pray_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) , some things which can be observed; for you will be persuaded that, though a man does not keep or has not performed the [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) decrees, he may assuredly be saved.

  


**Trypho:** **To keep the**[** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) , to be [ _circumcised_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) , to observe months, and to be washed if you touch anything prohibited by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , or after sexual intercourse.

  


**Justin:** Do you think that [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Isaac, Jacob, [ _Noah_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11088a.htm>) , and Job, and all the rest before or after them equally righteous, also Sarah the wife of [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Rebekah the wife of Isaac, Rachel the wife of Jacob, and Leah, and all the rest of them, until the mother of [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) the faithful servant, **who observed none of these [statutes], will be saved?**

  


**Trypho:** Were not [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) and his descendants [ _circumcised_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>)?

  


**Justin:** I [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) and his descendants were [ _circumcised_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>). The reason why [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) was given to them I stated at length in what has gone before; and if what has been said does not convince you, let us again search into the matter. But you are aware that, up to [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , no one in fact who was righteous observed any of these [ _rites_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13064b.htm>) at all of which we are talking, or received one commandment to observe, except that of [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) , which began from [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>).

  


**Trypho:** We [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) it, **and admit that they are saved**.

  


**Justin:** You perceive that God by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) **laid all such ordinances upon you on account of the hardness of your people’s hearts** , in order that, by the large number of them, you might keep God continually, and in every action, before your eyes, and never begin to act [ _unjustly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08010c.htm>) or impiously. For He enjoined you to place around you [a fringe] of purple dye, [ _Numbers 15:38_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/num015.htm#verse38>) in order that you might not forget [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and He commanded you to wear a phylactery, [ _Deuteronomy 6:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu006.htm#verse6>) certain characters, which indeed we consider [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) , being engraved on very thin parchment; and by these means stirring you up to retain a constant remembrance of God: at the same time, however, convincing you, that in your hearts you have not even a faint remembrance of God’s worship. Yet not even so were you dissuaded from [ _idolatry_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>) : for in the times of Elijah, when [God] recounted the number of those who had not bowed the knee to [ _Baal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02175a.htm>) , He said the number was seven thousand; and in Isaiah He rebukes you for having [ _sacrificed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) your children to [ _idols_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>). But we, **because we refuse to**[** _sacrifice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)**to those to whom we were of old accustomed to**[** _sacrifice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)**, undergo extreme penalties, and**[** _rejoice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>)**in death** — believing that God will raise us up by His Christ, **and will make us incorruptible, and undisturbed, and**[** _immortal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) ; and we [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that the ordinances imposed by reason of the hardness of your people’s hearts, contribute nothing to the performance of righteousness and of [ _piety_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>).

  


Chapter 47. Justin communicates with Christians who observe the law. Not a few Catholics do otherwise

  


**Trypho:** But if some one, [ _knowing_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that this is so, after he recognises that this man is Christ, and has [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in and obeys Him, wishes, however, to observe these [institutions], will he be saved?

  


**Justin:** **In my opinion, Trypho, such an one will be saved, if he does not strive in every way to persuade other men** **— I mean those**[** _Gentiles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) who have been [ _circumcised_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) from [ _error_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) by Christ, to observe the same things as himself, telling them that they will not be saved unless they do so. This you did yourself at the commencement of the discourse, when you declared that I would not be saved unless I observe these institutions.

  


**Trypho:** Why then have you said, ‘In my opinion, such an one will be saved,’ unless there are some who affirm that such will not be saved?

  


**Justin:** There are such people, Trypho, **and these do not venture to have any intercourse with or to extend hospitality to such persons; but I do not agree with them**. But if some, through weak-mindedness, wish to observe such institutions as were given by Moses, from which they expect some virtue, **but which we believe were appointed by reason of the hardness of the people’s hearts** , along with their hope in this Christ, and [wish to perform] the eternal and natural acts of righteousness and piety, **yet choose to live with the Christians and the faithful, as I said before, not inducing them either to be circumcised like themselves, or to keep the Sabbath, or to observe any other such ceremonies, then I hold that we ought to join ourselves to such, and associate with them in all things as kinsmen and brethren**. But if, Trypho, some of your race, who say they believe in this Christ, **compel those Gentiles who believe in this Christ to live in all respects according to the law given by Moses, or choose not to associate so intimately with them, I in like manner do not approve of them**. But I believe that even those, who have been persuaded by them to observe the legal dispensation along with their confession of God in Christ, shall probably be saved. And I hold, further, that such as have confessed and known this man to be Christ, yet who have gone back from some cause to the legal dispensation, and have denied that this man is Christ, **and have repented not before death, shall by no means be saved**. Further, I hold that those of the seed of Abraham who live according to the law, and do not believe in this Christ before death, **shall likewise not be saved** , and especially those who have anathematized and do anathematize this very Christ in the synagogues, and everything by which they might obtain salvation and escape the vengeance of fire. For the goodness and the loving-kindness of God, and His boundless riches, hold righteous and sinless the man who, as Ezekiel tells, repents of sins; and reckons sinful, unrighteous, and impious the man who fails away from piety and righteousness to unrighteousness and ungodliness. Wherefore also our Lord Jesus Christ said, ‘In whatsoever things I shall take you, in these I shall judge you.’” (Dialogue with Trypho the Jew, [**_Chapters 31-47_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/01283.htm>))

[** _Isaiah 29:13_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa029.htm#verse13>) Now assuredly, Trypho, [the [** _proof_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>)] that this man is the Christ of God does not fail, though I be unable to prove **that He**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**formerly as Son of the Maker of all things, being**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and was born a man by the**[ _Virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>). **_4\. Epiphanius._**

  


When we come to Epiphanius (born and raised in Palestine), we finally get a by-name mention of the Nazarenes. His Panarion (generally known as the Refutation of All Heresies) was written during the period 374-376. Panarion 29 is a rather extensive treatment of his sources and data on the Nazarenes, and the salient facts about them are listed below:

  


a. The use both the Old and New Testaments, without excluding any books known to Epiphanius (7,2):

  


“For they use not only the New Testament but also the Old, like the Jews. For the Legislation and the Prophets and the Scriptures, which are called the Bible by the Jews, are not rejected by them as they are by those mentioned above [Manicheans, Marcionites, Gnostics]. “

  


b. They have a good knowledge of Hebrew and read the OT and at least one gospel in that language (7,4; 9,4):

  


“They a good mastery of the Hebrew language. For the entire Law and the Prophets and what is called the Scriptures, I mention the poetical books, Kings, Chronicles and Ester and all the others, are read by them in Hebrew as in the case with the Jews, of course.”

  


“They have the entire Gospel of Matthew in Hebrew. It is carefully preserved by them in Hebrew letters.”

  


c. They believe in the resurrection of the dead (7,3):

  


“For they also accept the resurrection of the dead “

  


d. They believe that God is the creator of all things (7,3):

  


“…and that everything has its origin in God”

  


e. They believe in One God and His Son Jesus Christ (remember the patristic defn. of divine Son!) (7,3; 7,5):

  


“They proclaim one God and his Son Jesus Christ.”

  


“Only in this respect they differ from the Jews and Christians: with the Jews they do not agree because of their belief in Christ, with the Christians because they are trained in the Law, in circumcision, the Sabbath, and the other things.” (Note how significant this is–they did NOT differ from Christians in Christology! This demonstrates a High Christology on their part!).

  


f. They observe the Law of Moses (7,5; 5,4; 8,1ff)

  


“Only in this respect they differ from the Jews and Christians: with the Jews they do not agree because of their belief in Christ, with the Christians because they are trained in the Law, in circumcision, the Sabbath, and the other things.”

  


“By birth they are Jews and they dedicate themselves to the Law and submit to circumcision.”

  


g. They are hated by the Jews and are officially ostracized in the synagogue prayer–probably the birkat ha-minim (9,2-3):

  


“However, they are very much hated by the Jews. For not only the Jewish children cherish hate against them but the people also stand up in the morning, at noon, and in the evening, three times a day and they pronounce curses and maledictions over them when they say their prayers in the synagogues. Three times a day they say: ‘May God curse the Nazarenes.’ For they are more hostile against them because they proclaim as Jews that Jesus is the Christ.”

  


[Ray] Pritz summarizes the data from the most important section of Epiphanius (Panarion 29,7) [[_NT:NJC:_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://christianthinktank.com/bookabs.html#NT:NJC>) 44]:

  


“The data in this section present us with a body in every way ‘orthodox’ except for its adherence to the Law of Moses. If we remember that the Jewish Church of Jerusalem also kept the Law through the period covered by the books of Acts, then we have a picture of the earliest Jewish Christian community…The picture is not full, certainly, but what we are given in very way confirms the identity of the Nazarenes as the heirs of the earliest Jerusalem congregation.”

  


Thus, Epiphanius is our first source on the Nazarenes, and he describes them as decidedly orthodox in all matters (including the deity of Christ), except that of observance of Jewish customs.

  


**_5\. Jerome_**

  


Jerome is one of our more important sources, especially since he quotes from Nazarene written works. Let’s look at his testimony about them first.

  


“They believe in Christ, the Son of God, born of Mary the Virgin, and they say about him that he suffered under Pontius Pilate and rose again” (Epis. To Augustine, 112,13)

  


From their commentary on Isaiah at 29.17-21, the Nazarenes accuse the Scribes and Pharisees that they ‘made men sin against the Word of God in order that they should deny that Christ was the Son of God’

  


Also from the commentary, at 31.6-9: “The Nazarenes understand this passage in this way: O sons of Israel, who deny the Son of God with such hurtful resolution'”

Pritz summarizes [[_NT:NJC:_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://christianthinktank.com/bookabs.html#NT:NJC>) 55]:

  


“According to Jerome, then, Nazarene Christology is basically what we have noted previously, a belief in the divine origins and virgin birth of Jesus in accordance with accepted doctrines of the great Church. Here we also see an express avowal of Jesus’ death and resurrection.

  


In Jerome’s commentary on Isaiah, he gives 5 citations or comments from a Nazarene commentary on the book. These five selections preserve some interesting data about the group:

  


1\. (on Isaiah 8.14): “The Nazarenes, who accept Christ in such a way that they do not cease to observe the old law, explain the two houses as the two families, viz. Of Shammai and Hillel, from whom originated the Scribes and the Pharisees.” [In this passage, Jerome does not even hint at censure of the Nazarenes, but rather uses them as a source. The data in the subsequent Nazarene discussion of the Shammai and Hillel show that they had significant antipathy toward the rabbis.]

  


2\. (on Isaiah 8.20-21). “For the rest the Nazarenes explain the passage in this way: when the Scribes and Pharisees tell you to listen to them, men who do everything for the love of the belly and who hiss during their incantations in the way of magicians in order to deceive you, you must answer them like this…” [Note again the strong anti-rabbinical polemic, and the appeal to scripture–not halakah–for proof.]

  


3\. (on Isaiah 9.1-4). “The Nazarenes, whose opinion I have set forth above, try to explain this passage in the following way: When Christ came and this preaching shone out, the land of Zebulon and Naphtali first of all were freed from the errors of the Scribes and Pharisees and he shook off their shoulders the very heavy yoke of the Jewish traditions. Later, however, the preaching became more dominant, that means the preaching was multiplied, through the Gospel of the apostle Paul who was the last of all the apostles. And the Gospel of Christ shone to the most distant tribes and the way of the whole sea. Finally the whole world, which earlier walked or sat in darkness and was imprisoned in the bonds of idolatry and death, has seen the clear light of the Gospel.”

  


This is a crucial passage and Pritz’ careful statement brings out the import and implications [[_NT:NJC:_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://christianthinktank.com/bookabs.html#NT:NJC>) 64-65]:

  


“Let us note once again the polemic against Scribes and Pharisees and the Jewish traditions. The two most significant things about this excerpt from the Nazarene work are its positive view of Paul, and the refusal to bind Gentile Christians to keeping the Law. We see here that the Nazarene view of Paul’s mission corresponded very closely to that of Paul himself (Gal 2.2-9). In none of the remains of Nazarene doctrine can one find a clear rejection of Paul or his mission or his message. This, of course, is quite the opposite of what we usually hear described as ‘Jewish Christian,’ which almost by definition opposes itself to Paul. What we have here, then, is an endorsement of Paul’s mission to the Gentiles. This spreading of the Gospel to the Gentiles was, according to the Nazarenes, a natural, even a glorious development. One is often led to expect a sort of bitterness on the part of the Jewish Christians that they were swamped, their position usurped by the Gentile Church. But here we find only a positive reaction to the flow events.”

  


Needless to say, this data about the pro-Pauline and high-Christology Nazarenes would not sit well with Jochen’s disputant!

  


4\. (on Isaiah 29.20-21): “What we have understood to have been written about the devil and his angels, the Nazarenes believe to have been said against the Scribes and the Pharisees…who made men sin against the Word of God in order that they should deny that Christ was the Son of God” [We have referred to this passage earlier, in pointing out that the Nazarx held to a divine Sonship, of patristic content.]

  


5\. (on Isaiah 31.6-9): “The Nazarenes understand this passage in this way: O Sons of Israel, who deny the Son of God with a most vicious opinion, turn to him and his apostles…” [Note the ‘Sonship’ Christology and the evangelistic appeal to their people.]

The data from Jerome is significant for many reasons, but not the least of which is that it contains the self-testimony of the Nazarenes. The excerpts from their Commentary on Isaiah show an incredibly ‘orthodox’, evangelistic, and universal outlook on God’s actions in the world. As such, the best data we have indicates that the Nazarene ‘sect’ was unquestionably mainstream ‘Christian’ and exalted Jesus as the pre-existent and absolutely unique Son of God (in the Patristic understanding).

  


**_6\. Filaster_**

  


Filaster was a bishop who wrote his work (Book of Diverse Heresies) roughly at the same time of Epiphanius. He discusses 156 heresies or heretical teachers (some very borderline heresies, to be sure!), but omits the Nazarenes! Pritz points out this amazing fact, before analysing Filaster sources [[_NT:NJC:_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://christianthinktank.com/bookabs.html#NT:NJC>) 71]:

  


“His diversarum haereseon liber was written in 385…and covered 156 heresies or heretical teachings. The Jewish Christian Nazarene sect is not mentioned by Filaster. This fact naturally causes one to wonder why the Nazarenes were omitted from so extensive a work when Filaster went so far as to condemn even those who differed from the Church only in their belief that the stars occupied fixed positions in the heavens (as against the then-current teaching that God set them in place every evening).”

  


After analyzing Filaster’s sources [Hippolytus, drawing from Ireneaus, drawing from Justin and/or Theophilus of Antioch], Pritz comes to the following position [[_NT:NJC:_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://christianthinktank.com/bookabs.html#NT:NJC>) 75]:

  


“Where does all this leave us? In tracing Filaster’s literary heritage back to near its beginnings, we may at least hazard the suggestion that the earliest hersiographers did not include the Nazarenes for the simple reason that they did not consider them heretics. This, of course, was not true of the offshoot Ebionites, who even by the time of Irenaeus (and earlier Justin, who, however, does not mention them by name) had been recognized as heretics. If we extend this logic into the late fourth century, we arrive at this important conclusion: the lack of polemic against the Nazarenes until the fourth century does not show that they were a later phenomenon; rather, it shows that no one until Epiphanius considered them heretical enough to add them to older catalogues. The very existence of Filaster’s contemporary anti-heretical work with its omission of the Nazarenes in accord with his inherited tradition lends weight to the suggestion that Epiphanius is solely responsible for their inclusion in his own heresiography, and this despite the fact that he could not deny their ancient beginnings. While each author used the lists of his predecessors and added to them where he saw fit, no one until Epiphanius felt it necessary to include the Nazarenes, even though they had existed from the earliest times and their gospel was known.”

  


Unfortunately, this scenario changed with Augustine. He accepted the verdict of Epiphanius, and his authority swayed the rest of the church after that. The confusion of Nazarene and Ebionite in Epiphanius is still operative. So Pritz ([_NT:NJC:_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250907135555/https://christianthinktank.com/bookabs.html#NT:NJC>) 82]:

  


“The most important conclusion of this chapter is that the Nazarenes were not mentioned by earlier fathers not because they did not exist by rather because they were still generally considered to be acceptably orthodox. The history of the Nazarene sect must be clearly distinguished from that of the Ebionites. Once Epiphanius failed to do so, he introduced a confusion which continues to the present day.”

  


** _Summary of the data from the Church Fathers:_**

  


The Nazarene sect is a very orthodox group of believers. They hold to a very high Christology (i.e. virgin birth, pre-existence, more-than-man, divine sonship, deity of Christ). They have a high regard of Paul and the ministry to the Gentiles. They are both in polemic with and witnessing to, the Jewish people in mainline Palestine. They accept the Tanakh/OT and New Testament. They were not considered heretical until the mistake of Epiphanius (who confused them with the Ebionites).
