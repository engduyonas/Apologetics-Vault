# IRENAEUS & THE DEITY OF CHRIST

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 30, 2025
  * 34 min read


Updated: Oct 30, 2025

Irenaeus was the bishop of the church in Lyon, France, and was a disciple of the bishop and martyr Polycarp, who was a disciple of the Apostles, especially John. The bishop of France was himself martyred for his faith in the Lord Jesus.

  


Here are a few snippets from his writings where he bears witness to Jesus Christ being the eternal Word/Son of God who himself is God that became Man, and is the very Creator of all things who came to save the creation that he made: 

  


[** _Chapter 19_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103319.htm>) Jesus Christ was not a mere man, begotten from Joseph in the ordinary course of nature, **but was true God, begotten of the Father Most High** , and true man, born of the Virgin

  


1\. But again, those who assert that He was simply a mere man, begotten by Joseph, remaining in the bondage of the old disobedience, are in a state of death having been not as yet joined to the [** _Word of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) the [** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , nor receiving liberty through the [** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , as He does Himself declare:  _If the Son shall make you free, you shall be free indeed_. [** _John 8:36_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh008.htm#verse36>) But, being [** _ignorant_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) of Him who from the [** _Virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>) is [** _Emmanuel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>) , they are deprived of His gift, which is [** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) life; [** _Romans 6:23_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/rom006.htm#verse23>) and not receiving the incorruptible Word, they remain in mortal flesh, and are debtors to death, not obtaining the antidote of life. To whom **the Word says** , mentioning His own gift of [** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) :  _I said, You are all the sons of the Highest, and gods; but you shall die like men_. **He speaks undoubtedly these words to those who have not received the gift of adoption, but who despise the**[** _incarnation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07706b.htm>)**of the pure generation of the**[** _Word of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) , defraud [** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [** _nature_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of promotion into [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and prove themselves ungrateful to the [** _Word of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) , **who became flesh for them**. For it was for this end **that the**[** _Word of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**was made man, and He who was the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**became the**[** _Son of man_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>)**, that man, having been taken into the Word, and receiving the adoption, might become the**[** _son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). For by no other means could we have attained to incorruptibility and [** _immortality_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) , unless we had been united to incorruptibility and [** _immortality_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>). But how could we be joined to incorruptibility and [** _immortality_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) , unless, first, incorruptibility and [** _immortality_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) had become that which we also are, so that the corruptible might be swallowed up by incorruptibility, and the mortal by [** _immortality_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) , that we might receive the adoption of sons?

  


2\. For this reason [it is, said], Who shall declare His generation? [ _Isaiah 53:8_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/isa053.htm#verse8>) since He is a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and who shall recognise Him? [ _Jeremiah 17:9_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/jer017.htm#verse9>) But he to whom the Father which is in heaven has revealed Him, [ _Matthew 16:16_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat016.htm#verse16>) [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) Him, so that he understands that He who was not born either by the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of the flesh, or by the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , [ _John 1:13_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse13>) is the [ _Son of man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) , this is Christ, the Son of the living God. For I have shown from the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , that no one of the sons of [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) is as to everything, and absolutely, called [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or named Lord. **But that He is Himself in His own right, beyond all**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**who ever lived,**[**_God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and Lord, and King Eternal, and the Incarnate Word** , proclaimed by all the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , and by the Spirit Himself, may be seen by all who have attained to even a small portion of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). Now, the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) would not have testified these things of Him, if, like others, He had been a mere man. But that He had, beyond all others, in Himself that pre-eminent birth which is from the Most High Father, and also experienced that pre-eminent generation which is from the Virgin, [ _Isaiah 7:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/isa007.htm#verse14>) the [ _divine Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) do in both respects testify of Him: also, that He was a man without comeliness, and liable to suffering; [ _Isaiah 53:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/isa053.htm#verse2>) that He sat upon the foal of an ass; [ _Zechariah 9:9_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/zec009.htm#verse9>) that He received for drink, vinegar and gall; that He was despised among the people, and humbled Himself even to death **and that He is the**[** _holy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>)**Lord, the Wonderful, the Counsellor, the Beautiful in appearance, and the Mighty**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**,**[**_Isaiah 9:6_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/isa009.htm#verse6>)**coming on the clouds as the Judge of all**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) ; [ _Daniel 7:13_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/dan007.htm#verse13>) — all these things did the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) prophesy of Him.

  


3\. For as He became man in order to undergo [** _temptation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14504a.htm>) , so also was He the Word that He might be [** _glorified_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) ; the Word remaining quiescent, that He might be capable of being tempted, dishonoured, crucified, and of suffering death, but the [** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [** _nature_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) being swallowed up in it (the divine), when it conquered, and endured [without yielding], and performed acts of kindness, and rose again, and was received up [into heaven]. **He therefore, the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, our Lord, being the Word of the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and the**[** _Son of man_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>)**, since He had a generation as to His**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)****[**_nature_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>)**from Mary** — who was descended from [** _mankind_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and who was herself a [** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) being — was made the [** _Son of man_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>). [** _Isaiah 7:13_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/isa007.htm#verse13>) Wherefore also the Lord Himself gave us a sign, in the depth below, and in the height above, which man did not ask for, because he never expected that a [** _virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) could conceive, **or that it was possible that one remaining a**[** _virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>)**could bring forth a son, and that what was thus born should be**** _God with us_** , and descend to those things which are of the earth beneath, seeking the sheep which had perished, **which was indeed His own peculiar handiwork** , and ascend to the height above, offering and commending to His Father that [** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [** _nature_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) (_hominem_) which had been found, making in His own person the [** _first-fruits_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06082a.htm>) of the resurrection of man; that, as the Head rose from the dead, so also the remaining part of the body — [namely, the body] of everyman who is found in life — when the time is fulfilled of that condemnation which [** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) by reason of disobedience, may arise, blended together and strengthened through means of joints and bands [** _Ephesians 4:16_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/eph004.htm#verse16>) by the increase of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , each of the members having its own proper and fit position in the body. For there are many mansions in the Father’s house, [** _John 14:2_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh014.htm#verse2>) inasmuch as there are also many members in the body. (_Against Heresies_ , Book III)

  


4\. For the one and the same [ _Spirit of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , **who proclaimed by the**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**what and of what sort the advent of the Lord should be** , did by these elders give a [ _just_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) interpretation of what had been [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) prophesied; and He did Himself, by the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , announce that the fullness of the times of the adoption had arrived, that the [ _kingdom of heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>) had drawn near, and that  _He_ was dwelling within those that [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in Him who was born [ _Emmanuel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>) of the [ _Virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>). To this effect they testify, [saying,] that before Joseph had come together with Mary, while she therefore remained in [ _virginity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) , she was found with child of the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) ; [ _Matthew 1:18_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat001.htm#verse18>) and that the [ _angel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) Gabriel said to her, The [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) shall come upon you, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow you; therefore also that [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) thing which shall be born of you shall be called the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) ; [ _Luke 1:35_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/luk001.htm#verse35>) and that the [ _angel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) said to Joseph in a [ _dream_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05154a.htm>) , Now this was done, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Isaiah the [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , Behold, a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) shall be with child. [ _Matthew 1:23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat001.htm#verse23>) But the elders have thus interpreted what Esaias said: And the Lord, moreover, said to Ahaz, Ask for yourself a sign from the Lord your God out of the depth below, or from the height above. And Ahaz said, I will not ask, and I will not tempt the Lord. And he said, It is not a small thing for you to weary men; and how does the Lord weary them? Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) shall conceive, and bear a son; and you shall call His name [ _Emmanuel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>). Butter and honey shall He eat: before He [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) or chooses out things that are [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) , He shall exchange them for what is [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) ; for before the child [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) good or [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) , He shall not consent to [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) , that He may choose that which is [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>). [ _Isaiah 7:10-17_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/isa007.htm#verse10>) **Carefully, then, has the**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**pointed out, by what has been said, His birth from a**[** _virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>)**, and His**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**, that He is God (for the name**[** _Emmanuel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>)**indicates this)**. And He shows that He is a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , when He says, Butter and honey shall He eat; and in that He terms Him a child also, [in saying,] before He [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) good and [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) ; for these are all the tokens of a [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) infant. But that He will not consent to [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) , that He may choose that which is [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) ,— **this is proper to**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; that by the fact, that He shall eat butter and honey, we should not understand that He is a mere man only, nor, on the other hand, from the name [ _Emmanuel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>) , should suspect Him to be God without flesh. (Ibid., [_Chapter 21_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103321.htm>) A vindication of the prophecy in Isaiah (7:14) against the misinterpretations of Theodotion, Aquila, the Ebionites, and the Jews. Authority of the Septuagint version. Arguments in proof that Christ was born of a Virgin.)

  


7\. For it was fitting that the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) should receive testimony from all, and should become [a means of] judgment for the [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) indeed of those who [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) , but for the condemnation of those who [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) not; that all should be fairly judged, and that the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) in the Father and Son should be approved by all, that is, that it should be established by all [as the one means of [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)], receiving testimony from all, both from those belonging to it, since they are its friends, and by those having no connection with it, though they are its enemies. (Ibid., [_Chapter 20_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103420.htm>))

  


God the Father **and His Word have formed all created things** (which They use) **by Their own power and wisdom** , not out of defect or ignorance. The Son of God, who received all power from the Father, would otherwise never have taken flesh upon Him.

1\. And such or so important a dispensation He did not bring about by means of the creations of others, but by His own; neither by those things which were created out of [** _ignorance_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) and defect, but by those which had their substance from the wisdom and power of His Father. For indeed the creation could not have sustained Him [on the cross], if He had sent forth [simply by commission] what was the fruit of [** _ignorance_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) and defect. Now we have repeatedly shown **that the incarnate**[** _Word of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**was suspended upon a tree, and even the very**[** _heretics_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**do acknowledge that He was crucified**. How, then, could the fruit of [** _ignorance_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) and defect **sustain Him who contains the**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**of all things, and is**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**and perfect**? And if this world were made by the [** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) (it matters not whether we suppose their [** _ignorance_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) or their cognizance of the Supreme God), when the Lord declared,  _For I am in the_[** __Father__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and the Father in Me_ , [** _John 14:11_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh014.htm#verse11>) how could this workmanship of the [** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) have borne to be burdened at once with the Father and the [** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250904002646/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)?
