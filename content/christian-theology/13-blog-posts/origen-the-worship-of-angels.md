# Origen & the Worship of Angels

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Mar 29
  * 26 min read


Origen is often misquoted as denying that Christians were to pray to or invoke angels or the saints in heaven to intercede for believers on earth. They base this on Origen’s response to the Christian critic and pagan Celsus, whom this prolific Christian wrote a thorough refutation to.

  


In this post I will quote Origen’s entire response to show that he wasn’t censuring the practice of invoking angels to pray for believers. Rather, as the context will amply demonstrate, Origen was refuting Celsus’ assertion that Jews worship angels as gods similarly to the way the pagans worshiped the sun, moon, stars, etc. Origen’s point was that no true Jew or Christian would ever worship angelic creatures or the constellations such as the sun and moon as gods or goddesses, since such divine worship is expressly condemned in the sacred writings. As Origen points out, true believers are to worship the Father and his only-begotten Son alone as God.

  


As such, Origen’s statements are no directed against seeking the aid of angels by praying to them for help. This is simply an abuse and misuse of Origen’s words.

I will be quoting from [_BOOK V_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04165.htm>) of Origen’s response titled, Contra Celsus ([_Against Celsus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0416.htm>)). All emphasis will be mine.

  


Chapter 2

  


We have now, then, to refute that statement of his which runs as follows:  _O_[ __Jews__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) _and_[ __Christians__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) _, no God or son of a God either came or will come down (to earth). But if you mean that certain_[ __angels__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _did so,_**_then what do you call them? Are they gods, or some other race of beings? Some other race of beings (doubtless), and in all probability_**[** __demons__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>). Now as Celsus here is guilty of repeating himself (for in the preceding pages such assertions have been frequently advanced by him), it is unnecessary to discuss the matter at greater length, seeing what we have already said upon this point may suffice. We shall mention, however, a few considerations out of a greater number, such as we deem in harmony with our former arguments, but which have not altogether the same bearing as they, and by which we shall show that in asserting generally that no [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or [ _son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , ever descended (among [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)), he overturns not only the opinions entertained by the majority of [ _mankind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) regarding the manifestation of Deity, but also what was formerly admitted by himself. For if the general statement, that  _no God or son of God has come down or will come down_ , be [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) maintained by Celsus, it is manifest that we have here overthrown the belief in the [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) of gods upon the earth who had descended from heaven either to predict the future to [ _mankind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) or to heal them by means of divine responses; and neither the Pythian Apollo, nor Æsculapius, nor any other among those supposed to have done so, would be a god descended from heaven. He might, indeed, either be a god who had obtained as his lot (the obligation) to dwell on earth for ever, and be thus a fugitive, as it were, from the abode of the gods, or he might be one who had no power to share in the society of the gods in heaven; or else Apollo, and Æsculapius, and those others who are [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to perform acts on earth, would not be gods, but only certain [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) , much inferior to those wise men among [ _mankind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , who on account of their [ _virtue_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) ascend to the vault of heaven…

Chapter 4

But since he says, in the next place, as if the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) or [ _Christians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) had answered regarding those who come down to visit the [ _human race_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , that they were [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) :  _But if you say that they are_[ __angels__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, what do you call them?_ he continues _, Are they gods, or some other race of beings?_ and then again introduces us as if answering,_Some other race of beings, and probably_[ __demons__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) ,— let us proceed to notice these remarks. For we indeed acknowledge that [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) are ministering spirits, and we say that they are sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) ; and that they ascend, bearing the supplications of [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , to the purest of the heavenly places in the [ _universe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>) , or even to supercelestial regions purer still; and that they come down from these, conveying to each one, according to his deserts, something enjoined by [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) to be conferred by them upon those who are to be the recipients of His benefits. **Having thus learned to call these beings**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**from their employments, we find that because they are divine they are sometimes termed god in the sacred Scriptures,****_but not so that we are commanded to_**[** _honour_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)** _and worship_**** __in place of God__**** _those who minister to us, and bear to us His blessings_****. For every**[** _prayer_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)**, and supplication, and intercession, and thanksgiving,****_is to be sent up to the Supreme God through the High Priest, who is above all the_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)** _, the living Word and God. And to the_**[** _Word_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)** _Himself shall we also_**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _and make intercessions, and offer thanksgivings and supplications to Him_****, if we have the capacity of distinguishing between the proper use and abuse of**[** _prayer_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>).

Chapter 5

**For to invoke**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)******_without having obtained a_**[** __knowledge__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _of their nature_****greater than is possessed by**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**, would be contrary to reason. But, conformably to our hypothesis, let this**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**of them, which is something wonderful and**[** _mysterious_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)**, be obtained**. Then this [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , making [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to us their nature, and the offices to which they are severally appointed, **will not permit us to**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)**with confidence to any other than to the Supreme**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, who is sufficient for all things, and that through our Saviour the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, who is the Word, and Wisdom, and Truth, and everything else which the writings of God’s**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**and the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**of**[** _Jesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**entitle Him**. And it is enough to secure that the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of God be propitious to us, and that they do all things on our behalf, that our disposition of mind towards God should imitate as far as it is within the power of [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) the example of these [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , who again follow the example of their [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and that the conceptions which we entertain of His Son, the Word, so far as attainable by us, should not be opposed to the clearer conceptions of Him which the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) possess, but should daily approach these in clearness and distinctness. But because Celsus has not read our [ _holy Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , he gives himself an answer as if it came from us, saying that we  _assert that the_[ __angels__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _who come down from heaven to confer benefits on_[ __mankind__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) _are a different race from the gods_ , and adds that  _in all probability they would be called_[ __demons__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) _by us_ : not observing that the name [ __demons__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) is not a term of indifferent meaning like that of  _men_ , among whom some are good and some bad, nor yet a term of excellence like that of the gods, which is applied not to [ _wicked_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) , or to [ _statues_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13641b.htm>) , or to animals, but (by those who [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) divine things) to what is [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) divine and blessed; **whereas the term**[** _demons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>)**is always applied to those**[** _wicked_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>)**powers, freed from the encumbrance of a grosser body, who lead men astray, and fill them with distractions and drag them down from God and supercelestial thoughts to things here below**.

  


Chapter 6

  


He next proceeds to make the following statement about the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) :—  _The first point relating to the_[ __Jews__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) _which is fitted to excite wonder, is that they should worship the heaven and the_[ __angels__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _who dwell therein, and yet pass by and neglect its most venerable and powerful parts, as the sun, the moon, and the other heavenly bodies, both fixed stars and planets, as if it were possible that ‘the whole’ could be_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and yet its parts not divine; or (as if it were reasonable) to treat with the greatest respect those who are said to appear to such as are in darkness somewhere, blinded by some crooked sorcery, or dreaming dreams through the influence of shadowy spectres, while those who prophesy so clearly and strikingly to all_[ __men__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) _, by means of whom rain, and heat, and clouds, and thunder (to which they offer worship), and lightnings, and fruits, and all kinds of productiveness, are brought about — by means of whom God is revealed to them — the most prominent heralds among those beings that are above — those that are_[ __truly__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _heavenly_[ __angels__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _— are to be regarded as of no account_! In making these statements, Celsus appears to have fallen into confusion, and to have penned them from false ideas of things which he did not understand; for it is patent **to all who investigate the practices of the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**, and compare them with those of the**[** _Christians_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>)**, that the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**who follow the law, which, speaking in the person of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, says,****_You shall have no other gods before Me: you shall not make unto you an image, nor a likeness of anything that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the waters under the earth; you shall not bow down to them, nor serve them_****,****_worship nothing else than the Supreme_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, who made the heavens, and all things besides_**. Now it is evident that those who live according to the law, and worship the  _Maker_ of heaven, **_will not worship the heaven at the same time with_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Moreover, no one who obeys the [ _law of Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>) ** _will bow down to the_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)** _who are in heaven; and, in like manner, as they do not bow down to sun, moon, and stars, the_**[** _host_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13286a.htm>)** _of_**[** _heaven_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>)** _, they refrain from doing obeisance to heaven and its_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , [ _obeying_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) the law which declares:  _Lest you lift up your eyes to heaven, and when you see the sun, and the moon, and the stars, even all the_[ __host__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13286a.htm>) _of_[ __heaven__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>) _, should be driven to worship them, and serve them, which the Lord your God has divided unto all nations_.

  


Chapter 7

  


Having, moreover, assumed that the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) consider the heaven to be [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , he adds that this is absurd; **finding fault with those who bow down to the heaven, but not also to the sun, and moon, and stars, saying that the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**do this** , as if it were possible that the whole should be [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and its several parts not divine. And he seems to call the heaven a whole, and sun, moon, and stars its several parts. Now, certainly neither [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) nor [ _Christians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) call the heaven God. Let it be granted, however, that, as he alleges, the heaven is called God by the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , and suppose that sun, moon, and stars  _are_ parts of heaven,— which is by no means [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , for neither are the animals and plants upon the earth any portion of it — how is it [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , even according to the opinions of the Greeks, that if God be a whole, His parts also are divine? Certainly they say that the Cosmos taken as the whole is [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the [ _Stoics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14299a.htm>) calling it the First [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the followers of [ _Plato_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12159a.htm>) the Second, and some of them the Third. According to these [ _philosophers_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) , then, seeing the whole Cosmos is [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , its parts also are divine; **so that not only are**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**beings divine, but the whole of the irrational creation, as being**** _portions_****of the Cosmos; and besides these, the plants also are divine. And if the rivers, and mountains, and seas are portions of the Cosmos, then, since the whole Cosmos is**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, are the rivers and seas also gods?** But even this the Greeks will not assert. Those, however, who preside over rivers and seas (either [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) or gods, as they call them), they would term gods. Now from this it follows that the general statement of Celsus, even according to the Greeks, who hold the doctrine of Providence, is false, **that if any whole be a god, its parts necessarily are divine**. But it follows from the doctrine of Celsus, **that if the Cosmos be**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, all that is in it is divine, being parts of the Cosmos**. Now, according to this view, **animals, as flies, and gnats, and worms, and every species of serpent, as well as of birds and fishes, will be divine** , — an assertion which would not be made even by those who maintain that the Cosmos is [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **But the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**, who live according to the**[** _law of Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>) , although they may not [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) how to receive the secret meaning of the law, which is conveyed in obscure language, **_will not maintain that either the heaven or the_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)** _are God_**.

  


Chapter 8

  


As we allege, however, that he has fallen into confusion in consequence of false notions which he has imbibed, come and let us point them out to the best of our ability, and show that although Celsus considers it to be a Jewish custom to bow down to the heaven and the [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) in it, **_such a practice is not at all Jewish, but is in violation of_**[** _Judaism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)** _, as it also is to do obeisance to sun, moon, and stars, as well as images_**. You will find at least in the book of Jeremiah the words of God **_censuring by the mouth of the_**[** _prophet_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)** _the Jewish people for doing obeisance to such objects, and for_**[** _sacrificing_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** _to the queen of heaven, and to all the_**[** _host_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13286a.htm>)** _of_**[** _heaven_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>). The writings of the [ _Christians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) , moreover, show, in censuring the [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) committed among the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , that when God abandoned that people on account of certain [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , **_these_**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _(of idol-worship) also were committed by them_**. For it is related in the Acts of the Apostles regarding the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , that [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) turned, and gave them up to worship the [ _host_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13286a.htm>) of [ _heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>) ; as it is written in the book of the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , _O you house of_[ __Israel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, have you offered to Me slain beasts and_[ __sacrifices__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) _by the space of forty years in the wilderness? Yea, you took up_** _the tabernacle of_**[** __Moloch__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10443b.htm>)** _, and the star of your god Remphan, figures which you made to worship them_**. And in the writings of [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , who was carefully trained in Jewish customs, and converted afterwards to [ _Christianity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) by a miraculous appearance of [ _Jesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , the following words may be read in the Epistle to the Colossians:  _Let no man beguile you of your reward in a_[ __voluntary__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15506a.htm>) _humility_** __and worshipping of__**[** __angels__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, intruding into those things which he has not seen, vainly puffed up by his fleshly mind; and not holding the Head, from which all the body by joint and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increases with the increase of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). But Celsus, having neither read these verses, nor having learned their contents from any other source, has represented, I [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) not how, **_the_**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)** _as not transgressing their law in bowing down to the heavens, and to the_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)** _therein_**.

  


Chapter 9

  


And still continuing a little confused, and not taking care to see what was relevant to the matter, he expressed his opinion **_that the_**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)** _were induced by the incantations employed in jugglery and sorcery (in consequence of which certain phantoms appear, in_**[** _obedience_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)** _to the spells employed by the magicians) to bow down to the_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)** _in heaven, not observing that this was contrary to their law_** , which said to them who practised such observances:  _Regard not them which have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards, to be defiled by them: I am the Lord your God_. He ought, therefore, either not to have at all attributed this practice to the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , seeing he has observed that they keep their law, and has called them those who live according to their law; or if he did attribute it, he ought to have shown that the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) did this in violation of their code. But again, **as they transgress their law who offer worship to those who are said to appear to them who are involved in darkness and blinded by sorcery, and who dream dreams, owing to obscure phantoms presenting themselves;****_so also do they transgress the law who offer_**[** _sacrifice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** _to sun, moon, and stars_**. And there is thus great inconsistency in the same individual saying that the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) are careful to keep their law **_by not bowing down to sun, and moon, and stars, while they are not so careful to keep it in the matter of heaven and the_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>).

  


Chapter 10

  


And if it be necessary for us **to offer a defense of our refusal to recognise**** _as gods, equally with_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**, and sun, and moon, and stars, those who are called by the Greeks manifest and visible divinities, we shall answer that the**[** _law of Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>)****[**_knows_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**that these latter have been apportioned by**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**among all the nations under the heaven, but not among those who were selected by**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**as His chosen people above all the nations of the earth**. For it is written in the book of Deuteronomy:  _And lest you lift up your eyes unto heaven, and when you see the sun, and the moon, and the stars, even all the_[ __host__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13286a.htm>) _of_[ __heaven__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>) _, should be driven to worship them, and serve them, which the Lord your God has divided unto all nations unto the whole heaven. But the Lord has taken us, and brought us forth out of the iron furnace, even out of_[ __Egypt__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) _, to be unto Him a people of inheritance, as you are this day_. The Hebrew people, then, being called by [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) a chosen generation, and a royal [ _priesthood_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>) , and a [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) nation, and a purchased people, regarding whom it was foretold to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) by the voice of the Lord addressed to him,  _Look now towards heaven, and tell the stars, if you are able to number them_ : and He said to him, _So shall your seed be_ ; and having thus a hope that they would become as the stars of heaven, **were not likely to bow down to those objects which they were to resemble as a result of their understanding and observing the law of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). For it was said to them:  _The Lord our God has multiplied us; and, behold, you are this day as the stars of heaven for multitude_. In the book of Daniel, also, the following prophecies are found relating to those who are to share in the resurrection:  _And at that time your people shall be delivered, every one that has been written in the book. And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt. And they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament, and (those) of the many righteous as the stars for ever and ever_ , etc. And hence [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , too, when speaking of the resurrection, says:  _And there are also celestial bodies, and bodies terrestrial: but the_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _of the celestial is one, and the_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _of the terrestrial is another. There is one_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _of the sun, and another_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _of the moon, and another_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _of the stars; for one star differs from another star in_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _. So also is the resurrection of the dead_. 

  


It was not therefore consonant to reason that those who had been taught sublimely to ascend above all [ _created_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm>) things, and to hope for the enjoyment of the most [ _glorious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) rewards with God on account of their [ _virtuous_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) lives, and who had heard the words,  _You are the light of the world, and, Let your light so shine before men, that they, seeing your good works, may glorify your Father who is in heaven_ , and who possessed through practice this brilliant and unfading wisdom, or who had secured even the very reflection of everlasting light, should be so impressed with the (mere)  _visible_ light of sun, and moon, and stars, that, on account of that sensible light of theirs, they should deem themselves (although possessed of so great a rational light of [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , and of the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) light, and the light of the world, and the light of men) to be somehow inferior to them, **_and to bow down to them; seeing they ought to be worshipped, if they are to receive worship at all_** , not for the sake of the sensible light which is admired by the multitude, but because of the rational and [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) light, if indeed the stars in heaven are rational and [ _virtuous_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) beings, **and have been illuminated with the light of**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**by that wisdom which is the reflection of everlasting light**. For that sensible light of theirs is the work of the Creator of all things, while that rational light is derived perhaps from the principle of [ _free-will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06259a.htm>) within them.

  


Chapter 11

  


But even this rational light itself ought not to be worshipped by him who beholds and understands the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) light, by sharing in which these also are enlightened; nor by him who beholds [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the Father of the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) light — of whom it has been said, [ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _is light, and in Him there is no darkness at all_. ** _Those, indeed, who worship sun, moon, and stars because their light is visible and celestial_** , would not bow down to a spark of fire or a lamp upon earth, because they see the incomparable superiority of those objects which are deemed worthy of homage to the light of sparks and lamps. So those who understand that God is light, **and who have apprehended that the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**is**** _the_**[** __true__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** _light which lights every man that comes into the world_** , and who comprehend also how He says,  _I am the light of the world_ , **would not rationally offer worship to that which is, as it were, a spark in sun, moon, and stars, in comparison with**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , who is light of the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) light. Nor is it with a view to depreciate these great works of God’s creative power, or to call them, after the fashion of Anaxagoras, fiery masses, that we thus speak of sun, and moon, and stars; **but because we perceive the inexpressible superiority of the divinity of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and that of His only-begotten Son, which surpasses all other things.****_And being persuaded that the sun himself, and moon, and stars_**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _to the Supreme God through His only-begotten Son, we judge it improper to_**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _to those beings who themselves offer up_**[** _prayers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _(to God), seeing even they themselves would prefer that we should send up our requests to the God to whom they_**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) , rather than send them downwards to themselves, or apportion our power of [ _prayer_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) between God and them. And here I may employ this illustration, as bearing upon this point: Our Lord and Saviour, hearing Himself on one occasion addressed as Good Master, referring him who used it to His own Father, said,  _Why do you call Me good? There is none good but one, that is,_[__God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _the Father._ [ _Matthew 19:17_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat019.htm#verse17>) And since it was in accordance with sound reason that this should be said by the Son of His Father’s [ _love_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) , as being the image of the goodness of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , why should not the sun say with greater reason to those that bow down to him, **_Why do you worship me?_********_for you will worship the Lord your_**[** __God__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, and Him only shall you serve; for it is He whom I and all who are with me serve and worship_**. And although one may not be so exalted (as the sun), **nevertheless let such an one**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)**to the**[** _Word of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**(who is able to heal him),****_AND STILL MORE TO HIS FATHER_** , who also to the righteous of former times  _sent His word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions_.

  


Chapter 12

  


God accordingly, in His kindness, condescends to [ _mankind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , not in any local sense, but through His [ _providence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12510a.htm>) ; while the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , not only (when on earth), but at  _all_ times, is with His own [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , fulfilling the promise,  _Lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the world_. And if a branch cannot bear fruit except it abide in the vine, it is evident that the [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) also of the Word, who are the rational branches of the Word’s [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) vine, cannot produce the fruits of [ _virtue_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) unless they abide in the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) vine, the Christ of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , who is with us locally here below upon the earth, and who is with those who cleave to Him in all parts of the world, and is also in all places with those who do not [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) Him. Another is made manifest by that John who wrote the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , when, speaking in the person of John the Baptist, he said,  _There stands one among you whom you_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _not; He it is who comes after me_. And it is absurd, when He who fills heaven and earth, and who said,  _Do I not fill heaven and earth? Says the Lord_ , is with us, and near us (for I [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) Him when He says,  _I am a God near at hand, and not afar off, says the Lord_ **) to seek to**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)**to sun or moon, or one of the stars** , whose influence does not reach the whole of the world. But, to use the very words of Celsus, let it be granted that the sun, moon, and stars  _do_ foretell rain, and heat, and clouds, and thunders, why, then, if they really do foretell such great things, **ought we not rather to do homage to**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, whose servant they are in uttering these predictions, and show reverence to**** _Him_****rather than His**[** __prophets__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**?** Let them predict, then, the approach of lightnings, and fruits, and all manner of productions, and let all such things be under their administration; **_yet we shall not on that account worship those who themselves offer worship, as we do not worship even_**[** _Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)** _, and those_**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)** _who came from God after him_** , and who predicted better things than rain, and heat, and clouds, and thunders, and lightnings, and fruits, and all sorts of productions visible to the senses. Nay, even if sun, and moon, and stars were able to prophesy better things than rain, **_not even then shall we worship_**** __them__****, but the**** _Father_****of the prophecies which are in them, and the**** _Word_****of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, their minister**. But grant that they are His heralds, and [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) messengers of heaven, **why, even then ought we not to worship the**** _God_****whom they only proclaim and announce, rather than those who are the**** _heralds_****and**** _messengers_**?

  


Chapter 13

  


Celsus, moreover, assumes that sun, and moon, and stars are regarded by us as of no account. Now, with regard to these, we acknowledge that they too are _waiting for the manifestation of the sons of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , being for the present subjected to the _vanity_ of their material bodies, by reason of Him who has subjected the same in hope. But if Celsus had read the innumerable other passages where we speak of sun, moon, and stars, and especially these —P _raise Him, all you stars, and you, O light, and, Praise Him, you heaven of heavens_ , — he would not have said of us that we regard such mighty beings, which greatly praise the [ _Lord God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , as of no account. Nor did Celsus [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the passage:  _For the earnest expectation of the creature waits for the manifestation of the sons of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _. For the creature was made subject to vanity, not willingly, but by reason of Him who has subjected the same in hope; because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the_[ __glorious__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _liberty of the children of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). And with these words let us terminate **_our defense against the charge of not worshipping sun, moon, and stars_**. And let us now bring forward those statements of his which follow, that we may, God willing, address to him in reply such arguments as shall be suggested by the light of [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>).

  


Elsewhere in his response to Celsus, Origen hammers the point that true believers do not offer divine worship such as sacrifices to any beings besides the Father and the Son:

  


Chapter 13

  


He further supposes, **__that because we join along with the worship of God the worship of His Son__** _, it follows that, in our view, not only_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, but also the servants of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, are to be worshipped_. If he had meant this to apply to those who are [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) the servants of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , after His only-begotten Son, — to Gabriel and Michael, and the other [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) and archangels, **and if he had said of these that they ought to be worshipped, —**** __if also he had clearly defined the meaning of the word worship, and the duties of the worshippers__****, — we might perhaps have brought forward such thoughts as have occurred to us on so important a subject**. But as he reckons among the servants of God **_the_**[** _demons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>)** _which are worshipped by the_**[** _heathen_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>) , he cannot induce us, on the plea of consistency, to worship such as are declared by the word to be servants of the [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) one, the prince of this world, who leads astray from God as many as he can. **_We decline, therefore, altogether to worship and serve those whom other men worship, for the reason that they are not servants of_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). For if we had been taught to regard them as servants of the Most High, we would not have called them [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>). **_Accordingly, we worship with all our power the one_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, and His only Son, the Word and the Image of_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, by_**[** _prayers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _and supplications; and we offer our petitions to the_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _of the_**[** _universe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>)** _through His only-begotten Son. To the Son we first present them, and beseech Him, as the propitiation for our_**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _, and our High Priest, to offer our desires, and_**[** _sacrifices_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** _, and_**[** _prayers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _, to the Most High_**. Our [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , therefore, is directed to God through His Son, who strengthens it in us; and Celsus can never show that the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) is the [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) of any sedition or disloyalty in the [ _kingdom of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>). We [ _honour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) the Father when we admire His Son, the Word, and Wisdom, and Truth, and Righteousness, and all that He who is the Son of so great a Father is said in Scripture to be. So much on this point…

  


Chapter 57

  


Celsus supposes that men discharge the duties of life until they are loosened from its bonds, when, in accordance with commonly received customs, **they offer**[** _sacrifices_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)**to each of the gods recognised in the state** ; and he fails to perceive the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) duty which is fulfilled by an earnest [ _piety_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>). For we say that he [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) discharges the duties of life who is ever mindful who is his Creator, and what things are agreeable to Him, and who acts in all things so that he may please God. **Again, Celsus wishes us to be thankful**** __to these__**[** __demons__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>)**, imagining that we owe them thank-offerings**. But we, while recognising the duty of thankfulness, maintain that we show no ingratitude by refusing to give thanks to beings who do us no good, but who rather set themselves against us **_when we neither_**[** _sacrifice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** _to them nor worship them_**. We are much more concerned lest we should be ungrateful to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , who has loaded us with His benefits, whose workmanship we are, who cares for us in whatever condition we may be, and who has given us hopes of things beyond this present life. And we have a [ _symbol_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14373b.htm>) of gratitude to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) in the bread which we call the [ _Eucharist_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05572c.htm>). Besides, as we have shown before, the [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) have not the control of those things which have been created for our use; we commit no wrong, therefore, when we partake of [ _created_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm>) things, **_and yet refuse to offer_**[** _sacrifices_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** _to beings who have no concern with them_**. Moreover, as we [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that it is not [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) , but [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , who have been set over the fruits of the earth, and over the birth of animals, **_it is the latter_**** __that we praise and bless__**** _, as having been appointed by_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _over the things needful for our race; yet even to them we will not give the_**[** _honour_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)** _which is due to_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**. For this would not be pleasing to**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, nor would it be any pleasure to the**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**themselves to whom these things have been committed. Indeed, they are much more pleased**** _if we refrain from offering_**[** _sacrifices_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** _to them_****than if we offer them; for they have no desire for the sacrificial odours which rise from the earth** …

  


Chapter 64

  


There is therefore One whose favour we should seek, and to whom we ought to [ _pray_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) that He would be gracious to us — the Most High [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , whose favour is gained by [ _piety_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>) and the practice of every [ _virtue_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>). And if he would have us to seek the favour of others after the Most High [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , let him consider that, as the motion of the shadow follows that of the body which casts it, so in like manner it follows, that when we have the favour of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **_we have also the good-will of all_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)** _and spirits who are friends of_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**. For they**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**who are worthy of the divine approval, and they are not only well disposed to them** , but they co-operate with them in their endeavours to please God: **_they seek His favour on their behalf; with their_**[** _prayers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _they join their own_**[** _prayers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _and intercessions for them_**. We may indeed boldly say, that men who aspire after better things **_have, when they_**[** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _to_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, tens of thousands of sacred powers upon their side_****. These,****_even when not asked,_**[**_pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** _with them_****, they bring succour to our mortal race** , and if I may so say, take up arms alongside of it: for they see [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) warring and fighting most keenly against the [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) of those who devote themselves to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and despise the hostility of [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) ; they see them savage in their [ _hatred_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) of the man **who refuses to serve them with the blood and fumes of**[** _sacrifices_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) , but rather strives in every way, by word and deed, to be in peace and union with the Most High through Jesus, who put to flight multitudes of [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) when _He went about healing,_ and delivering  _all who were oppressed by the_[ __devil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>). ([_BOOK VIII_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20251028034235/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04168.htm>) ; emphasis mine)

  


Note here that Origen refers to praising and blessing the angelic servants of God, which would be an odd thing for him to say if he rejected altogether _ANY_ type of invocation of or communion with God’s heavenly host. He even explicitly states that believers have a multitude of angels and spirits praying and interceding for them.

  


As if this wasn’t sufficient to refute the notion that Origen was against invoking angels, note what this prolific writer of the Church wrote in his homilies on Ezekiel:

  


7\. (1) **“The heavens were opened.”** 110 It is not enough for one heaven to be opened; very many are opened, so that not from one heaven but from all the heavens, **angels may come down to those who are to be saved—the angels who ascended and descended upon the Son of Man** ,111 and came to him, and ministered to him.112 Moreover, the angels descended because Christ had descended earlier, fearing to descend before the Lord of all Powers113 and of [all] things went ahead of them.114 When they saw, however, the leader of the heavenly host lingering in earthly places, then they came out through the opened way, following their Lord, and obeying the will of the one **who apportioned them as guardians of those who believe in his name. Yesterday you were subject to a demon; today you are under an angel**. The Lord says, “Do not despise one of these smallest ones” who are in the Church; “truly I say to you that their angels always behold the face of my Father who is in the heavens.”115 The angels devote themselves to your salvation; they have declared themselves116 for the service of the Son of God, and they say to each other, “If _he_ descended, and descended into a body, if he was clothed with mortal flesh and endured the cross, and died on behalf of mankind, why do we rest? Why do we spare ourselves? Come, let us all descend, all the angels, from heaven!” For this reason, too, there was a multitude of the heavenly host praising and glorifying God, when Christ was born.117

  


(2) All things are full of angels: **_Come, angel, take up an old man who has been converted from his original error, from the teaching of demons, from iniquity speaking loftily,_****_118_**** _and as you take him up, cherish and instruct him like a good physician_**. He is a little child: today an old man is born, an old man who becomes a little boy again. **And when you have taken him up, grant him the baptism of second birth,****119****and summon to yourself others as companions****120****in your mission,****121****so that you may all together give an education in faith to those who at one time were deceived. For “there is more joy in the heavens over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous people who do not need repentance.”****122****All creation exults, rejoices together, and applauds those who are to be saved**. For “the expectation of the creation awaits the revealing of the children of God.”123 And although those **who have adulterated****124****the Apostolic Scriptures** do not wish there to be in their books statements of this sort, **by which Jesus Christ could be proved to be the Creator** ,125 nevertheless in that passage all creation awaits the children of God—awaits the time when they will be freed from transgression, when they will be taken out of the hands of the devil,126 **when they will be regenerated by Christ**.

  


But it is now time also to touch on some matters regarding the passage before us. The prophet sees not a vision, but visions of God.127 Why does he not see one, but _many_ visions? Listen to the Lord, who says as a promise, “I have multiplied visions.”128
