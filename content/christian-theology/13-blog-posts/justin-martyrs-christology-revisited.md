# JUSTIN MARTYR'S CHRISTOLOGY REVISITED

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 30, 2025
  * 42 min read


In this post I am going to revisit what the second century apologist Justin Martyr wrote in respect to Christ’s Deity. I want to focus on Justin’s statements where he emphatically identifies the prehuman Jesus as that very YHWH God who appeared to the OT saints. This will help solidify the fact that Justin did not think that Christ was a creature whom the Father brought into being.

  


I cite from the following section of Justin Martyr’s Dialogue with Trypho [**_Chapters 55-68_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/01285.htm>). All emphasis will be mine.

  


Chapter 55. Trypho asks that Christ be proved God, but without metaphor. Justin promises to do so

  


**Trypho:** We shall remember this your exposition, if you strengthen [your solution of] this difficulty by other arguments: but now resume the discourse, and show us **that the Spirit of**[** _prophecy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>)**admits another God besides the Maker of all things** , taking care not to speak of the sun and moon, which, it is written, God has given to the nations to worship as gods; and oftentimes the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , employing this manner of speech, say that ‘your God is a God of gods, and a Lord of lords,’ adding frequently, ‘the great and strong and terrible [God].’ For such expressions are used, not as if they really were gods, **but because the**[** _Scripture_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**is teaching us that the**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)****[**_God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, who made all things, is Lord alone of those who are reputed gods and lords**. And in order that the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) may convince [us] of this, He said by the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) David, ‘The gods of the [ _nations_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) , reputed gods, are [ _idols_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>) of [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) , and not gods;’ and He denounces a curse on those who worship them.

  


**Justin:** I would not bring forward these [ _proofs_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) , Trypho, by which I am aware those who worship these [[_idols_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>)] and such like are condemned, **but such [**[**_proofs_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>)**] as no one could find any objection to**. They will appear strange to you, **although you read them every day** ; so that even from this fact we understand that, **because of your**[** _wickedness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>)**, God has withheld from you the ability to discern the wisdom of His Scriptures** ; yet [there are] some exceptions, to whom, according to the [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of His long-suffering, as Isaiah said, He has left a seed of [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) , **lest your race be utterly destroyed, like**[** _Sodom_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>)**and**[** _Gomorrha_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>). Pay attention, therefore, to what I shall record out of the [ _holy Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , **which do not need to be expounded, but only listened to**.

Chapter 56. God who appeared to Moses is distinguished from God the Father

**Justin:** [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , then, the blessed and faithful servant of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **declares that He who appeared to**[** _Abraham_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**under the oak in Mamre is**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , sent with the two [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) in His company to judge [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) by Another who remains ever in the supercelestial places, invisible to all [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , holding personal intercourse with none, whom we [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to be Maker and Father of all things; for he speaks thus: ‘God appeared to him under the oak in Mamre, as he sat at his tent-door at noontide. And lifting up his eyes, he saw, and behold, three men stood before him; and when he saw them, he ran to meet them from the door of his tent; and he bowed himself toward the ground, and said …’ [ _Genesis 18:1-2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen018.htm#verse1>) ‘[ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) went up early in the morning to the place where he stood before the Lord: and he looked toward [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) and [ _Gomorrha_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) , and toward the adjacent country, and beheld, and, lo, a flame went up from the earth, like the smoke of a furnace.’

  


And when I had made an end of quoting these words, I asked them if they had understood them. And they said they had understood them, but that the passages adduced brought forward no [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) that there is any other God or Lord, or that the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) says so, besides the Maker of all things.

  


**Justin:** I shall attempt to persuade you, since you have understood the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , [of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)] of what I say, **that there is, and that there is said to be, another God and Lord subject to the Maker of all things; who is also called an Angel, because He announces to men whatsoever the Maker of all things** — above whom there is no other God — wishes to announce to them.

I quoted once more the previous passage.

  


**Justin:** Do you think that God appeared to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) under the oak in Mamre, as the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) asserts?

  


**Trypho:** Assuredly.

  


**Justin:** Was He one of those three whom [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) saw, and whom the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) of [ _prophecy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>) describes as men?

  


**Trypho:** No; but God appeared to him, before the vision of the three. Then those three whom the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) calls men, were [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) ; two of them sent to destroy [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) , and one to announce the joyful tidings to Sarah, that she would bear a son; for which [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) he was sent, and having accomplished his errand, went away.

  


**Justin:** How then does the one of the three, who was in the tent, and who said, ‘I shall return to you hereafter, and Sarah shall have a son,’ [ _Genesis 18:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen018.htm#verse10>) appear to have returned when Sarah had begotten a son, and to be there declared, by the prophetic word, God? But that you may clearly discern what I say, listen to the words expressly employed by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) ; they are these: ‘And Sarah saw the son of Hagar the [ _Egyptian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) bond-[_woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) , whom she bore to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , sporting with Isaac her son, and said to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Cast out this bond-[_woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) and her son; for the son of this bond-[_woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) shall not share the inheritance of my son Isaac. And the matter seemed very grievous in [ _Abraham’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) sight, because of his son. But God said to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Let it not be grievous in your sight because of the son, and because of the bond-[_woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>). In all that Sarah has said to you, hearken to her voice; for in Isaac shall your seed be called.’ [ _Genesis 21:9-12_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen021.htm#verse9>) Have you perceived, then, that He who said under the oak that He would return, since He [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) it would be necessary to advise [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) to do what Sarah wished him, came back as it is written; and is [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , as the words declare, when they so speak: ‘God said to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Let it not be grievous in your sight because of the son, and because of the bond-[_woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>)?’

  


**Trypho:** Certainly; but you have not [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) from this that there is another God besides Him who appeared to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , and who also appeared to the other patriarchs and [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>). You have [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) , however, that we were wrong in believing that the three who were in the tent with [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) were all [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>).

  


**Justin:** If I could not have [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) to you from the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) **that one of those three is**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and is called Angel, because, as I already said, He brings messages to those to whom God the Maker of all things wishes** [messages to be brought], then in regard **to Him who appeared to**[** _Abraham_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**on earth in**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)****[**_form_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06137b.htm>) in like manner as the two [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) who came with Him, **and who was God even BEFORE THE CREATION OF THE WORLD** , it were reasonable for you to entertain the same belief as is entertained by the whole of your nation.

  


**Trypho:** Assuredly, for up to this moment this has been our belief.

  


**Justin:** Reverting to the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , I shall endeavour to persuade you, that He who is said to have appeared to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , and to Jacob, and to [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , **and who is called**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, is distinct from Him who made all things** — numerically, I mean, not [distinct] in will. For I affirm that He has never at any time done anything which He who made the world— above whom there is no other God — has not wished Him both to do and to engage Himself with.

  


**Trypho:** Prove now that this is the case, that we also may agree with you. For we do not understand you to affirm that He has done or said anything contrary to the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of the Maker of all things.

  


**Justin:** The Scripture just quoted by me will make this plain to you. It is thus: ‘The sun was risen on the earth, and [ _Lot_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09366a.htm>) entered into Segor (Zoar); **and the Lord rained on** [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) sulphur and fire **from the Lord out of heaven** , and overthrew these cities and all the neighbourhood.’ [ _Genesis 19:23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen019.htm#verse23>)

  


**The fourth of those who had remained with Trypho:** It must therefore necessarily be said that one of the two [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) who went to [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) , and is named by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) in the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) Lord, is different from Him who also is God and appeared to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>).

  


**Justin:** It is not on this ground solely that it must be admitted absolutely **that some other one is called Lord by the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**besides Him who is considered Maker of all things** ; not solely [for what is said] by [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , **but also [for what is said] by David. For there is written by him: ‘The Lord says to my Lord** , Sit on My right hand, until I make Your enemies Your footstool,’ as I have already quoted. And again, in other words: **‘Your throne, O**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, is for ever and ever**. A sceptre of equity is the sceptre of Your kingdom: You have loved righteousness and [ _hated_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) iniquity: **therefore**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, even Your**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , has anointed You with the oil of [ _gladness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) above Your fellows.’ If, therefore, you assert **that the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**calls some other one God and Lord, besides the Father of all things and His Christ** , answer me; for I undertake to prove to you from Scriptures themselves, **that He whom the**[** _Scripture_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**calls Lord is not one of the two**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) that went to [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) , **but He who was with them, and is called**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , that appeared to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>).

  


**Trypho:** Prove this; for, as you see, the day advances, and we are not prepared for such perilous replies; since never yet have we heard any man investigating, or searching into, or proving these matters; nor would we have tolerated your conversation, had you not referred everything to the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) : for you are very [ _zealous_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15753a.htm>) in adducing [ _proofs_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) from them; and you are of opinion that there is no God above the Maker of all things.

  


**Justin:** You are aware, then, that the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) says, ‘And the Lord said to [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Why did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) conceive? For I am old. Is anything impossible with God? At the time appointed shall I return to you according to the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son.’ [ _Genesis 18:13-14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen018.htm#verse13>) And after a little interval: ‘And the men rose up from thence, and looked towards [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) and [ _Gomorrha_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) ; and [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) went with them, to bring them on the way. And the Lord said, I will not conceal from [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , my servant, what I do.’ [ _Genesis 18:16-17_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen018.htm#verse16>) And again, after a little, it thus says: ‘The Lord said, The cry of [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) and [ _Gomorrha_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) is great, and their [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) are very grievous. I will go down now, and see whether they have done altogether according to their cry which has come unto me; and if not, that I may [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>). And the men turned away thence, and went to [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>). But [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) was standing before the Lord; and [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) drew near, and said, Will You destroy the righteous with the [ _wicked_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>)?’ [ _Genesis 18:20-23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen018.htm#verse20>)

  


And so on, for I do not think fit to write over again the same words, having written them all before, but shall of necessity give those by which I established the [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) to Trypho and his companions. Then I proceeded to what follows, in which these words are recorded:

_If I shall declare to you what happens daily, I shall call to mind events from everlasting, and review them. The Lord made me the beginning of His ways for His works. These are the words: ‘And God said, Behold, [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) has become as one of us, to [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) good and [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>).’ [ _Genesis 3:22_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/gen003.htm#verse22>) **In saying, therefore, ‘as one of us,’ [**[**_Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)**] has declared that [there is a certain] number of**[** _persons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>)**associated with one another, and that they are at least two**. And Joshua fell on his face on the ground, and said to Him, Lord, what do You command Your servant? And the Lord’s Captain says to Joshua, Loose the shoes off your feet; for the place whereon you stand is [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250917093447/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) ground. Chapter 100. In what sense Christ is [called] Jacob, and Israel, and Son of Man

**SUMMATION**

  


Here is the list of scriptural citations Justin cites to prove that there are two distinct Persons described as YHWH God, and that the YHWH God who appeared throughout the Hebrew Bible is not the Father, but Jesus Christ the Son in his prehuman existence: Genesis 1:26; 3:22; 18:1-33; 19:1-29; 21:9-12; 28:10-22; 31:10-13; 32:22-30; 35:6-10; Exodus 3:1-16; Joshua 5:13-15; Psalm 45:1-11; 110:1-3; Proverbs 8:22-36.

  


Justin clearly affirms that Christ is not a creature that came into being with the creation. Rather, Justin emphatically states that Christ was begotten out of the Father himself from before all creatures since it was the prehuman Jesus who was the One appointed to begin the creation. Justin, therefore, did not believe that Christ was a created being but, instead, proclaims him to be the uncreated, eternal Logos (Word) of the Father.

  


This is further confirmed by Justin identifying Christ as that very YHWH who appeared to the patriarchs and prophets. Justin even quotes the Psalmists to prove that Jesus is that very YHWH of Hosts proclaimed in these very Psalms!
