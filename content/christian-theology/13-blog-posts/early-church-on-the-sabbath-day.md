# EARLY CHURCH ON THE SABBATH DAY

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 2, 2025
  * 18 min read


Updated: Nov 6, 2025

The current post has been adapted and modified from the following article:****[**_What the Early Church Believed: Sabbath or Sunday?_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.catholic.com/tract/sabbath-or-sunday>). All emphasis shall be mine.

  


The following quotations show that the first Christians understood that Sunday became the Lord’s Day as a result of Christ’s resurrection, and therefore replaced the Sabbath. This is why believers gathered for worship on first day of the week.

  


**The Didache**

  


Chapter 14. Christian Assembly on the Lord’s Day

  


**But every Lord’s day gather yourselves together** , and break bread, and give thanksgiving **after having confessed your transgressions** , that your [** _sacrifice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) may be pure. But let no one that is at variance with his fellow come together with you, until they be reconciled, that your [** _sacrifice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) may not be profaned. For this is that which was spoken by the Lord: In every place and time offer to me a pure [** _sacrifice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) ; for I am a great King, says the Lord, and my name is wonderful among the nations. ([**__The Didache__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0714.htm>) [A.D. 70]).

  


[**_Epistle of Barnabas_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0124.htm>)

  


Chapter 15. The false and the true Sabbath

Further, also, it is written concerning the [** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) in the Decalogue which [the Lord] spoke, face to face, to [** _Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) on Mount Sinai, And sanctify the [** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) of the Lord with clean hands and a pure heart. [** _Exodus 20:8_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/exo020.htm#verse8>) ; [** _Deuteronomy 5:12_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu005.htm#verse12>) And He says in another place, If my sons keep the [** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) , then will I [** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) my mercy to rest upon them. [** _Jeremiah 17:24-25_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/jer017.htm#verse24>) The [** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) is mentioned at the beginning of the creation [thus]: And God made in six days the works of His hands, and made an end on the seventh day, and rested on it, and sanctified it. Attend, my children, to the meaning of this expression, He finished in six days. This implies that the Lord will finish all things in six thousand years, for a day is with Him a thousand years. And He Himself testifies, saying, Behold, today will be as a thousand years. Therefore, my children, in six days, that is, in six thousand years, all things will be finished. And He rested on the seventh day. This means: when His Son, coming [again], shall destroy the time of the [** _wicked_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) man, and judge the ungodly, and change the-sun, and the moon, and the stars, then shall He [** _truly_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) rest on the seventh day. Moreover, He says, You shall sanctify it with pure hands and a pure heart. If, therefore, any one can now sanctify the day which [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) has sanctified, except he is pure in heart in all things, we are deceived. Behold, therefore: certainly then one properly resting sanctifies it, when we ourselves, having received the promise, [** _wickedness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) no longer existing, and all things having been made new by the Lord, shall be able to work righteousness. Then we shall be able to sanctify it, having been first sanctified ourselves. **Further, He says to them, Your new moons and your**[** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**I cannot endure.**[**_Isaiah 1:13_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa001.htm#verse13>)**You perceive how He speaks: Your present**[** _Sabbaths_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**are not acceptable to Me, but that is which I have made, [namely this,] when, giving rest to all things, I shall make a beginning of the eighth day, that is, a beginning of another world. Wherefore, also, we keep the eighth day with joyfulness, the day also on which**[** _Jesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**rose again from the dead. And when He had manifested Himself, He ascended into the heavens**. (Ibid. [A.D. 74]) ​​​​​​​

  


**Ignatius of Antioch​​​​​​​**

  


Chapter 8. Caution against false doctrines

  


Be not deceived with [** _strange doctrines_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , nor with old fables, which are unprofitable. For if we still live according to the [** _Jewish law_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10582c.htm>) , we acknowledge that we have not received [** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)**. For the divinest**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**lived according to**[** _Christ Jesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). On this account also they were [** _persecuted_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) , being inspired by His [** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) to fully convince the unbelieving that there is [** _one God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10499a.htm>) , who has manifested Himself by [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) His [** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , who is His [** _eternal Word_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) , not proceeding forth from silence, and who in all things pleased Him that sent Him.

  


Chapter 9. Let us live with Christ

  


If, therefore, those who were brought up in the ancient order of things have come to the possession of a new [** _hope_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07465b.htm>) , **no longer observing the**[** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**, but living in the observance of the**[** _Lord’s Day_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14335a.htm>)**, on which also our life has sprung up again by Him and by His death** — whom some deny, by which [** _mystery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) we have obtained [** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , and therefore endure, that we may be found the [** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , our only Master — how shall we be able to live apart from Him, whose [** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) the [** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) themselves in the [** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) did wait for Him as their Teacher? And therefore He whom they rightly waited for, having come, [** _raised them from the dead_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12792a.htm>). [** _Matthew 27:52_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat027.htm#verse52>) ([__Epistle to the Magnesians__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0105.htm>) [A.D. 110])

  


**Justin Martyr​​​​​​​**

  


Chapter 67. Weekly worship of the Christians

  


And we afterwards continually remind each other of these things. And the [** _wealthy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15571a.htm>) among us help the needy; and we always keep together; and for all things wherewith we are supplied, we bless the Maker of all through His Son [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and through the [** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). And on the day called Sunday, all who live in cities or in the country gather together to one place, and the memoirs of the [** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) or the writings of the [** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) are read, as long as time permits; then, when the reader has ceased, the president verbally instructs, and exhorts to the imitation of these [** _good_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) things. Then we all rise together and [** _pray_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) , and, as we before said, when our [** _prayer_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) is ended, bread and wine and water are brought, and the president in like manner offers [** _prayers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) and thanksgivings, according to his ability, and the people assent, saying [** _Amen_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01407b.htm>) ; and there is a distribution to each, and a participation of that over which thanks have been given, and to those who are absent a portion is sent by the [** _deacons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>). And they who are well to do, and willing, give what each thinks fit; and what is collected is deposited with the president, who succours the [** _orphans_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11322b.htm>) and [** _widows_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15617c.htm>) and those who, through sickness or any other [** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) , are in want, and those who are in bonds and the strangers sojourning among us, and in a word takes care of all who are in need. **But Sunday is the day on which we all hold our common assembly, because it is the first day on which**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, having wrought a change in the darkness and matter, made the world; and**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**our Saviour on the same day rose from the dead**. For He was crucified on the day before that of Saturn (Saturday); **and on the day after that of Saturn, which is the day of the Sun, having appeared to His**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**and**[** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**, He taught them these things, which we have submitted to you also for your consideration**. ([__First Apology__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0126.htm>) [A.D. 155])

  


**The Didascalia**

  


“The apostles further appointed: On the first day of the week let there be service, and the reading of the holy scriptures, and the oblation [sacrifice of the Mass], because on the first day of the week [i.e., Sunday] our Lord rose from the place of the dead, and on the first day of the week he arose upon the world, and on the first day of the week he ascended up to heaven, and on the first day of the week he will appear at last with the angels of heaven.” (_Didascalia_ 2 [A.D. 225])

  


**Origen**

  


27\. Significance of the Names of John and of His Parents.

  


The force that is in names may be applied in many matters, and it may be worth our while to ask at this point what is the significance of the names John and Zacharias. The [** _relatives_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>) wish, as the giving of a name is a thing not to be lightly disposed of, to call the child Zacharias, and are surprised that [** _Elisabeth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05387b.htm>) should want him to be called John. Zacharias then writes, His name is John, and is at once freed from his troublesome silence. On examining the names, then, we find Joannes to be Joa without the nes. The [** _New Testament_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>) gives Hebrew names a Greek form and treats them as Greek words; Jacob is changed into Jacobus, Symeon into Simon, and Joannes is the same as Joa. Zacharias is said to be memory, and [** _Elisabeth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05387b.htm>) [** _oath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11176a.htm>) of my [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or strength of my God. John then came into the world from [** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of God (=Joa=Joannes), and his [** _parents_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) were Memory (about God) and the Oath of our [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , about the fathers. Thus was he born to make ready for the Lord a people fit for Him, at the end of the Covenant now grown old, **which is the end of the Sabbatic period**. Hence it is not possible that the rest after the [** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) should have come into [** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) from the seventh of our [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; on the contrary, it is our Saviour who, after the pattern of His own rest, caused us to be made in the likeness of His death, and hence also of His [** _resurrection_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>). (_Commentary on John_ , [**_Book II_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/101502.htm>) [A.D. 229])

  


**Victorinus**

  


On the fifth day the land and water brought forth their progenies. On the sixth day the things that were wanting were created; and thus God raised up man from the soil, as lord of all the things which He created upon the earth and the water. Yet He created [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) and archangels before He created man, placing spiritual beings before earthly ones. For light was made before sky and the earth. This sixth day is called parasceve, that is to say, the preparation of the kingdom. For He perfected Adam, whom He made after His image and likeness. But for this reason He completed His works before He created [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) and fashioned man, lest perchance they should [ _falsely_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>) assert that they had been His helpers. On this day also, on account of the passion of the [ _Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , we make either a station to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or a fast. On the seventh day He rested from all His works, and blessed it, and sanctified it. **On the former day we are accustomed to fast rigorously, that on the Lord’s day we may go forth to our bread with giving of thanks. And let the parasceve become a rigorous fast, lest we should appear to observe any**[** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**with the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**, which Christ Himself, the Lord of the**[** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**, says by His**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**that His**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)****[**_hates_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) ; [ _Isaiah 1:13-14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa001.htm#verse13>) **which**[** _Sabbath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**He in His body abolished** , although, nevertheless, He had formerly Himself commanded [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) that [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) should not pass over the eighth day, which day very frequently happens on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) , as we read written in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>). [ _John 7:22_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh007.htm#verse22>) [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , foreseeing the hardness of that people, on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) raised up his hands, therefore, and thus figuratively fastened himself to a cross. [ _Exodus 22:9, 12_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/exo022.htm#verse9>) And in the battle they were sought for by the foreigners on the [ _Sabbath day_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) , that they might be taken captive, and, as if by the very strictness of the law, might be fashioned to the avoidance of its teaching. [ _1 Maccabbees 2:31-41_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ma002.htm#verse31>)

And thus in the sixth Psalm for the eighth day, David asks the Lord that He would not rebuke him in His [ _anger_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01489a.htm>) , nor judge him in His fury; **for this is indeed the eighth day of that future judgment, which will pass beyond the order of the sevenfold arrangement. Jesus also, the son of Nave, the successor of**[** _Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)**, himself broke the**[** _Sabbath day_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**; for on the**[** _Sabbath day_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>)**he commanded the children of**[** _Israel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>)****[**_Joshua 6:4_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/jos006.htm#verse4>)**to go round the walls of the city of**[** _Jericho_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08339a.htm>)**with trumpets, and declare**[** _war_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15546c.htm>)**against the aliens**. Matthias also, prince of Judah, broke the [ _Sabbath_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) ; for he slew the prefect of Antiochus the king of [ _Syria_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14399a.htm>) on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250919100905/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) , and subdued the foreigners by pursuing them. **John Chrysostom**

  


9… How was it then when he [God] said, “You shall not kill” . . . he did not add, “because murder is a wicked thing.” The reason was that conscience had taught this beforehand, and he speaks thus, as to those who know and understand the point. Wherefore when he speaks to us of another commandment, not known to us by the dictate of conscience, he not only prohibits, but adds the reason. What else can I call these [men] but preachers of Antichrist, who when he comes will cause the Sabbath day as well as the Lord’s day to be kept free from all work. For because he [the Antichrist] pretends to die and rise again, he wishes the Lord’s day to be held in reverence; and because he compels the people to Judaize that he may bring back the outward rite of the law, and subject the perfidy of the Jews to himself, he wishes the Sabbath to be observed. For this which is said by the prophet, ‘You shall bring in no burden through your gates on the Sabbath day’ [Jer. 17:24] could be held to as long as it was lawful for the law to be observed according to the letter. But after that the grace of almighty God, our Lord Jesus Christ, has appeared, the commandments of the law which were spoken figuratively cannot be kept according to the letter. For if anyone says that this about the Sabbath is to be kept, he must needs say that carnal sacrifices are to be offered. He must say too that the commandment about the circumcision of the body is still to be retained. But let him hear the apostle Paul saying in opposition to him: ‘If you be circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing’ [Gal. 5:2]” (_Letters_ 13:1 [A.D. 597])
