# Christ: The Physical Seed of David

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Jan 6
  * 9 min read


In this article I will cite all the relevant NT verses which affirm that Jesus is a physical descendant of king David, making him an Israelite from the tribe of Judah. Since Jesus was born of the Holy Virgin by the Holy Spirit, this means that the Lord Jesus traces his human ancestry solely through his blessed Mother. 

“The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, **the son of David** , the son of Abraham:… 18 Now the birth of Jesus Christ was as follows: when His mother Mary had been betrothed to Joseph, before they came together she was found to be with child **by the Holy Spirit**. And Joseph her husband, being a righteous man and not wanting to disgrace her, planned to send her away secretly. But when he had considered this, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, ‘Joseph, **son of David** , do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife; for the One who has been conceived in her is **of the Holy Spirit**. And she will bear a Son; and you shall call His name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins.’” Matthew 1:1, 18-21

“And the disciples went and did just as Jesus had instructed them, and brought the donkey and the colt, and laid their garments on them; and He sat on the garments. And most of the crowd spread their garments in the road, and others were cutting branches from the trees and spreading them in the road. And the crowds going ahead of Him, and those who followed, were crying out, saying, ‘Hosanna **to the Son of David** ; Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord; Hosanna in the highest!’…

“And the blind and the lame came to Him in the temple, and He healed them. But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the marvelous things which He had done, and the children who were shouting in the temple, saying, ‘Hosanna **to the Son of David** ,’ they became indignant and said to Him, ‘Do You hear what these children are saying?’ And Jesus said to them, ‘Yes; have you never read, “Out of the mouth of infants and nursing babies You have prepared praise for Yourself”?’” Matthew 21:6-9, 14-16

“Then they came to Jericho. And as He was leaving Jericho with His disciples and a large crowd, a blind beggar named Bartimaeus, the son of Timaeus, was sitting by the road. And when he heard that it was Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out and say, ‘**Jesus, Son of David** , have mercy on me!’ And many were sternly telling him to be quiet, but he kept crying out all the more, ‘**Son of David** , have mercy on me!’” Mark 10:46-48

“Now in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city in Galilee called Nazareth, to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, **of the house of David** , and the virgin’s name was Mary. And coming in, he said to her, ‘Greetings, favored one! The Lord is with you.’ But she was very perplexed at this statement, and was pondering what kind of greeting this was. And the angel said to her, ‘Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God. And behold, you will conceive in your womb and bear a son, and you shall name Him Jesus. He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High, and the Lord God will give Him the throne **of His father David** , and He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and there will be no end of His kingdom.’ But Mary said to the angel, ‘How will this be, **since I am a virgin?** ’ The angel answered and said to her, ‘**The Holy Spirit will come upon you** , and the power of the Most High will overshadow you; and for that reason the holy Child shall be called the Son of God.” Luke 1:26-35

“And his father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit, and prophesied, saying: ‘Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, For He visited and accomplished redemption for His people, And raised up **a horn of salvation** for us **In the house of David** His servant—’” Luke 1:67-69

“Now it happened that in those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus for a census to be taken of all the inhabited earth. This was the first census taken while Quirinius was governor of Syria. And everyone was going to be registered for the census, each to his own city. And Joseph also went up from Galilee, from the city of Nazareth, to Judea, **to the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David** , in order to register along with Mary, who was betrothed to him, and was with child. Now it happened that while they were there, the days were fulfilled for her to give birth. And she gave birth to her firstborn son; and she wrapped Him in cloths, and laid Him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the guest room.

“In the same region there were some shepherds staying out in the fields and keeping watch over their flock by night. And an angel of the Lord stood before them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them; and they were terribly frightened. But the angel said to them, ‘Do not be afraid; for behold, I bring you good news of great joy which will be for all the people. For today in **the city of David** there has been born for you **a Savior, who is Christ the Lord**.’” Luke 2:1-11

“Men, brothers, I may confidently say to you regarding **the patriarch David** that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day. And so, because he was a prophet and knew that God had sworn to him with an oath to set **one of the fruit of his body** on his throne, he looked ahead and spoke of the resurrection **of the Christ** , that He was neither forsaken to Hades, nor did His flesh see corruption. This Jesus God raised up again, to which we are all witnesses. Therefore having been exalted to the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, He has poured out this which you both see and hear. For **David** did not ascend into the heavens, but he himself says: ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand, Until I put Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet.”’ Therefore let all the house of Israel know for certain that God has made Him both Lord and Christ—this Jesus whom you crucified.” Acts 2:29-36 

“And after He had removed him, He raised up **David** to be their king, about whom He also said, bearing witness, ‘I have found **David** the son of Jesse, a man after My heart, who will do all My will.’ **From the seed of this man** , according to promise, God has brought to Israel **a Savior, Jesus** ,” Acts 13:22-23 

“concerning His Son, **who was born of the seed of David according to the flesh** , who was designated as the Son of God in power, according to the Spirit of holiness, by the resurrection from the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,” Romans 1:3-4

“**who are Israelites** , to whom belongs the adoption as sons, and the glory and the covenants and the giving of the Law and the temple service and the promises, whose are the fathers, **and from whom is the Christ according to the flesh** , who is God over all, blessed forever. Amen.” Romans 9:4-5 

“Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, **of the seed of David** , according to my gospel,” 2 Timothy 2:8

“And one of the elders said to me, ‘Stop crying! Behold, the Lion that is **from the tribe of Judah, the Root of David** , has overcome so as to open the scroll and its seven seals.’” Revelation 5:5

“I, Jesus, sent My angel to bear witness to you of these things for the churches. **I am** the root and **the descendant of David** , the bright morning star.” Revelation 22:16

**Letters of St. Ignatius**

****

St. Ignatius was a disciple of the holy Apostles and the Bishop of the church at Antioch, Syria. He was taken to Rome where he willfully died as a martyr of the Lord Jesus Christ by being mauled by the lions. On his way there he wrote seven letters to specific Churches, which have been preserved. I quote from some of them in which the holy martyr describes Jesus as a physical descendant of David. All emphasis will be mine. 

Chapter 7. Reason of desiring to die

The [ _prince of this world_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) would fain carry me away, and corrupt my disposition towards God. Let none of you, therefore, who are [in Rome] help him; rather be on my side, that is, on the side of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Do not speak of [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and yet set your desires on the world. Let not [ _envy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08326b.htm>) find a dwelling-place among you; nor even should I, when present with you, exhort you to it, be persuaded to listen to me, but rather give credit to those things which I now write to you. For though I am alive while I write to you, yet I am eager to die. My [ _love_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) has been crucified, and there is no fire in me desiring to be fed; but there is within me a water that lives and speaks, saying to me inwardly, Come to the Father. I have no delight in corruptible food, nor in the pleasures of this life. I desire the bread of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the heavenly bread, the bread of life, which is the flesh of [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , **who became afterwards of the seed of**[** _David_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04642b.htm>)**and**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) ; and I desire the drink of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , namely His blood, which is incorruptible [ _love_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) and [ _eternal_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) life. ([_Epistle to the Romans_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0107.htm>))

Chapter 18. The glory of the cross

Let my [ _spirit_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14220b.htm>) be counted as nothing for the sake of the [ _cross_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04529a.htm>) , which is a stumbling-block [ _1 Corinthians 1:18_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co001.htm#verse18>) to those that do not [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) , but to us [ _salvation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) and life eternal. Where is the wise man? Where the disputer? [ _1 Corinthians 1:20_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co001.htm#verse20>) Where is the boasting of those who are styled [ _prudent_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12517b.htm>)? For our [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , was, according to the appointment of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **conceived in the womb by**[** _Mary_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>)**, of the seed of**[** _David_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04642b.htm>) , but by the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). He was born and [ _baptized_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , that by His [ _passion_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11530a.htm>) He might purify the water…

Chapter 20. Promise of another letter

If [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) shall graciously permit me through your [ _prayers_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) , and if it be His [ _will_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) , I shall, in a second little work which I will write to you, make further manifest to you [the [ _nature_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of] the dispensation of which I have begun [to treat], with respect to the new man, [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , in His [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) and in His [ _love_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) , in His [ _suffering_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11530a.htm>) and in His [ _resurrection_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>). Especially [will I do this ] if the [ _Lord_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) make known to me that you come together man by man in common through [ _grace_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) , individually, in one [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , and in [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , **who was of the seed of**[** _David_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04642b.htm>)**according to the flesh** , being both the [ _Son of man_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) and the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , so that you [ _obey_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) the [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) and the [ _presbytery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>) with an undivided [ _mind_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10321a.htm>) , breaking one and the same [ _bread_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05572c.htm>) , which is the medicine of [ _immortality_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) , and the antidote to prevent us from dying, but [which causes] that we should live for ever in [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). ([_Epistle to the Ephesians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0104.htm>))

Chapter 9. Reference to the history of Christ

Stop your ears, therefore, when any one speaks to you at variance with [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , **who was descended from David, and was also of Mary** ; who was [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) born, and ate and drank. He was [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _persecuted_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) under [ _Pontius Pilate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12083c.htm>) ; He was [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) crucified, and [[_truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)] died, in the sight of beings in heaven, and on earth, and under the earth. He was also [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) raised from the dead, His Father quickening Him, even as after the same manner His Father will so raise up us who [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in Him by [ _Christ Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , apart from whom we do not possess the [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) life. ([_Epistle to the Trallians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0106.htm>))

Chapter 1. Thanks to God for your faith

I Glorify [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , even [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , who has given you such wisdom. For I have observed that you are perfected in an immoveable [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , as if you were nailed to the cross of [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , both in the flesh and in the spirit, and are established in [ _love_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) through the blood of [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , being fully persuaded with respect to our Lord, **that He was**[** _truly_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**of the seed of**[** _David_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04642b.htm>)**according to the flesh** , [ _Romans 1:3_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom001.htm#verse3>) and the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) according to the will and power of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; that He was [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) born of a [ _virgin_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) , was [ _baptized_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) by John, in order that all righteousness might be fulfilled [ _Matthew 3:15_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat003.htm#verse15>) by Him; and was [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , under [ _Pontius Pilate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12083c.htm>) and [ _Herod_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07289c.htm>) the tetrarch, nailed [to the cross] for us in His flesh. Of this fruit we are by His divinely-blessed passion, that He might set up a standard [ _Isaiah 5:26_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa005.htm#verse26>) , [ _Isaiah 49:22_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa049.htm#verse22>) for all ages, through His [ _resurrection_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) , to all His [ _holy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) and faithful [followers], whether among [ _Jews_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) or [ _Gentiles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) , in the one body of His [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). ([_Epistle to the Smyrnæans_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0109.htm>))
