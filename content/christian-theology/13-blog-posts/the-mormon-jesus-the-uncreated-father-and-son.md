# The Mormon Jesus: The Uncreated Father and Son?

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Mar 16
  * 19 min read


The official religious sources of the Mormon religion present an incoherent, contradictory picture of Jesus Christ. In some of their writings, Jesus is described as an uncreated, eternal divine Person who created and sustains all things, being personally distinct from the Father.

I cite a few examples. All emphasis will be mine:

I am the_**[** _Father_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note14_c>)** _and the Son_**. In me shall all mankind have [ _life_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note14_d>) , and that eternally, even they who shall believe on my name; and they shall become my [ _sons_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note14_e>) and my daughters.

****

**15** And never have I [ _showed_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note15_a>) myself unto man **whom I have created** , for never has man [ _believed_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note15_b>) in me as thou hast. Seest thou that ye are created **after mine own**[** _image_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note15_c>)? Yea, even all men were created in the beginning **after mine own image**.

****

**16** Behold, this [ _body_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note16_a>) , which ye now [ _behold_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note16_b>) , **is the**[** _body_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note16_c>)**of my**[** _spirit_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note16_d>) ; and man have I created after the body of my spirit; and even as I appear unto thee to be in the spirit will I appear unto my people in the flesh.

****

**17** And now, as I, Moroni, said I could [ _not_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note17_a>) make a full account of these things which are written, therefore it sufficeth me to say **that Jesus showed himself unto this man in the spirit** , even after the manner and in the likeness of the same body even as he [ _showed_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note17_b>) himself unto the Nephites.

****

**18** And he ministered unto him even as he ministered unto the Nephites; and all this, **that this man might know that he was God** , because of the many great works which the Lord had showed unto him.

****

**19** And because of the [ _knowledge_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note19_a>) of this man he could not be kept from beholding within the [ _veil_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note19_b>) ; **and he saw the finger of Jesus** , which, when he saw, he fell with fear; **for he knew that it was the finger of the Lord** ; and he had [ _faith_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note19_c>) no longer, for he knew, nothing [ _doubting_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note19_d>).

**20** Wherefore, having this perfect knowledge of God, he could [ _not_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note20_a>) be kept from within the veil; **therefore he**[** _saw_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/3?lang=eng#note20_b>)**Jesus** ; and he did minister unto him.
