# JWS ADMIT: JESUS IS THE ETERNAL CREATOR!

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 30, 2025
  * 3 min read


Paul, in Colossians 1:15-17, describes Jesus as the eternal Creator and Sustainer of all creation:

  


“who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation; for **in him were**** _ALL THINGS_****created, in the heavens and upon the earth** , things visible and things invisible, whether thrones or dominions or principalities or powers; **_ALL THINGS_****have been created through him** , and unto him; **and he IS before**** _ALL THINGS_****,** and **in him**** _ALL THINGS_****consist**.” [_American Standard Version_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/american-standard-version/>)

  


“Who is image of the invisible God, firstborn of all creation, **because in him**** _EVERYTHING_****was created in the heavens and on earth** , the visible and the invisible, be it thrones or lordships or governments or dominions—** _EVERYTHING_****has been created through him** and to come to him, and **before**** _EVERYTHING_****he IS** , and **in him**** _EVERYTHING_****holds together**.” [_The Bible in Living English_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/byington/>)

  


The Jehovah’s Witnesses’ Watchtower Bible and Tract Society couldn’t have this in their Bible and so decided to add the word “other” in their perversion of Scripture, since they admit that to not do so would identify Jesus as the Creator himself!

  


“He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation;because by means of him all **_other_** things were created in the heavens and on the earth, the things visible and the things invisible, whether they are thrones or lordships or governments or authorities. All **_other_** things have been created through him and for him. Also, he is before all **_other_** things, and by means of him all **_other_** things were made to exist,” 

  


**all other things:** ** _A literal rendering of the Greek text would be “all things_**.” (Compare  _Kingdom Interlinear._) **_However, such a rendering could give the impression that Jesus was not created but was the Creator himself_**. And that idea would not agree with the rest of the Bible [_sic_], including the preceding verse, which calls Jesus “the firstborn of all creation.” ([_Col 1:15_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/colossians/1/#v51001015>) ; compare [ _Re 3:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/revelation/3/#v66003014>) , where Jesus is called “the beginning of the creation by God.”) Also, the Greek word for “all” can in some contexts have the meaning “all other,” as for example at [ _Lu 13:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/luke/13/#v42013002>) (“all other”); [ _Lu 21:29_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/luke/21/#v42021029>) (“all the other”); [ _Php 2:21_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/philippians/2/#v50002021>) (“all the others”). This agrees with Paul’s inspired teaching found at [ _1Co 15:27_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/1-corinthians/15/#v46015027>) : “God ‘subjected all things under his [Christ’s] feet.’ But when he says that ‘all things have been subjected,’ it is evident that this does not include the One who subjected all things to him.” So both the Bible’s teachings as a whole and the probable meaning of the Greek word used here support the rendering “all other things.”​—Compare [ _study note on Php 2:9_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/philippians/2/#v50002009>). ([_New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures (Study Edition)_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20241119090523/https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/>) : [_https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/colossians/1/_](<https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/colossians/1/>) ; bold, italicized emphasis mine)

  


This candid admission shows just how pernicious and wicked the Watchtower Society happens to be, since they acknowledge that the plain and literal reading of the inspired Greek text of Colossians 1:16-17 portrays Christ as the uncreated Son of God whom the Father appointed to create and give life to all creation.

  


The Society, therefore, deliberately perverted the literal rendering of this God-breathed passage in order to hide this inspired truth of God that Jesus is not a part of creation, but is rather distinguished from all creation as its very Creator.
