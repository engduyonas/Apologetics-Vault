# Tertullian & Apostolic Succession

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 5 min read


I quote from Tertullian’s [_The Prescription Against Heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0311.htm>) , where he appealed to the Bishops taught and appointed by the holy Apostles to expose the heresies being spread by false teachers and so-called Christians. All emphasis will be mine.

Chapter 31. Truth First, Falsehood Afterwards, as Its Perversion. Christ’s Parable Puts the Sowing of the Good Seed Before the Useless Tares.

  


Let me return, however, from this digression to discuss the priority of [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , and the comparative lateness of [ _falsehood_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>) , deriving support for my argument even from that [ _parable_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm>) which puts in the first place the sowing by the Lord of the good seed of the wheat, but introduces at a later stage the adulteration of the crop by its enemy the [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) with the useless weed of the wild oats. For herein is figuratively described the difference of doctrines, since in other passages also the word of God is likened unto seed. **_From the actual order, therefore, it becomes clear, that that which was first delivered is of the Lord and is_**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , while that is strange and false which was afterwards introduced. This sentence will keep its ground in opposition to all later [ _heresies_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , which have no consistent quality of kindred [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) inherent in them — to claim the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) as on their side.

  


Chapter 32. None of the Heretics Claim Succession from the Apostles. New Churches Still Apostolic, Because Their Faith is that Which the Apostles Taught and Handed Down. The Heretics Challenged to Show Any Apostolic Credentials.

  


But if there be any ([_heresies_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)) which are bold enough **to plant themselves in the midst of the**[** _apostolic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**age, that they may thereby seem to have been handed down by the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, because they**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**in the time of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, we can say:****_Let them produce the original records of their churches; let them unfold the roll of their_**[** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _, running down in due succession from the beginning in such a manner that [that first_**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _of theirs ]_**[**_bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _shall be able to show for his ordainer and predecessor some one of the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _or of apostolic men, — a_**[** _man_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)** _, moreover, who continued steadfast with the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**. For this is the manner in which the**[** _apostolic churches_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01634a.htm>)**transmit their registers: as the church of Smyrna,****_which records that_**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)** _was placed therein by John_****; as also the**[** _church of Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07424b.htm>)**,****_which makes Clement to have been ordained in like manner by Peter_****. In exactly the same way the other churches likewise exhibit (their several worthies), whom, as having been appointed to their episcopal places**** _by_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, they regard as transmitters of the apostolic seed_****. Let the**[** _heretics_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**contrive something of the same kind**. For after their [ _blasphemy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) , what is there that is unlawful for them (to attempt)? But should they even effect the contrivance, they will not advance a step. **For their very doctrine, after comparison with that of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, will declare, by its own diversity and contrariety, that it had for its author neither an apostle nor an apostolic man; because, as the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**would never have taught things which were self-contradictory, so the apostolic men would not have inculcated teaching different from the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**,****_unless they who received their instruction from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _went and preached in a contrary manner_**. To this test, therefore will they be submitted for [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) by those churches, who, although they derive not their founder from [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) or apostolic men (as being of much later date, for they are in fact being founded daily), **_yet, since they agree in the same_**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)** _, they are accounted as not less apostolic because they are akin in doctrine_**. Then let all the [ _heresies_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , **_when challenged to these two tests by our apostolic church_** , offer their [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) of how they deem themselves to be apostolic. But in [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) they neither are so, nor are they able to prove themselves to be what they are not. **Nor are they admitted to peaceful relations and communion by such churches as are in any way connected with**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, inasmuch as they are in no sense themselves apostolic because of their diversity as to the**[** _mysteries_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)**of the**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>).

  


Chapter 33. Present Heresies (Seedlings of the Tares Noted by the Sacred Writers) Already Condemned in Scripture. This Descent of Later Heresy from the Earlier Traced in Several Instances.

  


Besides all this, I add a review of the doctrines themselves, which, existing as they did in the days of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , were both exposed and denounced by the said [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). For by this method they will be more easily reprobated, when they are detected to have been even then in [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , or at any rate to have been seedlings of the (tares) which then were. [** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**, in his first epistle to the Corinthians** , sets his mark on certain who denied and [ _doubted_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) the resurrection. [ _1 Corinthians 15:12_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co015.htm#verse12>) **This opinion was the special property of the**[** _Sadducees_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13323a.htm>)**. A part of it, however, is maintained by**[** _Marcion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>)**and**[** _Apelles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01593a.htm>)**and**[** _Valentinus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>)**, and all other impugners of the resurrection**. Writing also to the Galatians, he inveighs against such men as observed and defend [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) and the (Mosaic) law. [ _Galatians 5:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal005.htm#verse2>) **Thus runs Hebion’s**[** _heresy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**. Such also as forbid to marry he reproaches in his instructions to Timothy.**[**_1 Timothy 4:3_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti004.htm#verse3>)**Now, this is the teaching of**[** _Marcion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>)**and his follower**[** _Apelles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01593a.htm>)**.****_(The apostle) directs a similar blow against those who said that the resurrection was past already._**[**_2 Timothy 2:3_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2ti002.htm#verse3>)** _Such an opinion did the_**[** _Valentinians_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>)** _assert of themselves_**. When again he mentions endless genealogies, [ _1 Timothy 1:4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti001.htm#verse4>) one also recognises [ _Valentinus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) , in whose system a certain [ _Æon_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm>) , whosoever he be, of a new name, and that not one only, generates of his own [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) Sense and Truth; and these in like manner produce of themselves Word and Life, while these again afterwards beget Man and the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). From these primary eight ten other [ _Æons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm>) after them spring, and then the twelve others arise with their wonderful names, to complete the mere story of the thirty [ _Æons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01173c.htm>). **The same apostle, when disapproving of those who are in bondage to elements,**[**_Galatians 4:9_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal004.htm#verse9>)**points us to some**[** _dogma_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05089a.htm>)**of Hermogenes, who introduces matter as having no beginning, and then compares it with**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, who has no beginning**. By thus making the mother of the elements a goddess, he has it in his power to be in bondage to a being which he puts on a par with God. **John** , however, **in the Apocalypse** is charged to chastise those who eat things [ _sacrificed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) to [ _idols_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>) , and who commit fornication. [ _Revelation 2:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rev002.htm#verse14>) **There are even now another sort of**[** _Nicolaitans_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11067a.htm>)**. Theirs is called the Gaian**[** _heresy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). But **in his epistle** he especially designates those as Antichrists who denied that Christ had come in the flesh, [ _1 John 4:3_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo004.htm#verse3>) and who refused to think that [ _Jesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) was the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). **The one**[** _dogma_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05089a.htm>)****[**_Marcion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>)**maintained; the other, Hebion**. The doctrine, however, of Simon’s sorcery, which inculcated the worship of [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250911235539/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , was itself actually reckoned among idolatries and condemned by the Apostle Peter in Simon’s own person.
