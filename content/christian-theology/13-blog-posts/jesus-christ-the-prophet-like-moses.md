# JESUS CHRIST – THE PROPHET LIKE MOSES

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 5, 2025
  * 24 min read


God had promised the Israelites to raise up for them a prophet like Moses from among themselves:

  


“Yahweh your God will raise up to you a prophet  __from among you (miqqirbaka), of your brothers (me’aheka)__ ,  _like me_. You shall listen to him.**** This is according to all that you desired of Yahweh your God in Horeb in the day of the assembly, saying, ‘Let me not hear again Yahweh my God’s voice, neither let me see this great fire any more, that I not die.’**** Yahweh said to me, ‘They have well said that which they have spoken.**** I will raise them up a prophet  __from among their brothers (miqqereb ahehem)__ ,  _like you_. I will put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I shall command him.**** It shall happen, that whoever will not listen to my words which he shall speak in my name, I will require it of him.” Deuteronomy 18:15-19 World English Bible (WEB)

  


The Hebrew words “from among you,” and “of your brothers” are always used in reference to a fellow Israelite:

  


“you shall surely set him whom Yahweh your God chooses as king over yourselves. You shall set as king over you one  __from among your brothers (miqqereb aheka)__. You may not put a foreigner over you, who is not your brother.” Deuteronomy 17:15 WEB

  


“You shall serve Yahweh your God, and he will bless your bread and your water, and I will take sickness away  __from among you (miqqirbaka)__.” Exodus 23:25 WEB

  


“You shall not oppress a hired servant who is poor and needy,  _whether he is one_ __of your brothers (me’aheka)___or one of the foreigners who are in your land within your gates_.” Deuteronomy 24:14 WEB

  


“Your eyes have seen what Yahweh did because of Baal Peor; for Yahweh your God has destroyed all the men who followed Baal Peor  __from among you (miqqirbaka)__.” Deuteronomy 4:3 WEB

  


“That prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, to draw you aside out of the way which Yahweh your God commanded you to walk in. So you shall remove the evil  __from among you (miqqirbaka)__ … certain wicked fellows have gone out  __from among you (miqqirbaka)__ and have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, ‘Let’s go and serve other gods,’ which you have not known,” Deuteronomy 13:5, 13 WEB – Cf. 17:7; 19:19; 21:9, 21; 22:21, 24; 24:7

  


“‘It will happen in that day’, says Yahweh, ‘that I will cut off your horses out  __from among you (miqqirbaka)__ , and will destroy your chariots.’… I will cut off your engraved images and your pillars out  __from among you (miqqirbaka)__ ; and you shall no more worship the work of your hands… I will uproot your Asherah poles out  __from among you (miqqirbaka)__ ; and I will destroy your cities.” Micah 5:10-13-14 WEB

  


“In that day you will not be disappointed for all your doings, in which you have transgressed against me; for then I will take away out  __from among you (miqqirbek)__ your proudly exulting ones, and you will no more be arrogant in my holy mountain.” Zephaniah 3:11 WEB

  


As far as the New Testament is concerned Jesus was (still is) an Israelite living in the midst of Israel, and was therefore truly from among the Israelites:

  


**THE PROPHET**

  


“I will put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I shall command him.”

  


“to my words which he shall speak in my name,”

  


“of your brothers”

  


“from among their brothers”

The Torah further explains what it means for a prophet to be like Moses:

  


“Since then, there has not arisen a prophet in Israel  __like Moses, whom Yahweh knew face to face, in all the signs and the wonders__ which Yahweh sent him to do in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to all his land,  __and in all the mighty hand, and in all the awesome deeds, which Moses did__ in the sight of all Israel.” Deuteronomy 34:10-12 WEB

  


For a prophet to be like Moses he must do miracles like Moses, and speak directly with God as Moses did.

  


In this article I will show how Jesus fulfills these criteria, and highlight some additional similarities between the two.

### **ONE GREATER THAN MOSES HAS ARRIVED**

  


**** Even though Jesus is truly the prophet like Moses, he is much more than a prophet. As I have already shown, Christ is God who became flesh, and therefore infinitely better than Moses:

  


“For Jesus has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses—** _as much more glory as the builder of a house has more honor than the house itself_**. (For every house is built by someone, ** _but the builder of all things is God_**.) Now Moses was faithful in all God’s house ** _as a servant_** , to testify to the things that were to be spoken later, ** _but Christ is faithful over God’s house as a son_**. And we are his house, if indeed we hold fast our confidence and our boasting in our hope.” Hebrews 3:3-6 ESV

  


“Long ago, at many times and in many ways, God spoke to our fathers by the prophets, but in these last days he has spoken to us ** _by his Son_** , whom he appointed the heir of all things, ** _through whom also he created the world. He is the radiance of the glory of God_** ** _and the exact imprint of his nature, and he upholds the universe by the word of his power_**. After making purification for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high… And, ‘** _You, Lord [the Son], laid the foundation of the earth_** in the beginning, and the heavens ** _are the work of your hands_** ; they will perish, but you remain; they will all wear out like a garment, ** _like a robe you will roll them up_** , like a garment they will be changed. ** _But you are the same, and your years will have no end_**.’” Hebrews 1:1-3, 10-12 ESV

  


Hence, Jesus is like Moses in one sense, but infinitely greater than him in another sense. I.e. Christ is the eternal Son of God who created and sustains all creation, and who is the Lord over God’s household, in contrast to Moses who is a servant in God’s house.

After all, being like Moses doesn’t mean that Christ must be identical to Moses in every manner. Jesus is like Moses in all the ways that the Torah states that the prophet like Israel’s great lawgiver is supposed to be.
