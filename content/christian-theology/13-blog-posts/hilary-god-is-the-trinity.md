# Hilary: God is the Trinity

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * 1 day ago
  * 5 min read


Here I cite from St. Hilary of Poitier’s work [_On the Trinity_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3302.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , [_Book VII_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/330207.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , where this holy saint affirms that the term God refers the divine Persons who share the same name and nature. All emphasis will be mine.

  


31\. We see how the living Son of the living Father, He Who is God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , **reveals the unity of the Divine nature** , indissolubly One and the same, and the [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of His birth in these words, ** _I and the Father are One_**.

  


Because the seeming arrogance of them engendered a prejudice against Him, He made it more clear that He had spoken in the conscious possession of Divinity by saying, ** _You say that I have_**[** __blasphemed__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _because I said, I am the_**[** __Son of God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _;_** thus showing that the oneness of His nature with that of God was due to birth from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>).

  


And then, to clench their [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) in His birth by a positive assertion, and to guard them, at the same time, from imagining that the birth involves a difference of nature, He crowns His argument with the words, ** _Believe the works, that the Father is in Me, and I in the Father_**.

  


**Does His birth, as here revealed, display His Divinity as not His by nature, as not His own by right? Each is in the Other; the birth of the Son is from the Father only; no alien or unlike nature has been raised to Godhead and subsists as**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>).

  


God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , [ _eternally_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) abiding, owes His Godhead to none other than God. Import, if you see your opportunity, two gods into the Church’s [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; separate Son from Father as far as you can, consistently with the birth which you admit; yet still the Father is in the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and the Son is in the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , **and this by no interchange of emanations but by the perfect birth of the living nature**.

  


Thus you cannot add together [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) the Father and God the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and count Them as two Gods, **for They Two are One God. You cannot confuse Them together, for They Two are not One Person**.

  


**And so the Apostolic**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**rejects two gods** ; for it [ _knows_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) nothing of two Fathers or two Sons. In confessing the Father it confesses the Son; it believes in the Son in believing in the Father.

  


**For the name of Father involves that of Son, since without having a son none can be a father. Evidence of the**[** _existence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**of a son is**[** _proof_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**that there has been a father, for a son cannot exist except from a father. When we confess that God is One WE DENY THAT HE IS SINGLE; for the Son is the complement of the**[** _Father_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, and to the Father the Son’s**[** _existence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**is due**.

  


But birth works no change in the Divine nature; both in Father and in Son that nature is [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) to its kind. And the right expression for us of this unity of nature is the confession **that They, being Two by birth and generation, are One**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, not one Person**.

  


32\. We will leave it to him to preach two Gods, who can preach One God without confessing the unity; he shall proclaim that God is solitary, who can deny that there are two Persons, Each dwelling in the Other by the power of Their nature and the [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of birth given and received.

  


And that man may assign a different nature to Each of the Two, who is [ _ignorant_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) that the unity of Father and of Son is a revealed [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>).

  


**Let the**[** _heretics_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**blot out this record of the Son’s self-revelation**** _I in the Father and the Father in Me;_** then, and not till then, shall they assert that there are two Gods, or one God in loneliness.

  


There is no hint of more natures than one in what we are told of Their possession of the one Divine nature. **The**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**that God is from God does not multiply God by two; the birth destroys the supposition of a lonely God**.

  


And again, **__because They are interdependent They form an unity; and that They are interdependent is__**[** __proved__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** __by Their being One from One__**.

  


For the One, in begetting the One, conferred upon Him nothing that was not His own; and the One, in being begotten, received from the One only what belongs to one.

  


**Thus the apostolic**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, in proclaiming the**[** _Father_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, will proclaim Him as One**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, and in confessing the Son will confess Him as One**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**; since one and the same Divine nature exists in Both** , and because, the Father being God and the Son being [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , **__and the one name of God expressing the__**[** __nature__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** __of Both, the term ‘One God?’ signifies the Two__**** _. God from_**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, or God in_**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, does not mean that there are two Gods, for God abides, One from One,_**[**_eternally_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** __****__with the one Divine nature and the one Divine name__**** _; nor does God dwindle down to a single Person, for One and One can never be in solitude_**.
