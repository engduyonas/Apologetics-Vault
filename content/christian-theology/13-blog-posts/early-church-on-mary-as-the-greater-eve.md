# EARLY CHURCH ON MARY AS THE GREATER EVE

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 1, 2025
  * 12 min read


In this post I will cite from a few of the early Christian writers who viewed Mary as the new Eve, just as Christ was the new Adam, who through her obedience undid what Eve’s disobedience did to creation.

  


**JUSTIN MARTYR**

  


Chapter 100. In what sense Christ is [called] Jacob, and Israel, and Son of Man

  


** _Justin:_**_Then what follows—’But You, the praise of_[ __Israel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, inhabit the_[ __holy__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) _place’— declared that He is to do something worthy of praise and wonderment, being about to rise again from the dead on the third day after the crucifixion; and this He has obtained from the Father. For I have showed already that_[ __Christ__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _is called both Jacob and_[ __Israel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _; and I have_[ __proved__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) _that it is not in the blessing of Joseph and Judah alone that what relates to Him was proclaimed mysteriously, but also in the_[ __Gospel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) _it is written that He said: ‘All things are delivered unto me by My Father.’ and, ‘No man_[ __knows__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _the Father but the Son; nor the Son but the_[ __Father__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and they to whom the Son will reveal Him.’_[ __Matthew 11:27__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat011.htm#verse27>) _Accordingly He revealed to us all that we have perceived by His_[ __grace__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) _out of the_[ __Scriptures__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _, so that we_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him to be the first-begotten of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and to be before all creatures; likewise to be the Son of the patriarchs, since He assumed flesh by the Virgin of their_[ __family__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>) _, and submitted to become a man without comeliness, dishonoured, and subject to suffering. Hence, also, among His words He said, when He was discoursing about His future sufferings: ‘The_[ __Son of man__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) _must suffer many things, and be rejected by the_[ __Pharisees__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11789b.htm>) _and_[ __Scribes__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13634a.htm>) _, and be crucified, and on the third day rise again.’_[ __Matthew 16:21__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat016.htm#verse21>) _He said then that He was the_[ __Son of man__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) _, either because of His birth by the Virgin, who was, as I said, of the_[ __family__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>) _of David and Jacob, and Isaac, and_[ __Abraham__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _; or because Adam was the father both of Himself and of those who have been first enumerated from whom Mary derives her descent. For we_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _that the fathers of_[ __women__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) _are the fathers likewise of those children whom their daughters bear. For [Christ] called one of His_[ __disciples__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) _— previously_[ __known__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _by the name of Simon — Peter; since he recognised Him to be Christ the_[ __Son of God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, by the revelation of His Father: and since we find it recorded in the memoirs of His_[ __apostles__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) _that He is the_[ __Son of God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, and since we call Him the_[ __Son__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, we have understood that He proceeded before all creatures from the Father by His power and will (for He is addressed in the writings of the_[ __prophets__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) _in one way or another as Wisdom, and the Day, and the East, and a Sword, and a Stone, and a Rod, and Jacob, and_[ __Israel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _);_**_and that He became man by the Virgin, in order that the disobedience which proceeded from the serpent might receive its destruction in the same manner in which it derived its origin. For Eve, who was a_**[** __virgin__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>)** _and undefiled, having conceived the word of the serpent, brought forth disobedience and death_** _. But the Virgin Mary received_[ __faith__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) _and_[ __joy__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) _, when the_[ __angel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _Gabriel announced the good tidings to her that the_[ __Spirit of the Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) _would come upon her, and the power of the Highest would overshadow her: wherefore also the Holy Thing begotten of her is the_[ __Son of God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _; and she replied, ‘Be it unto me according to your word.’_[ __Luke 1:38__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk001.htm#verse38>) __**_And by her has He been born, to whom we have_**[** __proved__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>)** _so many Scriptures refer, and by whom God destroys both the serpent and those_**[** __angels__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)** _and men who are like him_** _; but works deliverance from death to those who repent of their_[ __wickedness__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _and_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _upon Him._(_Dialogue with Trypho the Jew_ , [_Chapters 89-108_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/01287.htm>))

  


**IRENAEUS**

  


1\. Those, therefore, who allege that He took nothing from the Virgin do greatly [ _err_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) , [since,] in order that they may cast away the inheritance of the flesh, they also reject the analogy [between Him and Adam]. For if the one [who sprang] from the earth had indeed formation and substance from both the hand and workmanship of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , but the other not from the hand and workmanship of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , then He who was made after the image and likeness of the former did not, in that case, preserve the analogy of [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and He must seem an inconsistent piece of work, not having wherewith He may show His wisdom. But this is to say, that He also appeared putatively as man when He was not man, and that He was made man while taking nothing from man. For if He did not receive the substance of flesh from a [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) being, He neither was made man nor the [ _Son of man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) ; and if He was not made what we were, He did no great thing in what He suffered and endured. But every one will allow that we are [composed of] a body taken from the earth, and a [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) receiving spirit from [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). This, therefore, the [ _Word of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) was made, recapitulating in Himself His own handiwork; and on this account does He confess Himself the [ _Son of man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) , and blesses the meek, because they shall inherit the earth. [ _Matthew 5:5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat005.htm#verse5>) The [ _Apostle Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , moreover, in the Epistle to the Galatians, declares plainly, [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) sent His Son, made of a [ _woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>). [ _Galatians 4:4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal004.htm#verse4>) And again, in that to the Romans, he says, Concerning His Son, who was made of the seed of [ _David_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04642b.htm>) according to the flesh, who was [ _predestinated_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12378a.htm>) as the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) with power, according to the spirit of [ _holiness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) , by the [ _resurrection from the dead_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) , [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) our Lord. [ _Romans 1:3-4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom001.htm#verse3>)

  


2\. Superfluous, too, in that case is His descent into Mary; for why did He come down into her if He were to take nothing of her? Still further, if He had taken nothing of [ _Mary_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>) , He would never have availed Himself of those kinds of food which are derived from the earth, by which that body which has been taken from the earth is nourished; nor would He have hungered, [ _fasting_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05789c.htm>) those forty days, like [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) and Elias, unless His body was craving after its own proper nourishment; nor, again, would John His [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) have said, when writing of Him, But [ _Jesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , being wearied with the journey, was sitting [to rest]; [ _John 4:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh004.htm#verse6>) nor would David have proclaimed of Him beforehand, They have added to the grief of my wounds; nor would He have wept over Lazarus, nor have sweated great drops of blood; nor have declared, My [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) is exceeding sorrowful; [ _Matthew 26:38_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat026.htm#verse38>) nor, when His side was pierced, would there have come forth blood and water. For all these are tokens of the flesh which had been derived from the earth, which He had recapitulated in Himself, bearing [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) to His own handiwork.

  


3\. Wherefore Luke points out that the pedigree which traces the generation of our Lord back to [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) contains seventy-two generations, connecting the end with the beginning, and implying that it is He who has summed up in Himself all nations dispersed from [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) downwards, and all languages and generations of [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , together with [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) himself. Hence also was [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) himself termed by [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) the figure of Him that was to come, [ _Romans 5:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom005.htm#verse14>) because the Word, the Maker of all things, had formed beforehand for Himself the future dispensation of the [ _human race_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , connected with the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) ; God having [ _predestined_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12378a.htm>) that the first man should be of an animal nature, with this view, that he might be saved by the spiritual One. For inasmuch as He had a pre-[_existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) as a saving Being, it was necessary that what might be saved should also be called into [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , in order that the Being who saves should not exist in vain.

  


4\. In accordance with this design, [ _Mary the Virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>) is found [ _obedient_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) , saying, Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to your word. [ _Luke 1:38_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk001.htm#verse38>) **But Eve was disobedient; for she did not**[** _obey_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**when as yet she was a**[** _virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>). And even as she, having indeed a husband, Adam, but being nevertheless as yet a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) (for in Paradise they were both naked, and were not ashamed, [ _Genesis 2:25_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen002.htm#verse25>) inasmuch as they, having been created a short time previously, had no understanding of the procreation of children: for it was necessary that they should first come to adult age, and then multiply from that time onward), **having become disobedient, was made the**[** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>)**of death, both to herself and to the entire**[** _human race_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**; so also did Mary, having a**[** _man_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)****[**_betrothed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02537c.htm>)**[to her], and being nevertheless a**[** _virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>)**, by yielding**[** _obedience_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**, become the**[** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>)**of**[** _salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)**, both to herself and the whole**[** _human race_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>). And on this account does the law term a [ _woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) [ _betrothed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02537c.htm>) to a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , the wife of him who had [ _betrothed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02537c.htm>) her, although she was as yet a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) ; thus indicating the back-reference from Mary to Eve, because what is joined together could not otherwise be put asunder than by inversion of the process by which these bonds of union had arisen; **so that the former ties be cancelled by the latter, that the latter may set the former again at liberty**. And it has, in fact, happened that the first compact looses from the second tie, but that the second tie takes the position of the first which has been cancelled. For this reason did the Lord declare that the first should in [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) be last, and the last first. [ _Matthew 19:30_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat019.htm#verse30>) , [ _Matthew 20:16_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat020.htm#verse16>) And the [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , too, indicates the same, saying, instead of fathers, children have been born unto you. For the Lord, having been born the First-begotten of the dead, [ _Revelation 1:5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rev001.htm#verse5>) and receiving into His bosom the ancient fathers, has regenerated them into the life of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **He having been made Himself the beginning of those that live, as**[** _Adam_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>)**became the beginning of those who die**. [ _1 Corinthians 15:20-22_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co015.htm#verse20>) Wherefore also Luke, commencing the genealogy with the Lord, carried it back to Adam, indicating that it was He who regenerated them into the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of life, and not they Him. **And thus also it was that the knot of Eve’s disobedience was loosed by the**[** _obedience_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**of Mary. For what the virgin Eve had bound fast through unbelief, this did the virgin Mary set free through**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). (_Against Heresies_ , Book III, [**_Chapter 22_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103322.htm>) Christ assumed actual flesh, conceived and born of the Virgin)

  


1\. That the Lord then was manifestly coming to His own things, and was sustaining them by means of that creation which is supported by Himself, and was making a recapitulation of that disobedience which had occurred in connection with a tree, through the [ _obedience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) which was [exhibited by Himself when He hung] upon a tree, [the effects] also of that deception being done away with, by which that virgin Eve, who was already espoused to a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , was unhappily misled — was happily announced, through means of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [spoken] by the [ _angel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) to the [ _Virgin Mary_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>) , who was [also espoused] to a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>). For just as the former was led astray by the word of an [ _angel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , so that she fled from God when she had transgressed His word; so did the latter, **by an angelic communication, receive the glad tidings that she should sustain (****_portaret_****)**[**_God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, being**[** _obedient_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**to His word**. And if the former did disobey [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , yet the latter was persuaded to be [ _obedient_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **in order that the Virgin Mary might become the patroness (****_advocata_****) of the virgin Eve**. And thus, as the [ _human race_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) fell into bondage to death by means of a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) , **so is it rescued by a**[** _virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) ; virginal disobedience having been balanced **in the opposite scale by virginal**[** _obedience_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>). For in the same way the [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) of the first created man (_protoplasti_) receives amendment **by the correction of the First-begotten** , and the coming of the serpent is conquered **by the harmlessness of the dove** , those bonds being unloosed by which we had been fast bound to death. **TERTULLIAN**

  


Chapter 17. The Similarity of Circumstances Between the First and the Second Adam, as to the Derivation of Their Flesh. An Analogy Also Pleasantly Traced Between Eve and the Virgin Mary

  


But, leaving Alexander with his syllogisms, which he so perversely applies in his discussions, as well as with the [ _hymns_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07595a.htm>) of [ _Valentinus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) , which, with consummate assurance, he interpolates as the production of some respectable author, let us confine our inquiry to a single point — Whether Christ received flesh from the virgin?— that we may thus arrive at a certain [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) that His flesh was [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , if He derived its substance from His mother’s womb, although we are at once furnished with clear evidences of the [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) character of His flesh, from its name and description as that of a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and from the [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of its constitution, and from the system of its sensations, and from its suffering of death. Now, it will first be necessary to show what previous reason there was for the [ _Son of God’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) being born of a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>). He who was going to consecrate a new order of birth, must Himself be born after a novel fashion, concerning which Isaiah foretold how that the Lord Himself would give the sign. What, then, is the sign? Behold a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) shall conceive and bear a son. [ _Isaiah 7:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa007.htm#verse14>) Accordingly, a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) did conceive and bear [ _Emmanuel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>) , God with us. [ _Matthew 1:23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat001.htm#verse23>) This is the new nativity; a man is born in [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). And in this man God was born, taking the flesh of an ancient race, without the help, however, of the ancient seed, in order that He might reform it with a new seed, that is, in a spiritual manner, and cleanse it by the re-moval of all its ancient stains. But the whole of this new birth was prefigured, as was the case in all other instances, in ancient type, the Lord being born as man by a dispensation in which a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) was the medium. The earth was still in a [ _virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) state, reduced as yet by no [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) labour, with no seed as yet cast into its furrows, when, as we are told, God made man out of it into a living [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>). [ _Genesis 2:7_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen002.htm#verse7>) As, then, the first [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) is thus introduced to us, it is a [ _just_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) inference that the second [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) likewise, as the apostle has told us, was formed by [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) into a quickening spirit out of the ground — in other words, out of a flesh which was unstained as yet by any [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) generation. But that I may lose no opportunity of supporting my argument from the name of Adam, why is Christ called [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) by the apostle, unless it be that, as man, He was of that earthly origin? And even reason here maintains the same conclusion, because it was by just the contrary operation that God recovered His own image and likeness, of which He had been [ _robbed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14564b.htm>) by the [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>). **For it was while Eve was yet a**[** _virgin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>)**, that the ensnaring word had crept into her ear which was to build the edifice of death. Into a**[** _virgin’s_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>)****[**_soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**, in like manner, must be introduced that**[** _Word of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**which was to raise the fabric of life; so that what had been reduced to ruin by this sex, might by the selfsame sex be recovered to**[** _salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)**. As Eve had**[** _believed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)**the serpent, so Mary**[** _believed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)**the**[** _angel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). The delinquency which the one occasioned by believing, the other by believing effaced. But (it will be said) Eve did not at the [ _devil’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) word conceive in her womb. Well, she at all events conceived; **for the**[** _devil’s_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>)**word afterwards became as seed to her that she should conceive as an outcast, and bring forth in sorrow**. Indeed she gave birth to a fratricidal [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) ; **while Mary, on the contrary, bare one who was one day to secure**[** _salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)**to**[** _Israel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>)**, His own brother after the flesh, and the murderer of** Himself. God therefore sent down into the virgin’s womb His Word, as the good Brother, who should blot out the memory of the [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) brother. Hence it was necessary that Christ should come forth for the [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) of [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , in that condition  _of flesh_ into which man had entered ever since his condemnation. ([**_On the Flesh of Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250916023339/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0315.htm>))
