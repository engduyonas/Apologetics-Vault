# Polycarp, Pope Anicetus, Irenaeus & Easter

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 31, 2025
  * 8 min read


Updated: Oct 31, 2025

Church historian Eusebius recorded the early controversy that arose among the faithful in respect to the celebration of Easter. Some Christians such as Polycarp insisted that the Church should follow the example within the Gospels by observing it on the fourteenth day of Nisan, which is the date fixed by Scripture for commencing the feast of the Passover. Since our Lord was betrayed and crucified around that time, it only makes sense that these holy men of the faith would want to continue that tradition. Others insisted that the Church needed to follow the custom of the Apostles in regards to celebrating Christ’s resurrection on the actual day he rose, which was on a Sunday, and which they began calling the “Lord’s Day” (_Kyriake_) because of it. All emphasis will be mine.

  


Chapter 22. The Bishops that were well known at this Time.

  


In the tenth year of the reign of Commodus, Victor succeeded [ _Eleutherus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05378a.htm>) , the latter having held the [ _episcopate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>) for thirteen years. In the same year, after Julian had completed his tenth year, Demetrius received the charge of the parishes at Alexandria. At this time the above-mentioned Serapion, the eighth from the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , was still well known as [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of the church at [ _Antioch_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01570a.htm>). Theophilus presided at [ _Cæsarea in Palestine_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03134b.htm>) ; and Narcissus, whom we have mentioned before, still had charge of the church at Jerusalem. Bacchylus at the same time was [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of Corinth in Greece, and Polycrates of the parish of Ephesus. And besides these a multitude of others, as is likely, were then prominent. But we have given the names of those alone, the soundness of whose [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) has come down to us in writing.

  


Chapter 23. The Question then agitated concerning the Passover.

  


1\. A question of no small importance arose at that time. **For the parishes of all Asia, as from an older tradition, held that the fourteenth day of the moon, on which day the**[** _Jews_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**were commanded to**[** _sacrifice_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)**the lamb, should be observed as the feast of the Saviour’s passover**. It was therefore necessary to end their fast on that day, whatever day of the week it should happen to be. **_But it was not the custom of the churches in the rest of the world to end it at this time, as they observed the practice which, from apostolic tradition, has prevailed to the present time, of terminating the fast on no other day than on that of the resurrection of_**[** _our Saviour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>).

2\. Synods and assemblies of [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) were held on this account, **_and all, with one consent, through mutual correspondence drew up an_**[** _ecclesiastical_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _decree, that the_**[** _mystery_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)** _of the resurrection of the Lord should be celebrated on no other but the Lord’s day, and that we should observe the close of the paschal fast on this day only_**. There is still extant a writing of those who were then assembled in Palestine, over whom Theophilus, [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of Cæsarea, **and Narcissus,**[**_bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of Jerusalem** , presided. And there is also another writing extant of those who were assembled at [ _Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) to consider the same question, which bears the name of Bishop Victor; also of the [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) in [ _Pontus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12234c.htm>) over whom Palmas, as the oldest, presided; and of the parishes in [ _Gaul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06395b.htm>) **of which**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)**was**[** _bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , and of those in Osrhoëne and the cities there; and a personal letter of Bacchylus, [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of the church at [ _Corinth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04363b.htm>) , and of a great many others, who uttered the same opinion and judgment, and cast the same vote.

3\. **_And that which has been given above was their unanimous decision_**.

  


Chapter 24. The Disagreement in Asia.

  


1\. But the [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of Asia, led by Polycrates, decided to hold to the old custom handed down to them. He himself, in a letter which he addressed to Victor and the [ _church of Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07424b.htm>) , set forth in the following words the tradition which had come down to him:

  


2\. We observe the exact day; neither adding, nor taking away. For in Asia also great lights have fallen asleep, **which shall rise again on the day of the Lord’s coming, when he shall come with**[** _glory_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)**from heaven, and shall seek out all the**[** _saints_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>)**. Among these are**** _Philip, one of the twelve_**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, who fell asleep in Hierapolis; and his two aged virgin daughters, and another daughter, who lived in the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _and now rests at Ephesus_****; and, moreover, John, who was both a**[** _witness_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>)**and a teacher,****_who reclined upon the bosom of the Lord, and, being a_**[** _priest_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)** _, wore the sacerdotal plate_**.

  


3\. **He fell asleep at Ephesus**.

  


4\. And [ _Polycarp_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>) in [ _Smyrna_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14060b.htm>) , who was a [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) and [ _martyr_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) ; and Thraseas, [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) and [ _martyr_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) from Eumenia, who fell asleep in [ _Smyrna_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14060b.htm>).

  


5\. Why need I mention the [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) and [ _martyr_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) Sagaris who fell asleep in [ _Laodicea_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08794a.htm>) , or the blessed Papirius, or [ _Melito_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10166b.htm>) , the Eunuch who lived altogether in the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , and who lies in [ _Sardis_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13472a.htm>) , **awaiting the**[** _episcopate_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>)**from heaven, when he shall rise from the dead**?

  


6\. **All these observed the fourteenth day of the passover according to the**[** _Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**, deviating in no respect, but following the rule of**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). And I also, Polycrates, the least of you all, do according to the tradition of my [ _relatives_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>) , some of whom I have closely followed. **For seven of my**[** _relatives_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)**were**[** _bishops_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**; and I am the eighth**. And my [ _relatives_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>) always observed the day when the people put away the leaven.

  


7\. I, therefore, brethren, who have lived sixty-five years in the [ _Lord_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and have met with the brethren throughout the world, and have gone through every [ _Holy Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , am not affrighted by terrifying words. For those greater than I have said ‘We ought to [ _obey_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) God rather than man.’ [ _Acts 5:29_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/act005.htm#verse29>)

  


8\. He then writes of all the [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) who were present with him and thought as he did. His words are as follows:

  


I could mention the [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) who were present, whom I summoned at your desire; whose names, should I write them, would constitute a great multitude. And they, beholding my littleness, gave their consent to the letter, [ _knowing_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that I did not bear my gray hairs in vain, **but had always governed my life by the**[** _Lord Jesus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>).

  


9\. Thereupon Victor, who presided over the church at [ _Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) , immediately attempted to cut off from the common unity the parishes of all Asia, with the churches that agreed with them, as heterodox; and he wrote letters and declared all the brethren there wholly excommunicate.

  


10\. But this did not please all the [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). And they besought him to consider the things of peace, and of neighborly unity and [ _love_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>). Words of theirs are extant, sharply rebuking Victor.

  


11\. **Among them was**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>) , who, sending letters in the name of the brethren in [ _Gaul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06395b.htm>) over whom he presided, **_maintained that the_**[** _mystery_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)** _of the resurrection of the Lord should be observed only on the Lord’s day_**. He fittingly admonishes Victor that he should not cut off whole churches of God **which observed the tradition of an ancient custom** and after many other words he proceeds as follows:

  


12\. For the controversy is not only concerning the day, but also concerning the very manner of the fast. For some think that they should fast one day, others two, yet others more; some, moreover, count their day as consisting of forty hours day and night.

13\. And this variety in its observance has not originated in our time; but long before in that of our ancestors. **It is likely that they did not hold to strict accuracy, and thus formed a custom for their posterity according to their own simplicity and peculiar mode**. Yet all of these lived none the less in peace, and we also live in peace with one another; and the disagreement in regard to the fast confirms the agreement in the [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>).

14\. He adds to this the following account, which I may properly insert:

Among these were the [ _presbyters_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) before [ _Soter_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03144c.htm>) , who presided over the church which you now rule. We mean [** _Anicetus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) , and [ _Pius_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12126b.htm>) , and [ _Hyginus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07593a.htm>) , and [ _Telesphorus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14477b.htm>) , and Xystus. They neither observed it themselves, nor did they permit those after them to do so. **And yet though not observing it, they were none the less at peace with those who came to them from the parishes in which it was observed** ; although this observance was more opposed to those who did not observe it.

  


15\. **But none were ever cast out on account of this form** ; but the [ _presbyters_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) before you who did not observe it, **sent the eucharist to those of other parishes who observed it**.

  


16\. And when **the blessed**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**was at**[** _Rome_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)**in the time of**[** _Anicetus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) , and they disagreed a little about certain other things,**they immediately made peace with one another, not caring to quarrel over this matter. For neither could**[** _Anicetus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>)**persuade**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**not to observe**** _what he had always observed with John the_**[** _disciple_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)** _of our Lord, and the other_**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _with whom he had associated_****; neither could**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**persuade**[** _Anicetus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>)**to observe it as he said that he ought to follow the customs of the**[** _presbyters_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**that had preceded him**.

  


17\. But though matters were in this shape, **they communed together, and**[** _Anicetus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>)**conceded the administration of the eucharist in the church to**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**, manifestly as a mark of respect.****_And they parted from each other in peace, both those who observed, and those who did not, maintaining the peace of the whole church_**.

  


18\. Thus [ _Irenæus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>) , who [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) was well named, became a peacemaker in this matter, exhorting and negotiating in this way in behalf of the peace of the churches. And he conferred by letter about this mooted question, not only with Victor, but also with most of the other rulers of the churches.

  


Chapter 25. How All came to an Agreement respecting the Passover.

  


1\. Those in Palestine whom we have recently mentioned, **Narcissus** and Theophilus, and with them Cassius, [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of the church of [ _Tyre_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15109a.htm>) , and Clarus of the church of Ptolemais, and those who met with them, having stated many things respecting the tradition concerning the passover **_which had come to them in succession from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , at the close of their writing add these words:

  


2\. Endeavor to send copies of our letter to every church, that we may not furnish occasion to those who easily deceive their [ _souls_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>). We show you indeed that also in [ _Alexandria_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01299d.htm>) they keep it on the same day that we do. For letters are carried from us to them and from them to us, so that in the same manner and at the same time we keep the sacred day.

  


Chapter 26. The Elegant Works of Irenæus which have come down to us.

Besides the works and letters of [ _Irenæus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>) which we have mentioned, a certain book of his On Knowledge, written against the Greeks, very concise and remarkably forcible, is extant; and another, which he dedicated to a brother Marcian, In Demonstration of the Apostolic Preaching; and a volume containing various Dissertations, in which he mentions the Epistle to the Hebrews **and the so-called Wisdom of Solomon, making quotations from them**. These are the works of [ _Irenæus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>) which have come to our [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>).
