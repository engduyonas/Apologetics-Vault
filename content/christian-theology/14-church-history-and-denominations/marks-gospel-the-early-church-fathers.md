# MARK’S GOSPEL & THE EARLY CHURCH FATHERS

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 18 min read


In this post I will cite the unanimous testimony of the early church fathers of the first four centuries that Mark wrote the Gospel attributed to him while in Rome, and that he essentially wrote down the Apostle Peter’s proclamation of the Gospel, having been taken on by the blessed Apostle as his spiritual son and protégé. All bold emphasis will be mine.

  


**PAPIAS (60-130 AD)**

  


15\. This also the [ _presbyter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) said: Mark, **having become the interpreter of Peter** , wrote down accurately, though not in order, whatsoever he remembered of the things said or done by [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). For he neither heard the [ _Lord_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) nor followed him, **but afterward** , as I said, **he followed Peter, who adapted his teaching to the needs of his hearers** , but with no intention of giving a connected account of the [ _Lord’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) discourses, so that Mark committed no [ _error_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) while he thus wrote some things as he remembered them. For he was careful of one thing, not to omit any of the things which he had heard, and not to state any of them [ _falsely_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>). These things are related by [ _Papias_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>) concerning Mark. (Eusebius, Church History, [_Book III_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250103.htm>) , Chapter 39. The Writings of Papias)

  


**JUSTIN MARTYR (100-165 AD)**

  


Chapter 106. Christ’s resurrection is foretold in the conclusion of the Psalm

  


**Justin:** The remainder of the Psalm makes it manifest that He [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) His Father would grant to Him all things which He asked, and would raise Him from the dead; and that He urged all who [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) God to praise Him because He had compassion on all races of believing men, through the [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) of Him who was crucified; and that He stood in the midst of His brethren the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) (who repented of their flight from Him when He was crucified, after He rose from the dead, and after they were persuaded by Himself that, before His [ _passion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11530a.htm>) He had mentioned to them that He must suffer these things, and that they were announced beforehand by the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)), and when living with them sang praises to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **as is made evident in the memoirs of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). The words are the following: ‘I will declare Your name to my brethren; in the midst of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) will I praise You. You that [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) the Lord, praise Him; all you, the seed of Jacob, glorify Him. Let all the seed of [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) Him.’ **And when it is said that He changed the name of one of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**to Peter; and when it is written in the memoirs of Him that this so happened, as well as that He changed the names of other two brothers, the sons of Zebedee, to Boanerges, which means sons of thunder** ; this was an announcement of the fact that it was He by whom Jacob was called [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) , and Oshea called Jesus (Joshua), under whose name the people who survived of those that came from [ _Egypt_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) were conducted into the land promised to the patriarchs. And that He should arise like a star from the seed of [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) showed before hand when he thus said, ‘A star shall arise from Jacob, and a leader from [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) ;’ [ _Numbers 24:17_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/num024.htm#verse17>) and another [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible>) says, ‘Behold a man; the East is His name.’ Accordingly, when a star rose in heaven at the time of His birth, as is recorded in the memoirs of His [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , the [ _Magi_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09527a.htm>) from Arabia, recognising the sign by this, came and worshipped Him.(_Dialogue with Trypho the Jew_ , [**_Chapters 89-108_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/01287.htm>))

  


Justine quotes the following passage from Mark, thereby identifying this Gospel as the memoirs of Peter:

  


“He went up into the mountain and called to Him those whom He desired, and they came to Him.He ordained twelve to be with Him, and to be sent out to preach,and to have authority to heal sicknesses and to cast out demons:Simon, whom He named Peter; **James, the son of Zebedee, and John, the brother of James, whom he surnamed Boanerges (meaning Sons of Thunder)** ; Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas; and James the son of Alphaeus; and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Zealot; and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Him.” Mark 3:13-19 Modern English Version (MEV)

  


**IRENAEUS OF LYONS (130-202 AD)**

  


_The apostles did not commence to preach the Gospel, or to place anything on record until they were endowed with the gifts and power of the Holy Spirit. They preached one God alone, Maker of heaven and earth._

  


1\. We have learned from none others the plan of our [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) , than from those through whom the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) has come down to us, which they did at one time proclaim in public, and, at a later period, by the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , handed down to us in the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , to be the ground and pillar of our [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). For it is unlawful to assert that they preached before they possessed perfect [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , as some do even venture to say, boasting themselves as improvers of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). For, after our Lord rose from the dead, [the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)] were invested with power from on high when the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) came down [upon them], were filled from all [His gifts], and had perfect [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) : they departed to the ends of the earth, preaching the glad tidings of the [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) things [sent] from God to us, and proclaiming the peace of heaven to [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , who indeed do all equally and individually possess the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Matthew also issued a written [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) among the Hebrews in their own dialect, while Peter and [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) were preaching at [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) , and laying the foundations of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). **After their departure, Mark, the**[** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**and interpreter of Peter, did also hand down to us in writing what had been preached by Peter**. Luke also, the companion of [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , recorded in a book the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) preached by him. Afterwards, John, the [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of the Lord, who also had leaned upon His breast, did himself publish a [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) during his residence at [ _Ephesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) in Asia.

  


2\. These have all declared to us that there is [ _one God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10499a.htm>) , Creator of heaven and earth, announced by the law and the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) ; and one Christ the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). If any one do not agree to these truths, he despises the companions of the Lord; nay more, he despises Christ Himself the Lord; yea, he despises the Father also, and stands self-condemned, resisting and opposing his own [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) , as is the case with all [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). (Against Heresies, Book III, [**_Chapter 1_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103301.htm>))

  


**TERTULLIAN (155-240 AD)**

  


Chapter 5. By the Rule of Antiquity, the Catholic Gospels are Found to Be True, Including the Real St. Luke’s. Marcion’s Only a Mutilated Edition. The Heretic’s Weakness and Inconsistency in Ignoring the Other Gospels.

  


On the whole, then, if that is evidently more [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) which is earlier, if that is earlier which is from the very beginning, if that is from the beginning which has the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) for its authors, then it will certainly be quite as evident, that that comes down from the apostles, which has been kept as a sacred deposit in the churches of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). Let us see what milk the Corinthians drank from [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ; to what rule  _of_[ __faith__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) the Galatians were brought for correction; what the Philippians, the Thessalonians, the Ephesians read  _by it_ ; what utterance also the Romans give, so very near (to the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)), to whom Peter and [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) conjointly bequeathed the gospel even sealed with their own blood. We have also  _St._ John’s foster churches. For although [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) rejects his Apocalypse, the order of the [ _bishops_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) (thereof), when traced up to their origin, will yet rest on John as their author. In the same manner is recognised the excellent source of the other churches. I say, therefore, that in them (and not simply such of them as were founded by [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , but in all those which are united with them in the fellowship of the [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)  _of the gospel of Christ_ ) that [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of Luke which we are defending with all our might has stood its ground from its very first publication; whereas [ _Marcion’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) is not [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to most people, and to none whatever is it [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) without being at the same time condemned. It too, of course, has its churches, but specially its own — as late as they are spurious; and should you want to [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) their original, you will more easily discover [ _apostasy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01624b.htm>) in it than apostolicity, with [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) forsooth as their founder, or some one of [ _Marcion’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) swarm.

  


Even wasps make combs; so also these Marcionites make churches. The same authority of the [ _apostolic churches_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01634a.htm>) will afford evidence to the other [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) also, which we possess equally through their means, and according to their usage — I mean the [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of John and Matthew — **while that which Mark published may be affirmed to be Peter’s whose interpreter Mark was**. For even Luke’s form of the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) men usually ascribe to [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>). And it may well seem that the works which [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) publish belong to their masters. Well, then, [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) ought to be called to a strict account concerning these (other [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)) also, for having omitted them, and insisted in preference on Luke; as if they, too, had not had free course in the [ _churches_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , as well as Luke’s [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , from the beginning. Nay, it is even more credible that they [ _existed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) from the very beginning; for, being the work of [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , they were prior, and coeval in origin with the churches themselves. But how comes it to pass, if the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) published nothing, that their [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) were more forward in such a work; for they could not have been [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , without any instruction from their masters? If, then, it be evident that these ([_Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)) also were current in the [ _churches_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , why did not [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) touch them — either to amend them if they were adulterated, or to acknowledge them if they were uncorrupt? For it is but natural that they who were perverting the gospel, should be more solicitous about the perversion of those things whose authority they [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to be more generally received.

  


Even the false [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) (were so called) on this very account, because they imitated the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) by means of their falsification. In  _as_ far, then, as he might have amended what there was to amend, if found corrupt, in  _so_ far did he firmly imply that all was free from corruption which he did not think required amendment. In short, he simply amended what he thought was corrupt; though, indeed, not even this [ _justly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) , because it was not really corrupt. For if the ([_Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)) of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) have come down to us in their integrity, while Luke’s, which is received among us, so far accords with their rule as to be on a par with them in permanency of reception in the [ _churches_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , it clearly follows that Luke’s [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) also has come down to us in like integrity until the [ _sacrilegious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13321a.htm>) treatment of [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>). In short, when [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) laid hands on it, it then became diverse and hostile to the [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). I will therefore advise his followers, that they either change these [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , however late to do so, into a conformity with their own, whereby they may seem to be in agreement with the apostolic writings (for they are daily retouching their work, as daily they are convicted by us); or else that they blush for their master, who stands self-condemned either way — when once he hands on the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of the gospel [ _conscience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>) smitten, or again subverts it by shameless tampering. Such are the summary arguments which we use, when we take up arms against [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) for the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) of the gospel, maintaining both that order of periods, which rules that a late date is the mark of forgers, and that authority of churches which lends support to the tradition of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) ; because [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) must needs precede the [ _forgery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06135b.htm>) , and proceed straight from those by whom it has been handed on. (_Against Marcion_ , [_Book IV_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/03124.htm>))

  


**HIPPOLYTUS (170-236 AD)**

  


Chapter 18. Source of Marcionism; Empedocles Reasserted as the Suggester of the Heresy.

  


When, therefore, [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) or some one of his hounds barks against the [ _Demiurge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm>) , and adduces reasons from a comparison of what is [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) and bad, we ought to say to them, that neither [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) the apostle **nor Mark, he of the maimed finger, announced such (tenets)**. For none of these (doctrines) has been written in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to Mark. But (the real author of the system) is Empedocles, son of Meto, a native of Agrigento. And ([_Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>)) despoiled this ([_philosopher_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>)), and imagined that up to the present would pass undetected his transference, under the same expressions, of the arrangement of his entire [ _heresy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) from [ _Sicily_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13772a.htm>) into the evangelical narratives. For bear with me, O [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) : as you have instituted a comparison of what is [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) and [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) , I also today will institute a comparison following up your own tenets, as you suppose them to be. You affirm that the [ _Demiurge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm>) of the world is [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) — why not hide your countenance in shame, (as thus) teaching to the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) the doctrines of Empedocles? You say that there is a good Deity who destroys the works of the [ _Demiurge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm>) : then do not you plainly preach to your pupils, as the good Deity, the Friendship of Empedocles. You forbid marriage, the procreation of children, (and) the abstaining from meats which [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) has created for participation by the [ _faithful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm>) , and those that [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>).[_1 Timothy 4:3_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti004.htm#verse3>) (Do you think, then,) that you can escape detection, (while thus) enjoining the purificatory [ _rites_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13064b.htm>) of Empedocles? For in point of fact you follow in every respect this ([_philosopher_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) of paganism), while you instruct your own [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) to refuse meats, in order not to eat any body (that might be) a remnant of a [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) which has been punished by the [ _Demiurge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm>). You dissolve marriages that have been cemented by the Deity. And here again you conform to the tenets of Empedocles, in order that for you the work of Friendship may be perpetuated as one (and) indivisible. For, according to Empedocles, matrimony separates unity, and makes (out of it) plurality, as we have [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>). (_Refutation of All Heresies_ , [**_BOOK 7_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/050107.htm>))

2\. The twenty-two books of the Hebrews are the following: That which is called by us Genesis, but by the Hebrews, from the beginning of the book, Bresith, which means, ‘In the beginning’; Exodus, Welesmoth, that is, ‘These are the names’; Leviticus, Wikra, ‘And he called’; Numbers, Ammesphekodeim; Deuteronomy, Eleaddebareim, ‘These are the words’; Jesus, the son of Nave, Josoue ben Noun; Judges and Ruth, among them in one book, Saphateim; the First and Second of Kings, among them one, Samouel, that is, ‘The called of God’; the Third and Fourth of Kings in one, Wammelch David, that is, ‘The kingdom of David’; of the Chronicles, the First and Second in one, Dabreïamein, that is, ‘Records of days’; Esdras, First and Second in one, Ezra, that is, ‘An assistant’; the book of Psalms, Spharthelleim; the Proverbs of Solomon, Meloth; Ecclesiastes, Koelth; the Song of Songs (not, as some suppose, Songs of Songs), Sir Hassirim; Isaiah, Jessia; Jeremiah, with Lamentations and the epistle in one, Jeremia; Daniel, Daniel; Ezekiel, Jezekiel; Job, Job; Esther, Esther. **ANTI-MARCIONITE PROLOGUES TO THE GOSPELS (2****nd****– 4****th****Centuries)**

  


**_Mark_**

  


… Mark recorded, who was called Colobodactylus [ _1_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/http://www.tertullian.org/fathers/anti_marcionite_prologues.htm#1>) , because he had fingers that were too small for the height of the rest of his body. He himself was the interpreter of Peter. After the death of Peter himself, the same man wrote this gospel in the parts of Italy. 

  


** _Luke_**

  


Indeed Luke was an Antiochene Syrian, a doctor by profession, a disciple of the apostles: later however he followed Paul until his martyrdom, serving the Lord blamelessly. He never had a wife, he never fathered children, and died at the age of eighty-four, full of the Holy Spirit, in Boetia. Therefore — although gospels had already been written —- indeed by Matthew in Judaea but by Mark in Italy —- moved by the Holy Spirit he wrote down this gospel in the parts of Achaia, signifying in the preface that the others were written before his, but also that it was of the greatest importance for him to expound with the greatest diligence the whole series of events in his narration for the Greek believers, so that they would not be led astray by the lure of Jewish fables, or, seduced by the fables of the heretics and stupid solicitations, fall away from the truth. And so at once at the start he took up the extremely necessary [story] from the birth of John, who is the beginning of the gospel, the forerunner of our Lord Jesus Christ, and was a companion in the perfecting of the people, likewise in the introducing of baptism and a companion in martyrdom. Of this disposition the prophet Malachi, one of the twelve, certainly makes mention. And indeed afterwards the same Luke wrote the Acts of the Apostles. Later the apostle John wrote the Apocalypse on the island of Patmos, and then the Gospel in Asia. 

  


**John**

  


The Gospel of John was revealed and given to the churches by John while still in the body, just as Papias of Hieropolis, the close disciple of John, related in the exoterics, that is, in the last five books. Indeed he wrote down the gospel, while John was dictating carefully. But the heretic Marcion, after being condemned by him because he was teaching [ _2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250906051912/http://www.tertullian.org/fathers/anti_marcionite_prologues.htm#2>) the opposite to him [John], was expelled by John. But he [Marcion] had brought writings or letters to him [John] from the brothers which were in Pontus.

**JEROME (340-420 AD)**

  


(2) The first of all is Matthew, the tax collector, who is also named Levi, who published a Gospel in Judea in the Hebrew language, chiefly for the sake of those from the Jews who had believed in Jesus and who were by no means observing the shadow of the Law, since the truth of the Gospel had succeeded it. Mark is the second, **the interpreter of the apostle Peter and the first bishop of the Alexandrian church** , who indeed did not himself see the Lord and Savior, but he narrated the things **which he heard his master preaching** in accordance with the reliability of the events rather than their sequence. The third is Luke, the physician, a Syrian by birth from Antioch whose praise is in his Gospel, who also himself, a disciple of the apostle Paul, composed his book in the regions of Achaia and Boetia, tracing out certain matters more deeply, and as he himself admits in the preface, describing things that had been heard rather than seen. The last is John the apostle and evangelist, whom Jesus loved very much. While reclining upon the Lord’s breast, he drank in the purest of doctrines. He alone deserved to hear from the cross: “Behold your mother.”

  


When he was in Asia, even then the seeds of the heretics were already sprouting, of Cerinthus, of Ebion, and of the others who deny that Christ came in the flesh. John himself in his epistle calls them antichrists, and the Apostle Paul frequently smites them. At that time John was compelled by nearly all the bishops of Asia and delegations from many churches to write more deeply concerning the divinity of the Savior and to break through, so to speak, unto the very Word of God, through a boldness that was not so much audacious as blessed. This is the source of the church’s historical tradition that when he was compelled by the brothers to write, he answered that he would pray to the Lord. When this had been carried out and he had been abundantly filled with revelation, he poured forth that heaven-sent prologue: “In the beginning was the Word and the Word was with God and the Word was God.
