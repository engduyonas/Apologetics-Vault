# Tertullian: Trinity is the Faith of the Ancient Church

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 30, 2025
  * 11 min read


Updated: Oct 30, 2025

In refuting the modalist heretic named Praxeas, the second-third century AD North African Apologist Tertullian stresses the fact that belief in the Trinity has always been the ancient belief of the Church, much older than the hereies spewed by Praxeus and others. Here’s what he wrote:

  


Chapter 2. The Catholic Doctrine of the Trinity and Unity, Sometimes Called the Divine Economy, or Dispensation of the Personal Relations of the Godhead

In the course of [ _time_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14726a.htm>) , then, the Father forsooth was born, and the Father suffered, [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) Himself, the Lord Almighty, whom in their preaching they declare to be [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). We, however, as we indeed always have done (and more especially since we have been better instructed by the Paraclete, who leads men indeed into all [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)), [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that there is one only [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , but under the following dispensation, or οἰκονομία, as it is called, that this one only God **has also a Son, His Word, who proceeded from Himself, by whom all things were made, and without whom nothing was made. Him**  _we_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to have been sent by the Father into the Virgin, and to have been born of her — being both Man and [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the [ _Son of Man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) and the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and to have been called by the name of [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) ;  _we_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) Him to have suffered, died, and been buried, according to the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , and, after He had been raised again by the Father and taken back to heaven, to be sitting at the right hand of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , ** _and_****that He will come to judge the quick and the dead; who sent also from heaven from the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, according to His own promise, the**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**, the Paraclete** , the sanctifier of the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) of those who [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and in the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and in the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). **_That this rule of_**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)** _HAS COME DOWN TO US FROM THE BEGINNING OF THE GOSPEL, EVEN BEFORE ANY OF THE OLDER HERETICS, MUCH BEFORE PRAXEAS,_****__a pretender__**** _of yesterday, will be apparent both from the lateness of date which marks all_**[** _heresies_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)** _, and also from the absolutely novel character of our new-fangled Praxeas_**. In this principle also we must henceforth find a presumption of equal force against all [ _heresies_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) whatsoever — that whatever is first is [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , whereas that is spurious which is later in date. But keeping this prescriptive rule inviolate, still some opportunity must be given for reviewing (the statements of [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)), with a view to the instruction and protection of various [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) ; were it only that it may not seem that each perversion  _of the_[ __truth__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) is condemned without examination, and simply prejudged; **especially in the case of this**[** _heresy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**, which supposes itself to possess the pure**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**, in thinking that one cannot**[** _believe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)**in One Only God in any other way than by saying that the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, and the**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**are the very selfsame Person**. As if in this way also one were not All, in that All are of One, **_by unity (that is) of substance_** ; while the [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) of the dispensation is still guarded, **which distributes the Unity into a Trinity, placing in their order the three**** _Persons_****— the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, and the**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) : three, however, not in condition, but in degree; not in substance, but in form; not in power, but in aspect; **yet of one substance, and of one condition, and of one power, inasmuch as He is**[** _one God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10499a.htm>)**, from whom these degrees and forms and aspects are reckoned, under the name of the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and of the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, and of the**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). How they are susceptible of number without division, will be shown as our treatise proceeds. ([_Against Praxeas_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0317.htm>) ; emphasis mine)

  


Tertullian then proceeds to quote texts to prove that the Father and the son are personally distinct from each other, and yet one in substance and essence:

  


Chapter 22. Sundry Passages of St. John Quoted, to Show the Distinction Between the Father and the Son. Even Praxeas’ Classic Text — I and My Father are One — Shown to Be Against Him

  


Again, whose doctrine does He announce, at which all were astonished? Was it His own or the Father’s? So, when they were in [ _doubt_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) among themselves whether He were the Christ (not as being the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , of course but as the Son), He says to them  _You are not_[ __ignorant__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) _whence I am; and I am not come of myself, but He that sent me is_[ __true__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _, whom you_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _not; but I_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him, because I am from Him_. **He did not say,** ** _Because I myself am He; and, I have sent my own self_** : but His words are,  _He has sent me_. When, likewise, the [ _Pharisees_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11789b.htm>) sent men to apprehend Him, He says:  _Yet a little while am I with you, and (then) I go unto Him that sent me_. When, however, He declares that He is not alone, and uses these words,  _but I and the Father that sent me_ , [ _John 8:16_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh008.htm#verse16>) **does He not show that there are Two — Two, and yet inseparable?** **Indeed, this was the sum and substance of what He was teaching them, that they were inseparably Two; since, after citing the law when it affirms the**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**of two men’s testimony, He adds** **at once** :  _I am one who am bearing_[ __witness__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) _of myself; and the Father (is another,) who has sent me, and bears_[ __witness__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) _of me_. ** _Now, if He were one — being at once both the Son and the Father — He certainly would not have quoted the sanction of the law, which requires not the testimony of one, but of two_**. Likewise, when they asked Him where His Father was, He answered them, that they had [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) neither Himself nor the Father; and in this answer He plainly told them of  _Two_ , whom they were [ _ignorant_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) of. Granted that if they had [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) Him, they would have [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the Father also, this certainly does not imply that He was Himself both Father and Son; **but that, by reason of the inseparability of the Two, it was impossible for one of them to be either acknowledged or unknown without the other**.  _He that sent me_ , says He,  _is_[ __true__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _; and I am telling the world those things which I have heard of Him_. [ _John 8:26_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh008.htm#verse26>) And the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) narrative goes on to explain in an exoteric manner, that they understood not that He spoke to them concerning the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , although they ought certainly to have [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that the Father’s words were  _uttered_ in the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , because they read in Jeremiah,  _And the Lord said to me, Behold, I have put my words in your mouth_ ; [ _Jeremiah 1:9_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/jer001.htm#verse9>) and again in Isaiah,  _The Lord has given to me the tongue of learning that I should understand when to speak a word in season_. [ _Isaiah 50:4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa050.htm#verse4>) In accordance with which,  _Christ_ Himself says:  _Then shall you_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _that I am He and that I am saying nothing of my own self; but that, as my Father has taught me, so I speak, because He that sent me is with me_. [ _John 8:28-29_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh008.htm#verse28>) This also amounts to a [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) that they were Two, (although) undivided.

  


Likewise, when upbraiding the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) in His discussion with them, because they wished to kill Him, He said,  _I speak that which I have seen with my Father, and you do that which you have seen with your father; but now you seek to kill me, a man that has told you the_[ __truth__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _which I have heard of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _; and again, If God were your Father, you would_[ __love__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) _me, for I proceeded forth and came from_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , (**still they are not hereby separated, although He declares that He proceeded forth**** _from the Father_**. Some [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) indeed seize the opportunity afforded them in these words  _to propound their_[ __heresy__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) _of His separation_ ; **_but His coming out from God is like the ray’s procession from the sun, and the river’s from the fountain, and the tree’s from the seed_**);  _I have not a_[ __devil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) _, but I_[ __honour__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _my Father; again, If I_[ __honour__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _myself, my_[ __honour__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _is nothing: it is my Father that honours me, of whom you say, that He is your God: yet you have not_[ __known__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him, but I_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him; and if I should say, I_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him not, I shall be a liar like you; but I_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him, and keep His saying_. [ _John 8:54-55_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh008.htm#verse54>) But when He goes on to say,  _Your father_[ __Abraham__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _rejoiced to see my day; and he saw it, and was glad_ , ** _He certainly proves that it was not the Father that appeared to_**[** _Abraham_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)** _, but the Son_**. In like manner He declares, in the case of the man  _born_ blind, that He must do the works of the Father  _which had sent Him_ ; [ _John 9:4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh009.htm#verse4>) and after He had given the man sight, He said to him, ** _Do you_**[** __believe__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)** _in the_**[** __Son of God__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _?_ Then, upon the man’s inquiring who  _He_ was, He proceeded to reveal Himself to him, as that Son  _of God_ whom He had announced to him as the right object of his [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). In a later passage He declares that He is [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) by the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the Father by Him; [ _John 10:15_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh010.htm#verse15>) adding that He was so wholly loved by the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , that He was laying down His life, because He had received this commandment from the Father.

  


When He was asked by the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) if He were the very Christ (meaning, of course, the Christ of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; **for to this day the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**expect not the Father Himself, but the Christ of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, it being nowhere said that the Father will come as the Christ**), He said to them,  _I am telling you, and yet you do not_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _: the works which I am doing, in my Father’s name, they actually bear_[ __witness__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) _of me_. Witness of what? Of that very thing, to be sure, of which they were making inquiry — whether He were the Christ of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Then, again, concerning His sheep, and (the assurance) that no man should pluck them out of His hand, He says,  _My Father, which gave them to me, is greater than all; adding immediately, I am and my Father are one_. Here, then, they take their stand, **_too infatuated, nay, too blind, to see in the first place that there is in this passage an intimation of Two Beings —_**** __I and my Father__**** _; then that there is a plural predicate,_****__are__**** _, inapplicable to one person only; and lastly, that (the predicate terminates in an abstract, not a personal noun)— we are one_**** __thing__**** __****__Unum__**** _, not one person_**** __Unus__**** _. For if He had said one Person, He might have rendered some assistance to their opinion._****__Unus__**** _, no_**[** _doubt_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>)** _, indicates the singular number; but (here we have a case where) Two are still the subject in the masculine gender. He accordingly says_**** __Unum__**** _, a neuter term, which does not imply singularity of number, but unity of_**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , likeness, conjunction, affection on the Father’s part, who loves the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and submission on the Son’s, who obeys the Father’s will. When He says, ** _I and my Father are one_********_in_**[** __essence__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**—**** _Unum_****— He shows that there are Two, whom He puts on an equality and unites in one**. He therefore adds to this very statement, that He had showed them many works from the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for none of which did He deserve to be [ _stoned_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14308a.htm>). [ _John 10:32_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh010.htm#verse32>) And to prevent their thinking Him deserving of this [ _fate_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05793a.htm>) , **as if He had claimed to be considered as God Himself, that is, the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, by having said, I and my Father are One** , representing Himself as the Father’s divine Son, and not as God Himself, He says,  _If it is written in your law, I said, You are gods; and if the_[ __Scripture__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _cannot be broken, say of Him whom the Father has sanctified and sent into the world, that He blasphemes, because He said, I am the_[ __Son of God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _?_  _If I do not the works of my Father,_[__believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _me not; but if I do, even if you will not_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _me, still_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _the works; and_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _that I am in the_[ __Father__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and the Father in me_. It must therefore be by the works that the Father is in the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and the Son in the Father; and so it is by the works that we understand that the Father is one  _with the Son_. All along did He therefore strenuously aim at this conclusion, **_that while they were of one power and_**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)** _, they should still be_**[** _believed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)** _to be Two; for otherwise, unless they were_**[** _believed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)** _to be Two, the Son could not possibly be_**[** _believed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)** _to have any_**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)** _at all_** …

  


Chapter 25. The Paraclete, or Holy Ghost. He is Distinct from the Father and the Son as to Their Personal Existence. One and Inseparable from Them as to Their Divine Nature. Other Quotations Out of St. John’s Gospel

  


What follows Philip’s question, and  _the Lord’s_ whole treatment of it, to the end of  _John’s_ [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , continues to furnish us with statements of the same kind, **distinguishing the Father and the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, with the properties of each**. Then there is the Paraclete  _or Comforter_ , also, which He promises to [ _pray_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) for to the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and to send from heaven after He had ascended to the Father.  _He is called_ another Comforter, indeed; [ _John 14:16_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh014.htm#verse16>) but in what way He is  _another_ we have already shown,  _He shall receive of mine_ , says Christ, [ _John 16:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh016.htm#verse14>) just as  _Christ_ Himself received of the Father’s. **_Thus the connection of the Father in the_**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _, and of the Son in the Paraclete, produces three coherent Persons,_****__who are yet distinct__**** _One from Another. These Three are one_**[** __essence__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)** _, not one_**** __Person__**** _, as it is said,_****__I and my Father are One__**** _,_**[**_John 10:30_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh010.htm#verse30>)** _in respect of unity of substance not singularity of number_**. Run through the whole [ __Gospel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , and you will find that He whom you [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to be the Father (described as acting for the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , although you, for your part, forsooth, suppose that the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , being the husbandman, [ _John 15:1_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh015.htm#verse1>) must surely have been on earth) is once more recognised by the Son as in heaven, when, lifting up His eyes thereto, [ _John 17:1_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh017.htm#verse1>) He commended His [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) to the safe-keeping of the Father. [ _John 17:11_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh017.htm#verse11>) We have, moreover, in that other [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) a clear revelation, i.e.  _of the Son’s distinction from the Father_ ,  _My_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, why have You forsaken me?_ [ _Matthew 27:46_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat027.htm#verse46>) and again, (in the third [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) ,)  _Father, into Your hands I commend my spirit_. [ _Luke 23:46_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk023.htm#verse46>) But even if (we had not these passages, we meet with satisfactory evidence) after His [ _resurrection_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) and [ _glorious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) victory over death. Now that all the restraint of His humiliation is taken away, He might, if possible, have shown Himself as the Father to so faithful a [ _woman_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) (as Mary Magdalene) when she approached to touch Him, out of [ _love_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) , not from curiosity, nor with [ _Thomas’_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14658b.htm>) incredulity.  _But not so_ ; Jesus says unto her,  _Touch me not, for I am not yet ascended to my Father; but go to my brethren_ (**and even in this He proves Himself to be the Son; for if He had been the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, He would have called them His**** _children_** , instead of His  _brethren_),  _and say unto them, I ascend unto my Father and your Father, and to my God and your God_. [ _John 20:17_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh020.htm#verse17>) ** _Now, does this mean, I ascend_****as the Father to the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and as God to God? Or as the Son to the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and as the Word to God?** Wherefore also does this [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , at its very termination, intimate that these things were ever written, if it be not, to use its own words,  _that you might_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _that_[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _is the_[ __Son of God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _?_ [ _John 20:31_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh020.htm#verse31>) ** _Whenever, therefore, you take any of the statements of this_**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _, and apply them to demonstrate the identity of the Father and the_**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _, supposing that they serve your views therein, you are contending against the definite purpose of the_**[** _Gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _. For these things certainly are not written that you may_**[** _believe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)** _that_**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _is the_**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194454/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, but the Son_**.
