# St. Cyprian: No Church Without the Bishop

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 5 min read


Here is what the blessed Cyprian wrote in respect to the authority, centrality and necessity of the Bishop:

  


3\. But with respect to what you have said, that [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) should be lowly, because both the Lord and His [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) were lowly; both all the brethren and [ _Gentiles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) also well [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) and [ _love_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) my humility; and you also [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) and loved it while you were still in the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , and were in communion with me. But which of us is far from humility: I, who daily serve the brethren, and kindly receive with good-will and [ _gladness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) every one that comes to the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) ; **or you, who appoint yourself**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of a**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, and judge of a judge, given for the time by**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**?** Although the Lord God says in Deuteronomy,  _And the man that will do presumptuously, and will not hearken unto the_[ __priests__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) _or unto the judge who shall be in those days, even that man shall die; and all the people, when they hear, shall_[ __fear__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _, and do no more presumptuously._ [ _Deuteronomy 17:12-13_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu017.htm#verse12>) And again He speaks to Samuel, and says,  _They have not despised you, but they have despised me._ [ _1 Samuel 8:7_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1sa008.htm#verse7>) And moreover the Lord, in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , when it was said to Him,  _Do you answer the_[ __high priest__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12407b.htm>) _so?_ guarding the [ _priestly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) dignity, and teaching that it ought to be maintained, would say nothing against the [ _high priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12407b.htm>) , but only clearing His own innocence, answered, saying,  _If I have spoken_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _, bear_[ __witness__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) _of the_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _; but if well, why do you smite me?_[_John 18:23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh018.htm#verse23>) The blessed apostle also, when it was said to him,  _Do you revile God’s_[ __high priest__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12407b.htm>) _?_ spoke nothing reproachfully against the [ _priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) , **when he might have lifted up himself boldly against those who had crucified the Lord, and who had already**[** _sacrificed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** _God and_**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and the temple and the [ _priesthood_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>) ; but even although in false and degraded [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) , considering still the mere empty shadow of the [ _priestly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) name, he said,  _I knew not, brethren, that he was the_[ __high priest__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12407b.htm>) _; for it is written, You shall not speak_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _of the ruler of your people._ [ _Acts 23:4-5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/act023.htm#verse4>)

  


4\. Unless perchance I was a [ _priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) to you before the [ _persecution_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) , when you held communion with me, and ceased to be a [ _priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) after the [ _persecution_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>)! For the [ _persecution_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) , when it came, **lifted you to the highest sublimity of**[** _martyrdom_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>). But it depressed me with the burden of proscription, since it was publicly declared, If any one holds or possesses any of the property of Caecilius Cyprian, [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of the [ _Christians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) ; so that even they **who did not**[** _believe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)**in God appointing a**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , could still [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in the [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) proscribing a [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). Nor do I boast of these things, but with grief I bring them forward, **since you constitute yourself a judge of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**and of**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , who says to the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , and thereby to all chief rulers, **who by vicarious ordination succeed to the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) :  _He that hears you, hears me; and he that hears me, hears Him that sent me; and he that despises you, despises me, and Him that sent me._ [ _Luke 10:16_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk010.htm#verse16>)

5\. For from this have arisen, and still arise, schisms and [ _heresies_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , **in that the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**who is one and rules over the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) is contemned by the [ _haughty_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12405a.htm>) presumption of some [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) ; **and the man who is honoured by God’s condescension** , is judged unworthy by [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>). For what swelling of [ _pride_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12405a.htm>) is this, what arrogance of [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , what inflation of [ _mind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10321a.htm>) , to call prelates and [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) to one’s own recognition, and unless I may be declared clear in your sight and absolved by your judgment, **behold now for six years the brotherhood has neither had a**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, nor the people a prelate, nor the flock a pastor, nor the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**a governor, nor Christ a representative, nor God a**[** _priest_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**!** Pupianus must come to the rescue, and give judgment, and declare the decision of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and Christ accepted, that so great a number of the [ _faithful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm>) who have been summoned away, under my rule, may not appear to have departed without hope of [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) and of peace; that the new crowd of [ _believers_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm>) may not be considered to have failed of attaining any [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) and the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) by my ministry; that the peace conferred upon so many lapsed and penitent [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) , and the communion vouchsafed by my examination, may not be abrogated by the authority of your judgment. Condescend for once, and deign to pronounce concerning us, and to establish our [ _episcopate_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>) by the authority of your recognition, that God and His Christ may thank you, in that by your means a representative and ruler has been restored as well to their altar as to their people…

  


8\. You have written also, that on my account the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) has now a portion of herself in a state of dispersion, although the whole people of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) are collected, and united, and joined to itself in an undivided concord: they alone have remained without, who even, if they had been within, would have had to be cast out. **Nor does the Lord, the protector of His people, and their guardian, suffer the wheat to be snatched from His floor** ; but the chaff alone can be separated from the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , since also the apostle says,  _For what if some of them have departed from the_[ __faith__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) _? Shall their unbelief make the_[ __faith__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) _of God of none effect? God forbid; for God is_[ __true__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _, but every man a liar._[_Romans 3:3-4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom003.htm#verse3>) And the Lord also in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , when [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) forsook Him as He spoke, turning to the twelve, said,  _Will you also go away? then Peter answered Him, Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the word of_[ __eternal__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) _life; and we_[ __believe__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _, and are sure,_**__that You are the Son of the living God__** _._[_John 6:67-69_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh006.htm#verse67>) Peter speaks there, on whom the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) was to be built, teaching and showing in the name of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , that although a rebellious and arrogant multitude of those who will not hear and [ _obey_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) may depart, **yet the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**does not depart from Christ; and they are the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**who are a people united to the**[** _priest_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**, and the flock which adheres to its pastor. Whence you ought to**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)******_that the_**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _is in the_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _, and the_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _in the_**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _; and if any one be not with the_**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _, that he is not in the_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , and that those flatter themselves in vain who creep in, not having peace with God’s [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) , and think that they communicate secretly with some; **while the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**,****_which is_**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)** _and one_****, is not cut nor divided, but is indeed connected and bound together by the cement of**[** _priests_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**who cohere with one another**. ([_Epistle 68_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212936/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/050668.htm>) ; emphasis will be mine)
