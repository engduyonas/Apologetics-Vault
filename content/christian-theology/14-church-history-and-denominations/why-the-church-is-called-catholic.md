# Why the Church is Called Catholic

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 6 min read


The oldest extant written mention of the term Catholic, as applied to the Church, is found in one of the letters of the holy martyr St. Ignatius, who was a disciple of the Apostles and the Bishop of Rome:

  


Chapter 8. Let nothing be done without the bishop

  


**See that you all follow the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, even as**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**does the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the [ _presbytery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>) as you would the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) ; and reverence the [ _deacons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>) , as being the institution of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **Let no man do anything connected with the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**without the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). Let that be deemed a proper [ _Eucharist_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05572c.htm>) , which is [administered] either by the [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , or by one to whom he has entrusted it. **Wherever the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**shall appear** , there let the multitude [of the people] also be; even as, **_wherever_**[** __Jesus Christ__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _is, there is the_**[** __Catholic__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)** __**[**__Church__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). It is not lawful without the [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) either to [ _baptize_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) or to celebrate a [ _love-feast_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01200b.htm>) ; but whatsoever he shall approve of, that is also pleasing to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , so that everything that is done may be secure and valid.

  


Chapter 9. Honour the bishop

  


Moreover, it is in accordance with reason that we should return to soberness [of conduct], and, while yet we have opportunity, exercise repentance towards God. **It is well to reverence both God and the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**. He who honours the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**has been honoured by**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**; he who does anything without the**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**of the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, does [in reality] serve the**[** _devil_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>). Let all things, then, abound to you through [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) , for you are worthy. You have refreshed me in all things, and [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) [shall refresh] you. You have loved me when absent as well as when present. May God recompense you, for whose sake, while you endure all things, you shall attain unto Him. ([_Epistle to the Smyrnæans_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0109.htm>) ; emphasis mine)

  


In [**_Lecture 18_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/310118.htm>) of his [_Catechetical Lectures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3101.htm>) , St. Cyril of Jerusalem explains why the Body of believers is called Catholic and why the term Church is applied to it:

22\. The Faith which we rehearse contains in order the following, And in one Baptism of repentance for the remission of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ; and in one Holy [ _Catholic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>) [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) ; and in the resurrection of the flesh; and in [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) life. Now of Baptism and repentance I have spoken in the earliest Lectures; and my present remarks concerning the resurrection of the dead have been made with reference to the Article In the resurrection of the flesh. Now then let me finish what still remains to be said for the Article, In one Holy [ _Catholic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>) [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , on which, though one might say many things, we will speak but briefly.

  


23**. It is called**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)**then because it extends over all the world, from one end of the earth to the other;****_and because it teaches universally and completely one and all the doctrines which ought to come to men’s_**[** __knowledge__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**, concerning things both visible and invisible, heavenly and earthly** ; and because it brings into subjection to godliness the whole race of [ _mankind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , governors and governed, learned and unlearned; and because it universally treats and heals the whole class of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , which are committed by [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) or body, and possesses in itself every form of [ _virtue_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) which is named, both in [ _deeds_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01115a.htm>) and words, and in every kind of spiritual gifts.

  


24\. **And it is rightly named (Ecclesia) because it calls forth and assembles together all**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**; according as the Lord says in Leviticus** ,  _And make an assembly for all the congregation at the door of the tabernacle of_[ __witness__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>). And it is to be noted, **that the word**** _assemble_****, is used for the first time in the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**here** , at the time when the Lord puts [ _Aaron_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01003a.htm>) into the High-[_priesthood_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>). And in Deuteronomy also the Lord says to [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) ,  _Assemble the people unto Me, and let them hear My words, that they may learn to_[ __fear__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _Me_. [ _Deuteronomy 4:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu004.htm#verse10>) And he again mentions the name of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , when he says concerning the Tables,  _And on them were written all the words which the Lord spoke with you in the mount out of the midst of the fire in the day of the Assembly_ ; as if he had said more plainly, in the day in which you were called and gathered together by [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). The Psalmist also says,  _I will give thanks unto You, O Lord, in the great Congregation; I will praise You among much people_.

  


25\. Of old the Psalmist sang,  _Bless ye God in the congregations, even the Lord, (ye that are) from the fountains of_[ __Israel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>). **But after the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**for the plots which they made against the Saviour were cast away from His**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)**, the Saviour built out of the**[** _Gentiles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>)**a second Holy Church, the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**of us**[** _Christians_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>)**, concerning which he said to Peter,****_And upon this rock I will build My Church, and the gates of_**[** __hell__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07207a.htm>)** _shall not prevail against it_**. [ _Matthew 16:18_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat016.htm#verse18>) And David prophesying of both these, said plainly of the first which was rejected,  _I have_[ __hated__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) _the Congregation of_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _doers_ ; but of the second which is built up he says in the same Psalm,  _Lord, I have loved the beauty of Your house_ ; and immediately afterwards,  _In the Congregations will I bless you, O Lord_. **For now that the one Church in Judæa is cast off, the Churches of Christ are increased over all the world; and of them it is said in the**[** _Psalms_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12533a.htm>) ,  _Sing unto the Lord a new song, His praise in the Congregation of Saints_. **Agreeably to which the**[** _prophet_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**also said to the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) ,  _I have no pleasure in you, says the Lord Almighty_ [ _Malachi 1:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mal001.htm#verse10>) ; **and immediately afterwards** ,  _For from the rising of the sun even unto the going down of the same, My name is_[ __glorified__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _among the_[ __Gentiles__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>). **Concerning this Holy**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)****[**_Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)****[**_Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**writes to Timothy,****_That you may_**[** __know__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _how you ought to behave yourself in the House of_**[** __God__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, which is the_**[** __Church__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _of the Living_**[** __God__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, the pillar and ground of the_**[** __truth__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _1 Timothy 3:15_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti003.htm#verse15>).

  


26\. But since the word Ecclesia is applied to different things (as also it is written of the multitude in the theatre of the Ephesians,  _And when he had thus spoken, he dismissed the Assembly_ [ _Acts 19:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/act019.htm#verse14>)), and since one might properly and [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) say that there is a  _Church of_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _doers_ , **I mean the meetings of the**[** _heretics_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**, the**[** _Marcionists_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>)**and Manichees, and the rest, for this**[** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>)**the Faith has securely delivered to you now the Article, And in one Holy**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)****[**_Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**; that you may avoid their wretched meetings, and ever abide with the Holy Church**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)**in which you were regenerated. And if ever you are sojourning in cities, inquire not simply where the Lord’s House is** (for the other [ _sects_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13674a.htm>) of the profane also attempt to call their own dens houses of the Lord), **nor merely where the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**is,****_but where is the_**[** __Catholic__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)** __**[**__Church__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _. For this is the peculiar name of this Holy Church_****, the mother of us all, which is the spouse of**[** _our Lord Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, the Only-begotten**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) (for it is written,  _As Christ also loved the_[ __Church__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) _and gave Himself for it_ [ _Ephesians 5:25_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/eph005.htm#verse25>) , and all the rest,) and is a figure and copy of  _Jerusalem which is above, which is free, and the mother of us all_ [ _Galatians 4:26_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal004.htm#verse26>) ; which before was barren, but now has many children.

27\. For when the first Church was cast off, in the second, which is the [ _Catholic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>) [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , God  _has set_ , as [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) says,  _first Apostles, secondly Prophets, thirdly teachers, then_[ __miracles__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10338a.htm>) _, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, various kinds of tongues_ [ _1 Corinthians 12:28_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co012.htm#verse28>) , and every sort of [ _virtue_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) , I mean wisdom and understanding, [ _temperance_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14481a.htm>) and [ _justice_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) , mercy and loving-kindness, and patience unconquerable in [ _persecutions_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>). She,  _by the armour of righteousness on the right hand and on the left, by_[ __honour__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _and dishonour_ [ _2 Corinthians 6:7-8_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2co006.htm#verse7>) , in former days amid [ _persecutions_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) and tribulations crowned the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _martyrs_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) with the varied and blooming chaplets of patience, and now in times of peace by God’s [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) receives her due honours from  _kings and those who are in high place_ [ _1 Timothy 2:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti002.htm#verse2>) , and from every sort and kindred of men. And while the kings of particular nations have bounds set to their authority, **the Holy Church**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)**alone extends her power without limit over the whole world** ;  _for God_ , as it is written,  _has made her border peace_. But I should need many more hours for my discourse, if I wished to speak of all things which concern her.

28\. **In this Holy**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)****[**_Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**receiving instruction and behaving ourselves virtuously, we shall attain the**[** _kingdom of heaven_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>)**, and inherit**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**life** ; for which also we endure all toils, that we may be made partakers thereof from the Lord. For ours is no trifling aim, but our endeavour is for [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) life. Wherefore in the profession of the Faith, after the words, And in the resurrection of the flesh, that is, of the dead (of which we have discoursed), we are taught to [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) also in the life [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) , for which as [ _Christians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212912/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) we are striving.
