# Augustine on 2 Cor. 5:21

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 5, 2025
  * 4 min read


I cite from St. Augustine’s [__The Enchiridion__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1302.htm>) , in order to illustrate how this blessed saint of the Church interpreted the phrase in 2 Cor. 5:21 that Jesus was made sin to mean that Christ came into the flesh so as to become a sacrifice for our sins. All emphasis will be mine.

  


_Chapter 41. Christ, Who Was Himself Free from Sin, Was Made Sin for Us, that We Might Be Reconciled to God._

  


**Begotten and conceived, then, without any indulgence of carnal**[** _lust_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09438a.htm>)**, and therefore bringing with Him no original**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , and by the [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of God joined and united in a wonderful and unspeakable way in one person with the Word, the Only-begotten of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **a son by nature, not by**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)**, and therefore having no**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**of His own; nevertheless, on account of the likeness of**[** _sinful_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**flesh in which He came,****__He was called__**[** __sin__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** __, that He might be__**[** __sacrificed__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>)** __to wash away__**[** __sin__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). For, under the Old Covenant, [ _sacrifices_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) for [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) were called [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). And He, of whom all these [ _sacrifices_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) were types and shadows, was Himself [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) made [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). Hence the apostle, after saying, We [ _pray_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) you in Christ’s stead, be ye reconciled to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , immediately adds: for He has made Him to be [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) for us who [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) no [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ; that we might be made the righteousness of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) in Him. He does not say, as some incorrect copies read, He who [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) no [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) did [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) for us, as if Christ had Himself [ _sinned_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) for our sakes; but he says, Him who [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) no [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , that is, Christ, [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , to whom we are to be reconciled, has made to be [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) for us, that is, has made Him a [ _sacrifice_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) for our [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , by which we might be reconciled to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). He, then, being made [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , just as we are made righteousness (our righteousness being not our own, but God’s, not in ourselves, but in Him); He being made [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , not His own, but ours, not in Himself, but in us, showed, by the likeness of [ _sinful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) flesh in which He was crucified, that though [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) was not in Him, yet that in a certain sense He died to [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , by dying in the flesh which was the likeness of [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ; and that although He Himself had never lived the old life of [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , yet by His [ _resurrection_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) He typified our new life springing up out of the old death in [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>).

  


_Chapter 42. The Sacrament of Baptism Indicates Our Death with Christ to Sin, and Our Resurrection with Him to Newness of Life._

  


And this is the meaning of the great sacrament of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) which is solemnized among us, that all who attain to this [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) should die to [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , **as He is said to have died to**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**, because He died in the flesh, which is the likeness of**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**;****_and rising from the font regenerate_****, as He arose alive from the grave, should begin a new life in the**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , whatever may be the age of the body?

  


_Chapter 43. Baptism and the Grace Which It Typifies are Open to All, Both Infants and Adults._

  


For from the infant newly born to the old man bent with age, **as there is none shut out from**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**, so there is none who in**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**does not die to**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). But infants die **only to original**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ; those who are older die also to all the [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) which their [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) lives have added to the [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) which they brought with them.

  


_Chapter 44. In Speaking of Sin, the Singular Number is Often Put for the Plural, and the Plural for the Singular._

  


But even these latter are frequently said to die to [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , **though undoubtedly they die not to one**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**, but to all the numerous actual**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**they have committed in thought, word, or deed** : for the singular number is often put for the plural, as when the poet says, They fill its belly with the armed soldier, though in the case here referred to there were many soldiers concerned. And we read in our own Scriptures: Pray to the Lord, that He take away the serpent from us. He does not say  _serpents_ though the people were suffering from many; and so in other cases. When, on the other hand, the original [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) is expressed in the plural number, as when we say that infants are [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) for the remission of [ __sins__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , instead of saying for the remission of [ __sin__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , this is the converse figure of speech, **by which the plural number is put in place of the singular** ; as in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) it is said of the death of [ _Herod_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07289c.htm>) ,  _for they are dead which sought the young child’s life_ , instead of saying, he is dead. And in Exodus:  _They have made them_ , [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) says,  _gods of gold_ , though they had made only one calf, of which they said:  _These be your gods, O_[ __Israel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, which brought you up out of the land of_[ __Egypt__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) , — here, too, putting the plural in place of the singular.

  


_Chapter 45. In Adam’s First Sin, Many Kinds of Sin Were Involved._

  


However, even in that one [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , which by one man entered into the world, and so passed upon all [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , **and on account of which infants are**[** _baptized_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , a number of distinct [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) may be observed, if it be analyzed as it were into its separate elements. For there is in it [** _pride_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12405a.htm>) , because man chose to be under his own dominion, rather than under the dominion of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and [** _blasphemy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) , because he did not [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and [** _murder_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07441a.htm>) , for he brought death upon himself; and **spiritual fornication** , for the purity of the [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) was corrupted by the seducing blandishments of the serpent; and **theft** , for man turned to his own use the food he had been forbidden to touch; and [** _avarice_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02148b.htm>) , for he had a craving for more than should have been sufficient for him; and whatever other [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) can be discovered on careful reflection to be involved in this one admitted [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818013819/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>).
