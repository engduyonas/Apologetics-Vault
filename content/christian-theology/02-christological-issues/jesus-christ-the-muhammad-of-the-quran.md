  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)

Many people may not be aware of the fact that the term Muhammad appears only four times in the Quran, with the name Ahmad appearing only once. Muslims have assumed that these all refer to their prophet Muhammad.

  


However, not all are convinced that this is the case since there are those who actually believe that in the case of the word Muhammad, these may be referring to Jesus. They base this on the assumption that the Muslim scripture may have initially been a heretical Christian text that promoted a heterodox view of Christ.**(1)**

  


According to this view, Muhammad wouldn’t be a proper name but a descriptive noun referring to the One who is to be praised most often since the word can be translated as “the Oft-Praised One.”

  


This would comport with the biblical revelation which proclaims that all creation is to praise and worship the Messianic King, whom the inspired NT writings identify as Jesus:

### **COMPARE**

  


Amazingly, the Quran cites the following Markan parable as the Gospel!

  


“And He said, ‘The kingdom of God is as if a man should scatter seed on the ground, and should sleep by night and rise by day, and the seed should sprout and grow, he himself does not know how. For the earth yields crops by itself: first the blade, then the head, after that the full grain in the head. But when the grain ripens, immediately he puts in the sickle, because the harvest has come.’” Mark 4:26-29

  


The following Muslim translators had no problem admitting that the aforementioned Quranic text is actually referring to Mark’s parable:

  


The similitude in the Gospel is about how the good seed is sown and grown gradually, even beyond the expectation of the sower: **_“the seed should spring and grow up, he knoweth not how; for the earth bringeth forth fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn in the earth”;_** **MARK, iv 27-28** … (Yusuf Ali, _The Holy Qur’an – Meaning and Translation_ , p. 1400, n. 4917; emphasis mine)

  


This parable is found in a sermon of the Prophet Jesus that has been reported in the New Testament, thus:

  


“ _And he said, So is the kingdom of God as if a man should east [___sic__ _] seed into the ground: And should sleep, and rise night and day, and the seed should spring and grow up, he knoweth not how. But when the fruit is brought forth, immediately he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come_ [Cf. Mark 4:26-29]. Mark 4:30-32].” The last portion of this sermon is also found in Matthew, 13:31-32. (S. Abul A’la Maududi, _Meaning of the Qur’an_ , Volume V, English rendering by A.A.
