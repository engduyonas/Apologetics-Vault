The Suttavibhanga contains the basic training rules for bhikkhus and bhikkhunis, along with the "origin story" for each one. These rules are summarized in the [Patimokkha](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati-intro), and amount to 227 rules for the bhikkhus, 311 for the bhikkhunis. The Patimokkkha rules are grouped as follows:

  * Parajika: rules entailing expulsion from the Sangha (Defeat) ([4 for bhikkhus](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#pr), [8 for bhikkhunis](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati#pr))
  * Sanghadisesa: rules entailing an initial and subsequent meeting of the Sangha ([13](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#sg), [17](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati#sg))
  * Aniyata (indefinite) rules ([2](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#ay), 0)
  * Nissaggiya pacittiya: rules entailing forfeiture and confession ([30](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#np), [30](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati#np))
  * Pacittiya: rules entailing confession ([92](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#pc), [166](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati#pc))
  * Patidesaniya: rules entailing acknowledgement ([4](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#pd), [8](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati#pd))
  * Sekhiya: rules of training ([75](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#sk), [75](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati#sk))
  * Adhikarana samatha: rules for settling disputes ([7](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati#as), [7](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati#as))


## Summaries and anthologies based on the Suttavibhanga:

  * [_The Bhikkhus' Rules — A Guide for Laypeople: The Theravadin Buddhist Monk's Rules Compiled and Explained,_](/lib/authors/ariyesako/layguide) by Bhikkhu Ariyesako.
  * [_The Bhikkhuni Patimokkha: The Bhikkhunis' Code of Discipline,_](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhuni-pati) Thanissaro Bhikkhu, trans. A concise summary of the bhikkhuni Patimokkha rules.
  * [_The Buddhist Monastic Code, Volume I: The Patimokkha Training Rules Translated and Explained,_](/lib/authors/thanissaro/bmc1) by Thanissaro Bhikkhu. This book provides an in-depth examination of each of the rules.
  * [_Introduction to the Patimokkha Training Rules,_](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati-intro) by Thanissaro Bhikkhu
  * [_The Patimokkha: The Bhikkhus' Code of Discipline,_](/tipitaka/vin/sv/bhikkhu-pati) Thanissaro Bhikkhu, trans. A concise summary of the bhikkhu Patimokkha rules.
