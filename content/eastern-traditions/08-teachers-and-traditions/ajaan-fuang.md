[Awareness Itself](https://dhammatalks.org/ebook_index.html#awarenessitself "Read online"), by Ajaan Fuang Jotiko, compiled and Translated by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (Geoffrey DeGraff) (1999; 48pp./143KB)
    This book contains a delightful and inspiring collection of anecdotal stories retold by an American monk who lived under Ajaan Fuang's tutelage for the last decade of Ajaan Fuang's life. These anecdotes reveal a teaching style that adapted readily to the particular needs of the listener at the moment. Collectively they bear the unmistakable mark of a masterful teacher with a profound grasp of Dhamma, offering valuable lessons for newcomers and experienced practitioners alike. 
[Jhana Not by the Numbers](https://dhammatalks.org/books/PurityOfHeart/Section0013.html), by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (2005; 4pp./12KB)
    The author recalls how [Ajaan Fuang](/lib/thai/#fuang) taught meditation to his students: he would give them just enough instruction to stay on-track, but would rarely "certify" them as having attained this or that level of jhana, thereby motivating them to develop self-reliance and ingenuity in their meditation. In the words of Ajaan Fuang: "If I have to explain everything, you'll get used to having things handed to you on a platter. And then what will you do when problems come up in your meditation and you don't have any experience in figuring things out on your own?" 

Included in the book _[Purity of Heart](https://dhammatalks.org/ebook_index.html#purityofheart)_

[Listen Well: January 1984](/lib/thai/fuang/listenwell), by Ajaan Fuang Jotiko, translated from the Thai by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (2001; 7pp./22KB)
    A beautiful outline of how meditation can be used to help take good care of this rare treasure we've all been given: birth as a human being. 
[A Single Mind: February 5, 1980](/lib/thai/fuang/single), by Ajaan Fuang Jotiko, translated from the Thai by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (1999; 5pp./14KB)
    Encouragement and ammunition for those who choose a life of celibacy. 
[Timeless and True: July, 1978](/lib/thai/fuang/timeless), by Ajaan Fuang Jotiko, translated from the Thai by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (1998; 5pp./15KB)
    Encouragement and advice for people getting started in concentration practice.
