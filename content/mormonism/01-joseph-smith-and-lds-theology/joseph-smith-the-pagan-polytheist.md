I quote the last sermon of Joseph Smith just eleven days before he was murdered. It shows that he was no monotheist, but a pagan idolater and false prophet who butchered the Scriptures to his shame and destruction (Cf. 2 Cor. 11:1-4, 13-15; Gal. 1:6-9; 2 Peter 3:15-16).
