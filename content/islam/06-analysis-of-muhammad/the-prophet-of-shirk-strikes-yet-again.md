** _Never_****did We send a Messenger or a Prophet before you** , but when he did recite the revelation or narrated or spoke, **_Shaitan_****(Satan) threw (some falsehood)** in it. **But Allah abolishes that which**** _Shaitan_****(Satan) throws in**. Then Allah establishes His Revelations. And Allah is All-Knower, All-Wise: That He (Allah) may make what is thrown in by _Shaitan_ (Satan) a trial for those in whose hearts is a disease (of hypocrisy and disbelief) and whose hearts are hardened. And certainly, the _Zalimun_ (polytheists and wrong-doers, etc.) are in an opposition far-off (from the truth against Allah's Messenger and the believers). S. 22:52-53 Hilali-Khan

Suffice it to say, this is a huge embarrassment to the credibility of Muhammad since no true prophet and apostle of God was ever duped into confusing Satan and his messages with God and his revelations.

After all, if Muhammad couldn’t differentiate the words of the devil from the words of his lord, then a Muslim has no way of knowing with absolute certainty that their prophet wasn’t actually deceived into thinking that the rest of the message came from Allah through Gabriel, when in reality this was simply another layer to the satanic deception.

In other words, what guarantee do Muslims have that Satan didn’t appear as Gabriel to Muhammad from the get-go, and later on inspired him to recite verses praising the pagan goddesses only to then appear as Gabriel once again to rebuke him, in order to keep Muhammad off his scent. What better way to deceive Muhammad from realizing that it was Satan all along who had been inspiring him to deceive people into following him to their eternal shame and destruction?

The fact is that this episode of the “Satanic Verses” merely proves Muhammad was a fraud who was being used of the devil to mislead mankind into thinking he was a messenger sent from God.

As if this wasn’t bad enough, these texts which were allegedly “sent down” in light of Satan inspiring his servant Muhammad introduces another huge blunder.
