# The Greek Quran Confirms That Allah Revealed the Holy Bible

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 16, 2025
  * 17 min read


A typical Muslim objection is that the Quran nowhere confirms the Bible since the term Bible is never mentioned within the Islamic scripture. Nothing could be further from the truth since the Quran repeatedly makes reference to the Book (Arabic – _kitab_) given to the Jews and Christians. What Muslims and Christians may be unaware of is that the term Bible is the transliteration of the Latin term _Biblia_ , meaning Book!

  


Latin _Biblia_ is derived from the Greek _Biblia_ , meaning books, being the plural of _Biblos_ , which is simply Greek for B/book. Hence, any reference to the _Book_ of the Jews and Christians is in fact a reference to the Bible since that is all the term Bible means, namely, the Book!

  


In this post I will establish this point by appealing to the word that the Greek version of the Quran employs to translate the Arabic term for the Book given to the Jews and Christians. The readers will see that that term is _Biblos_ , from which we get the word Bible!

  


The readers can verify this for themselves by accessing the following online Greek version of the Quran: [**_Holy Quran with Greek Translation – Al Islam_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250920181601/https://www.alislam.org/quran/view/guide/?region=GK>).
