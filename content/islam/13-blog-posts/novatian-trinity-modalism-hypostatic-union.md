# Novatian, Trinity, Modalism, Hypostatic Union

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * 2 days ago
  * 25 min read


The quotations which I will cite here are taken from Novatian's [_Treatise Concerning the Trinity_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0511.htm>). All emphasis will be mine.

Novatian sets forth biblical texts to refute the modalist lie that Jesus is the Father. He instead proves that the Father, Son and Spirit are personally distinct and yet one and the same Divinity. Novatian also demonstrates that the Son is fully God and fully human, being one eternal divine Person who became flesh from the Holy Virgin.

What makes Novatian’s apologetic so remarkable is that he cites a plethora of Old Testament texts, not just New Testaments, to prove both the Divinity of our risen Lord and the Triunity of God. He will quote verses such as Psalm 110:1, Hosea 1:6-7, Romans 9:5, etc., to set forth the doctrine of the Trinity, Hypostatic Union and so on.

With the foregoing in view, I now turn to Novatian’s response.

Chapter 12

****

**That Christ is God, is Proved by the Authority of the Old Testament Scriptures.**

Why, then, should we hesitate to say what Scripture does not shrink from declaring? Why shall the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) hesitate in that wherein the authority of Scripture has never hesitated? _For, behold, Hosea the_[ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) _says in the person of the Father:___I will not now save them by bow, nor by horses, nor by horsemen; but I will save them by the Lord their God__ _._ [ _Hosea 1:7_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/hos001.htm#verse7>)  _If God says that He saves by_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, still God does not save except by Christ. Why, then, should man hesitate to call Christ_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, when he observes that He is declared to be God by the Father according to the_[ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _? Yea, if_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _the Father does not save except by_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, no one can be saved by_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _the Father unless he shall have confessed Christ to be_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, in whom and by whom the Father promises that He will give him_[ _salvation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) : so that, reasonably, whoever acknowledges Him to be [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , may find [ _salvation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) in Christ [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; whoever does not acknowledge Him to be [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , would lose [ _salvation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) which he could not find elsewhere than in Christ God. For in the same way as Isaiah says,  _Behold, a_[ __virgin__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) _shall conceive and bear a son, and you shall call His name_[ __Emmanuel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>) _, which is, interpreted, God with us_ ; [ _Isaiah 7:14_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa007.htm#verse14>) so Christ Himself says,  _Lo, I am with you, even to the consummation of the world_. [ _Matthew 28:20_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat028.htm#verse20>)  _Therefore He is God with us; yea, and much rather, He is in us. Christ is with us, therefore it is He whose name is God with us, because He also is with us_ ; or is He not with us? How then does He say that He is with us? He, then, is with us. _But because He is with us He was called_[ _Emmanuel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>) _, that is, God with us._[_God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, therefore, because He is with us, was called God with us_ , The same [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) says:  _Be strengthened, you relaxed hands, and you feeble knees; be consoled, you that are cowardly in heart; be strong;_[__fear__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _not. Lo, our God shall return judgment; He Himself shall come, and shall save you: then shall the eyes of the blind be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall hear; then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb shall be eloquent_. Since the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) says that at God's advent these should be the signs which come to pass; let men acknowledge either that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) is the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , at whose advent and by whom these wonders of healings were performed; or, overcome by the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of [ _Christ's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) divinity, let them rush into the other [ _heresy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , and refusing to confess Christ to be the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , let them declare Him to be the Father.

 _For, being bound by the words of the_[ _prophets_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) _, they can no longer deny Christ to be God. What, then, do they reply when those signs are said to be about to take place on the advent of_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, which were manifested on the advent of Christ? In what way do they receive Christ as God? For now they cannot deny Him to be God_. As [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or as God the Son? If as the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , why do they deny that the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) is God? If as the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , why do they not follow those who appear to maintain [ _blasphemies_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) of that kind? Unless because in this contest against them concerning the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , this is in the meantime sufficient for us, that, being convinced in any kind of way, they should confess Christ to be [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , seeing they have even wished to deny that He is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). He says by Habakkuk the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) : [ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) shall come from the south, and the Holy One from the dark and dense mountain. Whom do they wish to represent as coming from the south? If they say that it is the [ _Almighty God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , then [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father comes from a place, from which place, moreover, He is thus excluded, and He is bounded within the straitnesses of some abode; _and thus by such as these, as we have said, the_[ _sacrilegious_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13321a.htm>) __[_heresy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) _of Sabellius is embodied_. Since Christ is [ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to be not the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , but the Father; since by them He is asserted to be in strictness a bare man, in a new manner, by those, again, Christ is [ _proved_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) to be [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father Almighty. But if in [ _Bethlehem_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02533a.htm>) , the region of which local division looks towards the southern portion of heaven, Christ is born, _who by the_[ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _is also said to be_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, this God is rightly described as coming from the south, because He was foreseen as about to come from_[ _Bethlehem_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02533a.htm>). Let them, then, choose of the two alternatives, the one that they prefer, that He who came from the south is the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , or the Father; for God is said to be about to come from the south. _If the_[ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, why do they shrink from calling Him Christ and God? For the_[ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _says that God shall come_. If the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , why do they shrink from being associated with the boldness of Sabellius, who says that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) is the Father? Unless because, whether they call Him Father or Son, from his [ _heresy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , however unwillingly, they must needs withdraw if they are accustomed to say that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) is merely man; when compelled by the facts themselves, they are on the eve of exalting Him as [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , whether in wishing to call Him Father or in wishing to call Him Son.

Chapter 13

**That the Same Truth is Proved from the Sacred Writings of the New Covenant.**

And thus also John, describing the nativity of [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , says:  _The_[ __Word__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _was_[ __made flesh__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07706b.htm>) _, and dwelt among us, and we saw His_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _, the_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _as of the only begotten of the_[ __Father__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, full Of_[ __grace__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) _and_[ __truth__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _… For, moreover, His name is called the_[ __Word of God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _,_ [ _Revelation 19:13_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rev019.htm#verse13>) and not without reason. _My heart has emitted a good word_ ; which word He subsequently calls by the name of the King inferentially,  _I will tell my works to the King_.  _For by Him were made all the works, and without Him was nothing made_. [ _John 1:3_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse3>)  _Whether_ says the apostle  _they be thrones or dominations, or powers, or mights, visible things and invisible, all things subsist by Him_. [ _Colossians 1:16_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/col001.htm#verse16>) Moreover, this is that word _which came unto His own, and His own received Him not. For the world was made by Him, and the world_[ __knew__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him not_. [ _John 1:10-11_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse10>) Moreover, this Word  _was in the beginning with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and God was the Word_. [ _John 1:1_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse1>) Who then can [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) , when in the last clause it is said,  _The_[ __Word__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _was_[ __made flesh__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07706b.htm>) _, and dwelt among us_ , that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , whose is the nativity, and because He was made flesh, is man; and because He is the [ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) , _who can shrink from declaring without hesitation that He is_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, especially when he considers the evangelical Scripture, that it has associated both of these substantial natures into one concord of the nativity of Christ_? For He it is who  _as a bride-groom goes forth from his bride-chamber; He exulted as a giant to run his way. His going forth is from the end of the heaven, and His return unto the ends of it._ Because, even to the highest,  __not any one has ascended into heaven save He who came down from heaven, the__[ __Son of man__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) __who is in heaven__. [ _John 3:13_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse13>) Repeating this same thing, He says:  _Father, glorify me with that_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _wherewith I was with You before the world was_.  _And if this Word came down from heaven as a bridegroom to the flesh, that by the assumption of flesh He might ascend there as the_[ _Son of man_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) _, whence the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _had descended as the Word, reasonably, while by the mutual connection both flesh wears the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _, and the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _assumes the frailty of the flesh; when the flesh being espoused ascending there, whence without the flesh it had descended, it at length receives that_[ _glory_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _which in being shown to have had before the foundation of the world, it is most manifestly_[ _proved_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) _. to be God_. And, nevertheless, while the world itself is said to have been founded after Him, _it is found to have been created by Him; by that very divinity in Him whereby, the world was made, both His_[ _glory_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _and His authority are_[ _proved_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) _. Moreover, if, whereas it is the property of none but God to_[ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _the secrets of the heart, Christ beholds the secrets of the heart; and if, whereas it belongs to none but God to remit_[ _sins_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _, the same Christ remits_[ _sins_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _; and if, whereas it is the portion of no man to come from heaven, He descended by coming from heaven; and if, whereas this word can be_[ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _of no man,___I and the Father are one__ _,_[_John 10:30_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh010.htm#verse30>) _Christ alone declared this word out of the consciousness of His divinity; and if, finally, the Apostle Thomas, instructed in all the_[ _proofs_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) _and conditions of_[ _Christ's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _divinity, says in reply to_[ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _,___My Lord and my__[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _;_[_John 20:28_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh020.htm#verse28>) _and if, besides, the_[ _Apostle Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) _says,___Whose are the fathers, and of whom Christ came according to the flesh, who is over all, God blessed for evermore__ _,_[_Romans 9:5_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom009.htm#verse5>) _writing in his epistles; and if the same apostle declares that_ __he was ordained an apostle__ ____not by__[ __men__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) __, nor of__[ __man__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) __, but by__[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _; and if the same contends that he learned the_[ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) ____not from men or by man, but received it from__[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, reasonably Christ is_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

Therefore, in this respect, one of two things must needs be established. _For since it is evident that all things were made by Christ, He is either before all things, since all things were by Him, and so He is_[ _justly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) __[_God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; or because He is man He is subsequent to all things, and [ _justly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) nothing was made by Him. _But we cannot say that nothing was made by Him, when we observe it written that all things were made by Him. He is not therefore subsequent to all things; that is, He is not man only, who is subsequent to all things, but God also, since God is prior to all_ things. For He is before all things, because all things are by Him, while if He were only man, nothing would be by Him; or if all things were by Him, He would not be man only, because if He were only man, all things would not be by Him; nay, nothing would be by Him. What, then, do they reply? That nothing is by Him, so that He is man only? _How then are all things by Him? Therefore He is not man only, but God also, since all things are by Him_ ; so that we reasonably ought to understand that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) is not man only, who is subsequent to all things, _but God also, since by Him all things were made_. For how can you say that He is man only, when you see Him also in the flesh, unless because when both aspects are considered, both truths are rightly [ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)?…

Chapter 17.

**It Is, Moreover, Proved by Moses in the Beginning of the Holy Scriptures.**

What if [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) pursues this same rule of [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , and delivers to us in the beginning of his sacred writings, this principle by which we may learn _that all things were created and founded by the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, that is, by the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)? For He says the same that John and the rest say; nay, both John and the others are perceived to have received from Him what they say. For if John says,  _All things were made by Him, and without Him was nothing made_ , the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)  _David_ too says,  _I tell my works to the King_. [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , moreover, introduces God commanding that there should be light at the first, that the heaven should be established, that the waters should be gathered into one place, that the dry land should appear, that the fruit should be brought forth according to its seed, that the animals should be produced, that lights should be established in heaven, and stars. He shows that none other was then present to God — by whom these works were commanded that they should be made — _than He_ __by whom all things were made, and without whom nothing was made__ _. And if He is the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _—_ __for my heart has uttered forth a good Word__ _— He shows that_ __in the beginning the Word was__ _, and that this Word was with the_[ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and besides that the Word was_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and that all things were made by Him. Moreover, this_[ __Word__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _was_[ _made flesh_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07706b.htm>) _and dwelt among us, — to wit, Christ the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) ; whom both on receiving subsequently as man according to the flesh, _and seeing before the foundation of the world to be the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _, and_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, we reasonably, according to the instruction of the Old and_[ _New Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>) _,_[_believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _and hold to be as well God as man, Christ Jesus_. What if the same [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) introduces God saying,  _Let us make man after our image and likeness_ ; and below,  _And God made man; in the image of God made He him, male and female made He them_? If, as we have already shown, _it is the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _by whom all things were made, certainly it was the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _by whom also man was ordained, on whose account all things were made_. Moreover, when God commands that man should be made, He is said to be God who makes man; _but the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _makes man, that is to say, the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _,___by whom all things were made, and without whom nothing was made. And this__[ __Word__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) __was__[ __made flesh__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07706b.htm>) __, and dwelt among us__ _: therefore Christ is_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _; therefore man was made by Christ as by the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). But God made man in the image of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; He is therefore God who made man in the image of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; _therefore Christ is God: so that with reason neither does the testimony of the_[ _Old Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14526a.htm>) _waver concerning the person of_[ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, being supported by the manifestation of the_[ _New Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>) ; nor is the power of the [ _New Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>) detracted from, while its [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) is resting on the roots of the same [ _Old Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14526a.htm>). Whence they who presume Christ the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) and man to be only man, and not God also, _do so in opposition to both Old and New Testaments, in that they corrupt the authority and the_[ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _both of the Old and New Testaments_. What if the same [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) everywhere introduces [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father [ _infinite_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08004a.htm>) and without end, not as being enclosed in any place, but as one who includes every place; _nor as one who is in a place, but rather one in whom every place is, containing all things and embracing all things, so that with reason He can neither descend nor ascend, because He Himself both contains and fills all things, and yet nevertheless introduces God descending to consider the tower which the sons of men were building, asking and saying_ ,  _Come_ ; and then,  _Let us go down and there confound their tongues, that each one may not understand the words of his neighbour_.  _Whom do they pretend here to have been the God who descended to that tower, and asking to visit those men at that time_? [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father? Then thus He is enclosed in a place; and how does He embrace all things? __Or does He say that it is an__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __descending with__[ __angels__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __, and saying__ ,  _Come_ ; and subsequently,  _Let us go down and there confound their tongues_?  _And yet in Deuteronomy we observe that God told these things, and that God said, where it is written_ ,  _When He scattered abroad the children of Adam, He determined the bounds of the nations according to the number of the_[ __angels__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Neither, therefore, did the Father descend, as the subject itself indicates; __nor did an__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __command these things, as the fact shows__. _Then it remains that He must have descended, of whom the_[ _Apostle Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) _says,___He who descended is the same who ascended above all the heavens, that He might fill all things__ _, that is, the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _. But the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _was made flesh, and dwelt among us. This must be Christ. Therefore Christ must be declared to be God_.

Chapter 18.

****

**Moreover Also, from the Fact that He Who Was Seen of Abraham is Called**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**; Which Cannot Be Understood of the Father, Whom No Man Hath Seen at Any Time; But of the Son in the Likeness of an Angel.**

Behold, the same [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) tells us in another place that [ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) was seen of [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>). And yet the same [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) hears from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , that  _no man can see God and live._ If God cannot be seen, how was God seen? Or if He was seen, how is it that He cannot be seen? For John also says,  _No man has seen God at any time_ ; and the [ _Apostle Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ,  _Whom no man has seen, nor can see_.  _But certainly the_[ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _does not lie; therefore,_[_truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _, God was seen. Whence it may be understood that it was not the Father who was seen, seeing that He never was seen;___but the__[ __Son__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) __, who has both been accustomed to descend, and to be seen because He has descended__. For He is the image of the invisible [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , as the imperfection and frailty of the [ _human_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) condition was accustomed sometimes even then to see [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father in the image of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , that is, in the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). For gradually and by progression [ _human_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) frailty was to be strengthened by the image to that [ _glory_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) of being able one day to see [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father. For the things that are great are dangerous if they are sudden. For even the sudden light of the sun after darkness, with its too great splendour, will not make manifest the light of day to unaccustomed eyes, but will rather strike them with blindness.

And lest this should occur to the injury of [ _human_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) eyes, the darkness is broken up and scattered by degrees; and the rising of that luminary, mounting by small and unperceived increments, gently accustoms men's eyes to bear its full orb by the  _gentle_ increase of its rays. Thus, therefore, Christ also — that is, the image of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) — is looked upon by [ _men_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , inasmuch as He could be seen. And thus the weakness and imperfection of the [ _human_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) destiny is nourished, led up, and [ _educated_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05295b.htm>) by Him; so that, being accustomed to look upon the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , it may one day be able to see [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father Himself also as He is, that it may not be stricken by His sudden and intolerable brightness, and be hindered from being able to see [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , whom it has always desired. _Wherefore it is the Son who is seen; but the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _is the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _: and the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _was made flesh, and dwelt among us; and this is_[ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _. What in the world is the reason that we should hesitate to call Him_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, who in so many ways is acknowledged to be_[ _proved_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) _God_? And if, moreover, the [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) meets with Hagar, Sarah's maid, driven from her home as well as turned away, near the fountain of water in the way to Shur; asks and learns the reason of her flight, and after that offers her advice that she should [ _humble_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07543b.htm>) herself; and, moreover, gives her the hope of the name of mother, and pledges and promises that from her womb there should be a numerous seed, and that she should have Ishmael to be born from her; and with other things unfolds the place of his habitation, and describes his mode of life; __yet Scripture sets forth this__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __as both Lord and God — for He would not have promised the blessing of seed unless the__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __had also been God__. Let them ask what the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) can make of this present passage. Was that the Father that was seen by Hagar or not? _For He is declared to be God. But far be it from us to call_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _the Father an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, lest He should be subordinate to another whose_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _He would be.___But they will say that it was an__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __. How then shall He be God if He was an__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _?_ Since this name is nowhere conceded to [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , except that on either side the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) compels us into this opinion, __that we ought to understand it to have been God the__[ __Son__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) __, who, because He is of__[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) __, is rightly called__[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) __, because He is the__[ __Son of God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) __. But, because He is subjected to the__[ __Father__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) __, and the Announcer of the Father's will, He is declared to be the Angel of Great Counsel. Therefore, although this passage neither is suited to the person of the__[ __Father__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) __, lest He should be called an__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __, nor to the person of an__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __, lest he should be called__[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) __; yet it is suited to the person of Christ that He should be both God because He is the__[ __Son of God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) __, and should be an__[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) __because He is the Announcer of the Father's mind__. And the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) ought to understand that they are setting themselves against the [ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , in that, while they say that they [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) Christ to have been also an [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , they are unwilling to declare Him to have been also [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , when they read in the [ _Old Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14526a.htm>) that He often came to visit the [ _human race_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>).

 _To this, moreover,_[_Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) _added the instance of God seen of_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _at the oak of Mature, when he was sitting at the opening of his tent at noon-day_. And nevertheless, although he had beheld three men,  _note_ that he called one of them Lord; and when he had washed their feet, he offers them bread baked on the ashes, with butter and abundance of milk itself, and urges them that, being detained as guests, they should eat. And after I this he hears also that he should be a father, and learns that Sarah his wife should bring forth a son by him; and acknowledges concerning the destruction of the people of [ _Sodom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) , what they deserve to suffer; and learns that God had come down on account of the cry of [ _Sodom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>). in which place, if they will have it that the Father was seen at that time to have been received with hospitality in company with two [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) have [ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) the Father to be visible. _But if an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, although of the three_[ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _one is called Lord, why, although it is not usual, is an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _called God_? Unless because, in order that His proper invisibility may be restored to the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the proper inferiority be remitted to the [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , _it was only God the_[ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, who also is_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, who was seen by_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _, and was_[ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _to have been received with hospitality_. For He anticipated sacramentally what He was hereafter to become. He was made a of [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , being about to be among the sons of [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>). And his children's feet, by way of proving what He was, He washed; returning in the children the claim of hospitality which formerly the Father had put out to interest to Him. Whence also, that there might be no [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) but _that it was He who was the of_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _on the destruction of the people of_[ _Sodom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _, it is declared:___Then the Lord rained upon__[ __Sodom__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) __and upon Gomorrha fire and brimstone from the Lord out of heaven__ _. For thus also said the_[ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) _in the person of God:___I have overthrown you, as the Lord overturned__[ __Sodom and Gomorrha__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _. Therefore the Lord overturned_[ _Sodom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _, that is, God overturned_[ _Sodom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _; but in the overturning of_[ _Sodom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _, the Lord rained fire from the Lord. And this Lord was the God seen by_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _; and this God was the of_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _, certainly seen because He was also touched_.

But although the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , being invisible, was assuredly not at that time seen, _He who was accustomed to be touched and seen was seen and received to hospitality. But this the_[ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _,___The Lord rained from the Lord upon__[ __Sodom and Gomorrha__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) __brimstone and fire__ _. And this is the_[ _Word of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _.___And the__[ __Word of God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) __was made flesh, and dwelt among us__ _; and this is_[ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _. It was not the_[ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, then, who was a with_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _, but Christ. Nor was it the Father who was seen then, but the Son; and Christ was seen. Rightly, therefore, Christ is both Lord and_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, who was not otherwise seen by_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _, except that as_[ _God the Word_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) _He was begotten of_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _the Father before_[ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _himself_. Moreover, says the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , _the same Angel and God visits and consoles the same Hagar_ when driven with her son from the dwelling of [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>). For when in the [ _desert_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04749a.htm>) she had exposed the infant, because the water had fallen short from the pitcher; and when the lad had cried out, and she had lifted up her weeping and lamentation, [ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _heard_ , says the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) ,  _the voice of the lad from the place where he was_. Having told that it was God who heard the voice of the infant, it adds:  _And the_[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _of the Lord called Hagar herself out of heaven_ ,  _saying that that was an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _whom it had called_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and pronouncing Him to be Lord whom it had set forth as an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _; which Angel and God moreover promises to Hagar herself greater consolations, in saying_ ,  _Fear not; for I have heard the voice of the lad from the place where he was. Arise, take up the lad, and hold him; for I will make of him a great nation_. Why does this [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , if [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) only, claim to himself this right of saying, _I will make of him a great nation_ , _since assuredly this kind of power belongs to_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and cannot belong to an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _?_ Whence also He is confirmed to be [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , since He is able to do this; because, by way of proving this very point, it is immediately added by the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) :  _And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of running water; and she went and filled the bottle from the well, and gave to the lad: and God was with the lad_. If, then, this God was with the Lord, who opened the eyes of Hagar that she might see the well of running water, and might draw the water on account of the urgent need of  _the lad's_ thirst, _and this God who calls her from heaven is called an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _when, in previously hearing the voice of the lad crying, He was rather_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _; is not understood to be other than_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, in like manner as He was God also. And since this cannot be applicable or fitting to the_[ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, who is God only, but may be applicable to_[ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, who is declared to be not only_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, but_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _also, it manifestly appears that it was not the Father who thus spoke to Hagar, but rather Christ, since He is_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _; and to Him also is applied the name of_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, since He became the_[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _of great counsel. And He is the_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, in that He declares the bosom of the_[ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, as John sets forth_. For if John himself says, that He Himself who sets forth the bosom of the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , as the Word, became flesh in order to declare the bosom of the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , _assuredly Christ is not only man, but_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _also; and not only_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, but He is shown by the_[ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _to be God also. And this is_[ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _to be the case by us_ ; so that, if we will not consent to apprehend that it was Christ who then spoke to Hagar, we must either make an [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or we must reckon [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father Almighty among the [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>).

Chapter 19.

**That God Also Appeared to Jacob as an Angel; Namely, the Son of God.**

 _What if in another place also we read in like manner that God was described as an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _?_ For when, to his wives Leah and Rachel, Jacob complained of the [ _injustice_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08010c.htm>) of their father, and when he told them that he desired now to go and return into his own land, he moreover inter posed the authority of his dream; and at this time he says that the [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of God had said to him in a [ _dream_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05154a.htm>) ,  _Jacob, Jacob. And I said, says he, What is it? Lift up your eyes, said He, and see, the he-goats and the rams leaping upon the sheep, and the she-goats are black and white, and many-colored, and grizzled, and speckled: for I have seen all that Laban has done to you. I am_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, who appeared to you in the place of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, where you anointed for me there the standing stone, and there vowed a vow unto me: now therefore arise, and go forth from this land, and go unto the land of your nativity, and I will be with you_.  _If the Angel of God speaks thus to Jacob, and the Angel himself mentions and says,___I am__[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) __, who appeared unto you in the house of__[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, we see without any hesitation that this is declared to be not only an_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, but God also; because He speaks of the vow directed to Himself by Jacob in the place of_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and He does not say, in_ __my place__ _. It is then the place of_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and He also is_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Moreover, it is written simply _in the place of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for it is not said in the place of the [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) and [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , but only of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; _and He who promises those things is manifested to be both God and Angel_ , so that reasonably there must be a distinction between Him who is called God only, and Him who is declared to be not God simply, but Angel also. Whence if so great an authority cannot here be regarded as belonging to any other [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , that He should also avow Himself to be [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and should bear [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) that a vow was made to Him, _except to Christ alone, to whom not as_[ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _only, but as to_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, a vow can be vowed; it is manifest that it is not to be received as the_[ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, but as the_[ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, God and Angel_. Moreover, if this is Christ, as it is, he is in terrible risk who says that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) is either man or [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) alone, withholding from Him the power of the divine name — _an authority which He has constantly received on the_[ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) _of the heavenly Scriptures, which continually say that He is both Angel and God_. To all these things, moreover, is added this, that in like manner _as the_[ _divine Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _has frequently declared Him both Angel and_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, so the same_[ _divine Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _declares Him also both man and_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , expressing thereby what He should be, and depicting even then in figure what He was to be in the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of His substance.  _For_ , it says,  _Jacob remained alone; and there wrestled with him a man even till daybreak. And He saw that He did not prevail against him; and He touched the broad part of Jacob's thigh while He was wrestling with him and he with Him, and said to him, Let me go, for the morning has dawned. And he said, I will not let You go, unless You bless me. And He said, What is your name? And he said, Jacob. And He said to him, Your name shall no longer be called Jacob, but_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _shall be your name; because you have prevailed with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and you are powerful with men. And it adds, moreover: And Jacob called the name of that place the Vision of God: for I have seen the Lord face to face, and my_[ __soul__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) _has been made safe. And the sun arose upon him. Afterwards he crossed over the Vision of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, but he halted upon his thigh_.  _A man_ , it says, _wrestled with Jacob_. If this was a mere man, who is he? Whence is he? Wherefore does he contend and wrestle with Jacob? What had intervened? What had happened? What was the [ _cause_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) of so great a dispute as that, and so great a struggle? Why, moreover, is Jacob, who is found to be strong enough to hold the man with whom he is wrestling, and asks for a blessing from Him whom he is holding, asserted to have asked therefore, except because this struggle was prefigured as that which should be between Christ and the sons of Jacob, which is said to be completed in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)? For against this man Jacob's people struggled, in which struggle Jacob's people was found to be the more powerful, because against Christ it gained the victory of its iniquity: at which time, on account of the crime that it committed, hesitating and giving way, it began most sorely to halt in the walk of its own [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) and [ _salvation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) ; and although it was found the stronger, in respect of the condemnation of [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , it still needs His mercy, still needs His blessing.

But, moreover, the man who wrestled with Jacob says,  _Moreover, your name shall no longer be called Jacob, but_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _shall be your name_ ; and if [ _Israel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) is the man who sees [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the Lord was beautifully showing that it was not only a man who was then wrestling with Jacob, _but God also. Certainly Jacob saw_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, with whom he wrestled_ , although he was holding the man in his own struggle. And in order that there might still be no hesitation, He Himself laid down the interpretation by saying,  _Because you have prevailed with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and art powerful with men_. For which reason the same Jacob, perceiving already the force of the Mystery, and apprehending the authority of Him with whom he had wrestled, called the name of that place in which he had wrestled, _the Vision of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). He, moreover, superadded the reason for his interpretation being offered of the Vision of God:  _For I have seen, said he,_[__God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _face to face, and my_[ __soul__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) _has been saved_.  _Moreover, he saw_[ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, with whom he wrestled as with a man_ ; but still indeed he held the man as a conqueror, though as an inferior he asked a blessing as from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Thus he wrestled with God and with man; and thus [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) was that struggle prefigured, and in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) was fulfilled, between Christ and the people of Jacob, wherein, although the people had the mastery, yet it [ _proved_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) to be inferior by being shown to be guilty. Who will hesitate to acknowledge that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , in whom this type of a wrestling was fulfilled, was not man only, but God also, since even that very type of a wrestling seems to have [ _proved_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) Him [ _man_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) and [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)? And yet, even after this, the same [ _divine Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) [ _justly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) does not cease to call the Angel [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and to pronounce God the Angel. For when this very Jacob was about to bless Manasseh and Ephraim, the sons of Joseph, with his hands placed across on the heads of the lads, he said,  _The God which fed me from my youth even unto this day, the Angel who delivered me from all_[ __evils__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _, bless these lads._ Even to such a point does he affirm the same Being to be an Angel, whom he had called [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , as in the end of his discourse, to express the person of whom he was speaking as one, when he said  _bless these lads._ For if he had meant the one to be understood as [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the other as an [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , he would have comprised the two [ _persons_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) in the plural number; but now he defined the singular number of one person in the blessing, whence he meant it to be understood that the same person is God and Angel. But yet He cannot be received as [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father; but as God and Angel, as Christ He can be received. And Him, as the author of this blessing, Jacob also signified by placing his hands crossed upon the lads, as if their father was Christ, and showing, from thus placing his hands, the figure and future form of the passion. Let no one, therefore, who does not shrink from speaking of Christ as an Angel, thus shrink from pronouncing Him God also, when he perceives that He Himself was invoked in the blessing of these lads, by the sacrament of the passion, intimated in the type of the  _crossed_ hands, as both God and Angel.
