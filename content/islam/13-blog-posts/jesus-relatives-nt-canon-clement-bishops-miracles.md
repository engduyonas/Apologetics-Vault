# Jesus’ Relatives, NT Canon, Clement, Bishops & Miracles

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Mar 10
  * 31 min read


In this post I will quote from St. Irenaeus and Eusebius as they discuss Apostolic succession, Jesus’ relatives and their Davidic lineage, the Bishop of Rome, one of whom was Clement, Clement’s letter, the canon of Scripture, and the miracles that were still be observed. Irenaeus mentions how even in his lifetime, believers were raising the dead and speaking in tongues. Eusebius even records a very moving anecdote of the Apostle John’s love and care for lost and wayward believers. All emphasis will be mine.

**Irenaeus**

[** _Chapter 3_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103303.htm>) A refutation of the heretics, from the fact that, in the various churches, a perpetual succession of bishops was kept up

1\. **It is within the power of all, therefore, in every**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, who may wish to see the**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**, to contemplate clearly the tradition of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**manifested throughout the whole world; and we are in a position to reckon up those who were by the**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**instituted**[** _bishops_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**in the**[** _Churches_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, and [to demonstrate] the succession of these men to our own times** ; those who neither taught nor [ _knew_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of anything like what these [[_heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)] rave about. For if the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) had [ _known_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) hidden [ _mysteries_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) , which they were in the [ _habit_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07099b.htm>) of imparting to  _the perfect_ apart and privily from the rest, they would have delivered them especially to those to whom they were also committing the Churches themselves. For they were desirous that these men should be very perfect and blameless in all things, whom also they were leaving behind as their successors, delivering up their own place of government to these men; which men, if they discharged their functions honestly, would be a great boon [to the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)], but if they should fall away, the direst calamity.

2\. Since, however, it would be very tedious, in such a volume as this, to reckon up the successions of all the [ _Churches_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , we do put to confusion all those who, in whatever manner, whether by an [ _evil_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) self-pleasing, by vainglory, or by blindness and perverse opinion, assemble in unauthorized meetings; [we do this, I say,] **by indicating that tradition derived from the**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**,****_of the very great, the very ancient, and universally_**[** _known_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** __**[**_Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _founded and organized at_**[** _Rome_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)** _by the two most_**[** _glorious_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)** __**[**_apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, Peter and_**[** _Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ; as also [by pointing out] the [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) preached to [ _men_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , **which comes down to our time by means of the successions of the**[** _bishops_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**.****_For it is a matter of necessity that every_**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _should agree with this_**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _, on account of its preeminent authority [potiorem principalitatem]_**.

3\. Of this Linus, [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) makes mention in the Epistles to Timothy. To this Clement there succeeded Evaristus. Alexander followed Evaristus; then, sixth from the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , Sixtus was appointed; after him, Telephorus, who was [ _gloriously_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) [ _martyred_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) ; then [ _Hyginus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07593a.htm>) ; after him, [ _Pius_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12126b.htm>) ; then after him, [ _Anicetus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>). In this order, and by this succession, the [ _ecclesiastical tradition_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15006b.htm>) from the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , and the preaching of the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , have come down to us. And this is most abundant [ _proof_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) that there is one and the same vivifying [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , which has been preserved in the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) from the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) until now, and handed down in [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>).

Chapter 15. Clement, the Third Bishop of Rome.

And of the fact that a sedition did take place in the [ _church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) of Corinth at the time referred to [ _Hegesippus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07194a.htm>) is a trustworthy witness.

Chapter 17. The Persecution under Domitian.

[ _Domitian_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) , having shown great cruelty toward many, and having [ _unjustly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08010c.htm>) [ _put to death_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12565a.htm>) no small number of well-born and notable men at [ _Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) , and having without cause exiled and confiscated the property of a great many other illustrious men, finally became a successor of [ _Nero_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) in his [ _hatred and enmity_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) toward [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **He was in fact the second that stirred up a**[** _persecution_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>)**against us, although his father**[** _Vespasian_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15379a.htm>)**had undertaken nothing prejudicial to us**.

** _Chapter 18. The Apostle John and the Apocalypse_**.

1\. It is said that in this [ _persecution_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) the apostle and [ _evangelist_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) John, who was still alive, was condemned to dwell on the island of [ _Patmos_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11547a.htm>) in consequence of his testimony to the divine word.

2**.**[**_Irenæus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)**, in the fifth book of his work Against Heresies, where he discusses the number of the name of**[** _Antichrist_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01559a.htm>)**which is given in the so-called Apocalypse of John** , speaks as follows concerning him:

3\.  _If it were necessary for his name to be proclaimed openly at the present time, it would have been declared by him who saw the revelation. For it was seen not long ago,_**_but almost in our own generation, at the end of the reign of_**[** __Domitian__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) _._

4\. To such a degree, indeed, did the teaching of our [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) flourish at that time that even those writers who were far from our religion did not hesitate to mention in their histories the [ _persecution_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) and the [ _martyrdoms_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) which took place during it.

5\. And they, indeed, accurately indicated the time. For they recorded that in the fifteenth year of [ _Domitian_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) Flavia Domitilla, daughter of a sister of Flavius Clement, who at that time was one of the consuls of Rome, was exiled with many others to the island of Pontia in consequence of testimony borne to [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>).

Chapter 19. Domitian commands the Descendants of David to be slain.

But when this same [ _Domitian_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) had commanded that the descendants of David should be slain, an ancient tradition says that some of the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) brought accusation **against the descendants of Jude (****_said to have been a_**[** _brother of the Saviour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02767a.htm>)** _according to the flesh_****), on the ground that they were of the lineage of David**** _and were related to_**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _himself_**. [ _Hegesippus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07194a.htm>) relates these facts in the following words.

Chapter 20. The Relatives of our Saviour.

1\. **_Of the_**[** _family_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)** _of the_**[** _Lord_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _there were still living the grandchildren of Jude_****, who is said to have been the**[** _Lord's brother_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02767a.htm>)******_according to the flesh_**.

2\. **Information was given that they belonged to the**[** _family_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)**of David** , and they were brought to the [ _Emperor Domitian_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) by the Evocatus. **For**[** _Domitian_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>)**feared the coming of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10212c.htm>)**as**[** _Herod_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07289c.htm>)**also had feared it. And he asked them if they were descendants of David, and they confessed that they were**. Then he asked them how much property they had, or how much money they owned. And both of them answered that they had only nine thousand denarii, half of which belonged to each of them.

4\. And this property did not consist of silver, but of a piece of land which contained only thirty-nine acres, and from which they raised their taxes and supported themselves by their own labor.

5\. Then they showed their hands, exhibiting the hardness of their bodies and the callousness produced upon their hands by continuous toil as evidence of their own labor.

6\. And when they were asked **concerning**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**and his kingdom** , of what sort it was and where and when it was to appear, **they answered that it was not a temporal nor an earthly kingdom,****_but a heavenly and angelic one, which would appear at the end of the world, when he should come in_**[** _glory_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)** _to_**[** _judge_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08552a.htm>)** _the quick and the dead, and to give unto every one according to his works_**.

7\. Upon hearing this, [ _Domitian_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) did not pass judgment against them, **but, despising them as of no account, he let them go, and by a decree put a stop to the**[** _persecution_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>)**of the**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>).

8\. But when they were released **they ruled the**[** _churches_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**because they were witnesses and were also**[** _relatives_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)**of the**[** _Lord_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). And peace being established, they lived until the time of [ _Trajan_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15015a.htm>). These things are related by [ _Hegesippus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07194a.htm>).

9\. [** _Tertullian_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14520c.htm>)**also has mentioned**[** _Domitian_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>)**in the following words** : [ __Domitian__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) also, who possessed a share of [ _Nero's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) cruelty, attempted once to do the same thing that the latter did. But because he had, I suppose, some intelligence, he very soon ceased, and even recalled those whom he had banished.

10\. But after [ _Domitian_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) had reigned fifteen years, and Nerva had succeeded to the empire, the Roman Senate, according to the writers that record the history of those days, voted that [ _Domitian's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) [ _honors_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) should be cancelled, and that those who had been [ _unjustly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08010c.htm>) banished should return to their homes and have their property restored to them.

11\. It was at this time that the apostle John returned from his banishment in the island and took up his abode at [ _Ephesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) , **according to an ancient**[** _Christian_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>)****[**_tradition_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15006b.htm>).

Chapter 21. Cerdon becomes the Third Ruler of the Church of Alexandria.

1\. After Nerva had reigned a little more than a year he was succeeded by [ _Trajan_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15015a.htm>). It was during the first year of his reign that Abilius, who had ruled the [ _church of Alexandria_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01300b.htm>) for thirteen years, was succeeded by Cerdon.

2\. He was the third that presided over that [ _church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) after Annianus, who was the first. **_At that time Clement still ruled the_**[** _church of Rome_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07424b.htm>)** _, being also the third that held the_**[** _episcopate_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>)** _there after_**[** _Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)** _and Peter_**.

3\. Linus was the first, and after him came Anencletus.

Chapter 22. Ignatius, the Second Bishop of Antioch.

** _At this time_**[** _Ignatius_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07644a.htm>)** _was_**[** _known_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _as the second_**[** _bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _of_**[** _Antioch_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01570a.htm>) , [ _Evodius_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05653a.htm>) having been the first. Symeon likewise was at that time the second ruler of the [ _church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) of Jerusalem, **_the_**[** _brother of our Saviour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>)** _having been the first_**.

Chapter 23. Narrative Concerning John the Apostle.

1\. At that time the apostle and [ _evangelist_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) John, the one whom [ _Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) loved, was still living in Asia, and governing the [ _churches_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) of that region, having returned after the death of [ _Domitian_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) from his exile on the island.

2\. And that he was still alive at that time may be established by the testimony of two witnesses. **_They should be trustworthy who have maintained the_**[** _orthodoxy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11330a.htm>)** _of the_**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _; and such indeed were_**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)** _and Clement of Alexandria_**.

3\. **The former in the second book of his work Against Heresies, writes as follows** :  _And all the elders that associated with John the_[ __disciple__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) _of the_[ __Lord__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _in Asia bear witness that John delivered it to them. For he remained among them until the time of_[ __Trajan__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15015a.htm>) _._

4\. **And in the third book of the same work he attests the same thing in the following words** :  _But the_[ __church__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) _in_[ __Ephesus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) _also, which was founded by_[ __Paul__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) _, and where John remained until the time of_[ __Trajan__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15015a.htm>) _, is a faithful witness of the apostolic tradition._

5\. **Clement likewise in his book entitled**** _What Rich Man can be saved?_** indicates the time, and subjoins a narrative which is most attractive to those that enjoy hearing what is beautiful and profitable. Take and read the account which runs as follows:

6\. Listen to a tale, which is not a mere tale, but a narrative concerning [ _John the apostle_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08492a.htm>) , which has been handed down and treasured up in memory. For when, after the tyrant's death, he returned from the isle of [ _Patmos_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11547a.htm>) to [ _Ephesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) , he went away upon their invitation to the neighboring territories of the [ _Gentiles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) , to appoint [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) in some places, in other places to set in order whole [ _churches_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , elsewhere to choose to the ministry some one of those that were pointed out by the Spirit.

7\. When he had come to one of the cities not far away (the name of which is given by some ), and had consoled the brethren in other matters, he finally turned to the [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) that had been appointed, and seeing a youth of powerful physique, of pleasing appearance, and of ardent temperament, he said, 'This one I commit to you in all earnestness in the presence of the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) and with [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) as witness.' And when the [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) had accepted the charge and had promised all, he repeated the same injunction with an appeal to the same witnesses, and then departed for Ephesus.

8\. But the [ _presbyter_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) taking home the youth committed to him, reared, kept, cherished, and finally [ _baptized_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) him. After this he relaxed his stricter care and watchfulness, with the idea that in putting upon him the [ _seal of the Lord_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03586a.htm>) he had given him a perfect protection.

9\. But some youths of his own age, idle and dissolute, and accustomed to [ _evil_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) practices, corrupted him when he was thus prematurely freed from restraint. At first they enticed him by costly entertainments; then, when they went forth at night for [ _robbery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14564b.htm>) , they took him with them, and finally they demanded that he should unite with them in some greater crime.

10\. He gradually became accustomed to such practices, and on account of the positiveness of his character, leaving the right path, and taking the bit in his teeth like a hard-mouthed and powerful horse, he rushed the more violently down into the depths.

11\. And finally despairing of [ _salvation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) in [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , he no longer meditated what was insignificant, but having committed some great crime, since he was now lost once for all, he expected to suffer a like [ _fate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05793a.htm>) with the rest. Taking them, therefore, and forming a band of [ _robbers_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14564b.htm>) , he became a bold bandit-chief, the most violent, most bloody, most cruel of them all.

12\. Time passed, and some necessity having arisen, they sent for John. But he, when he had set in order the other matters on account of which he had come, said, 'Come, O [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , restore us the deposit which both I and [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) committed to you, the [ _church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , over which you preside, being witness.'

13\. But the [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) was at first confounded, thinking that he was [ _falsely_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>) charged in regard to money which he had not received, and he could neither [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) the accusation respecting what he had not, nor could he disbelieve John. But when he said, 'I demand the young man and the [ _soul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) of the brother,' the old man, groaning deeply and at the same time bursting into tears, said, 'He is dead.' 'How and what kind of death?' 'He is dead to [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ,' he said; 'for he turned [ _wicked_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) and abandoned, and at last a robber. And now, instead of the [ _church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , he haunts the mountain with a band like himself.'

14\. But the Apostle rent his clothes, and beating his head with great lamentation, he said, 'A fine guard I left for a brother's [ _soul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)! But let a horse be brought me, and let some one show me the way.' He rode away from the [ _church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03041a.htm>) just as he was, and coming to the place, he was taken prisoner by the [ _robbers'_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14564b.htm>) outpost.

15\. He, however, neither fled nor made entreaty, but cried out, 'For this did I come; lead me to your captain.'

16\. The latter, meanwhile, was waiting, armed as he was. But when he recognized John approaching, he turned in shame to flee.

17\. But John, forgetting his age, pursued him with all his might, crying out, 'Why, my son, do you flee from me, your own father, unarmed, aged? Pity me, my son; fear not; you have still hope of life. I will give account to [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) for you. If need be, I will willingly endure your death as the [ _Lord_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) suffered death for us. For you will I give up my life. Stand, [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) ; [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) has sent me.'

18\. And he, when he heard, first stopped and looked down; then he threw away his arms, and then trembled and wept bitterly. And when the old man approached, he embraced him, making confession with lamentations as he was able, baptizing himself a second time with tears, and concealing only his right hand.

19\. But John, pledging himself, **and assuring him on**[** _oath_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11176a.htm>)**that he would find forgiveness with the**[** _Saviour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, besought him, fell upon his knees, kissed his right hand itself as if now purified by repentance, and led him back to the**[** _church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**. And making intercession for him with copious**[** _prayers_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)**, and struggling together with him in continual**[** _fastings_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05789c.htm>)**, and subduing his mind by various utterances, he did not depart, as they say, until he had restored him to the**[** _church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , furnishing a great example of [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) repentance and a great [ _proof_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) of regeneration, a trophy of a visible resurrection.

Chapter 24. The Order of the Gospels.

1\. This extract from Clement I have inserted here for the sake of the history and for the benefit of my readers. **Let us now point out the undisputed writings of this apostle**.

2\. **_And in the first place his_**[** _Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _, which is_**[** _known_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _to all the_**[** _churches_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _under heaven, must be acknowledged as genuine. That it has with good reason been put by the ancients in the fourth place, after the other three_**[** _Gospels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _, may be made evident in the following way_**.

3\. **Those great and**[** _truly_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**divine men, I mean the**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, were purified in their life** , and were adorned with every [ _virtue_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) of the [ _soul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , but were uncultivated in speech. They were confident indeed in their trust **_in the divine and wonder-working power which was granted unto them by the_**[** _Saviour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _, but they did not_**[** _know_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _how, nor did they attempt to proclaim the doctrines of their teacher in studied and artistic language, but employing only the demonstration of the_**[** _divine Spirit_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, which worked with them, and the wonder-working power of_**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _, which was displayed through them_** , they published the [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of the [ _kingdom of heaven_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>) throughout the whole world, **_paying little attention to the composition of written works_**.

4\. And this they did because **they were assisted in their ministry**** _by one greater than man_****.**[**_Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**, for instance, who surpassed them all in vigor of expression and in richness of thought** , committed to writing no more than the briefest epistles, although he had innumerable [ _mysterious_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) matters to communicate, **for he had attained even unto the sights of the third heaven, had been**[** _carried_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05277a.htm>)**to the very paradise of God, and had been deemed worthy to hear unspeakable utterances there**.

5\. **And the rest of the followers of**[** _our Saviour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, the twelve**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, the seventy**[** _disciples_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**, and countless others besides** , were not [ _ignorant_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) of these things. **Nevertheless, of all the**[** _disciples_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**of the**[** _Lord_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**,****_only Matthew and John have left us written memorials_****, and they, tradition says, were led to write**** _only under the pressure of necessity_**.

6\. For Matthew, who had at first preached to the Hebrews, when he was about to go to other peoples, **_committed his_**[** _Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _to writing in his native tongue_** , and thus compensated those whom he was [ _obliged_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11189a.htm>) to leave for the loss of his presence.

7\. And when [ _Mark_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09672c.htm>) and Luke had already published their [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , **_they say that John, who had employed all his time in proclaiming the_**[** _Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _orally_** , finally proceeded to write for the following reason. The three [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) already mentioned having come into the hands of all and into his own too, **they say that he accepted them and bore witness to their**[** _truthfulness_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**; but that there was lacking in them an account of the deeds done by**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**at the beginning of his ministry**.

8\. And this indeed is [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). For it is evident that the three [ _evangelists_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) recorded only the deeds done by the [ _Saviour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) for one year after the imprisonment of John the Baptist, and indicated this in the beginning of their account.

9\. For Matthew, after the forty days' fast and the [ _temptation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14504b.htm>) which followed it, indicates the [ _chronology_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08377a.htm>) of his work when he says:  _Now when he heard that_[ __John__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08486b.htm>) _was delivered up he withdrew from_[ __Judea__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08544a.htm>) _into_[ __Galilee__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06341c.htm>) _._ [ _Matthew 4:12_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat004.htm#verse12>)

10\. [ _Mark_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09672c.htm>) likewise says:  _Now after that John was delivered up_[ __Jesus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _came into_[ __Galilee__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06341c.htm>) _._ [ _Mark 1:14_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mar001.htm#verse14>) And Luke, before commencing his account of the deeds of [ _Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , similarly marks the time, when he says that [ _Herod_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07289c.htm>) ,  _adding to all the_[ __evil deeds__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _which he had done, shut up_[ __John__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08486b.htm>) _in prison._ [ _Luke 3:20_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk003.htm#verse20>)

11\. They say, therefore, that the apostle John, being asked to do it for this reason, gave in his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) an account of the period which had been omitted by the earlier [ _evangelists_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) , and of the deeds done by the [ _Saviour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) during that period; that is, of those which were done before the imprisonment of the Baptist. And this is indicated by him, they say, in the following words:  _This beginning of_[ __miracles__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10338a.htm>) _did_[ __Jesus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) ; and again when he refers to the Baptist, in the midst of the deeds of [ _Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , as still [ _baptizing_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in Ænon near Salim; [ _John 3:23_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse23>) where he states the matter clearly in the words:  _For_[ __John__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08486b.htm>) _was not yet cast into prison._

12\. John accordingly, in his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , records the deeds of [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) which were performed before the Baptist was cast into prison, but the other three [ _evangelists_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) mention the events which happened after that time.

13\. One who understands this can no longer think that the [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) are at variance with one another, **_inasmuch as the_**[** _Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _according to John contains the first acts of_**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _, while the others give an account of the latter part of his_**[** _life_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08377a.htm>). And the genealogy of our Saviour according to the flesh John quite naturally omitted, because it had been already given by Matthew and Luke, and began with the doctrine of his divinity, which had, as it were, been reserved for him, as their superior, **_by the_**[** _divine Spirit_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>).

14\. These things may suffice, which we have said concerning the [ _Gospel of John_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08438a.htm>). The [ _cause_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) which led to the composition of the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of Mark has been already stated by us.

15\. But as for Luke, in the beginning of his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , he states himself the reasons which led him to write it. He states that since many others had more rashly undertaken to compose a narrative of the events of which he had acquired perfect [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , he himself, feeling the necessity of freeing us from their uncertain opinions, delivered in his own [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) an accurate account of those events in regard to which he had learned the full [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , **_being aided by his intimacy and his stay with_**[** _Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)** _and by his acquaintance with the rest of the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>).

16\. So much for our own account of these things. **_But in a more fitting place we shall attempt to show by quotations from the ancients, what others have said concerning them_**.

17\. But of the writings of John, not only his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , but also the [ _former of his epistles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo000.htm>) , has been accepted without dispute both now and in ancient times. But the other two are disputed.

18\. In regard to the Apocalypse, the opinions of most men are still divided. But at the proper time this question likewise shall be decided from the testimony of the ancients.

Chapter 25. The Divine Scriptures that are accepted and those that are not.

1\. Since we are dealing with this subject it is proper to sum up the writings of the [ _New Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>) which have been already mentioned. **First then must be put the**[** _holy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>)**quaternion of the**[** _Gospels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**; following them the Acts of the Apostles**.

2\. After this must be reckoned the epistles of [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ; next in order the extant [ _former epistle of John_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo000.htm>) , and likewise the epistle of Peter, must be maintained. After them is to be placed, **_if it really seem proper, the Apocalypse of John, concerning which we shall give the different opinions at the proper time_**. These then belong among the accepted writings.

3\. **Among the disputed writings,****_which are nevertheless recognized by many_****, are extant**** _the so-called epistle of James and that of Jude, also the_**[** _second epistle of Peter_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2pe000.htm>)** _, and those that are called the_**[** _second_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2jo000.htm>)** _and_**[** _third of John_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/3jo000.htm>) , whether they belong to the [ _evangelist_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) ** _or to another person of the same name_**.

4\. Among the rejected writings must be reckoned also the Acts of Paul, **and the so-called Shepherd, and the Apocalypse of Peter, and in addition to these the extant**[** _epistle of Barnabas_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02299a.htm>)**, and the so-called**[** _Teachings of the Apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04779a.htm>)**; and besides, as I said,****_the Apocalypse of John, if it seem proper, which some, as I said, reject, but which others class with the accepted books_**.

5\. And among these some have placed also the Gospel according to the Hebrews, with which those of the Hebrews that have accepted [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) are especially delighted. **And all these may be reckoned among the disputed books**.

6\. But we have nevertheless felt compelled to give a catalogue of these also, distinguishing those works which according to [ _ecclesiastical tradition_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15006b.htm>) are [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) and genuine and commonly accepted, from those others which, although not canonical but disputed, are yet at the same time [ _known_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to most [ _ecclesiastical_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) writers — we have felt compelled to give this catalogue in order that we might be able to [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) both these works and those that are cited by the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) under the name of the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , including, for instance, such books as the Gospels of Peter, of Thomas, of Matthias, or of any others besides them, and the Acts of Andrew and John and the other [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , which no one belonging to the succession of [ _ecclesiastical_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) writers has deemed worthy of mention in his writings.

7\. And further, the character of the style is at variance with apostolic usage, and both the thoughts and the purpose of the things that are related in them are so completely out of accord with [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _orthodoxy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11330a.htm>) that they clearly show themselves to be the fictions of [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). Wherefore they are not to be placed even among the rejected writings, but are all of them to be cast aside as absurd and impious.

Let us now proceed with our history. ([__Church History__](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2501.htm>) , [_Book III_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250103.htm>))

Chapter 22. Hegesippus and the Events which he mentions.

1\. [ _Hegesippus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07194a.htm>) in the five books of Memoirs which have come down to us has left a most complete record of his own views. In them he states that on a journey to [ _Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) he met a great many [ _bishops_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , **and that he received the same doctrine from all**. It is fitting to hear what he says after making some remarks **about the epistle of Clement to the Corinthians**.

2\. His words are as follows: And the church of Corinth continued in the [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) until Primus was [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) in [ _Corinth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04363b.htm>). I conversed with them on my way to [ _Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) , and abode with the Corinthians many days, during which we were mutually refreshed in the [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) doctrine.

3\. And when I had come to [ _Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) I remained there until [ _Anicetus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) , whose [ _deacon_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>) was [ _Eleutherus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05378a.htm>). And [ _Anicetus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) was succeeded by [ _Soter_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03144c.htm>) , and he by [ _Eleutherus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05378a.htm>). In every succession, and in every city that is held which is preached by the law and the [ _prophets_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) and the [ _Lord_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>).

4\. The same author also describes the beginnings of the [ _heresies_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) which arose in his time, in the following words: **And after**[** _James the Just_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>)**had suffered**[** _martyrdom_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) , as the [ _Lord_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) had also on the same account, **Symeon,****_the son of the_**[** _Lord's_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _uncle, Clopas_****, was appointed the next**[** _bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). All proposed him as second [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) ** _because he was a_**[** _cousin of the Lord_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02767a.htm>).

Therefore, they called the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) a [ _virgin_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>) , for it was not yet corrupted by vain discourses.

5\. But Thebuthis, because he was not made [ _bishop_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , began to corrupt it. He also was sprung from the seven [ _sects_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13674a.htm>) among the people, like [ _Simon_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13797b.htm>) , from whom came the [ _Simonians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13797a.htm>) , and Cleobius, from whom came the Cleobians, and Dositheus, from whom came the [ _Dositheans_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05136c.htm>) , and Gorthæus, from whom came the Goratheni, and Masbotheus, from whom came the Masbothæans. From them sprang the Menandrianists, and [ _Marcionists_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , and Carpocratians, and [ _Valentinians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) , and [ _Basilidians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02326a.htm>) , and Saturnilians. Each introduced privately and separately his own peculiar opinion. **From them came**[** _false Christs_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01559a.htm>)**,**[**_false prophets_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07698b.htm>)**, false**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, who divided the**[** _unity of the Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15179a.htm>)**by corrupt doctrines uttered against**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**and against his**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10212c.htm>).

6\. The same writer also records the ancient [ _heresies_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) which arose among the [ _Jews_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , in the following words:  _There were, moreover, various opinions in the_[ __circumcision__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) _, among the children of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _. The following were those that were opposed to the_[ __tribe of Judah__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08536a.htm>) _and the_[ __Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10212c.htm>) _:_[__Essenes__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05546a.htm>) _,_[__Galileans__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06341c.htm>) _, Hemerobaptists, Masbothæans,_[__Samaritans__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13416a.htm>) _,_[__Sadducees__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13323a.htm>) _,_[__Pharisees__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11789b.htm>).

7\. And he wrote of many other matters, which we have in part already mentioned, introducing the accounts in their appropriate places. **And from the**[** _Syriac_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14408a.htm>)****[**_Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**according to the Hebrews he quotes some passages in the Hebrew tongue, showing that he was a**[** _convert_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04347a.htm>)**from the**[** _Hebrews_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , and he mentions other matters as taken from the unwritten tradition of the [ _Jews_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>).

8\. And not only he, but also [ _Irenæus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>) and the whole company of the ancients, called the Proverbs of Solomon All-virtuous Wisdom. And when speaking of the books called Apocrypha, he records that some of them were composed in his day by certain [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). But let us now pass on to another.

Chapter 23. Dionysius, Bishop of Corinth, and the Epistles which he wrote…

9\. There is extant also another epistle written by Dionysius to the Romans, **and addressed to**[** _Soter_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03144c.htm>)**, who was**[** _bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12260a.htm>)**at that time**. We cannot do better than to subjoin some passages from this epistle, in which he commends the practice of the Romans which has been retained down to the [ _persecution_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) in our own days. His words are as follows:

10\.  _For from the beginning it has been your practice to do good to all the brethren in various ways, and to send_[ __contributions__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01328f.htm>) _to many_[ __churches__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) _in every city. Thus relieving the want of the needy, and making provision for the brethren in the mines by the gifts which you have sent from the beginning, you Romans keep up the hereditary customs of the Romans, which_** _your blessed_**[** __bishop__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12260a.htm>)** __**[**__Soter__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03144c.htm>) _has not only maintained, but also added to, furnishing an abundance of supplies to the_[ __saints__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>) _, and encouraging the brethren from abroad with blessed words, as a loving father his children_.

11\. In this same epistle **he makes mention also of Clement's epistle to the Corinthians, showing that it had been the custom from the beginning to read it in the church**. His words are as follows:  _Today we have passed the Lord's holy day, in which we have read your epistle. From it, whenever we read it, we shall always be able to draw advice, as also from the former epistle, which was written to us through Clement_. (Ibid,, [_Book IV_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250104.htm>))

Chapter 6. Catalogue of the Bishops of Rome.

1\. The blessed [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) having founded and established the church, entrusted the office of the [ _episcopate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>) to Linus. [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) speaks of this Linus in his Epistles to Timothy. [ _2 Timothy 4:21_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2ti004.htm#verse21>)

2\. Anencletus succeeded him, and after Anencletus, in the third place from the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , Clement received the [ _episcopate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>). He had seen and conversed with the blessed [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , and their preaching was still sounding in his ears, and their tradition was still before his eyes. Nor was he alone in this, for many who had been taught by the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) yet survived.

3\. In the times of Clement, a serious dissension having arisen among the brethren in [ _Corinth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04363b.htm>) , the [ _church of Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07424b.htm>) sent a most suitable letter to the Corinthians, reconciling them in peace, renewing their [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , and proclaiming the doctrine lately received from the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>).

4\. A little farther on he says:

 _Evarestus succeeded Clement, and Alexander, Evarestus. Then Xystus, the sixth from the_[ __apostles__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) _, was appointed. After him_[ __Telesphorus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14477b.htm>) _, who suffered_[ __martyrdom__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) __[__gloriously__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _; then_[ __Hyginus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07593a.htm>) _; then_[ __Pius__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12126b.htm>) _; and after him_[ __Anicetus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) _;_[__Soter__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03144c.htm>) _succeeded_[ __Anicetus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) _; and now, in the twelfth place from the_[ __apostles__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) _,_[__Eleutherus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05378a.htm>) _holds the office of_[ __bishop__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) _._

5\. In the same order and succession the tradition in the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) and the preaching of the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) has descended from the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) unto us.

Chapter 7. Even down to those Times Miracles were performed by the Faithful.

1\. **These things**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)**, in agreement with the accounts already given by us, records in the work which comprises five books, and to which he gave the title Refutation and Overthrow of the Knowledge Falsely So-called**. In the second book of the same treatise **he shows that manifestations of divine and miraculous power continued to his time in some of the churches**.

2\. He says:

__

_But so far do they come short of raising the dead, as the Lord raised them, and the_[ __apostles__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) _through_[ __prayer__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) _. And oftentimes in the brotherhood, when, on account of some necessity,_**__our entire Church has besought with__**[** __fasting__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05789c.htm>)** __and much supplication, the spirit of the dead has returned, and the man has been restored through the__**[** __prayers__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>)** __of the__**[** __saints__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>) _._

3\. And again, after other remarks, he says:

 _If they will say that even the Lord did these things in mere appearance, we will refer them to the prophetic writings, and show from them that all things were beforehand spoken of him in this manner, and were strictly fulfilled; and that he alone is the_[ __Son of God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _. Wherefore his_[ __true__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) __[__disciples__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) _, receiving_[ __grace__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) _from him, perform such works in his Name for the benefit of other men, as each has received the gift from him._

4\. For some of them drive out [ _demons_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) effectually and [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , so that those who have been cleansed from [ _evil spirits_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) frequently [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) and unite with the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). **_Others have a foreknowledge of future events, and visions, and prophetic revelations_**. Still others heal the sick by the [ _laying on of hands_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07698a.htm>) , and restore them to health. **_And, as we have said, even dead_**[** _persons_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>)** _have been raised, and remained with us many years_**.

5\. But why should we say more? It is not possible to recount the number of gifts which the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , throughout all the world, has received from God in the name of [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , who was crucified under [ _Pontius Pilate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12083c.htm>) , and exercises every day for the benefit of the [ _heathen_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>) , never deceiving any nor doing it for money. **For as she has received freely from**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, freely also does she minister**.

6\. And in another place the same author writes:

__

_As also we hear_** _that many brethren in the_**[** __Church__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _possess_**[** __prophetic__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>)** __**[**__gifts__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06553a.htm>)** _,_****__and speak, through the__**[** __Spirit__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** __, with all kinds of tongues__** _, and bring to light the secret things of men for their good, and declare the_[ __mysteries__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) _of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _._

So much in regard to the fact that various gifts remained among those who were worthy even until that time.

Chapter 8. The Statements of Irenæus in regard to the Divine Scriptures.

1\. Since, in the beginning of this work, **we promised to give, when needful, the words of the ancient**[** _presbyters_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**and writers of the**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**,****_in which they have declared those traditions which came down to them concerning the canonical books, and since_**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)** _was one of them_****, we will now give his words and, first, what he says of the**[** _sacred Gospels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) :

2\. Matthew published his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) among the Hebrews **in their own language** , while Peter and [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) were preaching and founding the church in Rome.

3\. After their departure **Mark, the**[** _disciple_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**and interpreter of Peter, also transmitted to us in writing those things which Peter had preached** ; and Luke, the attendant of [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , recorded in a book the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) which [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) had declared.

4\. Afterwards John, the [ _disciple_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of the Lord, who also reclined on his bosom, published his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , while staying at Ephesus in Asia.

5\. **He states these things in the third book of his above-mentioned work. In the fifth book he speaks as follows concerning the Apocalypse of John, and the number of the name of**[** _Antichrist_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01559a.htm>) :

__

_As these things are so,_**__and this number is found in all the approved and ancient copies, and those who saw John face to face confirm it__** _, and reason teaches us that the number of the name of the beast, according to the mode of calculation among the Greeks, appears in its letters...._

6\. And further on he says concerning the same:

 _We are not bold enough to speak confidently of the name of_[ __Antichrist__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01559a.htm>) _. For if it were necessary that his name should be declared clearly at the present time, it would have been announced by him who saw the revelation. For it was seen, not long ago, but almost in our generation, toward the end of the reign of_[ __Domitian__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) _._

7\. He states these things concerning the Apocalypse in the work referred to. **He also mentions the first Epistle of John, taking many**[** _proofs_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>)**from it, and likewise the first Epistle of Peter**. And he not only [ _knows_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , **_but also receives, The Shepherd_** , writing as follows:

__

_Well did the_[ __Scripture__](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _speak, saying, 'First of all_[ __believe__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _that God is one, who has created and completed all things,'_ etc.

8\. **And he uses almost the precise words of the Wisdom of Solomon, saying** :  _The vision of God produces_[ __immortality__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) _, but_[ __immortality__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) _renders us near to_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _._ He mentions also the memoirs of a certain apostolic [ _presbyter_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) , whose name he passes by in silence, and gives his expositions of the sacred Scriptures.

9\. **_And he refers to Justin the Martyr, and to_**[** _Ignatius_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07644a.htm>) , using testimonies also from their writings. Moreover, he promises to refute [ _Marcion_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) from his own writings, in a special work.

10\. Concerning the translation of the inspired Scriptures by the Seventy, hear the very words which he writes:

[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __in__**[** __truth__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** __became man, and the Lord himself saved us, giving the sign of the virgin;__**_but not as some say, who now venture to translate the_[ __Scripture__](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _, 'Behold, a young_[ __woman__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>) _shall conceive and bring forth a son,' as Theodotion of Ephesus and Aquila of_[ __Pontus__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12234c.htm>) _, both of them Jewish proselytes, interpreted; following whom, the_[ __Ebionites__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05242c.htm>) _say that he was begotten by Joseph._

11\. Shortly after he adds:

 _For before the Romans had established their empire, while the Macedonians were still holding Asia, Ptolemy, the son of Lagus, being desirous of adorning the_[ __library__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09227b.htm>) _which he had founded in_[ __Alexandria__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01299d.htm>) _with the meritorious writings of all_[ __men__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) _, requested the people of Jerusalem to have their Scriptures translated into the Greek language._

12\. But, as they were then subject to the Macedonians, they sent to Ptolemy seventy elders, who were the most skilled among them in the [ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) and in both languages. Thus God accomplished his purpose.

13\. But wishing to try them individually, as he feared lest, by taking counsel together, they might conceal the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of the [ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) by their interpretation, he separated them from one another, and commanded all of them to write the same translation. He did this for all the books.

14\. But when they came together in the presence of Ptolemy, **and compared their several translations, God was**[** _glorified_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)**, and the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**were recognized as**[** _truly_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**divine**. For all of them had rendered the same things in the same words and with the same names from beginning to end, so that the [ _heathen_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>) perceived that the [ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) had been translated by the inspiration of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

15\. And this was nothing wonderful for [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) to do, who, in the captivity of the people under [ _Nebuchadnezzar_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10666c.htm>) , when the [ _Scriptures_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) had been destroyed, and the [ _Jews_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) had returned to their own country after seventy years, afterwards, in the time of Artaxerxes, king of the [ _Persians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11712a.htm>) , **_inspired Ezra the_**[** _priest_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)** _, of the tribe of Levi, to relate all the words of the former_**[** _prophets_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)** _, and to restore to the people the legislation of_**[** _Moses_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>).

Such are the words of [ _Irenæus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>). (Ibid., [_Book V_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250105.htm>))
