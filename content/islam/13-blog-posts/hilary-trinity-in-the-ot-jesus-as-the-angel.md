# Hilary: Trinity in the OT & Jesus as the Angel

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * 9 hours ago
  * 40 min read


In this somewhat lengthy post, I quote the words of another great saint, Hilary of Poitiers, from his writing  _On the Holy Trinity_ , [ _Book IV_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/330204.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>).

This holy saint not only argued that Jesus is that very divine Angel that appeared throughout the OT, he also quoted texts such as Genesis 1, Psalms 45:6-7, Isaiah 45:11-14, Hosea 1:6-7, Romans 9:5, etc., to prove God’s Triunity and the essential Deity of the Lord Christ. To make his case for the distinction of divine Persons, Hilary argued from Genesis 1 that the God who commanded things to exist is the Father, and the God who is said to have then made them is the Son. In other words, the God who said is the Father, and the God who did the creating is the Son! Hilary cites Genesis 1:26 to further bolster his argument since there God speaks in the plural. Another interesting point about Hilary’s defense of the Trinity is that he quotes 2 Maccabees 7:28 to prove that the Trinity created all things from nothing. Hilary also condemns the use of imperfect, finite, fallible, sinful human reasoning to contradict or explain away what God has said of himself in the inspired Scriptures.

I now turn to Hilary’s masterful defense of the blessed and holy Trinity, and exegesis of the God-breathed Scriptures. All emphasis will be mine.

11\. Now that we have exposed their plan of belittling the Son under cover of magnifying the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the next step is to listen to the exact terms in which they express their own belief concerning the Son. For, since we have to answer in succession each of their allegations and to display on the evidence of [ _Holy Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) the impiety of their doctrines, we must append, to what they say of the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the decisions which they have put on record concerning the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , that by a comparison of their confession of the Father with their confession of the Son we may follow a uniform order in our solution of the questions as they arise. They state as their verdict that the Son is not derived from any pre-existent matter, for through Him all things were created, nor yet begotten from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for nothing can be withdrawn from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; but that He was made out of what was nonexistent, that is, that He is a perfect creature of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , though different from His other creatures. They argue that He is a creature, because it is written,  _The Lord has created Me for a beginning of His ways_ [ _Proverbs 8:22_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/pro008.htm#verse22>) ; that He is the perfect handiwork of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , though different from His other works, they prove, as to the first point, by what [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) writes to the Hebrews,  _Being made so much better than the_[ __angels__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, as He possesses a more excellent name than they_ [ _Hebrews 1:4_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/heb001.htm#verse4>) , and again,  _Wherefore,_[__holy__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) _brethren, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our confession,_[__Jesus Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, who is faithful to Him that made Him_. For their depreciation of the might and majesty and Godhead of the Son they rely chiefly on His own words,  _The Father is greater than I_. [ _John 14:28_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh014.htm#verse28>) But they admit that He is not one of the common herd of creatures on the evidence of  _All things were made through Him_. [ _John 1:3_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse3>) **And so they sum up the whole of their**[** _blasphemous_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>)**teaching in these words which follow:—**

12\. We confess One [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , alone unmade, alone [ _eternal_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) , alone unoriginate, alone [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , alone possessing [ _immortality_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) , alone good, alone mighty, Creator, Ordainer and Disposer of all things, unchangeable and unalterable, righteous and good, of the Law and the Prophets and the [ _New Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>). We [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that this God gave birth to the Only-begotten Son before all worlds, through Whom He made the world and all things; that He gave birth to Him not in semblance, but in [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , following His own Will, so that He is unchangeable and unalterable, God's perfect creature but not as one of His other creatures, His handiwork, but not as His other works; not, as [ _Valentinus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) maintained, that the Son is a development of the Father; nor, as Manichæus has declared of the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , a consubstantial part of the Father; nor, as Sabellius, who makes two out of one, Son and Father at once; nor, as Hieracas, a light from a light, or a lamp with two flames; nor as if He was previously in being and afterwards born or created afresh to be a Son, a notion often condemned by yourself, blessed Pope , publicly in the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) and in the assembly of the brethren. But, as we have affirmed, we [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that He was created by the [ _will_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of God before times and worlds, and has His life and [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) from the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , Who gave Him to share His own [ _glorious_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) perfections. For, when the Father gave to Him the inheritance of all things, He did not thereby deprive Himself of attributes which are His without origination, He being the source of all things.

13\.  _So there are three Persons, Father, Son, and_[ __Holy Ghost__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) _._[__God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, for His part, is the_[ __cause__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) _of all things, utterly unoriginate and separate from all; while the_[ __Son__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, put forth by the Father outside time, and created and established before the worlds, did not exist before He was born, but, being born outside time before the worlds, came into being as the Only Son of the Only Father. For He is neither_[ __eternal__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) _, nor co-eternal, nor co-uncreate with the_[ __Father__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, nor has He an_[ __existence__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) _collateral with the_[ __Father__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, as some say, who postulate two unborn principles. But God is before all things, as being indivisible and the beginning of all. Wherefore He is before the Son also, as indeed we have learned from you in your public preaching. Inasmuch then as He has His being from_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and His_[ __glorious__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _perfections, and His life, and is entrusted with all things, for this reason God is His source, and has rule over Him, as being His_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, since He is before Him. As to such phrases as from Him, and from the womb, and I went out from the Father and have come, if they be understood to denote that the Father extends a part and, as it were, a development of that one substance, then the Father will be of a compound nature and divisible and changeable and corporeal, according to them; and thus, as far as their words go, the incorporeal God will be subjected to the properties of matter._

14\. **__Such is their__**[** __error__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>)** __, such their pestilent teaching__** ; to support it they borrow the words of Scripture, perverting its meaning and using the [ _ignorance_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) of men as their opportunity of gaining credence for their lies. Yet it is certainly by these same words of God that we must come to understand the things of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). For [ _human_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) feebleness cannot by any strength of its own attain to the [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of heavenly things; the faculties which deal with bodily matters can form no notion of the unseen world. **__Neither our created bodily substance, nor the reason given by__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __for the purposes of ordinary life, is capable of ascertaining and pronouncing upon the nature and work of__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __. Our wits cannot rise to the level of heavenly__**[** __knowledge__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** __, our powers of perception lack the strength to apprehend that limitless might__**. We must [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) God's word concerning Himself, and humbly accept such insight as He vouchsafes to give. We must make our choice between rejecting His [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) , as the [ _heathen_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>) do, **__or else believing in Him as He is, and this in the only possible way, by thinking of Him in the aspect in which He presents Himself to us. Therefore let private judgment cease; let__**[** __human__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)** __reason refrain from passing barriers divinely set. In this spirit we eschew all__**[** __blasphemous__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>)** __and reckless assertion concerning__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __, and cleave to the very letter of revelation__**. Each point in our enquiry shall be considered in the light of His instruction, Who is our theme; **__there shall be no stringing together of isolated phrases whose context is suppressed, to trick and misinform the unpractised listener. The meaning of words shall be ascertained by considering the circumstances under which they were spoken; words must be explained by circumstances not circumstances forced into conformity with words__**. We, at any rate, will treat our subject completely; we will state both the circumstances under which words were spoken, **__and the__**[** __true__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** __purport of the words__**. Each point shall be considered in orderly sequence.

15\. Their starting-point is this; We confess, they say, One only [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , because [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) says,  _Hear, O_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Lord your God is One_. [ _Deuteronomy 6:4_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu006.htm#verse4>) **But is this a**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**which anyone has ever dared to**[** _doubt_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>)**?** **Or was any believer ever**[** _known_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**to confess otherwise than that there is One God from Whom are all things, One Majesty which has no birth, and that He is that unoriginated Power? Yet this fact of the Unity of God offers no chance for denying the Divinity of His**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). For [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , or rather God through [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , laid it down as His first commandment to that people, devoted both in [ _Egypt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) and in the Desert to [ _idols_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>) and the worship of imaginary gods, that they must [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in One God. There was [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) and reason in the commandment, for [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , from Whom are all things, is One. **But let us see whether this**[** _Moses_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)**have not confessed that He, through Whom are all things, is also God**. God is not [ _robbed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14564b.htm>) , **He is still**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, if His Son share the Godhead. For the case is that of God from**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, of One from One, of God Who is One because God is from Him. And conversely the Son is not less God because**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**the Father is One, for He is the Only-begotten**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**; not**[** _eternally_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**unborn, so as to deprive the Father of His Oneness, nor yet different from**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, for He is born from Him**. We must not [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) that He is God by virtue of that birth from God which proves to us who [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that God is One; yet let us see whether [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , who announced to [ _Israel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) ,  _The Lord your God is One_ , **has also proclaimed the Godhead of the Son**. To make good our confession of the Divinity of [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) **we must employ the evidence of that same**[** _witness_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>)**on whom the**[** _heretics_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**rely for the confession of One Only**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, which they**[** _imagine_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07672a.htm>)**to involve the denial of the Godhead of the Son**.

16\. Since, therefore, the words of the Apostle,  _One_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _the_[ __Father__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, from Whom are all things, and one_[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, our Lord, through Whom are all things_ [ _1 Corinthians 8:6_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co008.htm#verse6>) , form an accurate and complete confession concerning [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , let us see what [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) has to say of the beginning of the world. His words are,  _And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the water, and let it divide the water from the water. And it was so, and God made the firmament and God divided the water through the midst_. [ _Genesis 1:6-7_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse6>) **Here, then, you have the God from Whom, and the God through Whom**. If you deny it, you must tell us **through whom it was that God's work in creation was done** , or else point for your explanation to an [ _obedience_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) in things yet uncreated, which, when God said  _Let there be a firmament_ , impelled the firmament to establish itself. Such suggestions are inconsistent with the clear sense of Scripture. **__For all things, as the Prophet says__**[** __2 Maccabbees 7:28__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2ma007.htm#verse28>)** __, were made out of nothing; it was no transformation of existing things, but the creation into a perfect form of the non-existent__**. Through whom? Hear the Evangelist:  _All things were made through Him_. If you ask Who this is, the same Evangelist will tell you:  _In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and the Word was God. He was in the beginning with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _. All things were made through Him_. [ _John 1:1-3_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse1>) If you are minded to combat the view that it was the Father Who said,  _Let there be a firmament_ , the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) will answer you:  _He spoke, and they were made; He commanded, and they were created_. The recorded words,  _Let there be a firmament_ , **reveal to us that the Father spoke**. But in the words which follow,  _And it was so_ , **in the statement that God did this thing, we must recognise the Person of the Agent**.  _He spoke, and they were made_ ; the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) does not say that He willed it, and did it.  _He commanded, and they were created;_ you observe that it does not say they came into [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , because it was His pleasure. In that case there would be no office for a Mediator between God and the world which was awaiting its creation. [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , from Whom are all things, **gives the order for creation which**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, through Whom are all things, executes. Under one and the same Name we confess Him Who gave and Him Who fulfilled the command. If you dare to deny that**** _God made_****is spoken of the**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, how do you explain**** _All things were made through Him?_**__ Or the Apostle's words,  _One_[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, our Lord, through Whom are all things?_ Or,  _He spoke, and they were made?_ If these inspired words succeed in convincing your stubborn [ _mind_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10321a.htm>) , **you will cease to regard that text,****_Hear, O_**[** __Israel__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>)** _, the Lord your God is One_****, as a refusal of Divinity to the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , since at the very foundation of the world **He Who spoke it proclaimed that His Son also is**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). But let us see what increase of profit we may draw **__from this distinction of__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __Who commands and God Who executes__**. For though it is repugnant even to our [ _natural_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) [ _reason_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12673b.htm>) to suppose that in the words,  _He commanded, and they were made_ , one single and isolated Person is intended, yet, for the avoidance of all doubts, we must expound the events which followed upon the creation of the world.

17\. When the world was complete and its inhabitant was to be created, the words spoken concerning him were,  _Let Us make man after Our image and likeness_. [ _Genesis 1:26_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse26>) **I ask you, Do you suppose that God spoke those words to Himself?****_Is it not obvious that He was addressing not Himself, but Another?_** If you reply that He was alone, then out of His own mouth He confutes you, for He says,  _Let Us make man after Our image and likeness._ God has spoken to us through the Lawgiver in the way which is intelligible to us; that is, He makes us acquainted with His action by means of language, the faculty with which He has been pleased to endow us. **There is, indeed, an indication of the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, through Whom all things were made, in the words,****_And God said, Let there be a firmament_****, and in,****_And God made the firmament_****, which follows** : but lest we should think these words of God were wasted and meaningless, **supposing that He issued to Himself the command of creation, and Himself**[** _obeyed_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**it** — for what notion could be further from the thought of a solitary God **than that of giving a verbal order to Himself, when nothing was necessary except an exertion of His**[** _will_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>)**?** — **_He determined to give us a more perfect assurance that these words refer to Another beside Himself. When He said,_**  _Let Us make man after Our image and likeness_ , **_His indication of a Partner demolishes the theory of His isolation_****. For an isolated being cannot be partner to himself; and again, the words,****_Let Us make_****, are inconsistent with solitude, while**** _Our_****cannot be used except to a companion. Both words,****_Us_****and**** _Our_****are inconsistent with the notion of a solitary God speaking to Himself, and equally inconsistent with that of the address being made to a stranger who has nothing in common with the Speaker**. If you interpret the passage to mean that He is isolated, **I ask you whether you suppose that He was speaking with Himself?** If you do not understand that He was speaking with Himself, how can you assume that He was isolated? If He were isolated, we should find Him described as isolated; if He had a companion, then as not isolated. ** _I_****and**** _Mine_****would describe the former state; the latter is indicated by**** _Us_****and**** _Our_**.

18\. Thus, when we read,  _Let Us make man after Our image and likeness_ , these two words  _Us_ and  _Our_ **reveal that there is neither one isolated**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, nor yet one God in two dissimilar Persons** ; and our confession must be framed in harmony with the second as well as with the first [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). For the words  _our image_ — not  _our images_ — **prove that there is one nature possessed by Both**. But an argument from words is an insufficient [ _proof_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) , unless its result be confirmed by the evidence of facts; and accordingly it is written,  _And God made man; after the image of God made He him_. [ _Genesis 1:27_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse27>) If the words He spoke, I ask, were the soliloquy of an isolated [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , what meaning shall we assign to this last statement? For in it I see a triple allusion, to the Maker, to the being made, and to the image. The being made is man; God made him, and made him in the image of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). If Genesis were speaking of an isolated [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , it would certainly have been  _And made him after His own image_. But since the book was foreshowing the Mystery of the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , **it spoke not of two Gods, but of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**and**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, for it speaks of man made through God in the image of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**. Thus we find that God wrought man after an image and likeness common to Himself and to**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; that the mention of an Agent forbids us to assume that He was isolated; and that the work, **done after an image and likeness which was that of Both** , proves that there is no difference in kind between the Godhead of the One and of the Other.

19\. It may seem waste of time to bring forward further arguments, **__for truths concerning God gain no strength by repetition; a single statement suffices to establish them__**. Yet it is well for us to [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) all that has been revealed upon the subject, for though we are not responsible for the words of Scripture, **__yet we shall have to render an account for the sense we have assigned to them__**. One of the many commandments which [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) gave to [ _Noah_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11088a.htm>) is,  _Whoever sheds man's blood, for his blood shall his life be shed, for after the image of God made I man_. Here again is the distinction between likeness, creature, and Creator. God bears [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) that He made man after the image of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). When He was about to make man, **because He was speaking of Himself, yet not to Himself** , God said,  _After our image;_ and again, after man was made,  _God made man after the image of God_. It would have been no inaccuracy of language, had He said, addressing Himself,  _I have made man after My image_ , **for He had shown that the Persons are one in nature** by,  _Let us make man after Our image_. But for the more perfect removal of all [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) as to whether God be, or be not, a solitary Being, when He made man He made him, we are told,  _After the image of God_.

20\. If you still wish to assert that [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father in solitude said these words to Himself, I can go with you as far as to admit the possibility that He might in solitude have spoken to Himself as if He were conversing with a companion, and that it is credible that He wished the words  _I have made man after the image of God_ to be equivalent to  _I have made man after My own image_. But your own confession of [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) will refute you. For you have confessed that all things are from the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **but all through the Son; and the words** ,  _Let Us make man_ , **show that the Source from Whom are all things is He Who spoke thus, while**  _God made him after the image of God_ **clearly points to Him through Whom the work was done**.

21\. And furthermore, to make all self-deception unlawful, that Wisdom, which you have yourself confessed to be Christ, shall confront you with the words,  _When He was establishing the fountains under the heaven, when He was making strong the foundations of the earth, I was with Him, setting them in order. It was I, over Whom He rejoiced. Moreover, I was daily rejoicing in His sight, all the while that He was rejoicing in the world that He had made, and in the sons of men_. [ _Proverbs 8:28-31_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/pro008.htm#verse28>) Every difficulty is removed; [ _error_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) itself must recognise the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). There is with God **Wisdom, begotten before the worlds; and not only present with Him, but setting in order** , for She was  _with Him, setting them in order_. Mark this work of setting in order, or arranging. The Father, by His commands, is the Cause; **the**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, by His execution of the things commanded, sets in order**. The distinction between the Persons is marked by the work assigned to Each. When it says  _Let us make_ , creation is identified with the word of command; but when it is written,  _I was with Him, setting them in order_ , **God reveals that He did not do the work in isolation. For He was rejoicing before Him, Who, He tells us** , rejoiced in return;  _Moreover, I was daily rejoicing in His sight, all the while that He was rejoicing in the world that He had made, and in the sons of men_. Wisdom has taught us the reason of Her [ _joy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>). **She rejoiced because of the**[** _joy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>)**of the**[** _Father_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , Who rejoices over the completion of the world and over the sons of men. For it is written,  _And God saw that they were good_. **__She rejoices that God is well pleased with His work, which has been made through Her, at His command__**. She avows that Her [ _joy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) results from the Father's [ _gladness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) over the finished world and over the sons of men; over the sons of [ _men_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , because in the one man [ _Adam_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) the whole [ _human race_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) had begun its course. **Thus in the creation of the world there is no mere soliloquy of an isolated Father; His Wisdom is His partner in the work, and rejoices with Him when their conjoint labour ends**.

22\. I am aware that the full explanation of these words involves the discussion of many and weighty problems. I do not shirk them, but postpone them for the present, reserving their consideration for later stages of the enquiry. For the present I devote myself to that article of the [ _blasphemers_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) ' [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , **or rather faithlessness** , which asserts that [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) proclaims the solitude of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). We do not forget that the assertion is [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) in the sense that there is One [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , from Whom are all things; **but neither do we forget that this**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**is no excuse for denying the Godhead of the**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, since**[** _Moses_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)**throughout the course of his writings clearly indicates the**[** _existence of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608b.htm>)**and God**. We must examine how the history of God's choice, and of the giving of the Law, **proclaims God co-ordinate with**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

23\. After God had often spoken with [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Sarah was moved to [ _wrath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01489a.htm>) against Hagar, being jealous that she, the mistress, was barren, while her handmaid had conceived a son. Then, when Hagar had departed from her sight, the Spirit speaks thus concerning her,  _And the_[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _of the Lord said to Hagar, Return to your mistress, and submit yourself under her hands. And the_[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _of the Lord said to her, I will multiply your seed exceedingly, and it shall not be numbered for multitude, and again, And she called the Name of the Lord that spoke with her, You are_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Who hast seen me_. **It is the Angel of God Who speaks, and speaks of things far beyond the powers which a messenger** , for that is the meaning of the word, could have. He says,  _I will multiply your seed exceedingly, and it shall not be numbered for multitude_. **The power of multiplying nations lies outside the ministry of an**[** _angel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). Yet what says the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) of Him Who is called the Angel of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , yet speaks words which belong to God alone?  _And she called the Name of the Lord that spoke with her, You are_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Who hast seen me_. **First He is the Angel of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**; then He is the Lord** , for  _She called the Name of the Lord;_ **then, thirdly, He is**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, for**** _You are_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, Who hast seen me_****. He Who is called the Angel of God is also Lord and God.****_The_**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _is also, according to the_**[** _prophet_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)** _, the_**** __Angel of great counsel__**** _. To discriminate clearly between the Persons, He is called the Angel of_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _; He Who is God from God is also the Angel of_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, but, that He may have the_**[** __honour__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)** _which is His due, He is entitled also Lord and God_**.

24\. **In this passage the one Deity is first the Angel of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and then, successively, Lord and God. But to**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**He is God only**. For when the distinction of Persons had first been made, as a safeguard against the delusion that God is a solitary Being, then His [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) and unqualified name could safely be uttered. And so it is written.  _And God said to_[ __Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _, Behold Sarah your wife shall bear you a son, and you shall call his name Isaac; and I will establish My covenant with him for an everlasting covenant, and with his seed after him. And as far Ishmael, behold. I have heard you and have blessed him, and will multiply him exceedingly; twelve nations shall he beget, and I will make him a great nation_. [ _Genesis 17:19-20_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen017.htm#verse19>) **Is it possible to**[** _doubt_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>)**that He Who was previously called the Angel of God is here, in the sequel, spoken of as God**? In both instances He is speaking of Ishmael; **in both it is the same Person Who shall multiply him.** **To save us from supposing that this was a different Speaker from Him who had addressed Hagar** , the Divine words expressly attest the identity, saying,  _And I have blessed him, and will multiply him_. **The blessing is repeated from a former occasion, for Hagar had already been addressed** ; the multiplication is promised for a future day, for this is God's first word to [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) concerning Ishmael. Now it is God Who speaks to [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) ; **to Hagar the Angel of God had spoken.****__Thus God and the Angel of God are One; He Who is the Angel of God is also God the__**[** __Son of God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** __. He is called the Angel because He is the Angel of great counsel; but afterwards He is spoken of as__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __, lest we should suppose that He Who is God is only an__**[** __angel__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). Let us now repeat the facts in order. **_The Angel of the Lord spoke to Hagar; He spoke also to_**[** __Abraham__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)** _as_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _. One Speaker addressed both_**. The blessing was given to Ishmael, and the promise that he should grow into a great people.

25\. In another instance the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) reveals through [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) **that it was God Who spoke**. He receives the further promise of a son, Isaac. Afterwards there appear to him three men. [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , though he sees three, **worships One, and acknowledges Him as Lord**. Three were standing before him, [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible>) says, **but he**[** _knew_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**well Which it was that he must worship and confess**. There was nothing in outward appearance to distinguish them, but by the eye of [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , the vision of the [ _soul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , he [ _knew_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) his Lord. Then the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) goes on,  _And He said to him, I will certainly return unto you at this time hereafter, and Sarah your wife shall have a son_ [ _Genesis 18:10_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen018.htm#verse10>) ; and afterwards the Lord said to Him,  _I will not conceal from_[ __Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _My servant the things that I will do_ ; and again,  _Moreover the Lord said, The cry of_[ __Sodom__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _and_[ __Gomorrha__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _is filled up, and their_[ __sins__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _are exceeding great_. Then after long discourse, which for the sake of brevity shall be omitted, [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , distressed at the destruction which awaited the innocent as well as the guilty, said,  _In no wise will You, Who judges the earth, execute this judgment. And the Lord said, If I find in_[ __Sodom__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _fifty righteous within the city, then I will spare all the place for their sakes_. Afterwards, when the warning to [ _Lot_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09366a.htm>) , [ _Abraham's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) brother, was ended, the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) says,  _And the Lord rained upon_[ __Sodom__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _and upon_[ __Gomorrha__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) _brimstone and fire from the Lord out of heaven_ ; and, after a while,  _And the Lord visited Sarah as He had said, and did unto Sarah as He had spoken, and Sarah conceived and bare_[ __Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _a son in his old age, at the set time of which_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _had spoken to him_. And afterwards, when the handmaid with her son had been driven from [ _Abraham's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) house, and was dreading lest her child should die in the wilderness for want of water, the same [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible>) says  _And the Lord God heard the voice of the lad, where he was, and the Angel of God called to Hagar out of heaven, and said to her, What is it, Hagar? Fear not, for God has heard the voice of the lad from the place where he is. Arise, and take the lad and hold his hand, for I will make him a great nation_.

26\. What blind faithlessness it is, what dulness of an unbelieving heart, what headstrong impiety, to abide in [ _ignorance_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) of all this, or else to [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) and yet neglect it! Assuredly it is written for the very purpose that [ _error_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) or oblivion may not hinder the recognition of the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). If, as we shall prove, it is impossible to escape [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of the facts, then it must be nothing less than [ _blasphemy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) to deny them. This record begins with the speech of the Angel to Hagar, His promise to multiply Ishmael into a great nation and to give him a countless offspring. She listens, **and by her confession reveals that He is Lord and God**. The story begins with His appearance as the Angel of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; **at its termination He stands confessed as God Himself**. Thus He Who, while He executes the ministry of declaring the great counsel **is God's Angel, is Himself in name and nature God**. The name corresponds to the nature; the nature is not falsified to make it conform to the name. Again, God speaks to [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) of this same matter; he is told that Ishmael has already received a blessing, and shall be increased into a nation; _I have blessed him_ , God says. This is no change from the Person indicated before; He shows that it was He Who had already given the blessing. The Scripture has obviously been consistent throughout in its progress from [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) to clear revelation; **it began with the Angel of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and proceeds to reveal that it was God Himself Who had spoken in this same matter**.

27\. The course of the Divine narrative is accompanied by a progressive development of doctrine. In the passage which we have discussed God speaks to [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , and promises that Sarah shall bear a son. Afterwards three men stand by him; **he worships One and acknowledges Him as Lord. After this worship** and acknowledgment by [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , the One promises that He will return hereafter at the same season, and that then Sarah shall have her son. **This One again is seen by**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**in the guise of a**[** _man_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and salutes him with the same promise. The change is one of name only; [ _Abraham's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) acknowledgment in each case is the same. **It was a Man whom he saw, yet**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**worshipped Him as Lord;****__he beheld, no__**[** __doubt__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>)** __, in a__**[** __mystery__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)** __the coming Incarnation__**. Faith so strong has not missed its recognition; **__the Lord says in the__**[** __Gospel__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** __, Your father__**[** __Abraham__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)** __rejoiced to see My day; and he saw it, and was glad.__**[**__John 8:56__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh008.htm#verse56>)** __To continue the history__** ; the Man Whom he saw promised that He would return at the same season. Mark the fulfilment of the promise, remembering meanwhile that it was a Man Who made it. What says the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)?  _And the Lord visited Sarah_. So this Man is the Lord, fulfilling His own promise. What follows next?  _And God did unto Sarah as He had said_. **The narrative calls His words those of a Man** , relates that Sarah was visited by the Lord, proclaims that the result was the work of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **You are sure that it was a Man who spoke, for**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**not only heard, but saw Him**. Can you be less certain that He was [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , when the same Scripture, **which had called Him Man, confesses Him God**? For its words are,  _And Sarah conceived, and bare_[ __Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _a son in his old age, and at the set time of which_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _had spoken to him_. **But it was the Man who had promised that He would come**. Believe that He was nothing more than man; unless, in fact, He Who came was God and Lord. Connect the incidents. It was, confessedly, **the Man who promised that He would come that Sarah might conceive and bear a son**. And now accept instruction, **and confess the**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**; it was the Lord God Who came that she might conceive and bear. The Man made the promise in the power of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; by the same power God fulfilled the promise. Thus God reveals Himself both in word and deed. Next, two of the three men whom [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) saw depart; **He Who remains behind is Lord and God. And not only Lord and**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, but also Judge, for**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**stood before the Lord** and said, _In no wise shall Thou do this things, to slay the righteous with the_[ __wicked__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _, for then the righteous shall be as the_[ __wicked__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _. In no wise will You Who judges the whole earth, execute this judgment._ [ _Genesis 18:25_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen018.htm#verse25>) Thus by all his words [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) instructs us in that [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , for which he was justified; **he recognises the Lord from among the three, he worships Him only, and confesses that He is Lord and Judge**.

28\. Lest you fall into the [ _error_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) of supposing that this acknowledgment of the One was a payment of [ _honor_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) to all the three whom [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) saw in company, mark the words of [ _Lot_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09366a.htm>) when he saw the two who had departed;  _And when_[ __Lot__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09366a.htm>) _saw them, he rose up to meet them, and he bowed himself with his face toward the ground; and he said, Behold, my lords, turn in to your servant's house_. Here the plural  _lords_ shows that this was nothing more than a vision of [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) ; **in the other case the faithful patriarch pays the**[** _honour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)**due to One only. Thus the sacred narrative makes it clear that two of the three were mere**[** _angels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**; it had previously proclaimed**** _the One as Lord and God_****by the words** ,  _And the Lord said to_[ __Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _, Wherefore did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I then bear a child? But I am grown old. Is anything from God impossible? At this season I will return to you hereafter, and Sarah shall have a son_. The Scripture is accurate and consistent; we detect no such confusion as the plural used of the One God and Lord, no Divine honours paid to the two [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). [ _Lot_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09366a.htm>) , no [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) , calls them  _lords_ , while the [ _Scripture_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) calls them [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). **The one is**[** _human_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**reverence, the other literal**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>).

29\. And now there falls on [ _Sodom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) and [ _Gomorrha_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) the vengeance of a righteous judgment. What can we learn from it for the purposes of our enquiry?  _The Lord rained brimstone and fire from the Lord_. **It is**** _The Lord from the Lord_****; Scripture makes no distinction, by difference of name, between Their natures, but discriminates between Themselves.****_For we read in the_**[** __Gospel__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)** _, The Father judges no man, but has given all judgment to the Son_** _._[__John 5:22__](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh005.htm#verse22>) Thus what the Lord gave, the Lord had received from the Lord.

30\. You have now had evidence of God the Judge as Lord and Lord; learn next that there is the same joint ownership of name in the case of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and God. Jacob, when he fled through [ _fear_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) of his brother, saw in his dream a ladder resting upon the earth and reaching to heaven, and the [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of God ascending and descending upon it, and the Lord resting above it, Who gave him all the blessings which He had bestowed upon [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) and Isaac. At a later time God spoke to him thus:  _And God said to Jacob, Arise, go up to the place Bethel, and dwell there, and make there an altar unto_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, that appeared unto you when you fled from the face of your brother_. [ _Genesis 35:1_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen035.htm#verse1>) **God demands**[** _honour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)**for**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and makes it clear that demand**** _is on behalf of Another than Himself_**.  _He who appeared to you when you fled_ are His words: He guards carefully against any confusion of the Persons. **It is God Who speaks, and God of Whom He speaks**. Their majesty is asserted by the combination of Both under Their [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) Name of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , while the words **plainly declare Their several**[** _existence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>).

31\. Here again there occur to me considerations which must be taken into account in a complete treatment of the subject. But the order of defense must adapt itself to the order of attack, and I reserve these outstanding questions for discussion in the next book. For the present, in regard to God Who demanded [ _honour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) for [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , it will suffice for me to point out **that He Who was the Angel of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, when He spoke with Hagar, was God and Lord when He spoke of the same matter with**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**; that the Man Who spoke with**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**was also God and Lord** , while the two [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , who were seen with the Lord and whom He sent to [ _Lot_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09366a.htm>) , **are described by the**[** _prophet_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**as**[** _angels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**, and nothing more.****_Nor was it to_**[** __Abraham__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)** _only that God appeared in_**[** __human__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)** _guise; He appeared as Man to Jacob also_**. And not only did He appear, but, **_so we are told, He wrestled_** ; and not only did He wrestle, **_but He was vanquished by His adversary_**. Neither the time at my disposal, nor the subject, will allow me to discuss the typical meaning of this wrestling. **_It was certainly God Who wrestled, for Jacob prevailed against_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, and_**[** __Israel__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>)** _saw God_**.

32\. And now let us enquire whether elsewhere than in the case of Hagar **the Angel of God has been discovered to be God Himself**. He has been so discovered, **and found to be not only**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, but the**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**of**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**and of Isaac and of Jacob**. For the Angel of the Lord appeared to [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) from the bush; and Whose voice, think you, are we to suppose was heard? **The voice of Him Who was seen** , or of Another? There is no room for deception; the words of Scripture are clear:  _And the Angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a flame of fire from a bush_ , and again,  _The Lord called unto him from the bush,_[__Moses__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) _,_[__Moses__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) _, and he answered, What is it? And the Lord said, Draw not near hither, put off your shoes from off your feet, for the place whereon you stand is_[ __holy__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) _ground. And He said to him, I am the_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _of_[ __Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _, and the_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _of Isaac, and the_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _of Jacob_. **He who appeared in the bush speaks from the bush; the place of the vision and of the voice is one; He Who speaks is none other than He Who was seen. He Who is the Angel of God when the eye beholds Him is the Lord when the ear hears Him, and the Lord Whose voice is heard is recognised as the**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**of**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**, and of Isaac, and of Jacob**. When He is styled the Angel of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **the fact is revealed that He is no self-contained and solitary Being: for He is the Angel of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**. When He is designated Lord and**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, He receives the full title which is due to His nature and His name**. You have, then, **in the Angel Who appeared from the bush, Him Who is Lord and God**.

33\. Continue your study of the [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) borne by [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) ; mark how diligently he seizes every opportunity of proclaiming the Lord and God. You take note of the passage,  _Hear, O_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Lord your God is One_. [ _Deuteronomy 6:4_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu006.htm#verse4>) Note also the words of that Divine song of his;  _See, See, that I am the Lord, and there is no God beside_ Me. While God has been the Speaker throughout the poem, he ends with,  _Rejoice, you heavens, together with Him and let all the sons of God praise Him. Rejoice, O you nations, with His people, and let all the Angels of God do Him_[ __honour__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>). God is to be [ _glorified_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) by the Angels of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and He says,  _For I am the Lord, and there is no God beside Me_. **For He is God the Only-begotten, and the title 'Only-begotten' excludes all partnership in that character, just as the title 'Unoriginate' denies that there is, in that regard, any who shares the character of the Unoriginate Father**. The Son is One from One. There is none unoriginate except God the Unoriginate, **and so likewise there is none only-begotten except God the Only-begotten**. They stand Each single and alone, being respectively the One Unoriginate and the One Only-begotten. And so They Two are One [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for between the One, and the One Who is His offspring **there lies no gulf of difference of nature in the**[** _eternal_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**Godhead. Therefore He must be worshipped by the sons of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**and**[** _glorified_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)**by the**[** _angels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Honour and reverence is demanded for God from the sons and from the [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Notice Who it is that shall receive this [ _honour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) , and by whom it is to be paid. It is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and they are the sons and [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **And lest you should**[** _imagine_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07672a.htm>)**that**[** _honour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)**is not demanded for God Who shares our nature** , but that [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) is thinking here of reverence due to [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) — though, indeed, it is in the Son that the Father must be honoured — examine the words of the blessing bestowed by [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) upon Joseph, at the end of the same book. They are, ** _And let the things that are well-pleasing to Him that appeared in the bush come upon the head and crown of Joseph_**. [ _Deuteronomy 33:16_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu033.htm#verse16>) Thus God is to be worshipped by the sons of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; **but God Who is Himself the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). And God is to be reverenced by the [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; **_but God Who is Himself the Angel of_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _. For God appeared from the bush as the Angel of_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the [ _prayer_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) for Joseph is that he may receive such blessings as He shall please. **_He is none the less God because He is the Angel of_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _; and none the less the Angel of God because He is_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**. A clear indication is given of the Divine Persons** ; the line is definitely drawn between the Unbegotten and the Begotten. A revelation of the [ _mysteries_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) of heaven is granted, and we are taught not to dream of God as dwelling in solitude, when [ _angels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) and sons of God shall worship Him, **Who is God's Angel and His**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>).

34\. Let this be taken as our answer from the books of [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , **or rather as the answer of**[** _Moses_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)**himself. The**[** _heretics_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)****[**_imagine_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07672a.htm>)**that they can use his assertion of the Unity of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**in disproof of the Divinity of God the Son; a**[** _blasphemy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) in defiance of the clear warning of their own [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) , for whenever he confesses that God is One **he never fails to teach the Son's Divinity**. Our next step must be to adduce the manifold utterance of the [ _prophets_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) concerning the same Son.

35\. You [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the words,  _Hear, O_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Lord your God is One;_ would that you [ _knew_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) them aright! As you interpret them, I seek in vain for their sense. **It is said in the**[** _Psalms_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12533a.htm>) , [ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Your_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, has anointed You_. [ _Psalm 45:7_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/psa044.htm#verse8>) **Impress upon the reader's mind the distinction between the Anointer and the Anointed; discriminate between the**** _You_****and the**** _Your_** _:_ make it clear to Whom and of Whom the words are spoken. For this definite confession is the conclusion of the preceding passage, which runs thus;  _Your throne, O_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, is for ever and ever; the sceptre of Your kingdom is a right sceptre. You have loved righteousness and_[ __hated__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) _iniquity._ And then he continues,  _Therefore_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Your_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, has anointed You_. **Thus the**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**of the**[** _eternal_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**kingdom** , in reward for His [ _love_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) of righteousness and [ _hatred_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) of iniquity, **is anointed by His God**. Surely some broad difference is drawn, some gap too wide for our mental span, between these names? No; **_the distinction of Persons is indicated by You and Your, but nothing suggests a difference of nature_****.****_Your_****points to the Author,****_You_****to Him Who is the Author's offspring. For He is God from**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , as these same words of the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) declare, [ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Your_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, has anointed You_. And His own words bear [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) that there is no God anterior to God the Un-originate;  _Be My witnesses, and I am_[ __witness__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) _, says the_[ __Lord God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and My Servant Whom I have chosen, that you may_[ __know__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _and_[ __believe__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _and understand that I am, and before Me there is no other_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, nor shall be after Me_. [ _Isaiah 43:10_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa043.htm#verse10>) Thus the majesty of Him that has no beginning is declared, and the [ _glory_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) of Him that is from the Unoriginate is safeguarded; for [ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Your_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, has anointed You_. That word  _Your_ declares His birth, yet does not contradict His nature; ** _Your God_****means that the Son was born from Him to share the Godhead**. But the fact that the Father is God **is no obstacle to the Son's being God also, for**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Your_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, has anointed You_. Mention is made both of Father and of Son; **the one title of God conveys the assurance that in character and majesty They are One**.

36\. But lest these words,  _For I am, and before Me there is no other_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, nor shall be after Me_ , be made a handle for [ _blasphemous_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) presumption, as proving that the Son is not [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , since after the [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , Whom no God precedes, there follows no other [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the purpose of the passage must be considered. God is His own best interpreter, but His chosen Servant joins with Him to assure us that there is no God before Him, nor shall be after Him. His own [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) concerning Himself is, indeed, sufficient, but He has added the [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of the Servant Whom He has chosen. Thus we have the united testimony of the Two, that there is no God before Him; we accept the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , because all things are from Him. We have Their [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) also that there shall be no God after Him; but They do not deny that God has been born from Him in the past. Already there was the Servant speaking thus, and bearing [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) to the Father; the Servant born in that tribe from which God's elect was to spring. He sets forth also the same [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) in the [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) :  _Behold, My Servant Whom I have chosen, My Beloved in Whom My_[ __soul__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) _is well pleased_. [ _Matthew 12:18_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat012.htm#verse18>) This is the sense, then, in which [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) says,  _There is no other God before Me, nor shall be after Me_. He reveals the infinity of His [ _eternal_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) and unchanging majesty by this assertion that there is no God before or after Himself. **But He gives His Servant a share** both in the bearing of [ _witness_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) **and in the possession of the Name of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

37\. The fact is obvious from His own words. **For He says to Hosea the**[** _prophet_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**,****_I will no more have mercy upon the house of_**[** __Israel__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>)** _, but will altogether be their enemy. But I will have mercy upon the children of Judah, and will save them in the Lord their God_****.**[**_Hosea 1:6-7_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/hos001.htm#verse6>)**Here**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**the Father gives the name of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, without any ambiguity, to the**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, in Whom also He chose us before countless ages**.  _Their God_ , He says, for while the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , being Unoriginate, is independent of all, **He has given us for an inheritance to His**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). In like manner we read,  _Ask of Me, and I will give You the_[ __Gentiles__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) _for Your inheritance_. None can be God to Him from Whom are all things , for He is [ _eternal_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) and has no beginning; **but the Son has**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, from Whom He was born, for His Father. Yet to us the Father is God and the Son is**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**;****_the Father reveals to us that the Son is our_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, and the Son teaches that the Father is_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _over us_**. The point for us to remember is that **in this passage the Father gives to the Son the name of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the title of His own unoriginate majesty. But I have commented sufficiently on these words of Hosea.

38\. Again, how clear is the declaration made by [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father through Isaiah concerning our Lord! He says,  _For thus says the Lord, the_[ __holy__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) _God of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, Who made the things to come, Ask me concerning your sons and your daughters, and concerning the works of My hands command ye Me. I have made the earth and man upon it, I have commanded all the stars, I have raised up a King with righteousness, and all His ways are straight. He shall build My city, and shall turn back the captivity of My people, not for price nor reward, says the Lord of Sabaoth._[__Egypt__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) _shall labour, and the merchandise of the_[ __Ethiopians__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05566a.htm>) _and Sabeans. Men of stature shall come over unto You and shall be Your servants, and shall follow after You, bound in chains, and shall worship You and make supplication unto You, for God is in You and there is no God beside You. For You are_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and we_[ __knew__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _it not, O God of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Saviour. All that resist Him shall be ashamed and confounded, and shall walk in confusion_. [ _Isaiah 45:11-16_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa045.htm#verse11>)

Is any opening left for gainsaying, or excuse for [ _ignorance_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>)? If [ _blasphemy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) continue, is it not in brazen defiance that it survives? God from Whom are all things, Who made all by His command, asserts that He is the Author of the [ _universe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>) , for, unless He had spoken, nothing had been created. He asserts that He has raised up a righteous King, who builds for Himself, that is, for [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , a city, and turns back the captivity of His people, for no gift nor reward, for freely are we all saved. Next, He tells how after the labours of [ _Egypt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) , and after the traffic of [ _Ethiopians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05566a.htm>) and Sabeans, men of stature shall come over to Him. How shall we understand these labours in [ _Egypt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) , this traffic of [ _Ethiopians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05566a.htm>) and Sabeans? **Let us call to mind how the**[** _Magi_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09527a.htm>)**of the East worshipped and paid tribute to the Lord** ; let us estimate the weariness of that long pilgrimage to [ _Bethlehem_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02533a.htm>) of Judah. In the toilsome journey of the Magian princes we see the labours of [ _Egypt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) to which the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) alludes. For when the [ _Magi_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09527a.htm>) executed, in their spurious, material way, the duty ordained for them by the power of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the whole [ _heathen_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>) world was offering in their person the deepest reverence of which its worship was capable. And these same [ _Magi_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09527a.htm>) presented gifts of gold and frankincense and myrrh from the merchandise of the [ _Ethiopians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05566a.htm>) and Sabeans; a thing foretold by another [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , who has said,  _The_[ __Ethiopians__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05566a.htm>) _shall fall down before His face, and His enemies shall lick the dust. The Kings of Tharsis shall offer presents, the Kings of the Arabians and Sabeans shall bring gifts, and there shall be given to Him of the gold of Arabia_. The [ _Magi_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09527a.htm>) and their offerings stand for the labour of [ _Egypt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) and for the merchandise of [ _Ethiopians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05566a.htm>) and Sabeans; the adoring [ _Magi_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09527a.htm>) **represent the**[** _heathen_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>)**world, and offer the choicest gifts of the**[** _Gentiles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>)**to the Lord Whom they adore**.

39\. As for the men of stature who shall come over to Him and follow Him in chains, there is no [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm>) who they are. **Turn to the**[** _Gospels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**; Peter, when he is to follow his Lord, is girded up. Read the Apostles:**[**_Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**, the servant of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, boasts of his bonds**. Let us see whether this 'prisoner of [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) ' conforms in his teaching to the prophecies uttered by [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) concerning God His [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). God had said,  _They shall make supplication, for God is in You_. **Now mark and digest these words of the Apostle** :—  _God was in_[ __Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, reconciling the world to Himself_. [ _2 Corinthians 5:19_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2co005.htm#verse19>) And then the [ _prophecy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>) continues,  _And there is no God beside You_. **The Apostle promptly matches this with**  _For there is one_[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _our Lord, through Whom are all things_. [ _1 Corinthians 8:6_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co008.htm#verse6>) Obviously there can be none other but He, for He is One. The third prophetic statement is,  _You are God and we_[ __knew__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _it not_. **_But_**[** _Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)** _, once the persecutor of the_**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _, says,_****__Whose are the fathers, from Whom is Christ, Who is__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __over all__**** _._**[**_Romans 9:5_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom009.htm#verse5>) Such is to be the message of these men in chains; men of stature, indeed, they will be, and shall sit on twelve thrones to judge the tribes of [ _Israel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) , and shall follow their Lord, witnesses to Him in teaching and in [ _martyrdom_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>).

40\. **Thus God is in**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and it is God in Whom God dwells**. But how is  _There is no God beside You_ [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , if God be within Him? Heretic! In support of your confession of a solitary Father you employ the words,  _There is no God beside Me;_ what sense can you assign to the solemn declaration of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ,  _There is no God beside You_ , if your explanation of  _There is no God beside Me_ **be a denial of the Godhead of the Son?** To whom, in that case, can God have said,  _There is no God beside You?_**You cannot suggest that this solitary Being said it to Himself**. It was to the King Whom He summoned that the Lord said, by the mouth of the men of stature who worshipped and made supplication,  _For God is in You._**The facts are inconsistent with solitude.****_In You_****implies that there was One present within range, if I may say so, of the Speaker's voice**. The complete sentence,  _God is in You_ , reveals not only God present, **but also God abiding in Him Who is present**. The words **distinguish the Indweller from Him in Whom He dwells,****__but it is a distinction of Person only__****, not of character**. God is in Him, and He, in Whom God is, is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **The residence of God cannot be within a nature strange and alien to His own**. He abides in One Who is His own, born from Himself. God is in [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , because God is from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).  _For You are_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and we_[ __knew__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _it not, O God of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Saviour._

41\. My next book is devoted to the refutation of your denial that God is in [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; for the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) continues,  _All that resist Him shall be ashamed and confounded and shall walk in confusion._ This is God's sentence, passed upon your unbelief. You set yourself in opposition to [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and it is on His account that the Father's voice is raised in solemn reproof; for He, Whose Godhead you deny, is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **And you deny it under cloak of reverence for**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , because He says,  _There is no other God beside Me_. **Submit to shame and confusion;****_the Unoriginate God has no need of the dignity you offer; He has never asked for this majesty of isolation which you attribute to Him. He repudiates your officious interpretation which would twist His words_** ,  _There is no other God beside Me,_ ** _into a denial of the Godhead of the Son Whom He begot from Himself_**. To frustrate your purpose of demolishing the Divinity of the Son by assigning the Godhead in some special sense to Himself, He rounds off the glories of the Only-begotten by the attribution of absolute Divinity: —  _And there is no God beside You_. **Why make distinctions between exact equivalents? Why separate what is perfectly matched? It is the peculiar characteristic of the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**that there is no God beside Him; the peculiar characteristic of**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**the Father that there is no God apart from Him**. Use His words concerning Himself; confess Him in His own terms, and entreat Him as King;  _For God is in You, and there is no God beside You. For You are_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and we_[ __knew__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _it not, O God of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Saviour_. A confession couched in words so reverent is free from the taint of presumption: its terms can excite no repugnance. Above all, we must remember that to refuse it means shame and ignominy. Brood in thought over these words of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; employ them in your confession of Him, and so escape the threatened shame. **For if you deny the Divinity of the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, you will not be augmenting the**[** _glory_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)**of God by adoring Him in lonely majesty; you will be slighting the Father by refusing to reverence the Son**. In [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) and veneration **confess of the Unoriginate God that there is no God beside Him; claim for God the Only-begotten that apart from Him there is no God**.

42\. As you have listened already to [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) and Isaiah, so listen now to Jeremiah inculcating the same [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) as they:—  _This is our_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and there shall be none other likened unto Him, Who has found out all the way of_[ __knowledge__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _, and has given it unto Jacob His servant and to_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _His beloved. Afterward did He show Himself upon earth and dwelt among_[ __men__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>). For previously he had said,  _And He is Man, and Who shall_[ __know__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _Him_? **Thus you have God seen on earth and dwelling among**[** _men_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**. Now I ask you what sense you would assign to**** _No one has seen God at any time, save the Only-begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father_******[_John 1:18_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse18>) , **when Jeremiah proclaims God seen on earth and dwelling among**[** _men_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**? The Father confessedly cannot be seen except by the Son; Who then is This who was seen and dwelt among**[** _men_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**? He must be our**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, for He is God visible in**[** _human_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)****[**_form_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06137b.htm>)**, Whom men can handle**. And take to heart the [ _prophet's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) words,  _There shall be none other likened to Him_. If you ask how this can be, listen to the remainder of the sentence, lest you be tempted to deny to the Father His share of the confession.  _Hear, O_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Lord your God is One_. The whole passage is,  _There shall be none likened unto Him, Who has found out all the way of_[ __knowledge__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _, and has given it unto Jacob His servant and to_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _His beloved. Afterward did He show Himself upon earth and dwelt among_[ __men__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>). For there is one Mediator between God and Men, **Who is both**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**and**[** _Man_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) ; Mediator both in giving of the Law and in taking of our body. Therefore none other can be likened unto Him**, for He is One, born from God into**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and He it was through Whom all things were created in heaven and earth, through Whom times and worlds were made**. Everything, in fine, that exists **owes its**[** _existence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**to His action. He it is that instructs**[** _Abraham_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**, that speaks with**[** _Moses_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>)**, that testifies to**[** _Israel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>)**, that abides in the**[** _prophets_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**, that was born through the Virgin from the**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**, that nails to the cross of His**[** _passion_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11530a.htm>)**the powers that are our foes, that slays death in**[** _hell_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07207a.htm>)**, that strengthens the assurance of our hope by His Resurrection, that destroys the corruption of**[** _human_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**flesh by the**[** _glory_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)**of His Body**. Therefore none shall be likened unto Him. For these are the peculiar powers **of God the Only-begotten** ; He alone was born from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the blissful Possessor of such great prerogatives. No second god can be likened unto Him, **for He is God from**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, not born from any alien being**. There is nothing new or strange or modern created in Him. When [ _Israel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) hears that its God is one, and that no second god is likened, that men may deem him [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , to God Who is God's Son, the revelation means **that**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**the Father and God the Son are One altogether,****__not by confusion of Person but by unity of substance__**. For the [ _prophet_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) forbids us, because God the Son is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , to liken Him to some second deity.
