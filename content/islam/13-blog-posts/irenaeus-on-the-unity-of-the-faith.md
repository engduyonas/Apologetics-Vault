# Irenaeus on the Unity of the Faith

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 31, 2025
  * 6 min read


I quote what the holy martyr and saint of the Church Irenaeus said in regards to the universal beliefs of Christians around the world. It is important to keep in mind that Irenaeus wrote around 180 AD, and was the Bishop of Lyons, France, being the disciples of the Bishop Polycarp, who himself was discipled and trained by the holy Apostles, specifical John.

  


Irenaeus affirms that all Christians throughout the world believe in the blessed Trinity, that Jesus Christ is the Lord, God, Savior and King who became flesh and was raised in his flesh into heaven. The holy saint further attests that the universal Church taught that Christ will return to physically resurrect the dead in order judge all mankind and angels. The wicked will be assigned to everlasting fire whereas those who have done good will be rewarded with immortality. This blessed martyr even attests that the invisible God would appear visibly in a variety of forms to various individuals.

  


Here I am citing his work called [__Adversus haereses__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103.htm>) , Book I. All emphasis will be mine.

2\. As I have already observed, **the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, having received this preaching and this**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**, although scattered throughout the whole world, yet, as if occupying but one house, CAREFULLY PRESERVES IT. She also believes these points [of doctrine] just as if she had but one**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**, and one and the same heart, and she proclaims them, and teaches them, and hands them down, with perfect harmony, as if she possessed only one mouth**. For, although the languages of the world are dissimilar, **yet the import of the tradition is one and the same. For the Churches which have been planted in Germany do not**[** _believe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)**or hand down anything different, nor do those in**[** _Spain_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14169b.htm>)**, nor those in**[** _Gaul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06395b.htm>)**, nor those in the East, nor those in**[** _Egypt_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>)**, nor those in Libya, nor those which have been established in the central regions of the world**. But as the sun, that creature of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , is one and the same throughout the whole world, so also the preaching of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) shines everywhere, and enlightens all [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) that are willing to come to a [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). **_Nor will any one of the rulers in the_**[** __Churches__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _, however highly gifted he may be in point of eloquence, teach doctrines different from these (for no one is greater than the Master)_** ; nor, on the other hand, will he who is deficient in power of expression inflict injury on the tradition. For the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) being ever one and the same, neither does one who is able at great length to discourse regarding it, make any addition to it, nor does one, who can say but little diminish it.

  


3\. It does not follow because men are endowed with greater and less degrees of intelligence, **that they should therefore change the subject-matter [of the**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**] itself, and should conceive of some other God besides Him who is the Framer, Maker, and Preserver of this**[** _universe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>)**, (as if He were not sufficient for them), or of another Christ, or another Only-begotten**. But the fact referred to simply implies this, that one may [more accurately than another] bring out the meaning of those things which have been spoken in [ _parables_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm>) , and accommodate them to the general scheme of the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) ; and explain [with special clearness] the operation and dispensation of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) connected with [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250722033212/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) ;
