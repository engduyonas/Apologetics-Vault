To sum up the data, God, the Memra/Word of the Lord, the Glory of the Lord, a Spirit of mercy from the Lord are all involved in creating and giving life to all creation. Gen. 2:1 even begins by referring to “They” having created the heavens and the earth and all things in them. This obvious use of the plural shows that the Jews were fully aware that the one true God who created all things was not a singular Person. These Jews could see from their reading of the Hebrew Bible that YHWH their God is a multi-personal Being. 

  


What makes this Aramaic paraphrase interesting is that it also acknowledges that it was God’s Memra, or Word, who appeared in the Garden shortly after Adam and Eve had sinned:
