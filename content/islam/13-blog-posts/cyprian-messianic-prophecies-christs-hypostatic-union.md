# Cyprian, Messianic Prophecies & Christ’s Hypostatic Union

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * 2 days ago
  * 27 min read


In this post I will be quoting from St. Cyprian’s Treatises, specifically [_Treatise XII (Book 2)_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/050712b.htm>). Cyprian quotes extensively from both the Old and New Testaments to prove that Jesus is the God-Man, the divine Son of God, whom the prophets announced would come to be killed for sins, raised from the dead, and who would return to Judge the living and the dead. This beloved saint’s knowledge of the prophetic scriptures was truly astounding. All emphasis will be mine.

****

1\. That Christ is the First-born, and that He is the Wisdom of God, by whom all things were made

In Solomon in the Proverbs:  _The Lord established me in the beginning of His ways, into His works: before the world He founded me. In the beginning, before He made the earth, and before He appointed the abysses, before the fountains of waters gushed forth, before the mountains were settled, before all the hills, the Lord begot me. He made the countries, and the uninhabitable places, and the uninhabitable bounds under heaven. When He prepared the heaven, I was present with Him; and when He set apart His seat. When He made the strong clouds above the winds, and when He placed the strengthened fountains under heaven, when He made the mighty foundations of the earth, I was by His side, ordering them: I was He in whom He delighted: moreover, I daily rejoiced before His face in all time, when He rejoiced in the perfected earth_. **Also in the same in Ecclesiasticus:****_I went forth out of the mouth of the Most High,_**[**__first-born__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>)** _before every creature: I made the unwearying light to rise in the heavens, and I covered the whole earth with a cloud: I dwelt in the high places, and my throne in the pillar of the cloud: I compassed the circle of heaven, and I penetrated into the depth of the abyss, and I walked on the waves of the sea, and I stood in all the earth; and in every people and in every nation I had the pre-eminence, and by my own strength I have trodden the hearts of all the excellent and the_**[** __humble__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07543b.htm>)** _: in me is all hope of life and_**[** __virtue__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>)** _: pass over to me, all you who desire me_**. Also in the eighty-eighth Psalm:  _And I will establish Him as my_[ __first-born__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) _, the highest among the kings of the earth. I will keep my mercy for Him for ever, and my faithful covenant for Him; and I will establish his seed for ever and ever. If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments; if they profane my judgments, and do not observe my precepts, I will visit their wickednesses with a rod, and their_[ __sins__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _with scourges; but my mercy will I not scatter away from them_. Also in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to John, the Lord says:  _And this is life_[ __eternal__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) _, that they should_[ __know__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _You, the only and_[ __true__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) __[__God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and_[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, whom You have sent. I have_[ __glorified__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _You on the earth: I have finished the work which You gave me to do. And now, glorify me with Yourself, with the_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _which I had with You before the world was made_. Also [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) to the Colossians:  _Who is the image of the invisible_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and the_[ __first-born__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) _of every creature. Also in the same place: The_[ __first-born__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) _from the dead, that He might in all things become the holder of the pre-eminence_. **In the Apocalypse too:****_I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give unto Him that is thirsting from the fountain of the water of life freely_**. That He also is both the wisdom and the power of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) proves in his first Epistle to the Corinthians.  _Because the_[ __Jews__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) _require a sign, and the Creeks seek after wisdom: but we preach Christ crucified, to the_[ __Jews__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) _indeed a stumbling-block, and to the_[ __Gentiles__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) _foolishness; but to them that are called, both_[ __Jews__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) _and Greeks, Christ the power of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _and the wisdom of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

2\. That Christ is the Wisdom of God; and concerning the sacrament of His incarnation and of His passion, and cup and altar; and of the apostles who were sent, and preached

In Solomon in the Proverbs:  _Wisdom has built herself a house, and she has placed under it seven pillars; she has slain her victims; she has mingled her wine in the goblet, and has made ready her table, and has sent her servants, calling with a loud announcement to the cup, saying, Let him who is foolish turn to me: and to them that want understanding she has said, Come, eat of my loaves, and drink the wine which I have mingled for you. Forsake foolishness, and seek wisdom, and correct_[ __knowledge__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _by understanding_.

3\. That the same Christ is the Word of God

**In the forty-fourth Psalm:****_My heart has breathed out a good Word. I tell my works to the King. Also in the thirty-second Psalm: By the_**[** __Word of God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)** _were the heavens made fast; and all their strength by the breath of His mouth_**. Also in Isaiah:  _A Word completing and shortening in righteousness, because a shortened word will God make in the whole earth_. Also in the cvith Psalm:  _He sent His Word, and healed them_. Moreover, in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to John:  _In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and God was the Word. The same was in the beginning with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _. All things were made by Him, and without Him was nothing made that was made. In Him was life; and the life was the light of men. And the light shines in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not_. Also in the Apocalypse:  _And I saw the heaven opened, and lo, a white horse; and he who sate upon him was called Faithful and True, judging rightly and_[ __justly__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) _; and He made_[ __war__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15546c.htm>) _. And He was covered with a garment sprinkled with blood; and His name is called the_[ __Word of God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>).

**4\. That Christ is the Hand and Arm of God**

Also in the same place:  _Lord, who has_[ __believed__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _our report? Also in the same place:  _Behold, I have made you as the wheels of a thrashing chariot, new and turned back upon themselves; and you shall thrash the mountains, and shall beat the bills small, and shall make them as chaff, and shall winnow them; and the wind shall seize them, and the whirlwind shall scatter them: but you shall_[ __rejoice__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) _in the_[ __saints__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>) _of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _; and the poor and needy shall exult. I the_[ __Lord God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, I the_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, will hear them, and will not forsake them; but I will open rivers in the mountains, and fountains in the midst of the fields. I will make the wildernesses watery groves, and a thirsty land into watercourses. I will establish in the land of drought the cedar-tree and the box-tree, and the myrtle and the cypress, and the elm and the poplar, that they may see and acknowledge, and_[ __know__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _and_[ __believe__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _together, that the Hand of the Lord has done these things, and the Holy One of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _has shown them_.

**5\. That Christ is at once Angel and God**

In Genesis, to [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) :  _And the Angel of the Lord called him from heaven, and said to him,_[__Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _,_[__Abraham__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) _! Also in the same place, to Jacob:  _And the Angel of the Lord spoke unto me in dreams, I am_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, whom you saw in the place of God where you anointed me a pillar of stone, and vowed to me a vow_. Also in Exodus:  _But God went before them by day indeed in a pillar of cloud, to show them the way; and by night in a pillar of fire_. And afterwards, in the same place:  _And the Angel of God moved forward, which went before the army of the children of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>). Also in the same place:  _Lo, I send my Angel before your face, to keep you in the way, that He may lead you into the land which I have prepared for you. Observe Him, and_[ __obey__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) _Him, and be not disobedient to Him, and He will not be wanting to you. For my Name is in Him_. **Whence He Himself says in the**[** _Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**:****_I came in the name of my Father, and you received me not. When another shall come in his own name, him you will receive_**. And again in the cxviith Psalm:  _Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord._ Also in Malachi:  _My covenant of life and peace was with Levi; and I gave him_[ __fear__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _, that he should_[ __fear__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _me, that he should go from the face of my name. The law of_[ __truth__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _was in his mouth, and unrighteousness was not found in his lips. In the peace of the tongue correcting, he walked with us, and turned many away from unrighteousness. Because the lips of the_[ __priests__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) _shall keep_[ __knowledge__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _, and they shall seek the law at His mouth; for He is the Angel of the Almighty_.

6\. That Christ is God

In Genesis:  _And God said to Jacob, Arise, and go up to the place of Bethel, and dwell there; and make there an altar to that God who appeared to you when you fled from the face of your brother_[ __Esau__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05527c.htm>) _._ Also in Isaiah:  _Thus says the Lord, the_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _of Sabaoth,_[__Egypt__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) _is wearied; and the merchandise of the_[ __Ethiopians__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05566a.htm>) _, and the tall men of the Sabeans, shall pass over unto You, and shall be Your servants; and shall walk after You bound with chains; and shall worship You, and shall_[ __pray__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) _to You, because God is in You, and there is no other God beside You. They shall all be confounded and_[ __fear__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _who oppose You, and shall fall into confusion_. Every channel shall be filled up, and every mountain and bill shall be made low, and all crooked places shall be made straight, and rough places plain; and the_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _of the Lord shall be seen, and all flesh shall see the_[ __salvation__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) _of_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, because the Lord has spoken it_. Moreover, in Jeremiah: _This is our_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and no other shall be esteemed beside Him, who has found all the way of_[ __knowledge__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _, and has given it to Jacob His son, and to_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _His beloved. After this He was seen upon earth, and He conversed with men_. Also in Zechariah God says:  _And they shall cross over through the narrow sea, and they shall smite the waves in the sea, and they shall dry up all the depths of the rivers; and all the haughtiness of the_[ __Assyrians__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02007c.htm>) _shall be confounded, and the sceptre of_[ __Egypt__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) _shall be taken away. And I will strengthen them in the Lord their_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and in His name shall they_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _, says the Lord_. Moreover, in Hosea the Lord says:  _I will not do according to the_[ __anger__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01489a.htm>) _of mine indignation, I will not allow Ephraim to be destroyed: for I am_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and there is not a_[ __holy__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) _man in you: and I will not enter into the city; I will go after God_. Also in the forty-fourth Psalm:  _Your throne, O_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, is for ever and ever: the sceptre of righteousness is the sceptre of Your kingdom. You have loved righteousness, and_[ __hated__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07149b.htm>) _iniquity: wherefore_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, Your_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, has anointed You with the oil of_[ __gladness__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) _above Your fellows_. So, too, in the forty-fifth Psalm:  _Be still, and_[ __know__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _that I am God. I will be exalted among the_[ __nations__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) _, and I will be exalted in the earth_. Also in the eighty-first Psalm:  _They have not_[ __known__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _, neither have they understood: they will walk on in darkness_. Also in the sixty-seventh Psalm:  _Sing unto_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, sing praises unto His name: make a way for Him who goes up into the west: God is His name_. Also in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to John:  _In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and God was the Word_. Also in the same:  _The Lord said to Thomas, Reach hither your finger, and behold my hands: and be not faithless, but believing. Thomas answered and said to Him, My Lord and my God. Jesus says unto him, Because you have seen me, you have_[ __believed__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _: blessed are they who have not seen, and yet have_[ __believed__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>). **Also**[** _Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**to the Romans** :  _I could wish that I myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren and my kindred according to the flesh: who are Israel-ires: whose are the adoption, and the_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _, and the covenant, and the appointment of the law, and the service (of God), and the promises; whose are the fathers, of whom, according to the flesh_** _, Christ came, who is_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _over all, blessed for evermore_****. Also in the Apocalypse:****_I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end I will give to him that is thirsty, of the fountain of living water freely. He that overcomes shall possess these things, and their inheritance; and I will be his_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, and he shall be my son_****. Also in the eighty-first Psalm** : [ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _stood in the congregation of gods, and judging gods in the midst. And again in the same place: I have said, You are gods; and you are all the children of the Highest: but you shall die like men_. **But if they who have been righteous, and have**[** _obeyed_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**the divine precepts, may be called gods, how much more is Christ, the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, God!** Thus He Himself says in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to John:  _Is it not written in the law, that I said, You are gods? If He called them gods to whom the word of God was given, and the_[ __Scripture__](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _cannot be relaxed, say to Him whom the Father has sanctified and sent into the world, that you blaspheme because I said, I am the_[ __Son of God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _?_  _But if I do not the works of my Father,_[__believe__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _me not; but if I do, and you will not_[ __believe__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _me,_[__believe__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) _the works, and_[ __know__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _that the Father is in me, and I in Him_. Also in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to Matthew:  _And you shall call His name_[ __Emmanuel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05404a.htm>) _, which is, being interpreted, God with us_.

7\. That Christ our God should come, the En-lightener and Saviour of the human race

In Isaiah:  _Be comforted, you weakened hands; and you weak knees, be strengthened. You who are of a timorous heart,_[__fear__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _not. Our God will recompense judgment, He Himself will come, and will save us. Then shall be opened the eves of the blind, and the ears of the deaf shall hear. Then the lame man shall leap as a stag, and the tongue of the dumb shall be intelligible; because in the wilderness the water is broken forth, and the stream in the thirsty land_. **Also in that place:**  _Not an elder nor an_[ __angel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) _, but the Lord Himself shall deliver them; because He shall_[ __love__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) _them, and shall spare them, and He Himself shall redeem them_. Also in the same place:  _I the Lord God have called You in righteousness, that I may hold Your hand, and I will comfort You; and I have given You for a covenant of my people, for a light of the nations; to open the eyes of the blind, to bring forth them that are bound from chains, and those who sit in darkness from the prison-house. I am the_[ __Lord God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, that is my name. I will not: give any_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _to another, nor my powers to given images_. Also in the twenty-fourth Psalm:  _Show me Your ways, Lord, and teach me Your paths, and lead me unto Your_[ __truth__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) _, and teach me; for You are the_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _of my_[ __salvation__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>). Whence, in the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to John, the Lord says:  _I am the light of the world. He that will follow me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life_. Moreover, in that according to Matthew, the [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) Gabriel says to Joseph:  _Joseph, you son of David,_[__fear__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) _not to take unto you Mary your wife. For that which shall be born to her is of the_[ __Holy Ghost__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) _. And she shall bring forth a son, and you shall call His name Jesus; for He shall save His people from their_[ __sins__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). Also in that according to Luke:  _And Zacharias was filled with the_[ __Holy Ghost__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) _, and prophesied, saying, Blessed be the Lord God of_[ __Israel__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, who has foreseen redemption for His people, and has raised up an horn of_[ __salvation__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) _for us in the house of His servant David._ Also in the same. place, the [ _angel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) said to the shepherds:  _Fear not; for, behold, I bring you tidings that unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ Jesus_.

8\. That although from the beginning He had been the Son of God, yet He had to be begotten again according to the flesh
