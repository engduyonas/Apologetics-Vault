# EUSEBIUS ON THE TRUE & ANCIENT FAITH

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Dec 16, 2025
  * 6 min read


In this post I will quote from Eusebius’ _History of Church_ , written around 312-324 AD, where he appeals to the writers, apologists, theologians, fathers, etc. of the Church to prove that the holy Apostles and their successors have always taught that Jesus Christ is God who became man. He will reference apologists and martyrs such as Clement, Irenaeus, Justin and others whose writings he had consulted to prove how they all affirmed the Deity of the Lord Jesus Christ.

  


Eusebius also mentions how the heretics were changing the holy Scriptures in order to make them agree with their heresies and false doctrines. Thankfully, true believers were copying the sacred writings as handed down to them from the Apostles and their companions, in order to preserve the true readings of the God-breathed Scriptures.

All emphasis will be mine. 

  


Chapter 28. Those who first advanced the Heresy of Artemon; their Manner of Life, and how they dared to corrupt the Sacred Scriptures.

  


1\. In a laborious work by one of these writers against the [ _heresy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of Artemon, which [ _Paul of Samosata_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11589a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) attempted to revive again in our day, there is an account appropriate to the history which we are now examining.

  


2\. For he criticises, as a late innovation, the above-mentioned [ _heresy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) which teaches that the Saviour was a mere man, because they were attempting to magnify it as ancient. Having given in his work many other arguments in refutation of their [ _blasphemous_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) [ _falsehood_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , he adds the following words:

  


3\. For they say that all the early teachers and the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) received and taught what they now declare, and that the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) was preserved until the times of Victor, who was the thirteenth [ _bishop of Rome_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12260a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) from Peter, but that from his successor, [ _Zephyrinus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15756c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) had been corrupted.

  


4\. And what they say might be plausible, **if first of all the**[** _Divine Scriptures_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**did not contradict them**. And there are writings of certain brethren older than the times of Victor, which they wrote in behalf of the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) against the [ _heathen_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and against the [ _heresies_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) which [ _existed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) in their day. **I refer to Justin and Miltiades and**[** _Tatian_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14464b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**and Clement and many others, in all of whose works CHRIST IS SPOKEN OF AS GOD**.

5\. For who does not [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) **the works of**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**and of**[** _Melito_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10166b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**and of others which teach THAT CHRIST IS GOD AND MAN?** **And how many psalms and**[** _hymns_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07595a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , written by the faithful brethren from the beginning, **celebrate Christ the**[** _Word of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, speaking of him as Divine**.

  


6\. How then since the opinion held by the [** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) has been preached for so many years, can its preaching have been delayed as they affirm, until the times of Victor? And how is it that they are not ashamed to speak thus [** _falsely_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of Victor, [** _knowing_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) well that he cut off from communion Theodotus, the cobbler, the leader and father of this God-denying [** _apostasy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01624b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and the first to declare that [** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is mere man? For if Victor agreed with their opinions, as their [** _slander_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14035b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) affirms, how came he to cast out Theodotus, the inventor of this [** _heresy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)?

  


7\. So much in regard to Victor. His [** _bishopric_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) lasted ten years, and [** _Zephyrinus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15756c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) was appointed his successor about the ninth year of the reign of Severus. The author of the above-mentioned book, concerning the founder of this [** _heresy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , narrates another event which occurred in the time of [** _Zephyrinus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15756c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , using these words:

8\. I will remind many of the brethren of a fact which took place in our time, which, had it happened in [** _Sodom_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , might, I think, have [** _proved_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) a warning to them. There was a certain confessor, Natalius, not long ago, but in our own day.

  


9\. This man was deceived at one time by Asclepiodotus and another Theodotus, a money-changer. Both of them were [** _disciples_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of Theodotus, the cobbler, who, as I have said, was the first person [** _excommunicated_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05678a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) by Victor, [** _bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) at that time, on account of this sentiment, or rather senselessness.

  


10\. Natalius was persuaded by them to allow himself to be chosen [** _bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of this [** _heresy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) with a salary, to be paid by them, of one hundred and fifty denarii a month.

11\. When he had thus connected himself with them, **he was warned oftentimes by the Lord through visions. For the compassionate God and**[** _our Lord Jesus Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**was not willing that a**[** _witness_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**of his own sufferings, being cast out of the**[** _Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, should perish**.

  


12\. **But as he paid little regard to the visions** , because he was ensnared by the first position among them and by that shameful [** _covetousness_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04462a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) which destroys a great many, **he was scourged by**[** _holy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)****[**_angels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, and punished severely through the entire night**. Thereupon having risen in the morning, he put on sackcloth and covered himself with ashes, and with great haste and tears he fell down before [** _Zephyrinus_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15756c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , the [** _bishop_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , rolling at the feet not only of the [** _clergy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04049b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , but also of the [** _laity_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08748a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; and he moved with his tears the compassionate Church of the merciful Christ. And though he used much supplication, and showed the welts of the stripes which he had received, yet scarcely was he taken back into communion.

  


13\. We will add from the same writer some other extracts concerning them, which run as follows:

  


**They have treated the**[** _Divine Scriptures_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**recklessly and without**[** _fear_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**. They have set aside the rule of ancient**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**; and Christ they have not**[** _known_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). They do not endeavor to learn what the [** _Divine Scriptures_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html?ref=answeringislam.blog>) declare, but strive laboriously **after any form of syllogism which may be devised to sustain their impiety**. And if any one brings before them a passage of Divine Scripture, **they see whether a conjunctive or disjunctive form of syllogism can be made from it**.

  


14\. And as being of the earth and speaking of the earth, and as [** _ignorant_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of him who comes from above, **they forsake the**[** _holy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**writings of God** to devote themselves to geometry. Euclid is laboriously measured by some of them; and Aristotle and Theophrastus are admired; and Galen, perhaps, by some is even worshipped.

15\. But that those who use the arts of unbelievers for their [** _heretical_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) opinions **and adulterate the simple**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**of the**[** _Divine Scriptures_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**by the craft of the godless** , are far from the [** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , what need is there to say? Therefore **they have laid their hands boldly upon the**[** _Divine Scriptures_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, alleging that they have corrected them**.

16\. That I am not speaking [** _falsely_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of them in this matter, whoever wishes may learn. **For if any one will collect their respective copies, and compare them one with another, he will find that they differ greatly**.

  


17\. **Those of Asclepiades, for example, do not agree with those of Theodotus**. And many of these can be obtained, **because their**[** _disciples_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**have assiduously written the corrections, as they call them, that is the corruptions, of each of them**. Again, **those of Hermophilus do not agree with these, and those of Apollonides are not consistent with themselves**. For you can compare those prepared by them at an earlier date with those which they corrupted later, and you will find them widely different.

  


18\. But how daring this offense is, it is not likely that they themselves are [** _ignorant_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). **For either they do not**[** _believe_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**that the**[** _Divine Scriptures_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**were spoken by the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and thus are unbelievers, **or else they think themselves wiser than the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and in that case what else are they than [** _demoniacs_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04711a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)? For they cannot deny the commission of the crime, **since the copies have been written by their own hand** s. For they did not receive such Scriptures from their instructors, nor can they produce any copies from which they were transcribed.

  


19\. **But some of them have not thought it worth while to corrupt them, but simply deny the law and the**[** _prophets_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and thus through their lawless and impious teaching under pretense of [** _grace_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , have sunk to the lowest depths of perdition.

Let this suffice for these things. (Eusebius, _Ecclesiastical History_ , [_Book V_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250105.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>))
