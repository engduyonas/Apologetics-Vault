### **CONCLUSION**

  


The foregoing data conclusively establishes the biblical basis for the Trinity. Note how this works out logically:

  


  1. YHWH is the one and only true Elohim.

  2. The Father is identified as YHWH Elohim.

  3. The Son is identified as YHWH Elohim.

  4. The Holy Spirit is described as YHWH Elohim as well.

  5. Therefore, Father, Son and Holy Spirit are all YHWH Elohim.

  6. The Father is not the Son, and neither of them is the Holy Spirit.

  7. YHWH, therefore, isn’t a singular Person but is a singular Being who eternally exists as three distinct, yet inseparable, Persons, namely, the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. 


  


In light of this, Mormonism is incompatible with God’s inspired revelation, the Holy Bible, and therefore cannot be a legitimate branch or expression of true historic Christianity, since it rejects the Triune God revealed in inspired Scripture.

  


The fact is that Mormonism is a false religion that has a completely foreign God, embraces another Jesus, believes in a different Spirit and proclaims a perverted Gospel. As such, Joseph Smith is nothing other than another false prophet whom the Scriptures forewarned believers of (Cf. Matt. 24:23-25; 2 Cor. 11:1-4, 13-15; Gal. 1:6-9).
