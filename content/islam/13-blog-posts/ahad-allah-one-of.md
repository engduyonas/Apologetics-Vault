**REVISITING THE ISSUE OF THE TERM AHAD**

  


In the following article, [_Ahad: Monotheism vs. Eloquence of the Quran_](<https://answeringislam.info/Shamoun/ahad.htm>) , I demonstrated that the phrase found in Q. 112:1 _qul huwa Allah ahadun_ literally translates as “Say he is Allah, one of.”

  


In this post I will share some of the verses proving that the word _ahad_ does not mean one, but really means “one of”.

  


Unless indicated otherwise, quranic references will be taken from Arthur J. Arberry’s English version, titled “Koran Interpreted”.
