# ST. AMBROSE & CHRIST’S DEITY

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 18, 2025
  * 14 min read


Updated: Mar 6

The following extract is taken from St. Ambrose who refutes the claim that Christ’s begetting implies that was created. All emphasis will be mine.

  


**Chapter 7.**

  


**The likeness of Christ to the Father is asserted on the authority of St. Paul, the prophets, and the Gospel, and especially in reliance upon the creation of man in God’s image.**

  


48\. The Apostle says that [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) is the image of the Father — for he calls Him the image of the invisible [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the first-begotten of all creation. **First-begotten, mark you, not first-created** , in order that He may be [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to be both begotten, in virtue of His nature, and first in virtue of His [ _eternity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>). In another place also the Apostle has declared that [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) made the Son heir of all things, by Whom also He made the worlds, Who is the brightness of His [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) , and the express image of His substance. [ _Hebrews 1:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/heb001.htm#verse2>) **The Apostle calls Christ the image of the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and**[** _Arius_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01718a.htm>)**says that He is unlike the Father. Why, then, is He called an image, if He has no likeness?** Men will not have their portraits unlike them, and [ _Arius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01718a.htm>) contends that the Father is unlike the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and would have it that the Father has begotten one unlike Himself, as though unable to generate His like.

  


49\. The [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) say: In Your light we shall see light; and again: Wisdom is the brightness of everlasting light, and the spotless mirror of God’s majesty, the image of His goodness. [ _Wisdom 7:26_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/wis007.htm#verse26>) See what great names are declared! Brightness, because in the Son the Father’s [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) shines clearly: spotless mirror, because the Father is seen in the Son: [ _John 12:45_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh012.htm#verse45>) image of goodness, because it is not one body seen reflected in another, but the whole power [of the Godhead] in the Son. The word image teaches us that there is no difference; expression, that He is the counterpart of the Father’s form; and brightness declares His [ _eternity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>). The image in [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) is not that of a bodily countenance, not one made up of colors, nor modelled in wax, but simply derived from [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , coming out from the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , drawn from the fountainhead.

  


50\. By means of this image the Lord showed Philip the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , saying, Philip, he that sees Me, sees the Father also. How then do you say, Show us the Father? Do you not [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that I am in the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the Father in Me? [ _John 14:9-10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh014.htm#verse9>) Yes, he who looks upon the Son sees, in portrait, the Father. Mark what manner of portrait is spoken of. It is Truth, Righteousness, the Power of God: not dumb, for it is the Word; not insensible, for it is Wisdom; not vain and foolish, for it is Power; not soulless, for it is the Life; not dead, for it is the Resurrection. You see, then, that while an image is spoken of, the meaning is that it is the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , Whose image the Son is, seeing that no one can be his own image.

  


51\. More might I set down from the Son’s testimony; howbeit, lest He perchance appear to have asserted Himself overmuch, **let us enquire of the Father. For the Father said** , Let us [ _make_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm>) [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) in Our image and likeness. [ _Genesis 1:26_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse26>) **The Father says to the Son in**** _Our_****image and likeness, and you say that the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**is unlike the Father**.

52\. John says, Beloved, we are sons of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and it does not yet appear what we shall be: we [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that if He be revealed, we shall be like Him. [ _1 John 3:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo003.htm#verse2>) O blind [ _madness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08041a.htm>)! O shameless obstinacy! **We are men, and, so far as we may, we shall be in the likeness of God: dare we deny that the Son is like God?**

  


53\. Therefore the Father has said: Let us [ _make_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm>) [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) in Our image and likeness. **At the beginning of the**[** _universe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>)**itself, as I read, the Father and the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)****[**_existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , and I see one creation. I hear Him that speaks. I acknowledge Him that does: but it is of one image, one likeness, that I read. **This likeness belongs not to diversity but to unity**. What, therefore, you claim for yourself, you take from the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , seeing, indeed, that you can not be in the image of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **save by help of the image of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

**Chapter 8.**

  


**The likeness of the Son to the Father being proved, it is not hard to prove the Son’s eternity, though, indeed, this may be established on the authority of the Prophet Isaiah and St. John the Evangelist, by which authority the heretical leaders are shown to be refuted.**

  


54\. It is plain, therefore, that the Son is not unlike the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **and so we may confess the more readily that He is also**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**, seeing that He Who is like the Eternal must needs be**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>). But if we say that the Father is [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) , and yet deny this of the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , **we say that the Son is unlike the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, for the temporal differs from the**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**. The Prophet proclaims Him**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**, and the Apostle proclaims Him**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**; the Testaments, Old and New alike, are full of**[** _witness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>)**to the Son’s**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>).

  


55\. Let us take them, then, in their order. In the [ _Old Testament_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14526a.htm>) — to cite one out of a multitude of testimonies — it is written: Before Me has there been no other [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and after Me shall there be none. [ _Isaiah 43:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa043.htm#verse10>) I will not comment on this place, but ask you straight: Who speaks these words — the Father or the Son? Whichever of the two you say, you will find yourself convinced, or, if a believer, instructed. Who, then, speaks these words, the Father or the Son? If it is the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , He says, Before Me has there been no other [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; if the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , He says, After Me shall there be none. The One has none before Him, the Other none that comes after; as the Father is [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) in the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , so also is the Son [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) in the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for whenever you speak of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , you speak also by implication of His Son, seeing that none is his own father; and when you name the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , you do also acknowledge His Father, inasmuch as none can be his own son. And so neither can the Son exist without the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , nor the Father without the Son. The Father, therefore, is [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) , and the Son also [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>).

  


56\. In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Was, mark you, with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **Was— see, we have was four times over. Where did the blasphemer find it written that He was not**. Again, John, in another passage — in his Epistle — speaks of That which was in the beginning. [ _1 John 1:1_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo001.htm#verse1>) T**he extension of the was is**[** _infinite_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08004a.htm>)**. Conceive any length of time you will, yet still the Son was**.

  


57\. Now in this short passage our fisherman has barred the way of all [ _heresy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). For that which was in the beginning is not comprehended in time, is not preceded by any beginning. Let [ _Arius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01718a.htm>) , therefore, hold his peace. Moreover, that which was with God is not confounded and mingled with Him, but is distinguished by the perfection unblemished which it has as the Word abiding with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; **and so let Sabellius keep silence. And the Word was God**. This Word, therefore, consists not in uttered speech, but in the designation of celestial excellence, so that Photinus’ teaching is refuted. Furthermore, by the fact that in the beginning He was with God **is proven the indivisible unity of**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**Godhead in Father and Son, to the shame and confusion of Eunomius**. Lastly, seeing that all things are said to have been made by Him, **He is plainly shown to be author of the Old and of the**[** _New Testament_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>)**alike** ; so that the [ _Manichæan_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09591a.htm>) can find no ground for his assaults. Thus has the good fisherman caught them all in one net, to make them powerless to deceive, albeit unprofitable fish to take.

  


**Chapter 9.**

  


**St. Ambrose questions the heretics and exhibits their answer, which is, that the Son existed, indeed, before all time, yet was not co-eternal with the Father, whereat the Saint shows that they represent the Godhead as changeable, and further, that each Person must be believed to be eternal.**

  


58\. Tell me, thou [ _heretic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) — for the surpassing clemency of the Emperor grants me this indulgence of addressing you for a short space, not that I desire to confer with you, or am greedy to hear your arguments, but because I am willing to exhibit them — **tell me, I say, whether there was ever a time when God Almighty was not the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and yet was God**. I say nothing about time, is your answer. Well and subtly objected! **For if you bring time into the dispute, you will condemn yourself** , seeing that you must acknowledge that there was a time when the Son was not, **whereas the Son is the ruler and creator of time. He cannot have begun to exist after His own work**. You, therefore, must needs allow Him to be the ruler and maker of His work.

  


59\. I do not say, do you answer, that the Son [ _existed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) not before time; but when I call Him Son, I declare that His Father [ _existed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) before Him, **for, as you say, father exists before son. But what means this?** **You deny that time was before the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, and yet you will have it that something preceded the**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**of the Son — some creature of time** — and you show certain stages of generation intervening, **whereby thou dost give us to understand that the generation from the Father was a process in time. For if He began to be a Father, then, in the first instance, He was**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and afterwards He became a Father. How, then, is God unchangeable? For if He was first**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and then the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, surely He has undergone change by reason of the added and later act of generation**.

  


60\. But may God preserve us from this [ _madness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08041a.htm>) ; for it was but to confute the impiety of the [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) that we brought in this question. **The devout spirit affirms a generation that is not in time, and so declares Father and Son to be co-eternal, and does not maintain that God has ever suffered change**.

  


61\. **Let Father and Son, therefore, be associated in worship, even as They are associated in Godhead** ; let not [ _blasphemy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) put asunder those whom the close bond of generation has joined together. Let us [ _honour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , that we may [ _honour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) the Father also, as it is written in the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>). [ _John 5:23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh005.htm#verse23>) The Son’s [ _eternity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) is the adornment of the Father’s majesty. If the Son has not been from everlasting, **then the Father has suffered change** ; but the Son is from all [ _eternity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) , **therefore has the Father never changed** , for He is always unchangeable. **And thus we see that they who would deny the Son’s**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**would teach that the Father is mutable**.

  


**Chapter 10.**

  


**Christ’s eternity being proved from the Apostle’s teaching, St. Ambrose admonishes us that the Divine Generation is not to be thought of after the fashion of human procreation, nor to be too curiously pried into. With the difficulties thence arising he refuses to deal, saying that whatsoever terms, taken from our knowledge of body, are used in speaking of this Divine Generation, must be understood with a spiritual meaning.**

  


62\. Hear now another argument, showing clearly the [ _eternity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) of the Son. **The Apostle says that God’s Power and Godhead are**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**, and that**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**is the Power of God — for it is written that**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**is the Power of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**and the Wisdom of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**. If, then, Christ is the Power of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, it follows that, forasmuch as God’s Power is**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**, Christ also is**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>).

  


63\. **You can not, then,**[**_heretic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**, build up a**[** _false doctrine_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**from the custom of**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**procreation** , nor yet gather the wherewithal for such work from our discourse, **for we cannot compass the greatness of**[** _infinite_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08004a.htm>)**Godhead, of Whose greatness there is no end, in our straitened speech**. If you should seek to give an account of a man’s birth, you must needs point to a time. **But the Divine Generation is above all things; it reaches far and wide, it rises high above all thought and feeling**. For it is written: No man comes to the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , save by Me. [ _John 14:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh014.htm#verse6>) Whatsoever, therefore, thou dost conceive concerning the Father — **yea, be it even His**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**— you can not conceive anything concerning Him save by the Son’s aid, nor can any understanding ascend to the Father save through the Son**. This is My dearly-beloved [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , the Father says. Is mark you — He Who is, what He is, forever. Hence also David is moved to say: O Lord, Your Word abides for ever in heaven, — for what abides fails neither in [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) nor in [ _eternity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>).

  


64\. Do you ask me how He is a Son, if He have not a Father existing before Him? **I ask of you, in turn, when, or how, do you think that the Son was begotten. For me the**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**of the**[** _mystery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)**of His generation is more than I can attain to, — the mind fails, the voice is dumb — ay, and not mine alone, but the**[** _angels’_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**also. It is above Powers, above Angels, above Cherubim, Seraphim, and all that has feeling and thought, for it is written: The peace of**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, which passes all understanding. If the peace of Christ passes all understanding, how can so wondrous a generation but be above all understanding?**

  


65\. Do thou, then (like the [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)), cover your face with your hands, for it is not given you to look into surpassing [ _mysteries_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)! **We are suffered to**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**that the Son is begotten, not to dispute upon the manner of His begetting. I cannot deny the one; the other I**[** _fear_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>)**to search into** , for if [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) says that the words which he heard when caught up into the third heaven might not be uttered, [ _2 Corinthians 12:2-5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2co012.htm#verse2>) how can we explain the secret of this generation from and of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , which we can neither hear nor attain to with our understanding?

  


66\. But if you will constrain me to the rule of [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) generation, that you may be allowed to say that the Father [ _existed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) before the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , **then consider whether instances, taken from the generation of earthly creatures, are suitable to show forth the Divine Generation**. If we speak according to what is customary among men, you cannot deny that, in man, **the changes in the father’s**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**happen before those in the son’s. The father is the first to grow, to enter old age, to grieve, to weep**. If, then, the son is after him in time, he is older in experience than the son. If the child comes to be born, the parent escapes not the shame of begetting.

  


67\. Why take such delight in that rack of questioning? You hear the name of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) ; abolish it, then, or acknowledge His [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) nature. **You hear speak of the womb — acknowledge the**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**of undoubted begetting.** **Of His heart —**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**that here is God’s word. Of His right hand — confess His power. Of His face — acknowledge His wisdom. These words are not to be understood, when we speak of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, as when we speak of bodies. The generation of the Son is incomprehensible, the Father begets impassibly, and yet of Himself and in ages inconceivably remote has very God begotten very God**. The Father loves the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , [ _John 5:20_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh005.htm#verse20>) and you anxiously examine His Person; the Father is well pleased in Him, you, joining the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , look upon Him with an [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) eye; the Father [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and you join the [ _heathen_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>) in reviling Him. [ _Luke 23:36-37_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk023.htm#verse36>)

  


**Chapter 11.**

  


**It cannot be proved from Scripture that the Father existed before the Son, nor yet can arguments taken from human reproduction avail to this end, since they bring in absurdities without end. To dare to affirm that Christ began to exist in the course of time is the height of blasphemy.**

  


68\. You ask me whether it is possible that He Who is the Father should not be prior in [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>). I ask you to tell me when the Father [ _existed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , the Son as yet being not; prove this, gather it from argument or evidence of Scripture. If you lean upon arguments, **you have doubtless been taught that God’s power is**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>). Again, you have read the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) that says: O [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) , if you will hearken unto Me, there shall be no new God in you, neither shall you worship a strange God. **The first of these commands betokens [the Son’s]**[**_eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**, the second His possession of an identical nature, so that we can neither**[** _believe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)**Him to have come into**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**after the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, nor suppose Him the Son of another Divinity. For if He**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**not always with the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, He is a new [God]; if He is not of one Divinity with the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, He is a strange [God]**. But He is not after the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for He is not a new [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; nor is He a strange [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for He is begotten of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **and because, as it is written, He is**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**above all, blessed forever.**[**_Romans 9:5_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom009.htm#verse5>)

  


69\. But if the [ _Arians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01707c.htm>) [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) Him to be a strange [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , why do they worship Him, when it is written: You shall worship no strange God? Else, if they do not worship the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , let them confess thereto, and the case is at an end — that they deceive no one by their professions of religion. This, then, we see, is the [ _witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>). If you have any others to produce, it will be your business to do so.

  


70\. Let us now go further, and gather the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) in conclusion from arguments. For although arguments usually give place, even to [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) evidence, still, [ _heretic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , argue as you will. **Experience teaches us, you say, that the being which generates is prior to that which is generated. I answer: Follow our customary experience through all its departments, and if the rest agree herewith** , I oppose not your claim that your point be granted; but if there be no such agreement, how can you claim assent on this one point, when in all the rest you lack support? **Seeing, then, that you call for what is customary, it comes about that the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**, when He was begotten of the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, was a little child. You have seen Him an infant, crying in the cradle. As the years passed, He has gone forward from strength to strength — for if He was weak with the weakness of things begotten, He must also have fallen under the weakness, not only of birth, but of life also**.

  


71\. But perchance you run to such a pitch of folly as not to flinch from asserting these things of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , measuring Him, as you do, by the rule of [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) infirmity. What, then, if, while you cannot refuse Him the name of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , you are bent to prove Him, by reason of weakness, to be a man? What if, while you examine the Person of the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , you are calling the Father in question, and while you hastily pass sentence upon the Former, you include the Latter in the same condemnation!

  


72\. If the Divine Generation has been subject to the limits of time — if we suppose this, **borrowing from the custom of**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**generation, then it follows, further, that the Father bare the Son in a bodily womb, and laboured under the burden while ten months sped their courses. But how can generation, as it commonly takes place, be brought about without the help of the other sex? You see that the common order of generation was not the commencement, and you think that the courses of generation, which are ruled by certain necessities whereunto bodies are subject, have always prevailed**. You require the customary course, I ask for difference of sex: you demand the supposition of time, I that of order: you enquire into the end, I into the beginning. Now surely it is the end that depends on the beginning, not the beginning on the end.

  


73\. Everything, say you, that is begotten has a beginning, and therefore because the Son is the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , He has a beginning, and came first into [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) within limits of time. Let this be taken as the word of their own mouth; as for myself, I confess that the Son is begotten, but the rest of their declaration makes me shudder. Man, do you confess [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and diminish His [ _honour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) by such [ _slander_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14035b.htm>)? From this [ _madness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08041a.htm>) may God deliver us. (Exposition of the Christian Faith, [_Book I_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213849/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/34041.htm>))
