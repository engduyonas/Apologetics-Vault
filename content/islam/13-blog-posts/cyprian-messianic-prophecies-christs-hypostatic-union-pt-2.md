I cite from the rest of St. Cyprian’s treatise where he quotes verse after verse from both testaments to prove that the prophets announced our Lord’s Deity, Humanity, vicarious death, resurrection, physical, heavenly ascension, physical, bodily return to the earth to judge all flesh, and his spiritual marriage to his spiritual Bride, the Church. 30\. That He Himself is both Judge and King

**In the seventy-first Psalm:****_O_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, give Your judgment to the king, and Your righteousness to the king's son, to judge Your people in righteousness_**.
