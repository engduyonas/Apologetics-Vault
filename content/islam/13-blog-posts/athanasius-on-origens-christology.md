# Athanasius on Origen’s Christology

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Dec 21, 2025
  * 8 min read


In his refutation to the Arians, the beloved saint and outstanding defenders of the most Glorious and Holy Trinity, St. Athanasius lists Origen among those who held to an orthodox of the Trinity and Deity of Christ. All emphasis will be mine. 

Chapter 6.— Authorities in Support of the Council.  _Theognostus; Dionysius of Alexandria; Dionysius of Rome; Origen._

25\. **This then is the sense in which they who met at Nicæa made use of these expressions**. But next that **they did not invent them for themselves (since this is one of their excuses),****_but spoke what they had received from their predecessors_****, proceed we to prove this also** , to cut off even this excuse from them. Know then, **O**[** _Arians_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01707c.htm>)**, foes of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , that Theognostus, a learned man, did not decline the phrase 'of the [ _essence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) ,' for in the second book of his _Hypotyposes_ , he writes thus of the Son: —

 __

The [ _essence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) of the Son i**s not one procured from without, nor accruing out of nothing , but it sprang from the Father's**[** _essence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**, as the radiance of light, as the vapour of water** ; for neither the radiance, nor the vapour, is the water itself or the sun itself, **nor is it alien** ; but it is an effluence of the Father's [ _essence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , **which, however, suffers no partition**. For as the sun remains the same, and is not impaired by the rays poured forth by it, so neither does the Father's [ _essence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) suffer change, though it has the Son as an Image of Itself. 

Theognostus then, after previously investigating in the way of an exercise, proceeds to lay down his sentiments in the foregoing words. Next, Dionysius, who was Bishop of Alexandria, **upon his writing against Sabellius** and expounding at large the Saviour's Economy according to the flesh, and thence proving against the Sabellians **that not the Father but His Word became flesh, as John has said** , was suspected of saying that the Son as a thing made and originated, and not one in [ _essence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) with the Father; on this he writes to his namesake **Dionysius,**[**_Bishop of Rome_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12260a.htm>)**, to allege in his defense that this was a**[** _slander_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14035b.htm>)**upon him. And he assured him that he had not called the Son made, nay, did confess Him to be even one in**[** _essence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>). And his words ran thus:—

 _And I have written in another letter a refutation of the false charge they bring against me, that I deny that Christ was one in_[ __essence__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) _with_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _._**__For though I say that I have not found this term anywhere in__**[** __Holy Scripture__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) _, yet my remarks which follow, and which they have not noticed,_**__are not inconsistent with that belief__** _. For I instanced_[ __human__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) _birth as being evidently homogeneous, and I observed that undeniably_[ __parents__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) _differed from their children only in not being the same individuals, otherwise there could be neither_[ __parents__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) _nor children. And my letter, as I said before, owing to present circumstances I am unable to produce; or I would have sent you the very words I used, or rather a copy of it all, which, if I have an opportunity, I will do still. But I am sure from recollection that I adduced parallels of things kindred with each other;_**_for instance, that a plant grown from seed or from root, was other than that from which it sprang, yet was altogether one in nature with it_** _: and that a stream flowing from a fountain, gained a new name, for that neither the fountain was called stream, nor the stream fountain,_**_and both_**[** __existed__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)** _, and the stream was the water from the fountain_**.

26\. **And that the**[** _Word of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**is not a work or creature, but an offspring proper to the Father's**[** _essence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**and indivisible, as the great Council wrote** , here you may see **_in the words of Dionysius,_**[**_Bishop of Rome_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12260a.htm>) , who, while writing against the Sabellians, thus inveighs against those who dared to say so:—

 _Next, I may reasonably turn to those who divide and cut to pieces and destroy_** _that most sacred doctrine of the_**[** __Church of God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _, the Divine Monarchy, making it as it were three powers and partitive subsistences and god-heads three_** _. I am told that some among you who are catechists and teachers of the Divine Word, take the lead in this tenet,_**_who are diametrically opposed, so to speak, to Sabellius's opinions; for he_**[** __blasphemously__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>)** _says that the Son is the_**[** __Father__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, and the Father the_**[** __Son__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _, but they in some sort preach three Gods, as dividing the sacred_**[** __Monad__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10447b.htm>)** _into three subsistences foreign to each other and utterly separate. For it must needs be_**** __that with the__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __of the Universe, the Divine Word is united, and the__**[** __Holy Ghost__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** __must repose and habitate in__**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _; thus in one as in a summit, I mean the_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _of the Universe,_**_must the Divine Triad be gathered up and brought together_** _. For it is the doctrine of the presumptuous_[ __Marcion__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) _, to sever and divide the Divine Monarchy into three origins —_** _a_**[** __devil's__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>)** _teaching, not that of_**[** __Christ's__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** __**[**__true__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** __**[**__disciples__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)** _and lovers of the Saviour's lessons._****__For they__**[** __know__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** __well that a Triad is preached BY DIVINE SCRIPTURE, but that neither__**[** __Old Testament__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14526a.htm>)** __nor New preaches three Gods. Equally must one censure those who hold the Son to be a work, and consider that the Lord has come into being, as one of things which really came to be; whereas THE DIVINE ORACLES__**[** __witness__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>)** __to a generation suitable to Him and becoming, but not to any fashioning or making__**** _. A_**[** __blasphemy__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>)** _then is it, not ordinary, but even the highest, to say that the Lord is in any sort a handiwork. For if He came to be Son, once He was not_** _; but He was always, if (that is) He be in the_[ __Father__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, as He says Himself,_**_and if the Christ be Word and Wisdom and Power (which, as you_**[** __know__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _, DIVINE SCRIPTURE says), and these attributes be powers of_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _._****__If then the Son came into being, once these attributes were not; consequently there was a time, when God was without them; which is most absurd__** _._

And why say more on these points to you, men full of the Spirit and well aware of the absurdities which come to view from saying that the Son is a work? Not attending, as I consider, to this circumstance, the authors of this opinion **have entirely missed the**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**, in explaining, contrary to the sense of divine and prophetic Scripture in the passage, the words** , _'The Lord created me a beginning of His ways unto His works_[ _Proverbs 8:22_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/pro008.htm#verse22>).' For the sense of 'He created,' as you [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , is not one, for we must understand _'He created'_ in this place, as 'He set over the works made by Him,' that is, 'made by the Son Himself.' And _'He created'_ here must not be taken for 'made,' for creating differs from making. 'Is not He your Father that has bought you? Has He not made you and created you [ _Deuteronomy 32:6_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu032.htm#verse6>)?'says [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) in his great song in Deuteronomy. And one may say to them, O reckless men, is He a work, who is _'the First-born of every creature, who is born from the womb before the morning star,'_ who said, as Wisdom, _'Before all the hills He begets me_[ _Proverbs 8:25_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/pro008.htm#verse25>)?' And in many passages of the divine oracles is the Son said to have been generated, **_but nowhere to have come into being_** ; which manifestly convicts those of misconception about the Lord's generation, who presume to call His divine and ineffable generation a making. Neither then may we divide into three Godheads the wonderful and divine [ _Monad_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10447b.htm>) ; nor disparage with the name of 'work' the dignity and exceeding majesty of the Lord; but we must [ _believe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father Almighty, and in Christ Jesus His Son, and in the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , and hold that to the [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) of the [ _universe_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>) the Word is united. For _'I,'_ says He, _'and the Father are one;'_ and _, 'I in the Father and the Father in Me.'_**For thus both the Divine Triad, and the**[** _holy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>)**preaching of the Monarchy, will be preserved**.

27\. **And concerning the everlasting co-existence of the Word with the**[** _Father_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and that He is not of another**[** _essence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**or subsistence, but proper to the Father's, as the Bishops in the Council said, YOU MAY HEAR AGAIN FROM THE LABOUR-LOVING ORIGEN ALSO**. For what he has written as if inquiring and by way of exercise, that let no one take as expressive of his own sentiments, but of parties who are contending in investigation, but what he definitely declares, that is the sentiment of the labour-loving man. After his prolusions then (so to speak) against the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , straightway he introduces his personal belief, thus:—

 _If there be an Image of the Invisible_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, it is an invisible Image;_**_nay, I will be bold to add, that, as being the likeness of the_**[** __Father__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, never was it not_** _. For when was that_[ __God__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, who, according to John, is called Light (for 'God is Light'), without a radiance of His proper_[ __glory__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _, that a man should presume to assert the Son's origin of_[ __existence__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) _, as if before He was not? But when was not that Image of the Father's Ineffable and Nameless and Unutterable subsistence, that Expression and Word, and He that_[ __knows__](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _the Father?_**_For let him understand well who dares to say, 'Once the Son was not,' that he is saying, 'Once Wisdom was not,' and 'Word was not,' and 'Life was not_** _.'_

And again elsewhere he says:—

 __

But it is not innocent nor without peril, if because of our weakness of understanding we deprive [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , as far as in us lies, **of the Only-begotten Word ever co-existing with Him** ; and the Wisdom in which He rejoiced; else He must be conceived as not always possessed of [ _joy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>).

**See, we are proving that this view has been transmitted from father to father** ; but you, O modern [ _Jews_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) and [ _disciples_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of [ _Caiaphas_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03143b.htm>) , how many fathers can you assign to your phrases? Not one of the understanding and wise; **_for all abhor you, but the_**[** _devil_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>)** _alone_****; none but he is your father in this**[** _apostasy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01624b.htm>)**,** who both in the beginning sowed you with the seed of this irreligion, and now persuades you to [ _slander_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14035b.htm>) the Ecumenical Council , for committing to writing, not your doctrines, **but that which from the beginning those who were eye-witnesses and ministers of the Word have handed down to us.****_For the_**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)** _which the Council has confessed in writing, that is the_**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)** _of the_**[** _Catholic_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)** __**[**_Church_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _; to assert this, the blessed Fathers so expressed themselves while condemning the_**[** _Arian_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01707c.htm>)** __**[**_heresy_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) ; and this is a chief reason why these apply themselves to [ _calumniate_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03190c.htm>) the Council. For it is not the terms which trouble them, but that those terms prove them to be [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , and presumptuous beyond other [ _heresies_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). ([_De Decretis_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2809.htm>))
