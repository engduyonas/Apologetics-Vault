# Hilary: God is the Trinity Pt. 2

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * 1 day ago
  * 13 min read


This post is a continuation of my previous one from blessed St. Hilary of Poitier’s work [ _On the Trinity_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3302.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , [ _Book VII_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/330207.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) : [**_Hilary: God is the Trinity_**](<https://answeringislam.blog/hilary-god-is-the-trinity/>).

  


Here I provide more quotes from that same section showing how this holy saint confirmed that the phrase “one God” does not refer to or mean the Father, As the citations will prove, St. Hilary taught that the expression refers the whole Trinity, and not to any one single individual Person of the Godhead. All emphasis will be mine.

  


2\. We call to mind that in the preceding books the reader has been urged to study the whole of that [ _blasphemous_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) manifesto , and mark how it is animated throughout by the one aim of propagating the belief that [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is neither [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , nor [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). Its authors argue that He is permitted to use the names of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) and of Son by virtue of a certain adoption, though neither Godhead nor Sonship be His by nature. They use the fact, [** _true_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _in itself, that God is immutable and incorporeal_** , as an argument against the birth of the Son from Him. They value the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , that [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) the Father is One, only as a weapon against our [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) in the Godhead of Christ; **pleading that an incorporeal nature cannot be rationally conceived as generating another** , and that our [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) in One God is inconsistent with the confession of God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). **But our earlier books have already refuted and foiled this argument of theirs**** _by an appeal to the Law and the Prophets_**. Our defense has followed, step by step, the course of their attack. We have set forth God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and at the same time confessed One [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; showing that this presentation of the [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) neither falls short of the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) by ascribing singleness of Person to the One [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , nor adds to the [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) by asserting the [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of a second Deity. **For we confess neither an isolated**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, nor yet two Gods. Thus, neither denying that God is One**** _nor maintaining that He is alone_****, we hold the straight road of**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**. Each Divine Person is in the Unity,****_YET NO PERSON IS THE ONE GOD_**. Next, our purpose being to demonstrate the irrefragable [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of this [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) by the evidence of the Evangelists and Apostles, our first duty has been to make our readers acquainted with the nature, [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) subsisting and [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) born, of the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; to demonstrate **_that He has no origin external to_**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, and was not created out of nothing_** , but is the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , born from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). This is a [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) which the evidence adduced in the last book has placed beyond all [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). The assertion that He bears the name of Son by virtue of adoption has been put to silence, and He stands forth as a [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) Son by a [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) birth. Our present task **is to prove from the**[** _Gospels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**that, because He is**[** _true_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**Son, He is**[** _true_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**God also**. For unless He be [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) Son He cannot be [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , nor [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) God unless He be [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) Son…

  


9\. Thus we have all these different assurances of the Divinity of [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) :— His name, His birth, His nature, His power, His own assertion. As to the name, I conceive that no [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is possible. It is written, ** _In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, and the Word was God_**. ** _John 1:1_** What reason can there be for suspecting that He is not what His name indicates? **And does not this name clearly describe His nature?** If a statement be contradicted, it must be for some reason. What reason, I demand, is there in this instance for denying that He is God? The name is given Him, plainly and distinctly, **and unqualified by any incongruous addition which might raise a**[** _doubt_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). The Word, we read, which was made flesh, was none other than God. **Here is no loophole for any such conjecture as that He has received this name as a favour or taken it upon Himself, so possessing a titular Godhead which is not His by nature**.

  


10\. Consider the other recorded instances in which this name was given by favour or assumed. To [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) it was said, ** _I have made you a god to_**[** __Pharaoh__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11788c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). ** _Exodus 7:1_** Does not this addition, ** _to_**[** __Pharaoh__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11788c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , account for the title? Did God impart to [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) the Divine nature? Did He not rather make [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) a god in the sight of [ _Pharaoh_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11788c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , who was to be smitten with terror when [ _Moses’_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) serpent swallowed the magic serpents and returned into a rod, when he drove back the venomous flies which he had called forth, when he stayed the hail by the same power wherewith he had summoned it, and made the locusts depart by the same might which had brought them; when in the wonders that he wrought the magicians saw the finger of God? That was the sense in which [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) was appointed to be god to [ _Pharaoh_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11788c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; he was feared and entreated, he chastised and healed. **It is one thing to be appointed a god; it is another thing to be God**. He was made a god to [ _Pharaoh_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11788c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; he had not that nature and that name wherein God consists. **I call to mind another instance of the name being given as a title; that where it is written,****_I have said, You are gods_****. But this is obviously the granting of a favour**. ** _I have said_** proves that it is no definition, but only a description by One Who chooses to speak thus. A definition gives us [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the object defined; a description depends on the arbitrary will of the speaker. When a speaker is manifestly conferring a title, that title has its origin only in the speaker’s words, not in the thing itself. The title is not the name which expresses its nature and kind.

  


11\. But in this case the ** _Word_** in very [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; **the**[** _essence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**of the Godhead exists in the Word, and that**[** _essence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**is expressed in the Word’s name. For the name Word is inherent in the**[** _Son of God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)******_as a consequence of His_**[** _mysterious_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _birth_****, as are also the names**** _Wisdom_****and**** _Power_**. These, together with the substance which is His by a [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) birth, were called into [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) to be the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; yet, since they are the elements of God’s nature, they are still immanent in Him in undiminished extent, **although they were born from Him to be His**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). For, as we have said so often, the [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) which we preach is that of a Son Who owes His [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) not to division but to birth. He is not a segment cut off, and so incomplete, but an Offspring born, and therefore perfect; **for birth involves no diminution of the Begetter, and has the possibility of perfection for the Begotten**. And therefore the titles of those substantive properties are applied to God the Only-begotten, for when He came into [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) by birth it was they which constituted His perfection; **and this although they did not thereby desert the**[** _Father_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, in Whom, by the immutability of His nature, they are**[** _eternally_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**present. For instance, the Word is God the Only-begotten,** ** _and yet the Unbegotten Father IS NEVER WITHOUT HIS WORD_**. Not that the [ _nature_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the Son is that of a sound which is uttered. He is God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , subsisting through a [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) birth; God’s own Son, born from the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , **indistinguishable from Him in nature, and therefore inseparable. This is the lesson which His title of the Word is meant to teach us**. And in the same way Christ is the Wisdom and the Power of [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; not that He is, as He is often regarded , the inward activity of the Father’s might or thought, but that His nature, possessing through birth a [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) substantial [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , is indicated by these names of inward forces. For an object, which has by birth an [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of its own, cannot be regarded as a property; a property is necessarily inherent in some being and can have no independent [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). But it was to save us from concluding that the Son is alien from the Divine nature of His Father that He, the Only-begotten from the [ _eternal_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) God His Father, born as God into a substantial [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of His own, has had Himself revealed to us under these names of properties, of which the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , out of Whom He came into [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , has suffered no diminution. **Thus He, being**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, is nothing else than God**. For when I hear the words, ** _And the Word was_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , they do not merely tell me that the Son was called [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; they reveal to my understanding that He is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). In those previous instances, where [ _Moses_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) was called god and others were styled gods, **there was the mere addition of a name by way of title**. Here a solid essential [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is stated; ** _The Word was God_**. That ** _was_** indicates no accidental title, **but an**[** _eternal_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**reality, a permanent element of His**[** _existence_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, an inherent character of His nature**.

  


12\. And now let us see whether the confession of Thomas the Apostle, when he cried, ** _My Lord and My_**[** __God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _,_** corresponds with this assertion of the Evangelist. We see that he speaks of Him, Whom he confesses to be [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , as ** _My God_**. **Now Thomas was undoubtedly familiar with those words of the Lord,****_Hear, O_**[** __Israel__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, the Lord your God is One_**. How then could the [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of an Apostle become so oblivious of that primary command as to confess Christ as [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , when life is conditional upon the confession of the Divine unity? It was because, in the light of the Resurrection, the whole [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) had become visible to the Apostle. He had often heard such words as, ** _I and the Father are One_** , and, ** _All things that the Father has are Mine_** , and, ** _I in the Father and the Father in Me_** ; **and now he can confess that the name of God expresses the**[** _nature_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, without peril to the**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). Without breach of loyalty to the One [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , his devotion could now regard the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) as [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , since he [ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) that everything contained in the [ _nature_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the Son was [ _truly_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the same nature with the Father. **_No longer need he_**[** _fear_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _that such a confession as his was the proclamation of a second_**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, a treason against the unity of the Divine nature;****_for it was not a second God Whom that perfect birth of the Godhead had brought into_**[** _being_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). Thus it was with full [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) that Thomas confessed his Lord and his God. It was not a title of [ _honour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; **_it was a confession of nature_**. He [ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) that Christ was God in substance and in power. **And the Lord, in turn, shows that this act of worship was the expression not of mere reverence, but of**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, when He says** , ** _Because you have seen, you have_**[** __believed__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _; blessed are they which have not seen, and have_**[** __believed__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). For Thomas had seen before he [ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). But, you ask, **_What was it that Thomas_**[** __believed__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _?_** That, beyond a [ _doubt_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05141a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , which is expressed in his words, ** _My Lord and my God_**. No nature but that of God could have risen by its own might from death to life; and it is this fact, that [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , which was confessed by Thomas with the confidence of an assured [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). Shall we, then, dream that His name of God is not a substantial reality, when that name has been proclaimed by a [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) based upon certain evidence? Surely a Son devoted to His Father, One Who did not His own will but the [ _will_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of Him that sent Him, Who sought not His own [ _glory_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) but the [ _glory_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of Him from Whom He came, would have rejected the adoration involved in such a name as destructive of that unity of God which had been the burden of His teaching. Yet, in fact, He confirms this assertion of the [ _mysterious_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , made by the believing Apostle; He accepts as His own the name which belongs to the [ _nature_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of the Father. And He teaches that they are blessed who, though they have not seen Him rise from the dead, yet have [ _believed_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , on the assurance of the Resurrection, that He is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>).

  


13\. Thus the name which expresses His nature proves the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of our confession of the [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). For the name, which indicates any single substance, points out also any other substance of the same kind; **and, in this instance, there are not two substances but one substance, of the one kind**. For the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; this is the [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) expressed in His name. The one name does not embrace two Gods; **_for the one name God is the name OF ONE INDIVISBLE NATURE_****. For since the Father is God and the Son is**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, and that name**** _WHICH IS PECULIAR TO THE DIVINE NATURE IS INHERENT IN EACH_****, therefore the Two are One**. For the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , though He subsists through a birth from the Divine nature, yet preserves the unity in His name; and this birth of the Son does not compel loyal [ _believers_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) to acknowledge two Gods, **since our confession declares that Father and Son are One, both in nature and in name**. Thus the [ _Son of God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) has the Divine name as the result of His birth. Now the second step in our demonstration was to be that of showing that it is by virtue of His birth that He is [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). I have still to bring forward the evidence of the Apostles that the Divine name is used of Him in an exact sense; but for the present I purpose to continue our enquiry into the language of the [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) …

  


31\. We see how the living Son of the living Father, He Who is God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , **reveals the unity of the Divine nature, indissolubly One and the same, and the**[** _mystery_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**of His birth in these words,****_I and the Father are One_**. Because the seeming arrogance of them engendered a prejudice against Him, He made it more clear that He had spoken in the conscious possession of Divinity by saying, ** _You say that I have_**[** __blasphemed__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _because I said, I am the_**[** __Son of God__**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _;_** thus showing that the oneness of His nature with that of God was due to birth from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). And then, to clench their [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) in His birth by a positive assertion, and to guard them, at the same time, from imagining that the birth involves a difference of nature, He crowns His argument with the words, ** _Believe the works, that the Father is in Me, and I in the Father_**. Does His birth, as here revealed, display His Divinity as not His by nature, as not His own by right? Each is in the Other; the birth of the Son is from the Father only; no alien or unlike nature has been raised to Godhead and subsists as [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , [ _eternally_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) abiding, owes His Godhead to none other than God. Import, if you see your opportunity, two gods into the Church’s [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) ; separate Son from Father as far as you can, consistently with the birth which you admit; yet still the Father is in the [ _Son_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and the Son is in the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and this by no interchange of emanations but by the perfect birth of the living nature. **Thus you cannot add together**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**the Father and God the**[** _Son_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, and count Them as two Gods,****_for They Two are One God_****. You cannot confuse Them together, for They Two are not One Person**. And so the Apostolic [ _faith_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) rejects two gods; for it [ _knows_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) nothing of two Fathers or two Sons. In confessing the Father it confesses the Son; it believes in the Son in believing in the Father. **For the name of Father involves that of Son, since without having a son none can be a father**. Evidence of the [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of a son is [ _proof_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) that there has been a father, for a son cannot exist except from a father. **_When we confess that God is One WE DENY THAT HE IS SINGLE_** ; for the Son is the complement of the [ _Father_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and to the Father the Son’s [ _existence_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) is due. But birth works no change in the Divine nature; both in Father and in Son that nature is [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) to its kind. And the right expression for us **of this unity of nature is the confession that They, being Two by birth and generation, are One**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, not one Person**.

  


32\. We will leave it to him to preach two Gods, who can preach One God without confessing the unity; he shall proclaim that God is solitary, who can deny that there are two Persons, Each dwelling in the Other by the power of Their nature and the [ _mystery_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) of birth given and received. And that man may assign a different nature to Each of the Two, who is [ _ignorant_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) that the unity of Father and of Son is a revealed [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>). Let the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) blot out this record of the Son’s self-revelation ** _I in the Father and the Father in Me;_** then, and not till then, shall they assert that there are two Gods, or one God in loneliness. **There is no hint of more natures than one in what we are told of Their possession of the one Divine nature. The**[** _truth_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**that God is from God does not multiply God by two;****_the birth destroys the supposition of a lonely God_**. And again, **_because They are interdependent_** They form an unity; and that **_They are interdependent_** is [ _proved_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) by Their being One from One. For the One, in begetting the One, conferred upon Him nothing that was not His own; and the One, in being begotten, received from the One only what belongs to one. **_Thus the apostolic_**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, in proclaiming the_**[** _Father_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, will proclaim Him as One_**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, and in confessing the Son will confess Him as One_**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _; since one and the same Divine nature exists in Both, and because, the Father being God and the Son being_**[** _God_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _, and the one name of God expressing the_**[** _nature_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)** _of Both, THE TERM ‘ONE GOD?’ signifies the Two_**. God from [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , or God in [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>) , does not mean that there are two Gods, for God abides, One from One, [** _eternally_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm?ref=answeringislam.blog>)**with the one Divine nature and the one Divine name; nor does God dwindle down to a single Person, for One and One can never be in solitude**.
