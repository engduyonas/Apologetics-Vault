# Irenaeus on Polycarp’s Letter to the Philippians

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 2, 2025
  * 2 min read


Here’s what St. Irenaeus wrote in respect to the letter that St. Polycarp had written to the Church at Philippi:

  


4\. **But**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**also was not only instructed by**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, and conversed with many who had seen Christ, but was also, by**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**in Asia, appointed**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**in**[** _Smyrna_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14060b.htm>)**, whom I also saw in my early youth** , for he tarried [on earth] a very long time, and, when a very old man, [ _gloriously_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) and most nobly suffering [ _martyrdom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) , departed this life, having always taught the things which he had learned from the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , and which the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) has handed down, and which alone are [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). To these things all the Asiatic Churches testify, as do also those men who have succeeded [ _Polycarp_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>) down to the present time — a man who was of much greater weight, and a more steadfast [ _witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , than [ _Valentinus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) , and [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , and the rest of the [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). He it was who, coming to [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) in the time of [ _Anicetus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) caused many to turn away from the aforesaid [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) to the [ _Church of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , proclaiming that he had received this one and sole [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) from the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) — that, namely, which is handed down by the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). There are also those who heard from him that John, the [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of the Lord, going to bathe at [ _Ephesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) , and perceiving [ _Cerinthus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03539a.htm>) within, rushed out of the bath-house without bathing, exclaiming, Let us fly, lest even the bath-house fall down, because [ _Cerinthus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03539a.htm>) , the enemy of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , is within. And [ _Polycarp_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>) himself replied to [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , who met him on one occasion, and said, Do you [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) me? I do [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) you, the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) of [ _Satan_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>). Such was the horror which the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) and their [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) had against holding even verbal communication with any corrupters of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) ; as [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) also says,  _A man that is an_[ __heretic__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) _, after the first and second admonition, reject;_[__knowing__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _that he that is such is subverted, and_[ __sins__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _, being condemned of himself._ [ _Titus 3:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/tit003.htm#verse10>) **There is also a very powerful Epistle of Polycarp written to the Philippians, from which those who choose to do so, and are anxious about their**[** _salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)**, can learn the character of his**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**, and the preaching of the**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). Then, again, the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) in [ _Ephesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) , founded by [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , and having John remaining among them permanently until the times of [ _Trajan_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15015a.htm>) , is a [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of the tradition of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). ([_Adversus haereses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103.htm>) (Against Heresies), Book III, [_Chapter 3_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250918131824/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103303.htm>) A refutation of the heretics, from the fact that, in the various churches, a perpetual succession of bishops was kept up; emphasis mine)
