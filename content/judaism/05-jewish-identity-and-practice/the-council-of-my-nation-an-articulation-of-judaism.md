### The Council of My Nation an Articulation of Judaism

By: Rabbi Yisroel C. Blumenthal

The purpose of this work is to help you understand and appreciate the belief system known as [Judaism](http://jewsforjudaism.org). When Constantine made Christianity the official religion of the Roman Empire, there were many belief systems that held sway in the Western world.

There were also numerous variants of Christianity that had taken root amongst the population of the Mediterranean basin. All of this changed with the rise of the Roman Church. From the time the Roman Bishops took hold of the reins of power, they saw it as their holy duty to obliterate every dissenting voice. They were quite successful. By the early Middle-Ages, the European identified himself as a Christian - with the exception of the Jew.
