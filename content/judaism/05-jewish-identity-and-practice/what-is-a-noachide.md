#### **What is a Noachide?**

The Seven Universal [Laws ](http://jewsforjudaism.org/knowledge/articles/maimonides-laws-pertaining-messiah/)that were conveyed to all of mankind through primordial man (Adam) and handed down to Noah, form the basis of God's moral expectations of mankind transmitted through the Torah. Those who choose to live by these principles are "_Noachides_ ".
