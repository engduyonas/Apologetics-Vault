What is the Mishnah and Talmud's purpose?

The Mishna and the Talmud are part of the oral tradition. They help us understand the context of the Bible and interpret the Laws so that they are applicable in modern times. 

There are many different sections of the Talmud. These include stories, discussions and practical application of biblical Law.
