# EARLY CHURCH ON JOHN 3:5 & WATER BAPTISM

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 23, 2025
  * 31 min read


The following is a list of quotes from the church fathers of the first five centuries all of whom affirm that John 3:5 refers to water baptism, and that it is in water baptism that God grants spiritual regeneration and forgiveness of transgressions by the Holy Spirit. I will also cite a few sources that, though not mentioning John 3:5, nonetheless affirm that spiritual renewal/birth and remission of sins take place at water baptism by the grace of the Holy Spirit.

  


**LETTER ATTRIBUTED TO BARNABAS**

  


Chapter 11. Baptism and the cross prefigured in the Old Testament

  


Let us further inquire whether the Lord took any care to foreshadow the water [of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)] and the cross. Concerning the water, indeed, it is written, in reference to the [ _Israelites_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) , that they should not receive that [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) which leads to the remission of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , but should procure another for themselves. The [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) therefore declares, Be astonished, O heaven, and let the earth tremble at this, because this people has committed two great [ _evils_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) : they have forsaken Me, a living fountain, and have hewn out for themselves broken cisterns. Is my [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) hill Zion a desolate rock? For you shall be as the fledglings of a bird, which fly away when the nest is removed. [ _Isaiah 16:1-2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa016.htm#verse1>) And again says the [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , I will go before you and make level the mountains, and will break the brazen gates, and bruise in pieces the iron bars; and I will give you the secret, hidden, invisible treasures, that they may [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that I am the Lord God. [ _Isaiah 45:2-3_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa045.htm#verse2>) And He shall dwell in a lofty cave of the strong rock. Furthermore, what says He in reference to the Son? His water is sure; you shall see the King in His [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) , and your [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) shall meditate on the [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) of the Lord. [ _Isaiah 33:16-18_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa033.htm#verse16>) And again He says in another [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , The man who does these things shall be like a tree planted by the courses of waters, which shall yield its fruit in due season; and his leaf shall not fade, and all that he does shall prosper. Not so are the ungodly, not so, but even as chaff, which the wind sweeps away from the face of the earth. Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in judgment, nor sinners in the counsel of the just; for the Lord [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the way of the righteous, but the way of the ungodly shall perish. Mark how He has described at once both the water and the cross. For these words imply, Blessed are they who, placing their trust in the [ _cross_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04529a.htm>) , have gone down into the water; for, says He, they shall receive their reward in due time: then He declares, I will recompense them. But now He says, Their leaves shall not fade. This means, that every word which proceeds out of your mouth in [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) and [ _love_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) shall tend to bring conversion and hope to many. Again, another [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) says, And the land of Jacob shall be extolled above every land. [ _Zephaniah 3:19_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/zep003.htm#verse19>) This means the vessel of His Spirit, which He shall glorify. Further, what says He? And there was a river flowing on the right, and from it arose beautiful trees; and whosoever shall eat of them shall live forever. [ _Ezekiel 47:12_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/eze047.htm#verse12>) ** _This means, that we indeed descend into the water full of_**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _and defilement, but come up, bearing fruit in our heart, having the_**[** _fear_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>)** _[of God] and trust in_**[** _Jesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _in our spirit_**. And whosoever shall eat of these shall live for ever, This means: Whosoever, He declares, shall hear you speaking, and [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) , shall live forever. ([_Epistle of Barnabas_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0124.htm>))

  


**RECOGNITIONS OF CLEMENT OF ROME**

  


Chapter 8. Necessity of Baptism.

  


But now I lead you to understanding by the same paths. **_For you see that all things are produced from waters_**. But water was made at first by the Only-begotten; and the [ _Almighty God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) is the head of the Only-begotten, by whom we come to the Father in such order as we have stated above. But when you have come to the Father you will learn that this is His [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) , **_that you be born anew by means of waters, which were first created. For he who is regenerated by water_** , having filled up the measure of good works, is made heir of Him **_by whom he has been regenerated in incorruption_**. Wherefore, with prepared minds, approach as sons to a father, that your [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) may be washed away, and it may be [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) before God that [ _ignorance_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) was their sole [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>). For if, after the learning of these things, you remain in unbelief, the [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) of your destruction will be imputed to yourselves, and not to [ _ignorance_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>). And do you suppose that you can have hope towards [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , even if you cultivate all [ _piety_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>) and all righteousness, but do not receive [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). Yea rather, he will be worthy or greater punishment, who does good works not well; for merit accrues to men from good works, but only if they be done as God commands. Now God has ordered every one who worships Him to be sealed by [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) ; but if you refuse, and [ _obey_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) your own will rather than God’s, you are doubtless contrary and hostile to His [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>).

  


Chapter 9. Use of Baptism.

  


But you will perhaps say, What does the [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) of water contribute towards the worship of God? In the first place, because that which has pleased God is fulfilled. In the second place, because, **_when you are regenerated and born again of water and of_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _,_** the frailty of your former birth, which you have through men, **_is cut off, and so at length you shall be able to attain_**[** _salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)** _; but otherwise it is impossible. For thus has the_**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** __**[**_prophet_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)** _testified to us with an_**[** _oath_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11176a.htm>)** _: ‘Verily I say to you, That unless a man is born again of water, he shall not enter into the_**[** _kingdom of heaven_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>)** _.’ Therefore make haste_** ; for there is in these waters a certain power of mercy which was borne upon them at the beginning, and acknowledges those who are [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) under the name of the threefold sacrament, and rescues them from future punishments, presenting as a gift to God the [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) that are [ _consecrated_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04276a.htm>) by [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). **_Betake yourselves therefore to these waters, for they alone can quench the_**[** _violence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15446a.htm>)** _of the future fire_** ; and he who delays to approach to them, it is evident that the idol of unbelief remains in him, and by it he is prevented from hastening to the waters which confer [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>). For whether you be righteous or unrighteous, [** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _is necessary for you in every respect_** : for the righteous, that perfection may be accomplished in him, and he may be born again to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; for the unrighteous, **_that pardon may be vouchsafed him of the_**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _which he has committed in_**[** _ignorance_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>)** _. Therefore all should hasten to be born again to God without delay, because the end of every one’s life is uncertain_**. ([_Book VI_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/080406.htm>))

  


**THE SHEPHERD OF HERMAS**

  


Chapter 3

  


And I said to him, I should like to continue my questions. Speak on, said he. And I said, I heard, sir, some teachers maintain **_that there is no other repentance than that which takes place, when we descended into the water and received remission of our former_**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _. He said to me, That was sound doctrine which you heard; for that is really the case_**. For he who has received remission of his [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ought not to [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) any more, but to live in purity. Since, however, you inquire diligently into all things, I will point this also out to you, not as giving occasion for [ _error_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) to those who are to [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) , or have lately [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) , in the Lord. For those who have now [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) , and those who are to [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) , have not repentance for their [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ; but they have remission of their previous [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). For to those who have been called before these days, the Lord has set repentance. For the Lord, [ _knowing_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the heart, and foreknowing all things, [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the weakness of [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) and the manifold wiles of the [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) , that he would inflict some [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) on the servants of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and would act wickedly towards them. The Lord, therefore, being merciful, has had mercy on the work of His hand, and has set repentance for them; and He has entrusted to me power over this repentance. And therefore I say to you, that if any one is tempted by the [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) , and [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) after that great and [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) calling in which the Lord has called His people to everlasting life, he has opportunity to repent but once. But if he should [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) frequently after this, and then repent, to such a man his repentance will be of no avail; for with difficulty will he live. And I said, Sir, I feel that life has come back to me in listening attentively to these commandments; for I [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that I shall be saved, if in future I [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) no more. And he said, You will be saved, you and all who keep. ([_SECOND COMMANDMENT_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/02012.htm>) — ON AVOIDING EVIL-SPEAKING, AND ON GIVING ALMS IN SIMPLICITY., Commandment 4 **On Putting One’s Wife Away for Adultery.**)

  


Chapter 16

  


Explain to me a little further, sir, I said. What is it that you desire? he asked. Why, sir, I said, did these stones ascend out of the pit, and be applied to the building of the tower, after having borne these spirits? They were [ _obliged_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11189a.htm>) , he answered, to ascend through water in order that they might be made alive; for, unless they laid aside the deadness of their life, they could not in any other way enter into the [ _kingdom of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>). Accordingly, those also who fell asleep received the seal of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). For, he continued, before a man bears the name of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) he is dead; **_but when he receives the seal he lays aside his deadness, and obtains life. The seal, then, is the water: they descend into the water dead, and they arise alive_**. And to them, accordingly, was this seal preached, and they made use of it that they might enter into the [ _kingdom of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>). Why, sir, I asked, did the forty stones also ascend with them out of the pit, having already received the seal? Because, he said, these [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) and teachers who preached the name of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , after falling asleep in the power and [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , preached it not only to those who were asleep, but themselves also gave them the seal of the preaching. Accordingly they descended with them into the water, and again ascended. [But these descended alive and rose up again alive; whereas they who had previously fallen asleep descended dead, but rose up again alive. ] By these, then, were they quickened and made to [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the name of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). For this reason also did they ascend with them, and were fitted along with them into the building of the tower, and, untouched by the chisel, were built in along with them. For they slept in righteousness and in great purity, but only they had not this seal. You have accordingly the explanation of these also. ([_NINTH SIMILITUDE_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/02013.htm>) — THE GREAT MYSTERIES IN THE BUILDING OF THE MILITANT AND TRIUMPHANT CHURCH, Similitude 9. **The Great Mysteries in the Building of the Militant and Triumphant Church.)**

  


**JUSTIN MARTYR**

  


Chapter 61. Christian baptism

  


I will also relate the manner in which we dedicated ourselves to God when we had been made new through Christ; lest, if we omit this, we seem to be unfair in the explanation we are making. As many as are persuaded and [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that what we teach and say is [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , and undertake to be able to live accordingly, are instructed to [ _pray_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) and to entreat God with [ _fasting_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05789c.htm>) , for the remission of their [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) that are past, we [ _praying_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) and [ _fasting_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05789c.htm>) with them. **_Then they are brought by us where there is water, and are regenerated in the same manner in which we were ourselves regenerated. For, in the name of_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, the Father and Lord of the_**[** _universe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>)** _, and of our Saviour_**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** _, and of the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, they then receive the washing with water. For Christ also said, Unless you be born again, you shall not enter into the_**[** _kingdom of heaven_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>)** _._**[**_John 3:5_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse5>) Now, that it is impossible for those who have once been born to enter into their mothers’ wombs, is manifest to all. And how those who have [ _sinned_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) and repent shall escape their [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , is declared by Esaias the [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , as I wrote above; he thus speaks: Wash you, make you clean; put away the [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) of your doings from your [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) ; learn to do well; judge the fatherless, and plead for the [ _widow_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15617c.htm>) : and come and let us reason together, says the Lord. And though your [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) be as scarlet, I will make them white like wool; and though they be as crimson, I will make them white as snow. But if you refuse and rebel, the sword shall devour you: for the mouth of the Lord has spoken it. [ _Isaiah 1:16-20_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/isa001.htm#verse16>)

**TERTULLIAN**

  


Chapter 12. Of the Necessity of Baptism to Salvation

  


**When, however, the prescript is laid down that without**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**,**[**_salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)**is attainable by none (chiefly on the ground of that declaration of the Lord, who says, Unless one be born of water, he has not life**), there arise immediately scrupulous, nay rather audacious, doubts on the part of some, how, in accordance with that prescript, [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) is attainable by the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , whom — [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) excepted — we do not find [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the Lord? Nay, since [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) is the only one of them who has put on  _the garment of_ Christ’s [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , either the peril of all the others who lack the water of Christ is prejudged, that the prescript may be maintained, or else the prescript is rescinded if [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) has been ordained even for the unbaptized. I have heard — the Lord is my [ _witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) — doubts of that kind: that none may [ _imagine_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07672a.htm>) me so abandoned as to excogitate, unprovoked, in the licence of my pen, ideas which would inspire others with scruple.

  


And now, as far as I shall be able, I will reply to them who affirm that the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) were unbaptized. For if they had undergone the [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) of John, and were longing for that of the Lord,  _then_ since the Lord Himself had defined [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) to be  _one_ ; (saying to Peter, who was desirous of being thoroughly bathed, He who has once bathed has no necessity  _to wash_ a second time; [ _John 13:9-10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh013.htm#verse9>) which, of course, He would not have said at all to one  _not_ [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) ; even here we have a conspicuous [ _proof_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) against those who, in order to destroy the sacrament of water, deprive the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) even of John’s [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). Can it seem credible that the way of the Lord, that is, the [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) of John, had not then been prepared in those [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) who were being destined to  _open_ the way of the Lord throughout the whole world? The Lord Himself, though no repentance was due from  _Him_ , was [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) : was [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) not necessary for  _sinners_? As for the fact, then, that others were not [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) — they, however, were not companions of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , but enemies of the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , doctors of the law and [ _Pharisees_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11789b.htm>). From which fact is gathered an additional suggestion, that, since the  _opposers_ of the Lord  _refused_ to be [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , they who  _followed_ the Lord  _were_ [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , and were not like-minded with their own rivals: especially when, if there were any one to whom they clave, the Lord had exalted John above him (by the testimony) saying, Among them who are born of [ _women_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm>)  _there is_ none greater than John the Baptist.

  


Others make the suggestion (forced enough, clearly that the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) then served the turn of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) when in their little ship, were sprinkled and covered with the waves: that Peter himself also was immersed enough when he walked on the sea. It is, however, as I think, one thing to be sprinkled or intercepted by the [ _violence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15446a.htm>) of the sea; another thing to be [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in [ _obedience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) to the discipline of religion. But that little ship did present a figure of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , in that she is disquieted in the sea, that is, in the world, by the waves, that is, by [ _persecutions_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) and [ _temptations_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14504a.htm>) ; the Lord, through patience, sleeping as it were, until, roused in their last extremities by the [ _prayers_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) of the [ _saints_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>) , He checks the world, and restores tranquillity to His own.

  


Now, whether they were [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in any manner whatever, or whether they continued unbathed to the end — so that even that saying of the Lord touching the one bath does, under the person of Peter, merely regard  _us_ — still, to determine concerning the [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) is audacious enough, because on  _them_ the prerogative even of first choice, and thereafter of undivided intimacy, might be able to confer the compendious [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , seeing they (I think) followed Him who was wont to promise [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) to every believer. Your [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , He would say, has saved you; and, Your [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) shall be remitted you, on your believing, of course, albeit you be not  _yet_ [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). If that was wanting to the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , I [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) not in the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) of what things it was, that, roused by one word of the Lord,  _one_ left the toll-booth behind for ever; [ _Matthew 9:9_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat009.htm#verse9>)  _another_ deserted father and ship, and the craft by which he gained his living; [ _Matthew 4:21-22_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat004.htm#verse21>)  _a third_ , who disdained his father’s obsequies, fulfilled, before he heard it, that highest precept of the Lord, He who prefers father or mother to me, is not worthy of me. [ _Matthew 10:37_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat010.htm#verse37>). ([_On Baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0321.htm>))

11\. **For since in**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**every one has his own**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**remitted** , the Lord proves and declares in His [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) that [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) can only be put away by those who have the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). For after His [ _resurrection_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) , sending forth His [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , He speaks to them, and says,  _As the Father has sent me, even so send I you. And when He had said this, He breathed on them, and said to them, Receive the_[ __Holy Ghost__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) _. Whose soever_[ __sins__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _you remit, they shall be remitted unto them; and whose soever_[ __sins__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _you retain, they shall be retained._ [ _John 20:21-23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh020.htm#verse21>) In which place He shows, **that he alone can**[** _baptize_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**and give remission of**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**who has the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). Moreover, John, who was to [ _baptize_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) Christ our Lord Himself, previously received the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) while he was yet in his mother’s womb, that it might be certain and manifest that none can [ _baptize_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) save those who have the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). Therefore those who patronize [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) or schismatics must answer us whether they have or have not the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). If they have, why are hands imposed on those who are [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) among them when they come to us, that they may receive the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , since He must surely have been received there, where if He was He could be given? But if [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) and schismatics [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) without have not the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , and therefore hands are imposed on them among us, that here may be received what there neither is nor can be given; it is plain, also, that remission of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) cannot be given by those who, it is certain, have not the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). And therefore, in order that, according to the divine arrangement and the evangelical [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , **they may be able to obtain remission of**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , and to be sanctified, and to become temples of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **they must all absolutely be**[** _baptized_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**with the**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**of the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**who come from adversaries and antichrists to the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**of**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>).

  


12\. You have asked also, dearest son, what I thought of those who obtain God’s [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) in sickness and weakness, whether they are to be accounted legitimate [ _Christians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) , for that they are not to be washed, but sprinkled, with the saving water. In this point, my diffidence and modesty prejudges none, so as to prevent any from feeling what he thinks right, and from doing what he feels to be right. As far as my poor understanding conceives it, I think that the divine benefits can in no respect be mutilated and weakened; nor can anything less occur in that case, where, with full and entire [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) both of the giver and receiver, is accepted what is drawn from the divine gifts. For in the sacrament of [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) the contagion of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) is not in such wise washed away, as the filth of the skin and of the body is washed away in the carnal and ordinary washing, as that there should be need of saltpetre and other appliances also, and a bath and a basin wherewith this vile body must be washed and purified. Otherwise is the breast of the believer washed; otherwise is the mind of man purified by the merit of [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). **In the**[** _sacraments_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13295a.htm>)**of**[** _salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) , when necessity compels, and God bestows His mercy, the divine methods confer the whole benefit on [ _believers_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm>) ; nor ought it to trouble any one that sick people seem to be sprinkled or affused, when they obtain the Lord’s [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) , **when**[** _Holy Scripture_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**speaks by the mouth of the**[** _prophet_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**Ezekiel, and says** ,  _Then will I sprinkle clean water upon you, and you shall be clean: from all your filthiness and from all your_[ __idols__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>) _will I cleanse you. And I will give you a new heart, and a new spirit will I put within you._ [ _Ezekiel 36:25-26_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/eze036.htm#verse25>) Also in Numbers: And the man that shall be unclean until the evening shall be purified on the third day, and on the seventh day shall be clean: but if he shall not be purified on the third day, on the seventh day he shall not be clean. And that [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) shall be cut off from [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) : because the water of sprinkling has not been sprinkled upon him. [ _Numbers 8:5-7_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/num008.htm#verse5>) And again: The water of sprinkling is a purification. [ _Numbers 19:9_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/num019.htm#verse9>) Whence it appears that the sprinkling also of water prevails equally with the washing of [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) ; and that when this is done in the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , where the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) both of receiver and giver is sound, all things hold and may be consummated and perfected by the majesty of the Lord and by the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). [ _Leviticus 21:12_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/lev021.htm#verse12>) Therefore let us prepare our temples for the Spirit of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and let us not grieve it that it may not depart from us. [ _Genesis 1:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse2>) And those that are [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) are clothed in it; for the Spirit stays aloof from all that are born of the flesh, **_until they come to the new birth by water, and then they receive the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). Be likewise contented with one [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) alone, that which is into the death of the Lord; not that which is conferred by [ _wicked_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , **_but that which is conferred by unblameable_**[** _priests_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)** _, in the name of the_**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, and of the_**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _, and of the_**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _:_**[**_Matthew 28:19_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat028.htm>) and let not that which comes from the ungodly be received by you, nor let that which is done by the godly be disannulled by a second. For do not delay to turn unto the Lord, for you know not what the next day will bring forth. Do you also [ _baptize_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) your infants, and bring them up in the nurture and admonition of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). For says He: Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not. [ _Matthew 19:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat019.htm#verse14>). **ATHANASIUS**

  


33\. Who will not admire this? Or who will not agree that such a thing is [ _truly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) divine? For if the works of the Word’s Godhead had not taken place through the body, man had not been deified; and again, had not the properties of the flesh been ascribed to the Word, man had not been thoroughly delivered from them ; but though they had ceased for a little while, as I said before, still [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) had remained in him and corruption, as was the case with [ _mankind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) before Him; and for this reason:— Many for instance have been made [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) and clean from all [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ; nay, Jeremiah was hallowed even from the womb, and John, while yet in the womb, leapt for [ _joy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) at the voice of Mary Bearer of God ; nevertheless ‘death reigned from [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) to [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , even over those that had not [ _sinned_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) after the similitude of Adam’s transgression [ _Romans 5:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom005.htm#verse14>) ;’ and thus man remained mortal and corruptible as before, liable to the affections proper to their nature. But now the Word having become man and having appropriated what pertains to the flesh, no longer do these things touch the body, because of the Word who has come in it, but they are destroyed by Him, and henceforth men no longer remain sinners and dead according to their proper affections, but having risen according to the Word’s power, they abide ever [ _immortal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) and incorruptible. Whence also, whereas the flesh is born of Mary Bearer of God , He Himself is said to have been born, who furnishes to others an origin of being; in order that He may transfer our origin into Himself, and we may no longer, as mere earth, return to earth, but as being knit into the Word from heaven, may be carried to heaven by Him. Therefore in like manner not without reason has He transferred to Himself the other affections of the body also; that we, no longer as being men, but as proper to the Word, may have share in [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) life. For no longer according to our former origin in [ _Adam_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>) do we die; but henceforward our origin and all infirmity of flesh being transferred to the Word, we rise from the earth, the curse from [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) being removed, because of Him who is in us , and who has become a curse for us. And with reason; for as we are all from earth and die in Adam, **_so being regenerated from above of water and Spirit_** , in the Christ we are all quickened; the flesh being no longer earthly, but being henceforth made Word , by reason of God’s Word who for our sake ‘became flesh.’ (_Against the Arians_ , [_Discourse III_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/28163.htm>))

  


**BASIL THE GREAT**

  


Chapter 15

  


**Reply to the suggested objection that we are baptized into water. Also concerning baptism.**

  


34\. What more? Verily, our opponents are well equipped with arguments. We are [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , they urge, into water, and of course we shall not [ _honour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) the water above all creation, or give it a share of the [ _honour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) of the Father and of the Son. The arguments of these men are such as might be expected from [ _angry_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01489a.htm>) disputants, leaving no means untried in their attack on him who has offended them, because their reason is clouded over by their feelings. We will not, however, shrink from the discussion even of these points. If we do not teach the [ _ignorant_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) , at least we shall not turn away before [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) doers. But let us for a moment retrace our steps.

  


35\. The dispensation of our God and Saviour concerning man is a recall from the fall and a return from the alienation caused by disobedience to close communion with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). This is the reason for the sojourn of Christ in the flesh, the pattern life described in the [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , the sufferings, the [ _cross_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04529a.htm>) , the tomb, the resurrection; so that the man who is being saved through imitation of Christ receives that old adoption. For perfection of life the imitation of Christ is necessary, not only in the example of gentleness, lowliness, and long suffering set us in His life, but also of His actual death. So [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , the imitator of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , says, being made conformable unto his death; if by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead. [ _Philippians 3:10-11_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/phi003.htm#verse10>) How then are we made in the likeness of His death? [ _Romans 6:4-5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom006.htm#verse4>) In that we were buried with Him by [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). What then is the manner of the burial? And what is the advantage resulting from the imitation? First of all, it is necessary that the continuity of the old life be cut. **_And this is impossible less a man be born again, according to the Lord’s word;_**[**_John 3:3_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse3>) for the regeneration, as indeed the name shows, is a beginning of a second life. So before beginning the second, it is necessary to put an end to the first. For just as in the case of runners who turn and take the second course, a kind of halt and pause intervenes between the movements in the opposite direction, so also in making a change in lives it seemed necessary for death to come as mediator between the two, ending all that goes before, and beginning all that comes after. How then do we achieve the descent into [ _hell_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07207a.htm>)? By imitating, through [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , the burial of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). For the bodies of the [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) are, as it were, buried in the water. Baptism then [ _symbolically_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14373b.htm>) signifies the putting off of the works of the flesh; as the apostle says, you were [ _circumcised_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) with the [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) made without hands, in putting off the body of the [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) of the flesh by the [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) of Christ; buried with him in [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). [ _Colossians 2:11-12_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/col002.htm#verse11>) And there is, as it were, a cleansing of the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) from the filth that has grown on it from the carnal [ _mind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10321a.htm>) , as it is written, You shall wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. On this account we do not, as is the fashion of the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , wash ourselves at each defilement, but own the [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) of [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) to be one. For there the death on behalf of the world is one, and one the resurrection of the dead, whereof [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) is a type. For this [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) the Lord, who is the Dispenser of our life, gave us the covenant of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , containing a type of life and death, for the water fulfils the image of death, and the Spirit gives us the earnest of life. Hence it follows that the answer to our question why the water was associated with the Spirit is clear: **_the reason is because in_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _two ends were proposed; on the one hand, the destroying of the body of_**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _, that it may never bear fruit unto death; on the other hand, our living unto the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, and having our fruit in_**[** _holiness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) ; the water receiving the body as in a tomb figures death, while the Spirit pours in the quickening power, renewing our [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) from the deadness of [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) unto their original life. **_This then is what it is to be born again of water and of the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, the being made dead being effected in the water, while our life is wrought in us through the Spirit. In three immersions, then, and with three invocations, the great_**[** _mystery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)** _of_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _is performed, to the end that the type of death may be fully figured, and that by the tradition of the divine_**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _the_**[** _baptized_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _may have their_**[** _souls_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)** _enlightened. It follows that if there is any_**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)** _in the water, it is not of the_**[** _nature_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>)** _of the water, but of the presence of the Spirit. For_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _is not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good_**[** _conscience_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>)** _towards God._**[**_1 Peter 3:21_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1pe003.htm#verse21>) So in training us for the life that follows on the resurrection the Lord sets out all the manner of life required by the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , laying down for us the law of gentleness, of endurance of wrong, of freedom from the defilement that comes of the [ _love_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) of pleasure, and from [ _covetousness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04462a.htm>) , to the end that we may of set purpose win beforehand and achieve all that the life to come of its inherent nature possesses. If therefore any one in attempting a definition were to describe the gospel as a forecast of the life that follows on the resurrection, he would not seem to me to go beyond what is meet and right. Let us now return to our main topic. ([_De Spiritu Sancto_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3203.htm>))
