**_Rasul_** (pl. rusul) in Islam has a specific definition.

  


Generally the word means: _ambassador_ , messenger, envoy, emissary, forerunner, apostle, and courier.

  


**_Mursal_** (pl. mursaloon) means:  _sent one_

  


This word, in the Quran can or can not refer to a prophet of Allah. For example in the Quran we see the following verse:
