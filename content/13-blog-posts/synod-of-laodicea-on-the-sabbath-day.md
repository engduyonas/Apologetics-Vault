# Synod of Laodicea on the Sabbath Day

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Dec 15, 2025
  * 3 min read


The [_Synod of Laodicea_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3806.htm>) has been dated by scholars anywhere between the years 343 and 381 AD, between the Sardican and the Second Ecumenical Council. I quote what this Synod wrote in respect to Sabbath keeping.

Canon 16

The [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) are to be read on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) [i.e. Saturday], with the other Scriptures.

Before the arrangement of the Ecclesiastical Psalmody was settled, neither the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) nor the other Scriptures were accustomed to be read on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>). But out of regard to the canons which forbade [ _fasting_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05789c.htm>) or kneeling on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) , there were no services, so that there might be as much feasting as possible. This the fathers prohibit, and decree that on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) the whole [ _ecclesiastical_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) office shall be said.

Neander (_Kirchengesch_., 2d ed., vol. iij., p. 565  _et seq_.) suggests in addition to the interpretation just given another, viz.: that it was the custom in many parts of the ancient Church to keep every Saturday as a feast in commemoration of the Creation. Neander also suggests that possibly some Judaizers read on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) only the [ _Old Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14526a.htm>) ; he, however, himself remarks that in this case εὐαγγέλια and ἑτέρων γραφῶν would require the article.

[ _Van Espen_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05541b.htm>).

Among the Greeks the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) was kept exactly as the Lord's day except so far as the cessation of work was concerned, wherefore the Council wishes that, as on Sundays, after the other lessons there should follow the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>).

For it is evident that by the intention of the [ _Church_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) the whole Divine Office was designed for the edification and instruction of the people, and especially was this the case on feast days, when the people were apt to be present in large numbers.

Here we may note the origin of our present [Western] discipline, by which on Sundays and feast days the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) is wont to be read with the other Scriptures in the canonical hours, while such is not the case on ferial days, or in the order for ferias and  _simples…_

Canon 29

[ _Christians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) must not judaize by resting on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) , but must work on that day, rather honouring the [ _Lord's Day_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14335a.htm>) ; and, if they can, resting then as [ _Christians_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>). But if any shall be found to be judaizers, let them be [ _anathema_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>) from Christ…

Canon 49

During Lent the Bread must not be offered except on the [ _Sabbath_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) Day and on the [ _Lord's Day_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14335a.htm>) only.

[_Hefele_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07191a.htm>).

This canon, which was repeated by the Trullan Synod in its fifty-second canon, orders that on ordinary week days during Lent, only a  _Missa Præsanctificatorum_ should take place, as is still the custom with the Greeks on all days of penitence and mourning, when it appears to them unsuitable to have the full liturgy, and as Leo Allatius says, for this reason, that the consecration is a joyful act. A comparison of the above sixteenth canon, however, shows that Saturday was a special exception.

To the Saturdays and Sundays mentioned by [ _Hefele_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07191a.htm>) must be added the feast of the Annunciation, which is always solemnized with a full celebration of the Liturgy, even when it falls upon Good Friday…

Canon 51

The [ _nativities_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10709a.htm>) of Martyrs are not to be celebrated in Lent, but commemorations of the [ _holy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) Martyrs are to be made on the [ _Sabbaths_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13287b.htm>) and Lord's days.
