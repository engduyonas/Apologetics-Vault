# TATIAN ON THE HOLY TRINITY

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 30, 2025
  * 9 min read


Updated: Oct 30, 2025

In this post I will be quoting from the works of Tatian who was a second century AD Assyrian Christian apologist and a disciple of St. Justin Martyr.

  


Tatian taught that God is an immaterial Spirit, and that Christ is the Logos (Word) who always subsisted within God and then sprung forth out of God, thereby showing that God’s Word is not a creature who began to exist with creation. He also proclaimed that Christ as the Logos is the Creator of all things, that angels and men are endowed with free will and are not fated, that humans are physically embodied souls who need to be united to and with the Holy Spirit in order to live immortally. Tatian further believed that a day would come when the dead will be resurrected physically, bodily. All emphasis will be mine.

  


**Chapter 4. The Christians Worship God ALONE**

  


For what reason, men of Greece, do you wish to bring the civil powers, as in a pugilistic encounter, into collision with us? And, if I am not disposed to comply with the usages of some of them, why am I to be abhorred as a vile miscreant? Does the sovereign order the payment of tribute, I am ready to render it. Does my master command me to act as a bondsman and to serve, I acknowledge the serfdom. Man is to be honoured as a fellow-man; God alone is to be feared, — He who is not visible to [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) eyes, nor comes within the compass of [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) art. **Only when I am commanded to deny Him, will I not**[** _obey_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**, but will rather die than show myself false and ungratefu** l**.****_Our God did not begin to be in time: He alone is without beginning, and He Himself is the beginning of all things_****. God is a Spirit,**[**_John 4:24_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh004.htm#verse24>)******_not pervading matter, but the Maker of material spirits, and of the forms that are in matter; He is invisible, impalpable_****, being Himself the Father of both sensible and invisible things**. Him we [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) from His creation, and apprehend His invisible power by His works. [ _Romans 1:20_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/rom001.htm#verse20>) I refuse to adore that workmanship which He has made for our sakes. The sun and moon were made for us: how, then, can I adore my own servants? How can I speak of stocks and stones as gods? For the Spirit that pervades matter is inferior **to the more divine spirit** ; and this, even when assimilated to the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , is not to be honoured equally with the perfect God. Nor even ought the ineffable God to be presented with gifts; for He who is in want of nothing is not to be misrepresented by us as though He were indigent. But I will set forth our views more distinctly.

  


**Chapter 5. The Doctrine of the Christians as to the Creation of the World**

  


God was in the beginning; **but the beginning, we have been taught, is the power of the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>). For the Lord of the [ _universe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15183a.htm>) , who is Himself the necessary ground (ὑπόστασις) of all being, **inasmuch as no creature was yet in**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**, was alone** ; but inasmuch as He was all power, Himself the necessary ground of things visible and invisible, with Him were all things; **with Him, by Logos-power (διὰ λογικῆς δυνάμεως), the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**Himself also, WHO WAS IN HIM, subsists. And by His simple will the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**SPRINGS FORTH; and the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**, not coming forth in vain, BECOMES THE FIRST-BEGOTTEN work of the Father. Him (the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**) we**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**to be the beginning of the world**. But He came into being by participation, **not by abscission** ; for what is cut off is separated from the original substance, but that which comes by participation, making its choice of function, does not render him deficient from whom it is taken. For just as from one torch many fires are lighted, but the light of the first torch is not lessened by the kindling of many torches, **so the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**, coming forth from the Logos-power of the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, has not divested of the Logos-power Him who begot Him**. I myself, for instance, talk, and you hear; yet, certainly, I who converse do not become destitute of speech (λόγος) by the transmission of speech, but by the utterance of my voice I endeavour to reduce to order the unarranged matter in your minds. **And as the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**, begotten in the beginning, begot in turn our world, having**** _first created for Himself_****the necessary matter** , so also I, in imitation of the [ _Logos_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) , being begotten again, and having become possessed of the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , am trying to reduce to order the confused matter which is kindred with myself. For matter is not, like [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , without beginning, nor, as having no beginning, is of equal power with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; it is begotten, and not produced by any other being, but brought into [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) by the Framer of all things alone.

  


**Chapter 6. Christians’ Belief in the Resurrection**

  


And on this account we [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) **that there will be a resurrection of bodies after the consummation of all things; not, as the**[** _Stoics_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14299a.htm>)**affirm, according to the return of certain cycles, the same things being produced and destroyed for no useful purpose, but a resurrection once for all** , when our periods of [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) are completed, and in consequence solely of the constitution of things under which men alone live, for the purpose of passing judgment upon them. Nor is sentence upon us passed by Minos or Rhadamanthus, before whose decease not a single [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , according to the mythic tales, was judged; but the Creator, [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) Himself, becomes the arbiter. And, although you regard us as mere triflers and babblers, it troubles us not, since we have [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) in this doctrine. For just as, not existing before I was born, I [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) not who I was, and only [ _existed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) in the potentiality (ὐπόστασις) of fleshly matter, but being born, after a former state of nothingness, I have obtained through my birth a certainty of my [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) ; in the same way, having been born, and through death existing no longer, and seen no longer, I shall exist again, just as before I was not, but was afterwards born. Even though fire destroy all traces of my flesh, the world receives the vaporized matter; and though dispersed through rivers and seas, or torn in pieces by wild beasts, **I am laid up in the storehouses of a**[** _wealthy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15571a.htm>)**Lord**. And, although the poor and the godless [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) not what is stored up, yet God the Sovereign, when He pleases, will restore the substance that is visible to Him alone to its pristine condition.

  


**Chapter 7. Concerning the Fall of Man**

  


**For**** _the heavenly_**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)** _, A SPIRIT EMANATING the Father_****and a**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**from the Logos-power, in imitation of the Father who begot Him made man an image of**[** _immortality_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) , so that, as incorruption is with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , in like manner, man, sharing in a part of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , might have the [ _immortal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) principle also. **The**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**, too, before the creation of**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**,****_was the Framer of_**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). And each of these two orders of creatures was made **free to act as it pleased** , not having the [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of good, which again is with God alone, but is brought to perfection in men **through their freedom of choice** , in order that the bad man may be [ _justly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>) punished, having become depraved **through his own fault** , but the just man be deservedly praised for his [ _virtuous_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) [ _deeds_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01115a.htm>) , since **in the exercise of his free choice** he refrained from transgressing the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **Such is the constitution of things in reference to**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**and men. And the power of the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**, having in itself a faculty to foresee future events, NOT AS FATED, but as taking place by the choice of free agents, foretold from time to time the issues of things to come** ; it also became a forbidder of [ _wickedness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) by means of prohibitions, and the encomiast of those who remained good. And, when men attached themselves to one who was more subtle than the rest, having regard to his being the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) , and declared him to be [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , though he was resisting the law of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , then the power of the [ _Logos_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) excluded the beginner of the folly and his adherents from all fellowship with Himself. And so he who was made in the likeness of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , since the more powerful spirit is separated from him, becomes mortal; **but that first-begotten one through his transgression and**[** _ignorance_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>)**becomes a**[** _demon_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) ; and they who imitated him, that is his illusions, have become a host of [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) , and through their freedom of choice have been given up to their own infatuation…

  


**Chapter 13. Theory of the Soul’s Immortality**

  


**The**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**is not in itself**[** _immortal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>)**, O Greeks, but mortal**. Yet it is possible for it not to die. If, indeed, it [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) not the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , it dies, **_and is dissolved with the body, but rises again at last at the end of the world with the body, receiving death by punishment in_**[** _immortality_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>). But, again, if it acquires the [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , it dies not, **although for a time it be dissolved**. In itself it is darkness, and there is nothing luminous in it. And this is the meaning of the saying, The darkness comprehends not the light. [ _John 1:5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse5>) For the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) does not preserve the spirit, but is preserved by it, and the light comprehends the darkness. **_The_**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)** _, in_**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** _, is the light of_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , but the [ _ignorant_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) is darkness. On this account, if it continues solitary, it tends downward towards matter, and dies with the flesh; **but, if it enters into union with**** _the Divine Spirit_****, it is no longer helpless** , but ascends to the regions **whither the Spirit guides it** : for the dwelling-place of the spirit is above, but the origin of the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) is from beneath. Now, in the beginning the spirit was a constant companion of the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , but the spirit forsook it because it was not willing to follow. Yet, retaining as it were a spark of its power, though unable by reason of the separation to discern the perfect, while seeking for God it fashioned to itself in its wandering many gods, following the sophistries of the [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>). **But the**[** _Spirit of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**is not with all, but, taking up its abode with those who live**[** _justly_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08571c.htm>)**, and intimately combining with the**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**, by prophecies it announced hidden things to other**[** _souls_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>). And the [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) that are [ _obedient_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) to wisdom have attracted to themselves the cognate spirit; but the disobedient, rejecting the minister of the suffering [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , have shown themselves to be fighters against [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , rather than His worshippers…

  


**Chapter 15. Necessity of a Union with the Holy Spirit**

  


But further, it becomes us now to seek for what we once had, but have lost, **to unite the**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**with the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , and to strive after union with [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). The [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) consists of many parts, and is not simple; it is composite, so as to manifest itself through the body; for neither could it ever appear by itself without the body, nor does the flesh rise again without the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>). Man is not, as the croaking [ _philosophers_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) say, merely a rational animal, capable of understanding and [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) ; for, according to them, even irrational creatures appear possessed of understanding and [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>). **But man alone is the image and likeness of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and I mean by man, not one who performs actions similar to those of animals, but one who has advanced far beyond mere humanity — to God Himself. This question we have discussed more minutely in the treatise concerning animals. But the principal point to be spoken of now is, what is intended by the image and likeness of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). That which cannot be compared is no other than abstract being; but that which is compared is no other than that which is like. **The perfect God is without flesh** ; but man is flesh. **The bond of the flesh is the**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**; that which encloses the**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**is the flesh**. Such is the [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of [ _man’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) constitution; **and, if it be like a temple, God is pleased to dwell in it by the spirit, His representative** ; but, if it be not such a habitation, man excels the wild beasts in articulate language only — in other respects his manner of life is like theirs, as one who is not a likeness of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). But none of the [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) possess flesh; their structure is spiritual, like that of fire or air. And only by those **whom the**[** _Spirit of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**dwells in and fortifies** are the bodies of the [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) easily seen, not at all by others — I mean those who possess only [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) ; for the inferior has not the ability to apprehend the superior. On this account the [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of the [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) has no place for repentance; for they are the reflection of matter and of [ _wickedness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>). But matter desired to exercise lordship over the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) ; and according to their [ _free-will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06259a.htm>) these gave [ _laws_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09053a.htm>) of death to men; but men, after the loss of [ _immortality_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>) , have conquered death by submitting to death in [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) ; and by repentance a call has been given to them, according to the word which says, Since they were made a little lower than the [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). And, for every one who has been conquered, it is possible again to conquer, if he rejects the condition which brings death. And what that is, may be easily seen by men who long for [ _immortality_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250514055445/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07687a.htm>).
