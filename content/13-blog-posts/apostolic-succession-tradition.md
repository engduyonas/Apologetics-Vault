# APOSTOLIC SUCCESSION & TRADITION

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 28 min read


In this post I will cite from a Church father and two early Christian writers, all of whom affirm that it is the sacred unwritten tradition found in the churches that go back the Apostles which preserve the correct meaning and application of the Scriptures and the Faith. All emphasis will be mine.

  


**IRENAEUS**

  


[** _Chapter 8_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103108.htm>) How the Valentinians pervert the Scriptures to support their own pious opinions

  


1\. Such, then, is their system, which neither the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) announced, nor the Lord taught, nor the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) delivered, but of which they boast that beyond all others they have a perfect [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>). They gather their views from other sources than the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) ; and, to use a common proverb, they strive to weave ropes of sand, while they endeavour to adapt with an air of probability to their own peculiar assertions the [ _parables_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm>) of the Lord, the sayings of the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , and the words of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , in order that their scheme may not seem altogether without support. In doing so, however, they disregard the order and the connection of the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , and so far as in them lies, dismember and destroy the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). **By transferring passages, and dressing them up anew, and making one thing out of another, they succeed in deluding many through their**[** _wicked art_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11197b.htm>)**in adapting the oracles of the Lord to their opinions**. Their manner of acting is just as if one, when a beautiful image of a king has been constructed by some skilful artist out of precious jewels, **should then take this likeness of the man all to pieces, should rearrange the gems, and so fit them together as to make them into the form of a dog or of a fox, and even that but poorly executed; and should then maintain and declare that**** _this_****was the beautiful image of the king which the skilful artist constructed,** pointing to the jewels which had been admirably fitted together by the first artist to form the image of the king, but have been with bad effect transferred by the latter one to the shape of a dog, and by thus exhibiting the jewels, should deceive the [ _ignorant_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) who had no conception what a king’s form was like, and persuade them that that miserable likeness of the fox was, in fact, the beautiful image of the king. In like manner do these [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) patch together old wives’ fables, **and then endeavour, by violently drawing away from their proper connection, words, expressions, and**[** _parables_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm>)**whenever found, to adapt the oracles of God to their baseless fictions**. We have already stated how far they proceed in this way with respect to the interior of the Pleroma. (_Against Heresies_ , Book I)

  


[**_Chapter 2_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103302.htm>) The heretics follow neither Scripture nor Tradition

  


1\. When, however, **they are confuted from the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**, they turn round and accuse these same Scriptures, as if they were not correct, nor of authority, and [assert] that they are ambiguous, and that the**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**cannot be extracted from them by those who are**[** _ignorant_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>)**of tradition**. For [they allege] that the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) was not delivered by means of written documents, **but**** _vivâ voce_** _:_ wherefore also [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) declared, But we speak wisdom among those that are perfect, but not the wisdom of this world. [ _1 Corinthians 2:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co002.htm#verse6>) And this wisdom each one of them alleges to be the fiction of his own inventing, forsooth; so that, according to their idea, the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) properly resides at one time in [ _Valentinus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) , at another in [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , at another in [ _Cerinthus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03539a.htm>) , then afterwards in [ _Basilides_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02326a.htm>) , or has even been indifferently in any other opponent, who could speak nothing pertaining to [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>). For every one of these men, being altogether of a perverse disposition, depraving the system of [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , is not ashamed to preach himself.

2\. But, again, **when we refer them to that tradition which originates from the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, [and]****__which is preserved by means of the succession of__**[** __presbyters__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)** __in the__**[** __Churches__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, they object to tradition, saying that they themselves are wiser not merely than the**[** _presbyters_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**, but even than the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, because they have discovered the unadulterated**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). For [they maintain] that the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) intermingled the things of the law with the words of the Saviour; and that not the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) alone, but even the Lord Himself, spoke as at one time from the [ _Demiurge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04707b.htm>) , at another from the intermediate place, and yet again from the Pleroma, but that they themselves, indubitably, unsulliedly, and purely, have [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of the hidden [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) : this is, indeed, to [ _blaspheme_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) their Creator after a most impudent manner! It comes to this, therefore, that these men do now consent neither to Scripture nor to tradition.

3\. Such are the adversaries with whom we have to deal, my very dear friend, endeavouring like slippery serpents to escape at all points. Where-fore they must be opposed at all points, if per-chance, by cutting off their retreat, we may succeed in turning them back to the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). For, though it is not an easy thing for a [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) under the influence of [ _error_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) to repent, yet, on the other hand, it is not altogether impossible to escape from [ _error_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) when the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) is brought alongside it. (Ibid., Book III)

  


[**_Chapter 26_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103426.htm>) The treasure hid in the Scriptures is Christ; **the true exposition of the Scriptures is to be found IN THE CHURCH ALONE**.

  


1\. If any one, therefore, reads the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) with attention, he will find in them an account of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and a foreshadowing of the new calling (_vocationis_). **For Christ is the treasure which was hid in the field,**[**_Matthew 13:44_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat013.htm#verse44>)**that is, in this world (for the field is the world**[** _Matthew 13:38_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat013.htm#verse38>)**); but the treasure hid in the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**is Christ** , since He was pointed out by means of types and [ _parables_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11460a.htm>). Hence His [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) could not be understood, prior to the consummation of those things which had been predicted, that is, the advent of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). **And therefore it was said to Daniel the**[** _prophet_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**: Shut up the words, and seal the book even to the time of consummation, until many learn, and**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**be completed. For at that time, when the dispersion shall be accomplished, they shall**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**all these things**. [ _Daniel 12:4, 7_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/dan012.htm#verse4>) **But Jeremiah also says, In the last days they shall understand these things**. [ _Jeremiah 23:20_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/jer023.htm#verse20>) **For every**[** _prophecy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>)**, before its fulfilment, is to men [full of] enigmas and ambiguities. But when the time has arrived, and the prediction has come to pass, then the prophecies have a clear and certain exposition**. And for this reason, indeed, when at this present time the law is read to the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , it is like a fable; for they do not possess the explanation of all things pertaining to the advent of the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , which took place in [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) ; **but when it is read by the**[** _Christians_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>)**, it is a treasure, hid indeed in a field, but brought to light by the cross of**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, and explained, both enriching the understanding of**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and showing forth the wisdom of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and declaring His dispensations with regard to [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and forming the kingdom of Christ beforehand, and preaching by anticipation the inheritance of the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) Jerusalem, and proclaiming beforehand that the man who loves God shall arrive at such excellency as even to see [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and hear His word, and from the hearing of His discourse be [ _glorified_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) to such an extent, that others cannot behold the [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) of his countenance, as was said by Daniel: Those who do understand, shall shine as the brightness of the firmament, and many of the righteous as the stars for ever and ever. [ _Daniel 12:3_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/dan012.htm#verse3>) Thus, then, I have shown it to be, if any one read the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>). For thus it was that the Lord discoursed with the [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) after His [ _resurrection_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) from the dead, proving to them from the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) themselves that [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10212c.htm>) must suffer, and enter into His [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) , and that remission of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) should be preached in His name throughout all the world. [ _Luke 24:26, 47_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk024.htm#26>) And the [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) will be [ _perfected_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11665b.htm>) , and [rendered] like the householder, who brings forth from his treasure things new and old. [ _Matthew 13:52_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat013.htm#52>)

  


2\. **Wherefore it is incumbent to**[** _obey_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>)**the**[** _presbyters_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**who are in the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**—**** __those who, as I have shown, possess the succession from the__**[** __apostles__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**; those who, together with the succession of the**[** _episcopate_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>)**, have received the certain gift of**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**, according to the good pleasure of the Father. But [it is also incumbent] to hold in suspicion others who depart from the primitive succession, and assemble themselves together in any place whatsoever** , [looking upon them] either as [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) of perverse minds, or as schismatics puffed up and self-pleasing, or again as hypocrites, acting thus for the sake of lucre and vainglory. For all these have fallen from the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). And the [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , indeed, who bring strange fire to the altar of God — namely, [ _strange doctrines_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) — shall be burned up by the fire from heaven, as were Nadab and Abiud. [ _Leviticus 10:1-2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/lev010.htm#verse1>) But such as rise up in opposition to the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , and exhort others against the [ _Church of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , [shall] remain among those in [ _hell_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07207a.htm>) (_apud inferos_), being swallowed up by an earthquake, even as those who were with Chore, Dathan, and Abiron. [ _Numbers 16:33_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/num016.htm#verse33>) **But those who cleave asunder, and separate the unity of the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, [shall] receive from God the same punishment as**[** _Jeroboam_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08340a.htm>)**did**. [ _1 Kings 14:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ki014.htm#verse10>)

  


3\. Those, however, who are [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to be [ _presbyters_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) by many, but serve their own [ _lusts_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09438a.htm>) , and, do not place the [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) of God supreme in their hearts, but conduct themselves with contempt towards others, and are puffed up with the [ _pride_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12405a.htm>) of holding the chief seat, and work [ _evil deeds_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) in secret, saying, No man sees us, shall be convicted by the Word, **who does not judge after outward appearance (****_secundum gloriam_****), nor looks upon the countenance, but the heart** ; and they shall hear those words, to be found in Daniel the [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) : O you seed of Canaan, and not of Judah, beauty has deceived you, and [ _lust_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09438a.htm>) perverted your heart. You that are waxen old in [ _wicked_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) days, now your [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) which you have committed aforetime have come to light; for you have pronounced false judgments, and have been accustomed to condemn the innocent, and to let the guilty go free, albeit the Lord says, The innocent and the righteous shall you not slay. Of whom also did the Lord say: But if the [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) servant shall say in his heart, My lord delays his coming, and shall begin to smite the man-servants and maidens, and to eat and drink and be drunken; the lord of that servant shall come in a day that he looks not for him, and in an hour that he is not aware of, and shall cut him asunder, and appoint him his portion with the unbelievers.

  


4\. From all such [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) , therefore, it behooves us to keep aloof, **but to adhere to those who, as I have already observed, do hold the doctrine of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, and who, together with the order of**[** _priesthood_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>)**(****_presbyterii ordine_****), display sound speech and blameless conduct for the confirmation and correction of others**. In this way, [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , to whom such a leadership was entrusted, relying on a good [ _conscience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>) , cleared himself before [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , saying, I have not in [ _covetousness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04462a.htm>) taken anything belonging to one of these men, nor have I done [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) to one of them. Just as they carefully prepared their will and testament, and committed it to a trust, and adjured (the trustees to be faithful to their charge), even so do I hold it. As for you, they have, it is certain, always held you as disinherited, and rejected you as strangers — as enemies. But on what ground are [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) strangers and enemies to the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , if it be not from the difference of their teaching, which each individual of his own mere will has either advanced or received in opposition to the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)?

  


Chapter 38. Harmony of the Church and the Scriptures. Heretics Have Tampered with the Scriptures, and Mutilated, and Altered Them. **Catholics Never Change the Scriptures, Which Always Testify for Them**.

  


**Where diversity of doctrine is found,****_there_****, then, must the corruption both of the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**and the expositions thereof be regarded as existing**. On those whose purpose it was to teach differently, lay the [ _necessity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10733a.htm>) of differently arranging the instruments of doctrine. They could not possibly have effected their diversity of teaching in any other way than by having a difference in the means whereby they taught. As in their case, corruption in doctrine could not possibly have succeeded without a corruption also of its instruments, **so to ourselves also integrity of doctrine could not have accrued, without integrity in those means by which doctrine is managed. Now, what is there in our Scriptures which is contrary to us?** What of our own have we introduced, that we should have to take it away again, or else add to it, or alter it, in order to restore to its natural soundness anything which is contrary to it, and contained in the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)? **What we are ourselves, that also the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**are (and have been) from the beginning**. Of them we have our being, before there was any other way, before they were interpolated by you. Now, inasmuch as all interpolation must be [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) to be a later process, for the express reason that it proceeds from rivalry which is never in any case previous to nor home-born with that which it emulates, **it is as incredible to every man of sense that we should seem to have introduced any corrupt text into the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**, existing, as we have been, from the very first** , and being the first, as it is that they have not in fact introduced it who are both later in date and opposed (to the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)). **One man perverts the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**with his hand, another their meaning by his exposition**. For although [ _Valentinus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) seems to use the entire volume, he has none the less laid violent hands on the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) only with a more cunning mind and skill than [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>). [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) expressly and openly used the knife, not the pen, since he made such an excision of the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) as suited his own subject-matter. [ _Valentinus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) , however, abstained from such excision, because he did not invent Scriptures to square with his own subject-matter, but adapted his matter to the [ _Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) ; and yet he took away more, and added more, by removing the proper meaning of every particular word, and adding fantastic arrangements of things which have no real [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>). **__leaving, however, the grounds of their statements to be examined into by those who should deserve the excellent gifts of the__**[** __Spirit__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** __, and who, especially by means of the__**[** __Holy Spirit__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** __Himself, should obtain the gift of language, of wisdom, and of__**[** __knowledge__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**: while on other subjects they merely stated the fact that things were so, keeping silence as to the manner or origin of their**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**;****__clearly in order that the more__**[** __zealous__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15753a.htm>)** __of their successors, who should be lovers of wisdom, might have a subject of exercise on which to display the fruit of their talents__****— those**[** _persons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>)**, I mean, who should prepare themselves to be fit and worthy receivers of wisdom**.

 _First_ , That there is [ _one God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10499a.htm>) , who created and arranged all things, **and who, when nothing**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**, called all things into being** — God from the first creation and foundation of the world — the [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) of all just men, of Adam, Abel, Seth, Enos, [ _Enoch_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07218a.htm>) , Noe, Sere, [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Isaac, Jacob, the twelve patriarchs, [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , and the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) ; and that this God in the last days, as He had announced beforehand by His [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , sent [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) to call in the first place [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) to Himself, and in the second place the [ _Gentiles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) , after the unfaithfulness of the people of [ _Israel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>). This just and good [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the Father of [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , Himself gave the law and the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , and the [ _Gospels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , being also the [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) and of the Old and New Testaments.

that, after He had been the servant of the Father in the creation of all things — For by Him were all things made** — He in the last times, divesting Himself (of His [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)), became a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , **and was incarnate although**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and while made a man**** _REMAINED THE GOD WHICH HE WAS_** ; **Then,****_Thirdly_****, the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**related that the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**was associated in**[** _honour_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)**and dignity with the Father and the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). 5\. **After these points, also, the apostolic teaching is that the**[** _soul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**, having a substance and life of its own, shall, after its departure from the world, be rewarded according to its deserts, being destined to obtain either an inheritance of**[** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**life and blessedness, if its actions shall have procured this for it, or to be delivered up to**[** _eternal fire_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07207a.htm>)**and punishments** , if the guilt of its crimes shall have brought it down to this: **and also, that there is to be a time of resurrection from the dead,****__when this body, which now is sown in corruption, shall rise in incorruption, and that which is sown in dishonour will rise in__**[** __glory__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)** __. This also is clearly defined in the teaching of the__**[** __Church__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** __, that every rational__**[** __soul__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)** __is possessed of__**[** __free-will__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06259a.htm>)** __and volition__****; that it has a struggle to maintain with the**[** _devil_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>)**and his**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**, and opposing influences, because they strive to burden it with**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**; but if we live rightly and wisely, we should endeavour to shake ourselves free of a burden of that kind. From which it follows, also,****__that we understand ourselves not to be subject to necessity, so as to be compelled by all means, even against our__**[** __will__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>)** __, to do either good or__**[** __evil__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>). For if we are our own masters, some influences perhaps may impel us to [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , and others help us to [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) ; **we are not forced, however, by any necessity either to act rightly or wrongly, which those**[** _persons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>)**think is the case who say that the courses and movements of the stars are the**[** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>)**of**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**actions, not only of those which take place beyond the influence of the freedom of the will, but also of those which are placed within our own power**. But with respect to the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , whether it is derived from the seed by a process of traducianism, so that the reason or substance of it may be considered as placed in the seminal particles of the body themselves, or whether it has any other beginning; and this beginning, itself, whether it be by birth or not, or whether bestowed upon the body from without or no, **is not distinguished with sufficient clearness in the teaching of the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>).

  


6\. Regarding the [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) and his [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) , and the opposing influences, the teaching of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) has laid down that these beings exist indeed; **but what they are, or how they exist, it has not explained with sufficient clearness. This opinion, however, is held by most, that the**[** _devil_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>)**was an**[** _angel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**, and that, having become an**[** _apostate_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01624b.htm>)**, he induced as many of the**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**as possible to fall away with himself, and these up to the present time are called his**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>).

  


7\. **This also is a part of the Church’s teaching** , that the world was made and took its beginning at a certain time, and is to be destroyed on account of its [ _wickedness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>). But what [ _existed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) before this world, or what will exist after it, has not become certainly [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to the many, for there is no clear statement regarding it in the teaching of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>).

  


8\. Then, finally, **that the**[** _Scriptures_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**were written by the**[** _Spirit of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**, and have a meaning, not such only as is apparent at first sight, but also another, which escapes the notice of most**. For those (words) which are written are the forms of certain [ _mysteries_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) , and the images of divine things. **_Respecting which there is one opinion throughout the whole_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , that the whole law is indeed spiritual; but that the spiritual meaning which the law conveys **is not**[** _known_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**to all, but to those only on whom the**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)**of the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**is bestowed in the word of wisdom and**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>).

  


The term ἀσώματον, i.e., incorporeal, is disused and unknown, not only in many other writings, but also in our own Scriptures. And if any one should quote it to us out of the little treatise entitled  _The Doctrine of Peter_ , in which the Saviour seems to say to His [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , I am not an incorporeal [ _demon_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) , **I have to reply, in the first place, that that work is not included among**[** _ecclesiastical_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**books; for we can show that it was not composed either by Peter or by any other person inspired by the**[** _Spirit of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). But even if the point were to be conceded, the word ἀσώματον there **does not convey the same meaning as is intended by Greek and Gentile authors when incorporeal nature is discussed by**[** _philosophers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>). For in the little treatise referred to he used the phrase incorporeal [ _demon_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) to denote that that form or outline of [ _demoniacal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) body, whatever it is, does not resemble this gross and visible body of ours; but, **agreeably to the intention of the author of the treatise** , it must be understood to mean that He had not such a body as [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) have, which is naturally fine, and thin as if formed of air (and for this reason is either considered or called by many incorporeal), but that He had a solid and palpable body. **Now, according to**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**custom, everything which is not of that nature is called by the simple or**[** _ignorant_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>)**incorporeal; as if one were to say that the air which we breathe was incorporeal, because it is not a body of such a nature as can be grasped and held, or can offer resistance to pressure**.

9\. We shall inquire, however, whether the thing which Greek [ _philosophers_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) call ἀσώματον, or incorporeal, is found in [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) Scripture under another name. For it is also to be a subject of investigation how God himself is to be understood — **whether as corporeal, and formed according to some shape, or of a different nature from bodies — a point which is not clearly indicated in our teaching**. And the same inquiries have to be made regarding Christ and the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , as well as respecting every [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) , and everything possessed of a rational nature.

  


10\. **This also is a part of the teaching of the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , that there are certain [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and certain good influences, which are His servants in accomplishing the [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) of men. **When these, however, were created, or of what nature they are, or how they exist, is not clearly stated**. Regarding the sun, moon, and stars, whether they are living beings or without life, there is no distinct deliverance.

Every one, therefore, must make use of elements and foundations of this sort, according to the precept, Enlighten yourselves with the light of [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , if he would desire to form a connected series and body of truths agreeably to the reason of all these things, that **by clear and necessary statements** he may ascertain the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) regarding each individual topic, and form, as we have said, **one body of doctrine** , by means of illustrations and arguments — **either those which he has discovered in**[** _holy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>)**Scripture, or which he has deduced by closely tracing out the consequences and following a correct method**. (_De Principiis_ (First Principles))

  


9\. Now the [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) , in all the points pre­viously enumerated, of the false opinions, and of the impious statements or [ _ignorant_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) assertions about [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , appears to be nothing else than the not understanding the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) according to its spiritual meaning, **but the interpretation of it agree­ably to the mere letter**. And therefore, to those who [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that the sacred books are not the compositions of [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , **but that they were composed by inspiration of the**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , agreeably to the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of the Father of all things through [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and that they have come down to us, **__we must point out the ways (of interpreting them) which appear (correct) to us, who cling to the standard of the heavenly Church of__**[** __Jesus Christ__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)** __according to the succession of the__**[** __apostles__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). Now, that there are certain mystical economies made [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) by the [ _holy Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) , all — even the most simple of those who adhere to the word — have [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) ; but what these are, candid and modest individuals confess that they [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) not. **If, then, one were to be perplexed about the intercourse of**[** _Lot_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09366a.htm>)**with his daughters, and about the two wives of**[** _Abraham_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>)**, and the two sisters married to Jacob, and the two handmaids who bore him children, they can return no other answer than this, that these are**[** _mysteries_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)**not understood by us**.

  


Nay, also, when the (description of the) fitting out of the tabernacle is read, believing that what is written is a type, they seek to adapt what they can to each particular related about the tabernacle — not being wrong so far as regards their belief that the tabernacle is a type of  _something_ , but erring sometimes in adapting the descrip­tion of that of which the tabernacle is a type, to some special thing in a manner worthy of Scripture. And all the history that is considered to tell of marriages, or the begetting of children, or of [ _wars_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15546c.htm>) , or any histories whatever that are in circula­tion among the multitude, they declare to be types; but of what in each individual instance, partly owing to their habits not being thoroughly exercised — partly, too, owing to their precipitation — sometimes, even when an individual does happen to be well trained and clear-sighted, owing to the excessive difficulty of discovering things on the part of [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) — the [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of each particular regarding these (types) is not clearly ascertained. (Ibid., [_BOOK IV_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04124.htm>))

  


Origen cites infant baptism as an apostolic tradition not written, but which is binding on Christians:

  


“The Church received from the apostles the tradition of giving baptism even to infants. The apostles, to whom were committed the secrets of the divine sacraments, knew there are in everyone innate strains of [original] sin, which must be washed away through water and the Spirit.” (_Commentary on Romans_ 5:9) 

  


**VINCENT OF LERINS**

  


Chapter 2.

  


**A General Rule for distinguishing the Truth of the Catholic Faith from the Falsehood of Heretical Pravity.**

  


[4.] I have often then inquired earnestly and attentively of very many men eminent for [ _sanctity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) and learning, **how and by what sure and so to speak universal rule I may be able to distinguish the**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**of**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)****[**_faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**from the**[** _falsehood_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>)**of**[** _heretical_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**pravity; and I have always, and in almost every instance, received an answer to this effect** : That whether I or any one else should wish to detect the frauds and avoid the snares of [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) as they rise, and to continue sound and complete in the [ _Catholic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>) [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , we must, the Lord helping, fortify our own belief in two ways; first, by the authority of the Divine Law, **__and then, by the Tradition of the__**[** __Catholic__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)** ____**[**__Church__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>).

  


[5.] But here some one perhaps will ask, **Since the canon of Scripture is complete, and sufficient of itself for everything, and more than sufficient, what need is there to join with it the authority of the Church’s interpretation?** For this reason — because, **owing to the depth of**[** _Holy Scripture_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)**, all do not accept it in one and the same sense, but one understands its words in one way, another in another** ; so that it seems to be capable of as many interpretations as there are interpreters. For [ _Novatian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11138a.htm>) expounds it one way, Sabellius another, Donatus another, [ _Arius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01718a.htm>) , Eunomius, Macedonius, another, Photinus, [ _Apollinaris_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01615b.htm>) , [ _Priscillian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12429b.htm>) , another, Iovinian, Pelagius, Celestius, another, lastly, [ _Nestorius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10755a.htm>) another. **Therefore, it is very necessary, on account of so great intricacies of such various**[** _error_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>)**, that the rule for the right understanding of the**[** _prophets_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>)**and**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**should be framed**** __in accordance with the standard of Ecclesiastical and__**[** __Catholic__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)** __interpretation__**.

  


[6.] Moreover, in the [ _Catholic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>) [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) itself, all possible care must be taken, **__that we hold that__**[** __faith__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)** __which has been__**[** __believed__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)** __everywhere, always, by all__****. For that is**[** _truly_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**and in the strictest sense**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)**, which,****_as the name itself and the reason of the thing declare_****, comprehends all universally**. **__This rule we shall observe if we follow universality, antiquity, consent__**. We shall follow universality if we confess that one [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) to be [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , **__which the whole__**[** __Church__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** __throughout the world confesses__** ; antiquity, if we in no wise depart from those interpretations which it is manifest **__were notoriously held by our__**[** __holy__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>)** __ancestors and fathers__** ; consent, in like manner, if in antiquity itself we adhere to the consentient definitions and determinations of all, or at the least of almost all [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) and doctors. ([_Commonitory for the Antiquity and Universality of the Catholic Faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212902/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3506.htm>))
