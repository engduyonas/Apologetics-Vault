# More Early Church Quotes On Baptism

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 23, 2025
  * 26 min read


I present additional quotes from some of the church’s greatest theologians and scholars on the necessity and centrality of water baptism for regeneration, salvation, and forgiveness of sins. All emphasis will be mine.

  


**St. Hilary of Poitiers**

  


57\. Keep, I [ _pray_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) You, this my [ _pious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>) [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) undefiled, and even till my spirit departs, grant that this may be the utterance of my convictions: so that I may ever hold fast that which I professed **__in the creed of my regeneration, when I was__**[** __baptized__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** __in the__**[** __Father__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** __, and the__**[** __Son__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** __, and the__**[** __Holy Spirit__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). Let me, in short, adore You our Father, and Your Son together with You; let me win the favour of Your [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , Who is from You, through Your Only-begotten. For I have a convincing Witness to my [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , Who says,  _Father, all Mine are Yours, and Yours are Mine_ [ _John 17:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh017.htm#verse10>) , even my Lord [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , abiding in You, and from You, and with You, for ever God: Who is blessed for ever and ever. [ _Amen_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01407b.htm>). ([_On the Trinity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3302.htm>) , [_Book XII_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/330212.htm>))

  


**St. Cyril of Alexandria**

  


Behold Him, therefore, as a man, enduring with us the things that belong to man’s estate, and fulfilling all righteousness, for the plan of salvation’s sake. And this thou learnest from what the Evangelist says: “ _And it came to pass that when all the people were baptized, Jesus also was baptized, and prayed_.” Was He too then in need of holy baptism? But what benefit could accrue to Him from it? The Only-begotten Word of God is Holy of the Holy: so the Seraphim name Him in their praises: so every where the law names Him: and the company of the holy prophets accords with the writings of Moses. **__What is it that we gain by holy baptism? Plainly the remission of our sins__**. But in Jesus there was nought of this; “for He did no sin: neither was guile found “in His mouth,” as the Scripture saith. “He was holy, harmless, undefiled, separate from sins, and made higher than the heavens,” according to the words of the divine Paul. ([_Sermon 11: Luke 3:21-23._](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/cyril_on_luke_01_sermons_01_11.htm#C11>))

  


**St. Fulgentius of Ruspe**

  


 _43\. Throughout history, by the mysteries Christ has instituted through the faith of his Incarnation, they have arrived at that kingdom, those whom God has freely saved_** _with no merits of good will or good works preceding_** _. Just as from that time onward when our Savior said, “If anyone is not reborn from water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the Kingdom of God”,__53_ __**__without the Sacrament of Baptism__** _, apart from those who poured out their blood for Christ in the Catholic Church but without Baptism,_**__no one can receive either the kingdom of heaven or eternal life__** _. And so, anyone who receives the Sacrament of Holy Baptism_** _in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit_** _, whether in the Catholic Church or in any heresy or schism, receives the complete Sacrament; but he will not_** __have salvation which is the effect of the Sacrament__** _if he receives the Sacrament outside the Catholic Church. Therefore, that person must return to the Church, not to receive the Sacrament of Baptism again, which no one must repeat in the case of any person already baptized, but in order that he receive eternal life in the Catholic community. Anyone who has received the Sacrament of Baptism but remained away from the Catholic Church is never prepared to obtain eternal life. Such a person, even if he is very generous with almsgiving and even pours out his blood for the name of Christ,_**_because of the fact that in this life he has not held tightly to the unity of the Catholic Church_** _, he will not have eternal salvation. Wherever Baptism can be of use to anyone, it is there that almsgiving can be of avail. Baptism indeed can exist outside the Church,_**_but it can be of no avail except within the Church_** _._ (Robert B. Eno, _The Fathers of the Church: Fulgentius, Selected Works_ [The Catholic University of America Press, Washington, DC, 1997], Volume 95, pp. 87-88)

  


**St. John Chrysostom**

  


2\. For he who is about to approach these [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) and dread [ _mysteries_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) must be awake and alert, must be clean from all cares of this life, full of much self-restraint, much readiness; he must banish from his mind every thought foreign to the [ _mysteries_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) , and on all sides cleanse and prepare his home, as if about to receive the king himself. Such is the preparation of your [ _mind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10321a.htm>) : such are your thoughts; such the purpose of your [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>). Await therefore a return worthy of this most excellent decision from [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , who overpowers with His recompense those who show forth [ _obedience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) to Him. But since it is necessary for his fellow servants to contribute of their own, then we will contribute of our own; yea rather not even are these things our own, but these too are our Master’s.  _For what have you_ , says He,  _that thou did not receive? But if you received it, why do you_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _, as if you had not received it?_ [ _1 Corinthians 4:7_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co004.htm#verse7>) I wished to say this first of all, why in the world our fathers, passing by the whole year, settled that the children of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) should be initiated at this season; and for what reason, after the instruction from us, removing your shoes and raiment, unclad and unshod, with but one garment on, they conduct you to hear the words of the exorcisers. For it is not thoughtlessly and rashly that they have planned this dress and this season for us. But both these things have a certain mystic and secret reason. And I wish to say this to you. But I see that our discourse now constrains us **_to something more necessary to say what_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _is, and for what reason it enters into our life, and what_**[** _good_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>)** _things it conveys to us_**.

But, if you will, let us discourse about the name **_which this mystic cleansing bears_** : for its name is not one, but very many and various. **__For this purification is called the laver of regeneration__**.  _He saved us_ , he says,  _through the laver of regeneration, and renewing of the_[ __Holy Ghost__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). [ _Titus 3:5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/tit003.htm#verse5>) It is called also illumination, and this [ _St. Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) again has called it,  _For call to remembrance the former days in which after you were illuminated ye endured a great conflict of sufferings_ ; [ _Hebrews 10:32_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/heb010.htm#verse32>) and again,  _For it is impossible for those who were once illuminated, and have tasted of the heavenly gift, and then fell away, to renew them again unto repentance_. [ _Hebrews 6:4-6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/heb006.htm#verse4>) It is called also, [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) :  _For as many of you as were_[ __baptized__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) _into Christ did put on_[ __Christ__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). [ _Galatians 3:27_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal003.htm#verse27>) It is called also burial:  _For we were buried_ says he,  _with him, through_[ __baptism__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) _, into death_. [ _Romans 6:4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom006.htm#verse4>) It is called [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) :  _In whom you were also_[ __circumcised__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) _, with a_[ __circumcision__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) _not made with hands, in the putting off of the body of the_[ __sins__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _of the flesh_. [ _Galatians 2:11_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal002.htm#verse11>) It is called a cross:  _Our old man was crucified with him that the body of_[ __sin__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _might be done away_. [ _Romans 6:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom006.htm#verse6>)

  


It is also possible to speak of other names besides these, but in order that we should not spend our whole time over the names of this free gift, come, return to the first name, and let us finish our discourse by declaring its meaning; but in the meantime, let us extend our teaching a little further. There is that laver by means of the baths, common to all [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , which is wont to wipe off bodily uncleanness; and there is the Jewish laver, more [ _honorable_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) than the other, **_but far inferior to that of_**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) ; and it too wipes off bodily uncleanness but not simply uncleanness of body, since it even reaches to the weak [ _conscience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>). For there are many matters, which by nature indeed are not unclean, but which become unclean from the weakness of the [ _conscience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>). And as in the case of little children, masks, and other bugbears are not in themselves alarming, but seem to little children to be alarming, by reason of the weakness of their nature, so it is in the case of those things of which I was speaking; just as to touch dead bodies is not naturally unclean, but when this comes into contact with a weak [ _conscience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>) , it makes him who touches them unclean. For that the thing in question is not unclean naturally, [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) himself who ordained this law showed, when he bore off the entire corpse of Joseph, and yet remained clean. On this account [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) also, discoursing to us about this uncleanness which does not come naturally but by reason of the weakness of the [ _conscience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04268a.htm>) , speaks somewhat in this way,  _Nothing is common of itself save to him who accounts anything to be common_. [ _Romans 14:14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom014.htm#verse14>) Do you not see that uncleanness does not arise from the [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of the thing, but from the weakness of the reasoning about it? And again:  _All things indeed are clean, howbeit it is_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _to that man who eats with offense_. [ _Romans 14:20_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom014.htm#verse20>) Do you see that it is not to eat, but to eat with offense, that is the [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) of uncleanness?…

THE THIRD INSTRUCTION…

  


The Many Graces of Baptism

  


5\. Let us say again: _Blessed be God, who alone does wonderful things_ ,8 who does all things and transforms them. Before yesterday you were captives, but now you are free and citizens of the Church; lately you lived in the shame of your sins, but now you live in freedom and justice. You are not only free, but also holy; not only holy, but also just; not only just, but also sons; not only sons, but also heirs; not only heirs, but also brothers of Christ; not only brothers of Christ, but also joint heirs; not only joint heirs, but also members; not only members, but also the temple; not only the temple, but also instruments of the Spirit.9

  


6\. _Blessed be God, who alone does wonderful things!_ 10 ** _You have seen how numerous are the gifts of baptism_**. Although many men think **__that the only gift it confers is the remission of sins__**** _, we have counted its honors to the number of ten_**.11 It is on this account that we baptize even infants, although they are sinless,12 that they may be given the further gifts **of sanctification, justice, filial adoption, and inheritance, that they may be brothers and members of Christ, and become dwelling places for the Spirit** …

  


THE NINTH INSTRUCTION

  


(Montf. 1 and PK 1)

  


To Those About to Be Baptized. **__Why It Is Called Bath of Regeneration__** and Not the Bath of the Forgiveness of Sins.

  


That It Is Dangerous Not Only to Swear Falsely But Also to Swear, Even If We Take a True Oath.1

  


1\. How I have loved and longed for2 the throng of my new3 brethren! For I call you brothers **__even now before the hour of your birth__** , and I rejoice in the kinship between us, even though you have not yet been born. Since I know and well understand to what a great honor and into what a great realm you will be introduced, I will do what people generally do when a man is going to acquire ruling power. Such a man is held in honor by all who have an eye to storing up future good will for themselves by paying court to him even before he actually succeeds to power. Let me do that now, for you will not be introduced into some poor dominion, but into the kingdom itself; not merely into any kingdom, but into the very kingdom of heaven. Therefore, I beg and entreat you to remember me when you come into that kingdom.4 ([__Baptismal instructions: John Chrysostom__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://archive.org/details/20191212st.johnchrysostombaptismalinstructions>)[ _; Paul W. **St. Basil**

  


**Chapter 10**

  


**Against those who say that it is not right to rank the Holy Spirit with the Father and the Son.**

  


24\. But we must proceed to attack our opponents, in the endeavour to confute those oppositions advanced against us which are derived from [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) [ _falsely_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>) so-called.

  


It is not permissible, they assert, for the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) to be ranked with the Father and Son, on account of the difference of His nature and the inferiority of His dignity. Against them it is right to reply in the words of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) ,  _We ought to_[ __obey__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) _God rather than men_. [ _Acts 5:29_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/act005.htm#verse29>)

  


For if our Lord, **__when enjoining the__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** __of__**[** __salvation__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) , charged His [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) **to**[** _baptize_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)**all nations in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the**[** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , [ _Matthew 28:19_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat028.htm#verse19>) not disdaining fellowship with Him, and these men allege that we must not rank Him with the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , is it not clear that they openly withstand the commandment of God? If they deny that coordination of this kind is declaratory of any fellowship and conjunction, let them tell us why it behooves us to hold this opinion, and what more intimate mode of conjunction they have.

  


If the Lord did not indeed conjoin the Spirit with the Father and Himself in [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , do not let them lay the blame of conjunction upon us, for we neither hold nor say anything different. If on the contrary the Spirit is there conjoined with the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and no one is so shameless as to say anything else, then let them not lay blame on us for following the words of Scripture.

  


25\. But all the apparatus of [ _war_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15546c.htm>) has been got ready against us; every intellectual missile is aimed at us; and now [ _blasphemers_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) ‘ tongues shoot and hit and hit again, yet harder than Stephen of old was smitten by the killers of the Christ. And do not let them succeed in concealing the fact that, while an attack on us serves for a pretext for the [ _war_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15546c.htm>) , the real aim of these proceedings is higher. It is against us, they say, that they are preparing their engines and their snares; against us that they are shouting to one another, according to each one’s strength or cunning, to come on. **Chapter 12**

  


**Against those who assert that the baptism in the name of the Father alone is sufficient.**

[ _Luke 3:16_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk003.htm#verse16>) But no one on this account **_would be justified in calling that_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _a perfect_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _wherein only the name of the Spirit was invoked_**. **St. Jerome**

  


2\. To what does all this tend, you ask. I reply; you remember the question that you proposed. It was this. A Spanish [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) named Carterius, old in years and in the [ _priesthood_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>) has married two wives, one before he was [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , and, she having died, another since he has passed through the laver; and you are of opinion that he has violated the precept of the apostle, who in his list of episcopal qualifications commands that a [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) shall be the husband of one wife. [ _1 Timothy 3:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti003.htm#verse2>) I am surprised that you have pilloried an individual when the whole world is filled with [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) ordained in similar circumstances; I do not mean [ _presbyters_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) or [ _clergy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04049b.htm>) of lower rank, but speak only of [ _bishops_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of whom if I were to enumerate them all one by one I should gather a sufficient number to surpass the crowd which attended the synod of Ariminum. **Still it does not become me to defend one by incriminating many;** **nor if reason condemns a**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)**, to make the number of those who commit it an excuse for it.** At Rome an eloquent pleader caught me, as the phrase goes, between the horns of a dilemma: whichever way I turned I was held fast. Is it [ _sinful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , said he, to marry a wife, or is it not [ _sinful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)? I in my simplicity, not being wary enough to avoid the snare laid for me, replied that it was not [ _sinful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). Then he propounded another question: **__Is it good__**[** __deeds__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01115a.htm>)** __which are done away with in__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** __or is it__**[** __evil__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>)** __? Here again my simplicity induced me to say that it was__**[** __sins__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** __which were forgiven__**. At this point, just as I began to fancy myself secure, the horns of the dilemma commenced to close in on me from this side and from that and their points hidden before began to show themselves. If, said he, to marry a wife is not [ _sinful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , **__and if__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** __forgives__**[** __sins__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , all that is not done away with is held over. On the instant a dark mist rose before my eyes as though I had been struck by a strong boxer. Yet recalling the sophism attributed to Chrysippus: Whether you lie or whether you speak the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , in either case you lie, I came to myself again and turned upon my opponent with a dilemma of my own.

  


Pray tell me, I said, **__does__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** __make a new man or does it not? He grudgingly admitted that it did. I pursued my advantage by saying, Does it make him wholly new or only partially so? He replied, Wholly. Then I asked, Is there nothing then of the old man held over in__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** __? He assented__**. Hereupon I propounded the argument; **__If__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** __makes a man new and creates a wholly new being, and if there is nothing of the old man held over in the new, that which once was in the old cannot be imputed to the new__**. At first my thorny friend held his tongue; afterwards however, making Piso’s mistake, though he had nothing to say he could not remain silent. Sweat stood upon his brow, his cheeks turned pale, his lips trembled, his tongue clove to his mouth, his throat became dry; and [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) (not age) made him cower. At last he broke out in these words, Have you not read how the apostle permits none to be ordained [ _priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) save the husband of one wife, and that what he lays stress upon is the fact of the marriage and not the time at which it is contracted? Now as the fellow had challenged me with syllogisms, and as I saw that he was feeling his way towards some intricate and awkward questions, I proceeded to turn his own weapons against him. I said therefore, Whom did the apostle select for the [ _episcopate_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>) , [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) or [ _catechumens_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03430b.htm>)? He refused to reply. I however made a fresh onslaught repeating my question a second time and a third. You would have taken him for Niobe changed to [ _stone_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14308a.htm>) by excessive weeping. I turned to the audience and said: It is all the same to me, good people, whether I bind my opponent awake or sleeping; but it is easier to fetter a man who offers no resistance. If those whom the apostle admits into the ranks of the [ _clergy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04049b.htm>) are not [ _catechumens_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03430b.htm>) but the [ _faithful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm>) , and if he who is ordained [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) is always one of the [ _faithful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm>) , being one of the faithful he cannot have the faults of a catechumen imputed to him. Such were the darts I hurled at my paralysed opponent. Such the quivering spears I cast at him. At last his mouth opened and he vomited forth the contents of his [ _mind_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10321a.htm>). Certainly, he blurted out, that is the doctrine of the [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) …

  


4\. [ _Matthew 7:5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat007.htm#verse5>) Truly like the [ _scribes_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13634a.htm>) and pharisees we strain out the gnat and swallow the camel, we pay [ _tithe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14741b.htm>) of mint and anise, and we omit the just judgment which [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) requires. What parallel can be drawn between a wife and a prostitute? **Is it fair to make a marriage now dissolved by death a ground of accusation, while dissolute living wins for itself a garland of praise?** He, had his former wife lived, would not have married another; but as for you, how can you defend the bestial unions you indiscriminately make? Perhaps indeed you will say that you feared to contract marriage lest by so doing you might disqualify yourself for ordination. He took a wife that he might have children by her; you by taking a harlot have lost the hope of children. He withdrew into the privacy of his own chamber when he sought to [ _obey_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) nature and to win God’s blessing:  _Be fruitful and multiply and replenish the earth._ [ _Genesis 1:28_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse28>) You on the contrary outraged public decency in the hot eagerness of your [ _lust_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09438a.htm>). He covered a lawful indulgence beneath a veil of modesty; you pursued an unlawful one shamelessly before the eyes of all. For him it is written  _Marriage is_[ __honourable__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _and the bed undefiled_ , while to you the words are read,  _but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge_ , [ _Hebrews 13:4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/heb013.htm#verse4>) and  _if any man destroys the temple of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, him shall God destroy_. ** __All iniquities, we are told, are forgiven us at our__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , and when once we have received God’s mercy we need not afterwards dread from Him the severity of a judge. The apostle says:—  _And such were some of you: but you are washed, but you are sanctified, but you are justified in the name of the_[ __Lord Jesus__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, and by the Spirit of our God_. [ _1 Corinthians 6:11_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co006.htm#verse11>) ** __All__**[** __sins__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** __then are forgiven__** ; it is an honest and faithful saying. But I ask you** __, how comes it that, while your uncleanness is washed away, my cleanness is made unclean__**? You reply, No, it is not made unclean, it remains just what it was. Had it been uncleanness, it would have been washed away like mine. I want to [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) what you mean by this shuffling. Your remarks seem to have no more point in them than the round end of a pestle. Is a thing [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) because it is not [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)? Or is a thing unclean because it is not unclean? The Lord, you say, has not forgiven because He had nothing to forgive; yet because He has not forgiven, that which has not been forgiven still remains…

[ _Acts 19:1-7_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/act019.htm#verse1>) **The voice of the Lord is upon the waters** …**The Lord is upon many waters** …**the Lord makes the flood to inhabit it**.  _His teeth are like a flock of sheep that are even shorn which came up from the washing; whereof everyone bear twins, and none is barren among them_. [ _Song of Songs 4:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/son004.htm#verse2>) If none is barren among them, all of them must have udders filled with milk and be able to say with the apostle:  _You are my little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you_ ; [ _Galatians 4:19_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal004.htm#verse19>)  _and I have fed you with milk and not with meat_. [ _1 Corinthians 3:2_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1co003.htm#verse2>) ** _And it is to the_**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)** _of_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _that the_**[** _prophecy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>)** _of Micah refers_** :  _He will turn again, he will have compassion upon us: he will subdue our iniquities,_ ** __and will cast all our__**[** __sins__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** __into the depths of the sea__**. [ _Micah 7:19_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mic007.htm#verse19>)

  


7\. How then can you say **__that all__**[** __sins__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** __are drowned in the baptismal laver__** if a man’s wife is still to swim on the surface as evidence against him? The psalmist says:—  _Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose_[ __sin__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _is covered. Blessed is the man unto whom the Lord imputes not iniquity_. It would seem that we must add something to this song and say Blessed is the man to whom the Lord imputes not a wife. Let us hear also the declaration which Ezekiel the so called [ _son of man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) [ _Ezekiel 2:1_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/eze002.htm#verse1>) makes concerning the [ _virtue_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) of him who is to be the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _son of man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>) , the [ _Christian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) :  _I will take you_ , he says,  _from among the heathen_ … _then will I sprinkle clean water upon you, and you shall be clean from all your filthiness_ … _a new heart also will I give you and a new spirit. From all your filthiness he says, will I cleanse you_. If all is taken away nothing can be left. If filthiness is cleansed, how much more is cleanness kept from defilement.  _A new heart also will I give you and a new spirit_. Yes, for in Christ Jesus neither [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) avails anything nor uncircumcision but a new nature. Wherefore the song also which we sing is a new song, [ _Revelation 14:3_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rev014.htm#verse3>) and putting off the old man [ _Ephesians 4:22_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/eph004.htm#verse22>) we walk not in the oldness of the letter but in the newness of the spirit. [ _Romans 7:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom007.htm#verse6>) This is the new stone wherein the new name is written, which no man [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) saving he that receives it. [ _Revelation 2:17_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rev002.htm#verse17>)  _Do you not_[ __know__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , says the apostle,  _that so many of us as were_[ __baptized__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) _into_[ __Jesus Christ__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _were_[ __baptized__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) _into his death? Therefore we are buried with him by_[ __baptism__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) _into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the_[ __glory__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) _of the_[ __Father__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, even so we also should walk in newness of life_. [ _Romans 6:3-4_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom006.htm#verse3>) Do we read so often of newness and of making new and yet can no renewing efface the stain which the word wife brings with it? _We are buried with Christ by_[ __baptism__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) _and we have risen again by_[ __faith__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) _in the working of God who has called Him from the dead. And when we were dead in our_[ __sins__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _and in the uncircumcision of our flesh, God has quickened us together with Him, having forgiven us all trespasses; blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way nailing it to His cross_. [ _Colossians 2:13-14_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/col002.htm#verse13>) Can it be that when our whole being is dead with Christ and when all the [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) noted down in the old handwriting are blotted out, the one word wife alone lives on? **_Time would fail me were I to try to lay before you in order_**** __all the passages in the__**[** __Holy Scriptures__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>)** __which relate to the efficacy of__**[** __baptism__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _or to explain the_**[** _mysterious_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)** _doctrine of that second birth_** which though it is our second is yet our first in [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250812221915/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) …
