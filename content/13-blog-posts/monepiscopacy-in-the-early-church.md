# MONEPISCOPACY IN THE EARLY CHURCH

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 18 min read


Monepiscopacy, also called monarchical episcopate, refers to a single bishop chosen to preside and rule over the church with a college of presbyters and deacons. The evidence shows that this was an early and widespread practice of the universal church. In fact, a strong case can be made that this structure was already in place during the lifetime of the Apostles, or at least around the time that John the Apostle was alive and wrote his Apocalypse (ca. 90-95 A.D.).

  


I share some of that evidence for the benefit of all serious students of the Holy Bible and the early church. All emphasis is mine.

  


**IGNATIUS**

  


Ignatius was the bishop of Antioch, Syria where believers were first called Christians and which was a hotbed of apostolic and prophetic activity:

  


“So Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul;**** and when he had found him, he brought him to Antioch. For a whole year they met with the church, and taught a large company of people; **and in Antioch the disciples were for the first time called Christians**. Now in these days **prophets came down from Jerusalem to Antioch**.And one of them named Ag′abus stood up and foretold **by the Spirit** that there would be a great famine over all the world; and this took place in the days of Claudius.And the disciples determined, every one according to his ability, to send relief to the brethren who lived in Judea;and they did so, sending it to the elders by the hand of Barnabas and Saul.” Acts 11:25-30

On his way to willingly becoming a martyr in Rome he wrote seven letters to specific Churches, some of which included congregations which the blessed Apostle Paul had personally written to.

  


Ignatius’ witness is therefore essential since he had been personally taught and appointed by the very disciples of Christ and inspired authors of the books that eventually became a part of the New Testament canon. Therefore, if anyone got the offices of the church it would definitely be this holy and blessed martyr of Christ.

Ignatius’s letters affirm that the monepiscopacy was an already well established and widespread structure, which needed no explanation or defense.

  


Ignatius evinces as hierarchy which consisted of one chief bishop, a college of presbyters, and a group of deacons. Ignatius does not tire of exhorting the churches to be in perfect submission to the bishop, doing nothing without him, since he stands in the place of God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. To, therefore, submit to him is to submit to God himself.

Chapter 1. Praise of the bishop

  


Which [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , I [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , **obtained the ministry** which pertains to the common [good], **not of himself, neither by**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**,**[**_Galatians 1:1_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gal001.htm#verse1>)**nor through vainglory, but by the**[** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>)**of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and the**[** _Lord Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) ; at whose meekness I am struck with admiration, and who by his silence is able to accomplish more than those who vainly talk. For he is in harmony with the commandments [of God], even as the harp is with its strings. Wherefore my [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) declares his mind towards God a [ _happy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07131b.htm>) one, [ _knowing_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) it to be [ _virtuous_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) and perfect, and that his stability as well as freedom from all [ _anger_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01489a.htm>) is after the example of the [ _infinite_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08004a.htm>) meekness of the living God.

  


Chapter 2. Maintain union with the bishop

  


Wherefore, as children of light and [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , flee from division and [ _wicked_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) doctrines; but where the shepherd is, **there follow as sheep**. For there are many wolves that appear worthy of credit, who, by means of a pernicious pleasure, carry captive [ _2 Timothy 3:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2ti003.htm#verse6>) those that are running towards [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; but in your unity they shall have no place.

  


Chapter 3. Avoid schismatics

  


Keep yourselves from those [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) plants which [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) does not tend, because they are not the planting of the Father. Not that I have found any division among you, but exceeding purity. For as many as are of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and of [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) **are also with the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). And as many as shall, in the exercise of repentance, return into the unity of the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , these, too, shall belong to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , that they may live according to [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). Do not [ _err_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) , my brethren. If any man follows him that makes a [ _schism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13529a.htm>) in the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , he shall not inherit the [ _kingdom of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>). If any one walks according to a strange opinion, he agrees not with the passion [of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>).].

  


Chapter 4. Have but one Eucharist, etc.

  


Take heed, then, to have but one [ _Eucharist_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05572c.htm>). For there is one flesh of [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and one cup to [show forth ] the unity of His blood; one altar; **as there is one**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, along with the**[** _presbytery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>)**and**[** _deacons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>)**, my fellow-servants** : that so, whatsoever you do, you may do it according to God. ([_Epistle to the Philadelphians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0108.htm>))

  


Chapter 8. Let nothing be done without the bishop

  


**See that you all follow the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, even as**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**does the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and the**[** _presbytery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>)**as you would the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) ; and reverence the [ _deacons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>) , as being the institution of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **Let no man do anything connected with the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**without the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). Let that be deemed a proper [ _Eucharist_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05572c.htm>) , which is [administered] **either by the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, or by one to whom he has entrusted it**. Wherever the [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) shall appear, there let the multitude [of the people] also be; **even as, wherever**[** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**is, there is the**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>)****[**_Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). It is not lawful without the [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) either to [ _baptize_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) or to celebrate a [ _love-feast_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01200b.htm>) ; but whatsoever he shall approve of, that is also pleasing to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , so that everything that is done may be secure and valid.

  


Chapter 9. Honour the bishop

  


Moreover, it is in accordance with reason that we should return to soberness [of conduct], and, while yet we have opportunity, exercise repentance towards God. **It is well to reverence both God and the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**. He who honours the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**has been honoured by**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**; he who does anything without the**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**of the**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, does [in reality] serve the**[** _devil_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>). Let all things, then, abound to you through [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) , for you are worthy. You have refreshed me in all things, and [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) [shall refresh] you. You have loved me when absent as well as when present. May God recompense you, for whose sake, while you endure all things, you shall attain unto Him. ([**_Epistle to the Smyrnæans_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0109.htm>))

  


**JEROME**

  


The next witness is the venerable church theologian and bible translator extraordinare St. Jerome. Writing in the 4th-5th centuries, Jerome’s testimony further corroborates that the monarchical episcopate was already well established from even before the times of Ignatius’ own bishopric.

  


Note what this brilliant scholar states in respect to the monepiscopacy:

1\. We read in Isaiah the words, the fool will speak folly, and I am told that some one has been mad enough to put [** _deacons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>) before [** _presbyters_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) , that is, before [** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). For when the apostle clearly teaches that [** _presbyters_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) are the same as [** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , must not a mere server of tables and of [** _widows_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15617c.htm>) [** _Acts 6:1-2_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/act006.htm#verse1>) be insane to set himself up arrogantly over men through whose [** _prayers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) the body and blood of Christ are produced? Do you ask for [** _proof_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) of what I say? Listen to this passage: [** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) and Timotheus, the servants of [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , to all the [** _saints_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>) in Christ Jesus which are at Philippi with the [** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) and [** _deacons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>). Do you wish for another instance? In the Acts of the Apostles [** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) thus speaks to the [** _priests_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) of a single church: Take heed unto yourselves and to all the flock, in the which the [** _Holy Ghost_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) has made you [** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) , to feed the church of God which He purchased with His own blood. And lest any should in a spirit of contention argue that there must then have been more [** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) than one in a single church, there is the following passage which clearly proves a [** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) and a [** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) to be the same. Writing to Titus the apostle says: For this [** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) left I you in Crete, that you should set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain [** _presbyters_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) in every city, as I had appointed you: if any be blameless, the husband of one wife, having faithful children not accused of riot or unruly. For a [** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) must be blameless as the steward of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). [** _Titus 1:5-7_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/tit001.htm#verse5>) And to Timothy he says: Neglect not the gift that is in you, which was given you by [** _prophecy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>) , with the laying on of the hands of the [** _presbytery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>). [** _1 Timothy 4:14_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1ti004.htm#verse14>) Peter also says in his first epistle: The [** _presbyters_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) which are among you I exhort, who am your fellow [** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) and a [** _witness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of the sufferings of Christ and also a partaker of the [** _glory_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) that shall be revealed: feed the flock of Christ. ..taking the oversight thereof not by constraint but willingly, according unto [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). In the Greek the meaning is still plainer, for the word used is επισκοποῦντες, that is to say, overseeing, and this is the origin of the name overseer or [** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). But perhaps the testimony of these great men seems to you insufficient. If so, then listen to the blast of the gospel trumpet, that son of thunder, [** _Mark 3:17_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mar003.htm#verse17>) the [** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) whom Jesus loved [** _John 13:23_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh013.htm#verse23>) and who reclining on the Saviour’s breast drank in the waters of sound doctrine. One of his letters begins thus: The [** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) unto the elect lady and her children whom I [** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) in the [** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) ; and another thus: The [** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) unto the well-beloved Gaius whom I [** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) in the [** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). **When subsequently ONE**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**was chosen TO PRESIDE OVER THE REST, this was done to remedy**[** _schism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13529a.htm>)**and to prevent each individual from rending the church of Christ by drawing it to himself. For even at Alexandria from the time of**[** _Mark the Evangelist_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09672c.htm>)**until the episcopates of Heraclas and Dionysius the**[** _presbyters_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**ALWAYS NAMED AS BISHOP ONE OF THEIR OWN NUMBER chosen by themselves and set in a more exalted position, just as an army elects a general, or as**[** _deacons_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>)**appoint one of themselves whom they**[** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**to be diligent and call him archdeacon**. For what function, excepting ordination, belongs to a [** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) that does not also belong to a [** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)? It is not the case that there is one church at [** _Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) and another in all the world beside. [** _Gaul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06395b.htm>) and Britain, Africa and [** _Persia_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11712a.htm>) , India and the East worship one Christ and observe one rule of [** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). If you ask for authority, the world outweighs its capital. **Wherever there is a**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**, whether it be at**[** _Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)**or at Engubium, whether it be at Constantinople or at Rhegium, whether it be at Alexandria or at Zoan, his dignity is one and his**[** _priesthood_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12409a.htm>)**is one**. Neither the command of [** _wealth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15571a.htm>) nor the lowliness of poverty makes him more a [** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) or less a [** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>). **All alike are successors of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). ([**_LETTER 146_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3001146.htm>) — TO EVANGELUS (?))

  


Elsewhere, Jerome not only confirms that Peter’s companion and Gospel writer John Mark established the church in Egypt, where he presided as the first bishop, he also attests that the Apostles had appointed James the so-called brother of the risen Lord as the first bishop of the church in Jerusalem:

  


1\. Simon Peter

[ _Peter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11744a.htm>) also mentions this Mark in his first epistle, figuratively indicating Rome under the name of [ _Babylon_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02179b.htm>) She who is in [ _Babylon_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250908212836/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02179b.htm>) elect together with you salutes you and so does Mark my son.
