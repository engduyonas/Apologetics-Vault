# Mary’s Parents: Descendants of David

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Jan 5
  * 4 min read


In this short post I will cite some references from apocryphal and/or pseudepigraphical writings stating that Mary and her parents were physical descendants of David, and from the tribe of Judah. All emphasis will be mine.

10\. And there was a council of the [ _priests_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) , saying: Let us make a veil for the temple of the Lord. And the [ _priest_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) said: **_Call to me the undefiled_**[** _virgins_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>)** _of the_**[** _family_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)** _of David_**. And the officers went away, and sought, and found seven [ _virgins_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15458a.htm>). **_And the_**[** _priest_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)** _remembered the child Mary, that she was of the_**[** _family_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)** _of David, and undefiled before God_**. And the officers went away and brought her. And they brought them into the temple of the Lord. And the [ _priest_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) said: Choose for me by lot who shall spin the gold, and the white, and the fine linen, and the silk, and the blue, and the scarlet, and the [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) purple. [ _Exodus 25:4_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/exo025.htm#verse4>) And the [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) purple and the scarlet fell to the lot of [ _Mary_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>) , and she took them, and went away to her house. And at that time Zacharias was dumb, and Samuel was in his place until the time that Zacharias spoke. And Mary took the scarlet, and span it. ([_Protoevangelium of James (c. 150)_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0847.htm>))

Chapter 1.

In those days there was a man in Jerusalem, Joachim by name, of the [ _tribe of Judah_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08536a.htm>). He was the shepherd of his own sheep, fearing the Lord in integrity and singleness of heart. He had no other care than that of his herds, from the produce of which he supplied with food all that feared [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , offering double gifts in the [ _fear_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) of God to all who laboured in doctrine, and who ministered unto Him. Therefore his lambs, and his sheep, and his wool, and all things whatsoever he possessed, he used to divide into three portions: one he gave to the [ _orphans_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11322b.htm>) , the [ _widows_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15617c.htm>) , the strangers, and the poor; the second to those that worshipped [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and the third he kept for himself and all his house. [ _Tobit 1:7_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/tob001.htm#verse7>) And as he did so, the Lord multiplied to him his herds, so that there was no man like him in the people of [ _Israel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>). This now he began to do when he was fifteen years old. **_And at the age of twenty he took to wife Anna, the daughter of Achar, of his own tribe, that is, of the_**[** _tribe of Judah_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08536a.htm>)** _, of the_**[** _family_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)** _of David_**. And though they had lived together for twenty years, he had by her neither sons nor daughters…

Chapter 13.

And it came to pass some little time after, that an enrolment was made according to the edict of Cæsar Augustus, that all the world was to be enrolled, each man in his native place. This enrolment was made by Cyrinus, the governor of [ _Syria_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14399a.htm>). [ _Luke 2:1-6_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk002.htm#verse1>) ** _It was necessary, therefore, that Joseph should enroll with the blessed Mary in_**[** _Bethlehem_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02533a.htm>)** _, because to it_**** __THEY BELONGED__**** _, being of the_**[** _tribe of Judah_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08536a.htm>)** _, and of the house and_**[** _family_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)** _of David_**. When, therefore, Joseph and the blessed Mary were going along the road which leads to [ _Bethlehem_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02533a.htm>) , Mary said to Joseph: I see two peoples before me, the one weeping, and the other rejoicing. And Joseph answered: Sit still on your beast, and do not speak superfluous words. Then there appeared before them a beautiful boy, clothed in white raiment, who said to Joseph: Why did you say that the words which Mary spoke about the two peoples were superfluous? For she saw the people of the [ _Jews_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) weeping, because they have departed from their [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and the people of the [ _Gentiles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) rejoicing, because they have now been added and made near to the Lord, according to that which He promised to our fathers [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , Isaac, and Jacob: for the time is at hand when in the seed of [ _Abraham_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) all nations shall be blessed. [ _Genesis 12:3_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen012.htm#verse3>) ([_Gospel of Pseudo-Matthew (c. 400)_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0848.htm>))

Chapter 1.

** _The blessed and_**[** _glorious_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)** _ever-virgin Mary, sprung from the royal stock and_**[** _family_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>)** _of David_** , born in the city of [ _Nazareth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10725a.htm>) , was brought up at Jerusalem in the temple of the Lord. Her father was named Joachim, and her mother Anna. Her father's house was from [ _Galilee_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06341c.htm>) and the city of [ _Nazareth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10725a.htm>) , but her mother's [ _family_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>) from [ _Bethlehem_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02533a.htm>). Their life was guileless and right before the Lord, and irreproachable and [ _pious_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>) before men. For they divided all their substance into three parts. One part they spent upon the temple and the temple servants; another they distributed to strangers and the poor; the third they reserved, for themselves and the necessities of their [ _family_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05782a.htm>). Thus, dear to [ _God_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , kind to [ _men_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , for about twenty years they lived in their own house, a chaste married life, without having any children. Nevertheless they vowed that, should the Lord happen to give them offspring, they would deliver it to the service of the Lord; on which account also they used to visit the temple of the Lord at each of the feasts during the year. ([_Gospel of the Nativity of Mary (unknown date; late)_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0849.htm>))
