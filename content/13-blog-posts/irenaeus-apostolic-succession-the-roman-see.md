# Irenaeus, Apostolic Succession & the Roman See

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 5 min read


The following is taken from St. Irenaeus’ [_Adversus haereses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103.htm>) , Book III. All emphasis will be mine.

  


[**_Chapter 3_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103303.htm>) ** _A refutation of the heretics, from the fact that, in the various Churches, a perpetual succession of bishops was kept up._**

  


1\. It is within the power of all, therefore, in every [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , who may wish to see the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , **_to contemplate clearly the tradition of the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _manifested throughout the whole world; and we are in a position to reckon up those who were by the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _instituted_**[** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _in the_**[** _Churches_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _, and [to demonstrate] the succession of these men to our own times_** ; those who neither taught nor [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of anything like what these [[_heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)] rave about. For if the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) had [ _known_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) hidden [ _mysteries_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) , which they were in the [ _habit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07099b.htm>) of imparting to the perfect apart and privily from the rest, **_they would have delivered them especially to those to whom they were also committing the Churches themselves. For they were desirous that these men should be very perfect and blameless in all things, whom also they were leaving behind as their successors, delivering up their own place of government to these men_** ; which men, if they discharged their functions honestly, would be a great boon [to the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)], but if they should fall away, the direst calamity.

  


2\. Since, however, it would be very tedious, in such a volume as this, to reckon up the successions of all the [ _Churches_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , we do put to confusion all those who, in whatever manner, whether by an [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) self-pleasing, by vainglory, or by blindness and perverse opinion, assemble in unauthorized meetings; [we do this, I say,] **_by indicating that tradition derived from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, of the very great, the very ancient, and universally_**[** _known_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** __**[**_Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _founded and organized at_**[** _Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)** _by the two most_**[** _glorious_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)** __**[**_apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, Peter and_**[** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ; as also [by pointing out] the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) preached to [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , **_which comes down to our time by means of the successions of the_**[** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _. For it is a matter OF NECESSITY that EVERY_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _should AGREE WITH THIS CHURCH, on account of its preeminent authority [potiorem principalitatem]_**.

3\. The blessed [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , then, having founded and built up the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , committed into the hands of Linus the office of the [ _episcopate_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>). **Of this Linus,**[**_Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**makes mention in the Epistles to Timothy**. To him succeeded Anacletus; and after him, **_in the third place from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, Clement was allotted the_**[** _bishopric_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _. This man, as he had seen the blessed_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, and had been conversant with them, might be said to have the preaching of the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _still echoing [in his ears], and their traditions before his eyes. Nor was he alone [in this], for there were many still remaining who had received instructions from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). In the time of this Clement, no small dissension having occurred among the brethren at [ _Corinth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04363b.htm>) , the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) in Rome dispatched a most powerful letter to the Corinthians, exhorting them to peace, renewing their [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , and declaring the tradition which it had lately received from the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , proclaiming the one [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , [ _omnipotent_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11251c.htm>) , the Maker of heaven and earth, the Creator of [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , who brought on the deluge, and called [ _Abraham_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , who led the people from the land of [ _Egypt_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) , spoke with [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) , set forth the law, sent the [ _prophets_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , and who has prepared fire for the [ _devil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) and his [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>). From this document, whosoever chooses to do so, may learn that He, the Father of [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , **was preached by the**[** _Churches_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, and may also understand the**[** _apostolic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**tradition of the**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**, since this Epistle is of older date than these men who are now propagating**[** _falsehood_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>) , and who conjure into [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) another god beyond the Creator and the Maker of all existing things. To this Clement there succeeded Evaristus. Alexander followed Evaristus; then, sixth from the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , Sixtus was appointed; after him, Telephorus, who was [ _gloriously_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) [ _martyred_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) ; then [ _Hyginus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07593a.htm>) ; after him, [ _Pius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12126b.htm>) ; then after him, [ _Anicetus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>). [ _Soter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03144c.htm>) having succeeded [ _Anicetus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) , [ _Eleutherius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05378a.htm>) does now, in the twelfth place from the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , hold the inheritance of the [ _episcopate_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05001a.htm>). **_In this order, and by this succession, the_**[** _ecclesiastical tradition_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15006b.htm>)** _from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, and the preaching of the_**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** _, have come down to us. And this is most abundant_**[** _proof_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>)** _that there is one and the same vivifying_**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)** _, which has been preserved in the_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _until now, and handed down in_**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>).

  


4\. **_But_**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)** _also was not only instructed by_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, and conversed with many who had seen Christ, but was also, by_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _in Asia, appointed_**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)** _of the_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _in_**[** _Smyrna_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14060b.htm>)** _, whom I also saw in my early youth, for he tarried [on earth] a very long time, and, when a very old man,_**[**_gloriously_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>)** _and most nobly suffering_**[** _martyrdom_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>)** _, departed this life, having always taught the things which he had learned from the_**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** _, and which the_**[** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)** _has handed down, and which alone are_**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). To these things all the Asiatic Churches testify, **as do also those men who have succeeded**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**down to the present time** — a man who was of much greater weight, and a more steadfast [ _witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , than [ _Valentinus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15256a.htm>) , and [ _Marcion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , and the rest of the [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). He it was who, coming to [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) in the time of [ _Anicetus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) caused many to turn away from the aforesaid [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) to the [ _Church of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , proclaiming that he had received this one and sole [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) from the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) — that, namely, which is handed down by the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). **There are also those who heard from him that John, the**[** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**of the Lord, going to bathe at**[** _Ephesus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>)**, and perceiving**[** _Cerinthus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03539a.htm>)**within, rushed out of the bath-house without bathing, exclaiming,****_Let us fly, lest even the bath-house fall down, because_**[** __Cerinthus__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03539a.htm>)** _, the enemy of the_**[** __truth__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)** _, is within._** **And**[** _Polycarp_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>)**himself replied to**[** _Marcion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>)**, who met him on one occasion, and said,****_Do you_**[** __know__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _me? I do_**[** __know__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _you, the_**[** __first-born__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>)** _of_**[** __Satan__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>)**. Such was the horror which the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**and their**[** _disciples_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**had against holding even verbal communication with any corrupters of the**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) ; as [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) also says,  _A man that is an_[ __heretic__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) _, after the first and second admonition, reject;_[__knowing__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _that he that is such is subverted, and_[ __sins__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) _, being condemned of himself._ [ _Titus 3:10_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/tit003.htm#verse10>) **There is also a very powerful Epistle of Polycarp written to the Philippians, from which those who choose to do so, and are anxious about their**[** _salvation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>)**, can learn the character of his**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**, and the preaching of the**[** _truth_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). Then, again, the [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) in [ _Ephesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) , founded by [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , and having John remaining among them permanently until the times of [ _Trajan_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15015a.htm>) , is a [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) of the tradition of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250902091032/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>).
