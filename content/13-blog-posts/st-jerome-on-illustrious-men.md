# St. Jerome on Illustrious Men

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 4, 2025
  * 24 min read


I quote a part of St. Jerome’s [_De Viris Illustribus (Illustrious Men)_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2708.htm>) , in which he writes in respect to some of the most influential Christians that ever lived, starting with specific Apostles of our Lord. The details he gives about their lives, works and even deaths are truly remarkable and humbling. What makes his work all the more interesting is that he even mentions the Jewish historian Flavius Josephus and Philo the Alexandrian, claiming that Philo actually met St. Peter and St. Mark and was so moved by the piety of the Christians that he even wrote about them!

All emphasis will be mine.

  


1\. Simon Peter

  


[ _Simon Peter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11744a.htm>) the son of John, from the village of [ _Bethsaida_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02536a.htm>) in the province of [ _Galilee_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06341c.htm>) , brother of [ _Andrew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01471a.htm>) the [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , **and himself chief of the**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**, after having been**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of the**[** _church of Antioch_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01567a.htm>) and having preached to the [ _Dispersion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04775c.htm>) — the believers in [ _circumcision_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>) , in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia — **pushed on to**[** _Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)**in the second year of Claudius to overthrow**[** _Simon Magus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13797b.htm>)**, and held the sacerdotal chair there for twenty-five years until the last, that is the fourteenth, year of**[** _Nero_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>)**. At his hands he received the crown of**[** _martyrdom_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>)**being nailed to the cross with his head towards the ground and his feet raised on high,****_asserting that he was unworthy to be crucified in the same manner as his_**[** _Lord_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**. He wrote two**[** _epistles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05509a.htm>)**which are called**[** _Catholic_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03453a.htm>) , the second of which, on account of its difference from the first in style, is considered by many not to be by him. **_Then too the_**[** _Gospel according to Mark_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mar000.htm>)** _, who was his_**[** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)** _and interpreter, is ascribed to him_**. On the other hand, the books, of which one is entitled his Acts, another his Gospel, a third his Preaching, a fourth his Revelation, a fifth his Judgment are rejected as [ _apocryphal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01601a.htm>).

  


Buried at [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) in the [ _Vatican_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15276b.htm>) near the triumphal way **he is**[** _venerated_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05188b.htm>)**by the whole world**.

  


2\. James the Just

  


[** _James_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>)**,****_who is called the_**[** _brother of the Lord_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02767a.htm>)** _, surnamed the Just, the son of_**[** _Joseph_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08504a.htm>)** _by another wife, as some think_****, but, as appears to me, the son of**[** _Mary sister of the mother of our Lord_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09748b.htm>)**of whom**[** _John_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08492a.htm>)**makes mention in his book** , after [ _our Lord’s passion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11527b.htm>) at once ordained by the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of [ _Jerusalem_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08344a.htm>) , wrote a single [ _epistle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05509a.htm>) , which is reckoned among the seven [ _Catholic Epistles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03453a.htm>) **and even this is claimed by some to have been published by some one else under his name** , and gradually, as time went on, to have gained authority. [ _Hegesippus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07194a.htm>) , who lived near the [ _apostolic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) age, in the fifth book of his Commentaries, writing of [ _James_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>) , says,

_After the_[ __apostles__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) _,_[__James__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>) _the_[ __brother of the Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02767a.htm>) _surnamed the Just was made head of the_[ __Church__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) _at_[ __Jerusalem__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08344a.htm>) _. Many indeed are called James. This one was_[ __holy__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) _from his mother’s womb. He drank neither_[ __wine nor strong drink__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01274a.htm>) _, ate no flesh, never shaved or anointed himself with ointment or bathed. He alone had the privilege of entering the Holy of Holies, since indeed he did not use woolen vestments but linen and went alone into the_[ __temple__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14499a.htm>) _and_[ __prayed__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) _in behalf of the people, insomuch that his knees were reputed to have acquired the hardness of camels’ knees._

  


He says also many other things, too numerous to mention. [** _Josephus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08522a.htm>)**also in the 20th book of his Antiquities, and**[** _Clement_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04045a.htm>)**in the 7th of his Outlines mention** that on the death of Festus who reigned over [ _Judea_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08544a.htm>) , Albinus was sent by [ _Nero_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) as his successor. Before he had reached his province, Ananias the [ _high priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12407b.htm>) , the youthful son of Ananus of the [ _priestly_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) class, taking advantage of the state of anarchy, assembled a council **and publicly tried to force**[** _James_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>)**to deny that**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**is the**[** _son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). When he refused Ananius ordered him to be [ _stoned_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14308a.htm>).**Cast down from a pinnacle of the**[** _temple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14499a.htm>)**, his legs broken, but still half alive, raising his hands to heaven he said,**[**__Lord__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _forgive them for they_**[** __know__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _not what they do_****. Then struck on the head by the club of a fuller such a club as fullers are accustomed to wring out garments with — he died**.

**This same**[** _Josephus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08522a.htm>)**records the**[** _tradition_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15006b.htm>)**that this**[** _James_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>)**was of so great**[** _sanctity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>)**and**[** _reputation_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12776c.htm>)**among the people that the downfall of**[** _Jerusalem_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08344a.htm>)**was believed to be on account of his death**. He it is of whom the [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) writes to the [ _Galatians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06336a.htm>) that  _No one else of the_[ __apostles__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) _did I see except_[ __James__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>) _the_[ __brother of the Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02767a.htm>) , and shortly after the event the Acts of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) bear witness to the matter. The [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) also which is called the Gospel according to the Hebrews, **and which I have recently translated into Greek and**[** _Latin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09023a.htm>)**and which also**[** _Origen_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11306b.htm>)**often makes use of** , after the account of the [ _resurrection of the Saviour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) says,  _but the_[ __Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _, after he had given his grave clothes to the servant of the_[ __priest__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) _, appeared to_[ __James__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>) _(for_[ __James__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>) _had sworn that he_[ __would not eat bread__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05789c.htm>) _from that hour in which he drank the cup of the_[ __Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _until he should see him_[ __rising again__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>) _from among those that sleep) and again, a little later, it says ‘Bring a table and bread,’ said the_[ __Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). And immediately it is added,  _He brought bread and_[ __blessed__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02599b.htm>) _and broke and gave to_[ __James the Just__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>) _and said to him,_**_‘my brother eat your bread, for the_**[** __son of man__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14144a.htm>)** _is_**[** __risen__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12789a.htm>)** _from among those that sleep_**.’ **And so he ruled the**[** _church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**of**[** _Jerusalem_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08344a.htm>)**thirty years** , that is until the seventh year of [ _Nero_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) , and was [ _buried_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03071a.htm>) near the [ _temple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14499a.htm>) from which he had been cast down. **His tombstone with its**[** _inscription_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08042a.htm>)**was well known until the siege of**[** _Titus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14746b.htm>)**and the end of**[** _Hadrian’s_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07104b.htm>)**reign**. Some of our writers think he was [ _buried_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03071a.htm>) in [ _Mount Olivet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11244b.htm>) , but they are mistaken.

  


3\. Matthew

  


[ _Matthew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10056b.htm>) , also called Levi, [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) and aforetimes [ _publican_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12553d.htm>) , **composed a**[** _gospel_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**of**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**at first published in**[** _Judea_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08544a.htm>)**in**[** _Hebrew_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07176a.htm>)**for the sake of those of the**[** _circumcision_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03777a.htm>)**who**[** _believed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>)**, but this was afterwards translated into Greek,****_though by what author is uncertain_****.****_The_**[** _Hebrew_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07176a.htm>)** _itself has been preserved until the present day in the_**[** _library_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09227b.htm>)** _at_**[** _Cæsarea_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03134b.htm>)** _which_**[** _Pamphilus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11436b.htm>)** _so diligently gathered_****. I have also had the opportunity of having the volume described to me by the Nazarenes of**[** _Berœa_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02513b.htm>)**, a city of**[** _Syria_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14399a.htm>)**, who use it**. In this it is to be noted that wherever the [ _Evangelist_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) , whether on his own account or in the person of [ _our Lord the Saviour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) quotes the testimony of the [ _Old Testament_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14526a.htm>) ** _he does not follow the authority of the translators of the_**[** _Septuagint_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13722a.htm>)** _but the_**[** _Hebrew_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07176a.htm>)**. Wherefore these two forms exist**** _Out of_**[** __Egypt__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>)** _have I called my son, and for he shall be called a_**[** __Nazarene__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10724b.htm>).

  


4\. Jude

  


[ _Jude_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08542b.htm>) the brother of [ _James_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08280a.htm>) , left a short [ _epistle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/jud001.htm>) which is reckoned among the seven [ _catholic epistles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03453a.htm>) , **_and because in it he quotes from the_**[** _apocryphal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01601a.htm>)** __**[**_Book of Enoch_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01602a.htm>)** _it is rejected by many_**. Nevertheless by age and use it has gained authority and is reckoned among the [ _Holy Scriptures_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>).

  


5\. Paul

  


[ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , formerly called Saul, an apostle outside the number of the twelve [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , was of the tribe of Benjamin and the town of Giscalis in [ _Judea_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08544a.htm>). When this was taken by the Romans he moved with his [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) to Tarsus in Cilicia. Sent by them to [ _Jerusalem_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08344a.htm>) to study law he was [ _educated_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05295b.htm>) by [ _Gamaliel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06374b.htm>) , a most learned man whom Luke mentions. But after he had been present at the death of the [ _martyr_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) Stephen and had received letters from the [ _high priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12407b.htm>) of the temple for the [ _persecution_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>) of those who [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , he proceeded to Damascus, where constrained to [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) by a revelation, as it is written in the Acts of the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , he was transformed from a persecutor into an elect vessel. As Sergius Paulus Proconsul of [ _Cyprus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04589a.htm>) was the first to [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in his preaching, he took his name from him because he had subdued him to [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) in [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and having been joined by Barnabas, after traversing many cities, he returned to [ _Jerusalem_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08344a.htm>) **and was ordained apostle to the**[** _Gentiles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>)**by Peter, James and John**.

  


And because a full account of his life is given in the Acts of the Apostles, I only say this, that the twenty-fifth year after [ _our Lord’s passion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11527b.htm>) , that is the second of [ _Nero_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) , at the time when Festus Procurator of [ _Judea_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08544a.htm>) succeeded Felix, he was sent bound to [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) , and remaining for two years in free custody, disputed daily with the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) concerning the advent of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). **_It ought to be said that at the first defense, the power of_**[** _Nero_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>)** _having not yet been confirmed, nor his_**[** _wickedness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>)** _broken forth to such a degree as the histories relate concerning him,_**[**_Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)** _was dismissed by_**[** _Nero_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>)** _, that the gospel of Christ might be preached also in the West_**. As he himself writes in the second epistle to Timothy, at the time when he was about to be [ _put to death_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12565a.htm>) dictating his epistle as he did while in chains;  _At my first defense no one took my part, but all forsook me: may it not be laid to their account. But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me; that through me the message might be fully proclaimed and that all the_[ __Gentiles__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) _might hear, and I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion_ — clearly indicating [ _Nero_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) as lion on account of his cruelty. And directly following he says  _The_[ __Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _delivered me from the mouth of the lion_ and again shortly  _The_[ __Lord__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) _delivered me from every_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) _work and saved me unto his heavenly kingdom_ , for indeed he felt within himself that his [ _martyrdom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) was near at hand, for in the same epistle he announced for _I am already being offered and the time of my departure is at hand_. **He then, in the fourteenth year of**[** _Nero_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>)******_on the same day with Peter_****, was beheaded at**[** _Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)**for**[** _Christ’s_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**sake and was buried in the Ostian way, the twenty-seventh year after**[** _our Lord’s passion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11527b.htm>).

  


He wrote nine epistles to seven churches:  _To the Romans_ one,  _To the Corinthians_ two,  _To the Galatians_ one,  _To the Ephesians_ one,  _To the Philippians_ one,  _To the Colossians_ one,  _To the Thessalonians_ two; and besides these to his [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) ,  _To Timothy_ two,  _To Titus_ one,  _To Philemon_ one. **The epistle which is called the**** _Epistle to the Hebrews_****is not considered his, on account of its difference from the others in style and language, but it is reckoned, either according to**[** _Tertullian_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14520c.htm>)**to be the work of Barnabas, or according to others, to be by Luke the Evangelist or Clement afterwards**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of the church at**[** _Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)**, who, they say, arranged and adorned the ideas of**[** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**in his own language, though to be sure, since**[** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**was writing to Hebrews and was in disrepute among them he may have omitted his name from the salutation on this account**. He being a Hebrew wrote Hebrew, that is his own tongue and most fluently while the things which were eloquently written in Hebrew **_were more eloquently turned into Greek and this is the reason why it seems to differ from other epistles of_**[** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>). Some read one also to the Laodiceans **but it is rejected by everyone**.

  


6\. Barnabas

  


[ _Barnabas_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02300a.htm>) the Cyprian, also called Joseph the Levite, ordained apostle to the [ _Gentiles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) with [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , **_wrote one_**** __Epistle__**** _, valuable for the edification of the church, which is reckoned among the_**[** _apocryphal writings_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01601a.htm>). He afterwards separated from [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) on account of John, a [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) also called Mark, none the less exercised the work laid upon him of preaching the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>).

  


7\. Luke

  


Luke a physician of [ _Antioch_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01570a.htm>) , as his writings indicate, was not unskilled in the Greek language. An adherent of the [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , and companion of all his journeying, he wrote a [ __Gospel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09420a.htm>) , concerning which the same [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) says,  _We send with him a brother whose praise in the gospel is among all the churches and to the Colossians Luke the beloved physician salutes you_ , and to Timothy  _Luke only is with me_. He also wrote another excellent volume **to which he prefixed the title**[** __Acts of the Apostles__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01117a.htm>)**, a history which extends to the second year of**[** _Paul’s_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**sojourn at**[** _Rome_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>)**, that is to the fourth year of**[** _Nero_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>)**, from which we learn that the book was composed in that same city**. Therefore the  _Acts of Paul and Thecla_ and all the fable about the lion [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) by him **we reckon among the**[** _apocryphal writings_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01601a.htm>) , for how is it possible that the inseparable companion of the apostle in his other affairs, alone should have been [ _ignorant_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) of this thing. **_Moreover_**[** _Tertullian_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14520c.htm>)** _who lived near those times, mentions a certain_**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)** _in Asia, an adherent of the_**[** _apostle_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)** __**[**_Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)** _, who was convicted by John of having been the author of the book, and who, confessing that he did this for_**[** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>)** _of_**[** _Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)** _, resigned his office of_**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>). Some suppose that whenever [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) in his epistle says according to my gospel **he means the book of Luke and that Luke not only was taught the gospel history by the**[** _apostle_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)****[**_Paul_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>)**who was not with the Lord in the flesh, but also by other**[** _apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>). This he too at the beginning of his work declares, saying  _Even as they delivered unto us, which from the beginning were eyewitnesses and ministers of the word_. So he wrote the gospel as he had heard it, **_but composed the_**[** _Acts of the Apostles_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01117a.htm>)** _as he himself had seen_**. He was buried at Constantinople to which city, in the twentieth year of Constantius, his bones together with the remains of [ _Andrew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01471a.htm>) the [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) were transferred.

  


8\. Mark

  


Mark the [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) and interpreter of [ _Peter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11744a.htm>) wrote a short gospel at the request of the brethren at [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) **embodying what he had heard**[** _Peter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11744a.htm>)**tell. When**[** _Peter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11744a.htm>)**had heard this,****_he approved it and published it to the churches to be read by his authority_****as Clemens in the sixth book of his Hypotyposes and**[** _Papias_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>)**,**[**_bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of Hierapolis, record**. [ _Peter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11744a.htm>) also mentions this Mark in his first epistle, **figuratively indicating Rome under the name of**[** _Babylon_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02179b.htm>)  _She who is in_[ __Babylon__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02179b.htm>) _elect together with you salutes you and so does Mark my son_. So, taking the gospel which he himself composed, he went to [ _Egypt_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05329b.htm>) and first preaching Christ at Alexandria he formed a church so admirable in doctrine and continence of living that he constrained all followers of Christ to his example. [** _Philo_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12023a.htm>)**most learned of the**[** _Jews_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>)**seeing the first church at Alexandria still Jewish in a degree, wrote a book on their manner of life as something creditable to his nation telling how** , as Luke says, **the**[** _believers_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05769a.htm>)**had all things in common at**[** _Jerusalem_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08344a.htm>)**, so he recorded what he saw was done at Alexandria,****_under the learned Mark_**. He died in the eighth year of [ _Nero_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) and was buried at Alexandria, Annianus succeeding him.

  


9\. John the Apostle

  


John, **the apostle whom Jesus most loved** , the son of Zebedee and brother of James, the apostle whom [ _Herod_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07289c.htm>) , after [ _our Lord’s passion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11527b.htm>) , beheaded, **most recently of all the**[** _evangelists_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>)**wrote a**[** __Gospel__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**, at the request of the**[** _bishops_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**of Asia, against**[** _Cerinthus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03539a.htm>)**and other**[** _heretics_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>)**and especially against the then growing**[** _dogma_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05089a.htm>)**of the**[** _Ebionites_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05242c.htm>)**, who assert that Christ did not exist before Mary. On this account he was compelled to maintain His divine nativity**. **But there is said to be yet another reason for this work** , in that when he had read Matthew, Mark, and Luke, **he approved indeed the substance of the history and declared that the things they said were**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**, but that they had given the history of only one year** , the one, that is, which follows the imprisonment of John and in which he was [ _put to death_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12565a.htm>). So passing by this year the events of which had been set forth by these, **he related the events of the earlier period before John was shut up in**[** _prison_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12430a.htm>)**, so that it might be manifest to those who should diligently read the volumes of the four Evangelists**. This also takes away the discrepancy which there seems to be between John and the others.

  


He wrote also one  _Epistle_ which begins as follows  _That which was from the beginning, that which we have heard, that which we have seen with our eyes and our hands handled concerning the word of life_**which is esteemed of by all**[** _men_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**who are interested in the church or in learning**. The other two of which the first is  _The elder to the elect lady and her children_ and the other  _The elder unto Gaius the beloved whom I_[ __love__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>) _in_[ __truth__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , **are said to be the work of John the**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>)**to the memory of whom another sepulchre is shown at Ephesus to the present day, though some think that there are two memorials of this same John the**[** _evangelist_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>). We shall treat of this matter in its turn **when we come to**[** _Papias_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11457c.htm>)**his**[** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>). In the fourteenth year then after [ _Nero_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10752c.htm>) [** _Domitian_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>)**having raised a second**[** _persecution_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm>)**he was banished to the island of**[** _Patmos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11547a.htm>)**, and wrote the**** _Apocalypse_****, on which**[** _Justin Martyr_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08580c.htm>)**and**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)**afterwards wrote commentaries**. But [ _Domitian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05114b.htm>) having been [ _put to death_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12565a.htm>) and his acts, on account of his excessive cruelty, having been annulled by the senate, he returned to [ _Ephesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05490a.htm>) under Pertinax and continuing there until the time of the [ _Emperor Trajan_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15015a.htm>) , founded and built churches throughout all Asia, **and, worn out by old age, died in the sixty-eighth year after**[** _our Lord’s passion_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11527b.htm>)**and was buried near the same city**.

  


10\. Hermas

  


[ _Hermas_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07268b.htm>) whom the [ _apostle_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) mentions in writing to the Romans  _Salute Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas and the brethren that are with them_ **is reputed to be the author of the book which is called**** _Pastor_****and which is also read publicly in some churches of Greece. It is in fact a useful book and many of the ancient writers quote from it as authority** , but among the Latins it is almost unknown.

11\. Philo the Jew

It appears through this catalogue of names that the John who is placed among the [ _disciples_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) is not the same as the elder John whom he places after Aristion in his enumeration. This we say moreover because of the opinion mentioned above, where we record that it is declared by many **that the last two epistles of John are the work not of the apostle but of the**[** _presbyter_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>).

  


He is said to have published a  _Second coming of Our Lord or Millennium._ [** _Irenæus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)** _and_**[** _Apollinaris_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01615b.htm>)** _and others who say that after the resurrection the Lord will reign in the flesh with the_**[** _saints_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>)** _, follow him._**[**_Tertullian_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14520c.htm>)** _also_** in his work  _On the hope of the faithful_ , **_Victorinus of Petau and Lactantius follow this view_** …

  


20\. Aristides

  


[ _Aristides_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01712d.htm>) a most eloquent Athenian [ _philosopher_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) , **and a**[** _disciple_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>)**of Christ** while yet retaining his [ _philosopher’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) garb, presented a work to [ _Hadrian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07104b.htm>) at the same time that [ _Quadratus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12589b.htm>) presented his. The work contained a systematic statement of our doctrine, that is, an  _Apology_  _for the_[ __Christians__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) , **which is still extant and is regarded by philologians as a monument to his genius** …

  


22\. Hegesippus

  


[ _Hegesippus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07194a.htm>) who lived at a period not far from the Apostolic age, writing a  _History of all_[ __ecclesiastical__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) _events from the passion of our Lord_ , down to his own period, and gathering many things useful to the reader, composed five volumes in simple style, trying to represent the style of speaking of those whose lives he treated. He says that he went to [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) in the time of [ _Anicetus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>) , **the tenth**[** _bishop_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>)**after Peter** , and continued there till the time of [ _Eleutherius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05378a.htm>) , [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of the same city, who had been formerly [ _deacon_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm>) under [ _Anicetus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01514a.htm>).

  


Moreover, arguing against [ _idols_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>) , he wrote a history, showing from what [ _error_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05525a.htm>) they had first arisen, and this work indicates in what age he flourished. He says,  _They built monuments and temples to their dead as we see up to the present day, such as the one to Antinous, servant to the_[ __Emperor Hadrian__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07104b.htm>) _, in whose_[ __honour__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _also games were celebrated, and a city founded bearing his name, and a temple with_[ __priests__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) _established. The_[ __Emperor Hadrian__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07104b.htm>) _is said to have been enamoured of Antinous_.

  


23\. Justin Martyr

  


Justin, a [ _philosopher_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) , and wearing the garb of [ _philosopher_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) , a citizen of Neapolis, a city of Palestine, and the son of Priscus Bacchius, laboured strenuously in behalf of the religion of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , insomuch that he delivered to [ _Antoninus Pius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01586a.htm>) and his sons and the senate, a work written  _Against the nations_ , and did not shun the ignominy of the cross. He addressed another book also to the successors of this [ _Antoninus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01586a.htm>) , [ _Marcus Antoninus Verus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02109a.htm>) and Lucius Aurelius Commodus. Another volume of his  _Against the nations_ , is also extant, where he discusses the [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of [ _demons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04710a.htm>) , and a fourth against the nations which he entitled,  _Refutation_ and yet another  _On the sovereignty of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and another book which he entitled,  _Psaltes_ , and another  _On the Soul_ , the  _Dialogue against the_[ __Jews__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , which he held against Trypho, the leader of the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) , and also notable volumes  _Against_[ __Marcion__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , **which**[** _Irenæus_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>)**also mentions in the fourth book**** _Against_**[** __heresies__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) , also another book  _Against all_[ __heresies__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) which he mentions in the  _Apology_ which is addressed to [ _Antoninus Pius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01586a.htm>). He, when he had held διατριβάς in the city of [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) , and had convicted Crescens the cynic, who said many [ _blasphemous_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02595a.htm>) things against the [ _Christians_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) , of [ _gluttony_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06590a.htm>) and [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) of death, and had [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) him devoted to luxury and [ _lusts_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09438a.htm>) , at last, accused of being a [ _Christian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) , through the efforts and wiles of Crescens, he shed his blood for Christ.

  


24\. Melito

  


[ _Melito_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10166b.htm>) of Asia, [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of [ _Sardis_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13472a.htm>) , addressed a book to the emperor [ _Marcus Antoninus Verus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02109a.htm>) , a [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of Fronto the orator, in behalf of the [ _Christian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) doctrine. He wrote other things also, among which are the following:  _On the passover_ , two books, one book  _On the lives of the_[ __prophets__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) , one book  _On the church_ , one book  _On the_  _Lord’s day_ , one book  _On_[ __faith__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) , one book  _On the psalms_ (?) one  _On the senses_ , one  _On the_[ __soul__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) _and body_ , one  _On_[ __baptism__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , one  _On_[ __truth__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , one  _On the generation of_[ __Christ__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) ,  _On His_[ __prophecy__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12473a.htm>) one  _On hospitality_ and another which is called the  _Key_ — one  _On the_[ __devil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04764a.htm>) , one  _On the Apocalypse of John_ , one  _On the corporeality of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and six books of  _Eclogues._ Of his fine oratorical genius, [ _Tertullian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14520c.htm>) , in the seven books which he wrote against the church on behalf of [ _Montanus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10521a.htm>) , satirically says that he was considered a [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) by many of us.

  


25\. Theophilus of Antioch

  


[ _Theophilus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14625a.htm>) , sixth [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of the [ _church of Antioch_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01567a.htm>) , in the reign of the emperor [ _Marcus Antoninus Verus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02109a.htm>) composed a book  _Against_[ __Marcion__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09645c.htm>) , which is still extant, also three volumes  _To Autolycus_ and one  _Against the_[ __heresy__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) _of Hermogenes_ and other short and elegant treatises, well fitted for the edification of the church. I have read, under his name, commentaries  _On the_[ __Gospel__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) and  _On the proverbs of Solomon_ which do not appear to me to correspond in style and language with the elegance and expressiveness of the above works…

  


29\. Tatian

  


[ _Tatian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14464b.htm>) who, while teaching oratory, won not a little [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) in the rhetorical art, **was a follower of**[** _Justin Martyr_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08580c.htm>)**and was distinguished so long as he did not leave his master’s side**. But afterwards, inflated by a swelling of eloquence, he founded a new [ _heresy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) which is called that of the [ _Encratites_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05412c.htm>) , the [ _heresy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) which Severus afterwards augmented in such wise that [ _heretics_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) of this party are called Severians to the present day. [ _Tatian_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14464b.htm>) wrote besides innumerable volumes, one of which, a most successful book  _Against the nations_ , is extant, and this is considered the most significant of all his works. He flourished in the reign of [ _Marcus Antoninus Verus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02109a.htm>) and Lucius Aurelius Commodus…

  


35\. Irenæus

  


[ _Irenæus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08130b.htm>) , a [ _presbyter_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) under Pothinus the [ _bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) who ruled the [ _church of Lyons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09472a.htm>) in [ _Gaul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06395b.htm>) , being sent to [ _Rome_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13164a.htm>) as legate by the [ _martyrs_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) of this place, on account of certain [ _ecclesiastical_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) questions, presented to [ _Bishop Eleutherius_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05378a.htm>) certain letters under his own name which are worthy of [ _honour_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>). Afterwards when Pothinus, nearly ninety years of age, received the crown of [ _martyrdom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) for [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , he was put in his place. It is certain too that he was a [ _disciple_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of [ _Polycarp_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12219b.htm>) , the [ _priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) and [ _martyr_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm>) , whom we mentioned above. He wrote five books  _Against_[ __heresies__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) and a short volume,  _Against the nations_ and another  _On discipline_ , a letter to Marcianus his brother  _On_[ __apostolic__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) _preaching_ , a book of  _Various treatises_ ; also to Blastus,  _On_[ __schism__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13529a.htm>) , to Florinus  _On monarchy_ or  _That God is not the author of_[ __evil__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) , also an excellent  _Commentary on the Ogdoad_ at the end of which indicating that he was near the apostolic period he wrote I [ _adjure_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01142c.htm>) you whosoever shall transcribe this book, by [ _our Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) and by his [ _glorious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) advent at which He shall judge the quick and the dead, that you diligently compare, after you have transcribed, and amend it according to the copy from which you have transcribed it and also that you shall similarly transcribe this [ _adjuration_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01142c.htm>) as you find it in your pattern. Other works of his are in circulation to wit: to Victor the [ _Roman bishop_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12260a.htm>)  _On the Paschal controversy_ in which he warns him not lightly to break the unity of the fraternity, if indeed Victor [ _believed_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that the many [ _bishops_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02581b.htm>) of Asia and the East, who with the [ _Jews_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194503/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08399a.htm>) celebrated the passover, on the fourteenth day of the new moon, were to be condemned. But even those who differed from them did not support Victor in his opinion. He flourished chiefly in the reign of the Emperor Commodus, who succeeded Marcus Antoninus Verus in power.
