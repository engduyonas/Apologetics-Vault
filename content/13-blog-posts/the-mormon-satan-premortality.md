# THE MORMON SATAN & PREMORTALITY

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Mar 5
  * 21 min read


In this post I will quote from official Mormon teachings and sources for the express purpose of highlighting just how unbiblical these doctrines are.

  


These sources reveal that Mormons believe that human beings living on earth existed as spirit creatures in heaven with God. Mormons also teach that humans are the spirit children of heavenly parents, indicating that their god has at least one wife. This belief in a heavenly wife and mother is an essential core doctrine of mormonism.

  


They further believe Jesus who was known as Jehovah is Lucifer/Satan’s older brother, and that a war broke out in the heavenly council when Father chose Jesus’ plan of salvation over against Satan.

  


As if it couldn’t get any more blasphemous, the Mormons actually believe that this plan included the exaltation of Father’s heavenly children to godhood which they could only realize and accomplish by first being sent into the world to become flesh so as to assume mortal physical bodies! 

  


These references will show that Mormonism is anything but Christian, since it is a blasphemous and polytheistic perversion of the historic Christian faith as taught in the inspired Scriptures, and which has been affirmed by true believers throughout the centuries.

  


All emphasis will be mine.

  


### **IN THEIR OWN WORDS**

  


#### **Heavenly Parents**

  


All human beings are beloved [ _spirit children of heavenly parents_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>). Because of our divine parentage, we each have divine potential. This divine origin defines our true identity.

In our premortal life, we learned the [ _plan of salvation_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , which provides the way to inherit [ _eternal life_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/eternal-life?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , the life of our heavenly parents. The purpose of our existence, including mortal life, is to prepare us to receive this glorious gift.

  


**Little has been revealed about our**[** _Heavenly Mother_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>)**beyond a knowledge of Her existence**. Although we do not worship Her, **we honor Her as a divine parent**. Following the example of the Savior, we pray only to our Heavenly Father.[_1_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/heavenly-parents?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1>) We receive guidance and direction from Heavenly Father and His Son through the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/holy-ghost?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>).

In this life, we strive to develop the godly attributes possessed by our heavenly parents. These attributes are exemplified in the life of Jesus Christ. ([_Heavenly Parents_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/heavenly-parents?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>))

  


#### **Mother in Heaven**

  


The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints teaches that all human beings, male and female, are beloved spirit children **of heavenly parents, a Heavenly Father and a Heavenly Mother**. This understanding is rooted in scriptural and prophetic teachings about the nature of God, our relationship to Deity, and the godly potential of men and women.[_1_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1>) **The doctrine of a Heavenly Mother is a cherished and distinctive belief among Latter-day Saints**.[_2_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2>)

  


While there is no record of a formal revelation to Joseph Smith on this doctrine, **some early Latter-day Saint women recalled that he personally taught them about a Mother in Heaven**.[_3_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3>) The earliest published references to the doctrine appeared shortly after Joseph Smith’s death in 1844, in documents written by his close associates.[_4_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4>) The most notable expression of the idea is found in a poem by Eliza R. Snow, entitled “My Father in Heaven” and now known as the hymn “O My Father.” This text declares: “In the heav’ns are parents single? / No, the thought makes reason stare; / Truth is reason—truth eternal / **Tells me I’ve a mother there**.”[ _5_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note5>)

  


**Subsequent Church leaders have affirmed the existence of a Mother in Heaven**. In 1909, the First Presidency taught that “all men and women **are in the similitude of the universal Father and Mother, and are literally the sons and daughters of Deity**.”[ _6_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6>) Susa Young Gates, a prominent leader in the Church, wrote in 1920 **that Joseph Smith’s visions and teachings revealed the truth that “the divine Mother, [is] side by side with the divine Father**.”[ _7_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note7>) And in “The Family: A Proclamation to the World,” issued in 1995, the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve Apostles declared, “Each [person] is a beloved spirit son or daughter of heavenly parents, and, as such, each has a divine nature and destiny.”[ _8_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note8>)

  


**Prophets have taught that our heavenly parents work together for the salvation of the human family**. “We are part of a divine plan designed **by Heavenly Parents who love us** ,” taught Elder M. Russell Ballard of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles.[_9_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note9>) President Harold B. Lee stated, “We forget that we have a Heavenly Father**and a Heavenly Mother who are even more concerned, probably, than our earthly father and mother** , and that influences from beyond are constantly working to try to help us when we do all we can.”[ _10_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note10>)

  


Latter-day Saints direct their worship to Heavenly Father, in the name of Christ**, and do not pray to Heavenly Mother**. In this, they follow the pattern set by Jesus Christ, who taught His disciples to “always pray unto the Father in my name.”[ _11_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note11>) Latter-day Saints are taught to pray to Heavenly Father, but as President Gordon B. Hinckley said, “**The fact that we do not pray to our Mother in Heaven in no way belittles or denigrates her**.”[ _12_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note12>) Indeed, as Elder Rudger Clawson wrote, “We honor woman when we acknowledge Godhood **in her eternal Prototype**.”[ _13_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note13>)

  


As with many other truths of the gospel, **our present knowledge about a Mother in Heaven is limited. Nevertheless, we have been given sufficient knowledge to appreciate the sacredness of this doctrine and to comprehend the divine pattern established for us as children of heavenly parents**. Latter-day Saints believe that this pattern is reflected in Paul’s statement that “neither is the man without the woman, neither the woman without the man, in the Lord.”[ _14_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note14>) Men and women cannot be exalted without each other. Just as we have a Father in Heaven, **we have a Mother in Heaven**. As Elder Dallin H. Oaks of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles has said, “**Our theology begins with heavenly parents. Our highest aspiration is to be like them**.”[ _15_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note15>) ([_Mother in Heaven_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/mother-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>))

  


#### **Premortality**

  


Premortality **refers to our life before we were born on this earth. In our pre-earth life, we lived in the presence of our Heavenly Father as His spirit children. We did not have a physical body**.

  


In this premortal existence, **we attended a**[** _council_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/council-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>)**with Heavenly Father’s other spirit children**. At that council, Heavenly Father presented His great plan of happiness.[_1_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/premortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1>)

In harmony with the [ _plan of happiness_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , the premortal [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , the Firstborn Son of the Father in the spirit, covenanted to be the Savior.[_2_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/premortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2>) Those who followed Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ **were permitted to come to the earth to experience mortality and progress toward eternal life**. Lucifer, another spirit son of God, rebelled against the plan and “sought to destroy the [ _agency_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/agency-and-accountability?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) of man.”[ _3_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/premortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3>) He became [ _Satan_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and he and his followers were cast out of heaven **and denied the privileges of receiving a physical body and experiencing mortality**.[_4_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/premortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4>)

  


Throughout our premortal life, we developed our identity and increased our spiritual capabilities. Blessed with the gift of agency, we made important decisions, such as the decision to follow Heavenly Father’s plan. These decisions affected our life then and now. We grew in intelligence and learned to love the truth, and we prepared to come to the earth, where we could continue to progress. ([_Premortality_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/premortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>))

  


#### **Plan of Salvation**

  


Before we were born on earth, **we lived with our**[** _heavenly parents_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/heavenly-parents?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>)**as Their**[** _spirit children_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/spirit-children-of-heavenly-parents?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>).[_1_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1>) At a council with all of His children, Heavenly Father presented a plan, known as the “plan of salvation” or “the great plan of happiness.”[ _2_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2>) The plan includes all the laws and ordinances of the gospel necessary to gain [ _eternal life_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/eternal-life?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , “the greatest of all the gifts of God.”[ _3_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3>)

  


**Sadly, one-third of Heavenly Father’s spirit children did not accept the plan**. They chose to follow Lucifer, who became the devil, and were cast out of the presence of God.[_4_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4>)

  


**We are on earth because we chose to follow Heavenly Father’s plan. One significant purpose of**[** _mortality_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/mortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>)**is to gain a physical body**. On earth we can have joy and peace, but we will also face temptation, opposition, and [ _adversity_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/adversity?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) and experience trials. Earthly trials are part of mortality and can help us grow to be more like our [ _Heavenly Father_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/god-the-father?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>). 

Essential to the plan is the [ _Atonement of Jesus Christ_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/atonement-of-jesus-christ?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>). Jesus overcame [ _spiritual_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/death-spiritual?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) and [ _physical death_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/death-physical?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) and took upon Himself our afflictions, sorrows, and sins.[_5_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note5>) This suffering, death, and [ _Resurrection_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/resurrection?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) are referred to as the Atonement. The Atonement was necessary since “no unclean thing can dwell with God.”[ _6_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6>) It is through Christ’s sacrifice that we can [ _repent_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/repentance?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) and live with God again. 

  


Also essential to God’s plan is [ _agency_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/agency-and-accountability?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , or the ability to choose. When we choose to repent of our sins, we choose to accept the gift of the Atonement of Jesus Christ. If we exercise our agency to keep the commandments, we have the blessing of participating in sacred [ _ordinances_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/ordinances?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , which are ceremonies that include making [ _covenants,_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/covenant?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) or promises, with God. These ordinances include baptism, confirmation, priesthood ordination for men, and temple ordinances. 

  


Part of our Heavenly Father’s plan is that someday we will all die. Death is the separation of the spirit and the physical body. **Our spirit, which existed in the**[** _premortal life_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/premortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, will live on after our death**. If we lived righteously—repenting as needed—to the end of our mortal life, we will enter spirit paradise. The Book of Mormon prophet Alma described this as “a state of rest, a state of peace.”[ _7_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note7>)

  


Because of Christ’s Atonement, all of God’s children will be resurrected and our bodies and spirits will be reunited.[_8_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note8>) Resurrection will be followed by the Final Judgment, where God will judge us according to our desires and our [ _obedience_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/obedience?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) to the commandments.[_9_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note9>) If we have qualified through repentance, we will be able to live forever with our loving heavenly parents.[_10_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note10>) ([_Plan of Salvation_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>))

  


#### **Jesus Christ Chosen as Savior**

  


When the [ _plan for our salvation_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) **was presented to us in the**[** _premortal_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/premortality?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>)**existence, we were so happy that we “shouted for joy.”**[** _1_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1>)**We understood that we would have to leave our heavenly home for a time**. While we were away, all of us would [ _sin_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/sin?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) and some of us would lose our way. Our [ _Heavenly Father_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/god-the-father?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) knew we would need help, so He planned a way to help us.

  


We needed a Savior to pay for our sins and teach us how to return to our Heavenly Father. Our Father said, “Whom shall I send?”[ _2_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2>) Jesus Christ, **who was called Jehovah** , said, “Here am I, send me.”[ _3_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3>)

  


[ _Jesus Christ_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) was willing to come to the earth, give His life for us, and take upon Himself our sins. He, like our Heavenly Father, wanted us to choose whether we would [ _obey_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/obedience?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) Heavenly Father’s [ _commandments_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/ten-commandments?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>). He knew we must be free to choose in order to prove ourselves worthy of exaltation. Jesus said, “Father, thy will be done, and the glory be thine forever.”[ _4_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4>)

  


[ _Satan,_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) who was called Lucifer, also came, saying, “Behold, here am I, send me, I will be thy son, and I will redeem all mankind, that one soul shall not be lost, and surely I will do it; wherefore give me thine honor.”[ _5_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note5>) **Under Satan’s plan, we would not be allowed to choose. He would take away the freedom of choice that our Father had given us**. Satan wanted to have all the honor for our salvation. Under his proposal, our purpose in coming to earth would have been frustrated.[_6_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6>)

  


After hearing both sons speak, Heavenly Father said, “I will send the first.”[ _7_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note7>) ([_Jesus Christ Chosen as Savior_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/jesus-christ-chosen-as-savior?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>))

  


#### **Satan**

  


Satan, also called the adversary or the devil, is the enemy of all righteousness and of those who seek to follow God. He is a spirit son of God who was once an angel “in authority in the presence of God.”[ _1_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1>) **But in the premortal**[** _Council in Heaven_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/council-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>)**, Lucifer, as Satan was then called, rebelled against God**. Since that time, he has sought to destroy the children of God on the earth and to make them miserable.

  


One primary issue in the conflict between God and Satan is [ _agency_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/agency-and-accountability?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>). Agency is a precious gift from God; it is essential to His plan for His children. In Satan’s rebellion against God, Satan “sought to destroy the agency of man.”[ _2_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2>) He said: “I will redeem all mankind, that one soul shall not be lost, and surely I will do it; wherefore give me thine honor.”[ _3_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3>)

  


Satan persuaded “a third part of the hosts of heaven” to turn away from the Father.[_4_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4>) As a result of this rebellion, Satan and his followers were cut off from God’s presence and denied the blessing of receiving a physical body.[_5_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note5>)

  


Heavenly Father allows Satan and Satan’s followers to tempt us as part of our experience in mortality.[_6_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6>) Because Satan “seeketh that all men might be miserable like unto himself,”[ _7_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note7>) he and his followers try to lead us away from righteousness. He directs his most strenuous opposition at the most important aspects of Heavenly Father’s [ _plan of happiness_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>). For example, he seeks to discredit the Savior and the [ _priesthood_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/priesthood?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , to cast doubt on the power of the [ _Atonement of Jesus Christ_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/atonement-of-jesus-christ?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , to counterfeit [ _revelation_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/revelation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , to distract us from the truth, and to contradict individual accountability. He attempts to undermine the family by confusing gender, promoting sexual relations outside of marriage, ridiculing [ _marriage_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/marriage?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>) , and discouraging childbearing by married adults who would otherwise raise children in righteousness.

  


Individuals do not have to give in to Satan’s [ _temptations_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/temptation?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>). Each person has the power to choose good over evil, and the Lord has promised to help all who seek Him through sincere prayer and faithfulness. ([_Satan_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/satan?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>))

  


#### **War in Heaven**

  


Because our Heavenly Father chose Jesus Christ to be our Savior, **Satan became angry and rebelled**. There was war in heaven. Satan and his followers fought against Jesus Christ and His followers. The Savior’s followers “overcame [Satan] by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony” ([_Revelation 12:11_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/rev/12.11?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#p11>)).

  


In this great rebellion, Satan and all the spirits who followed him were sent away from the presence of God and cast down from heaven. A third part of the hosts of heaven were punished for following Satan (see [ _Doctrine and Covenants 29:36_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29.36?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#p36>)). **They were denied the right to receive mortal bodies**.

  


Because we are here on earth and have mortal bodies, **we know that we chose to follow Jesus Christ and our Heavenly Father. Satan and his followers are also on the earth, but as spirits. They have not forgotten who we are** , and they are around us daily, tempting us and enticing us to do things that are not pleasing to our Heavenly Father. **In our premortal life, we chose to follow Jesus Christ and accept God’s plan**. We must continue to follow Jesus Christ here on earth. Only by following Him can we return to our heavenly home. ([_War in Heaven_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/war-in-heaven?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog>))

**1** And I, the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1a>)[ _Lord God_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1a>) , spake unto Moses, saying: That [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1b>)[ _Satan_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1b>) , whom thou hast commanded in the name of mine Only Begotten, is the same which was from the [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1c>)[ _beginning_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1c>) , and he came before me, saying—**Behold, here am I, send me, I will be thy son, and I will**[** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1d>)[** _redeem_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1d>)**all mankind, that one soul shall not be lost, and surely**[** __e__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1e>)[** _I_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1e>)**will do it; wherefore**[** __f__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1f>)[** _give_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note1f>)**me thine honor**.

**2** But, behold, my Beloved [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2a>)[ _Son_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2a>) , which was my Beloved and [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2b>)[ _Chosen_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2b>) from the beginning, said unto me—[** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2c>)[ _Father_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2c>) , thy [** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2d>)[ _will be_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2d>) done, and the [** __e__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2e>)[ _glory_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note2e>) be thine forever.

**3** Wherefore, because that [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3a>)[ _Satan_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3a>) [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3b>)[ _rebelled_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3b>) against me, **and sought to destroy the**[** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3c>)[** _agency_**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3c>)**of man, which I, the Lord God, had given him** , and also, that I should give unto him mine own power; by the power of mine Only Begotten, I caused that he should be [** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3d>)[ _cast down_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note3d>) ;

**4** And he became [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4a>)[ _Satan_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4a>) , yea, even the [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4b>)[ _devil_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4b>) , the father of all [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4c>)[ _lies_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4c>) , to [** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4d>)[ _deceive_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4d>) and to blind men, and to lead them [** __e__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4e>)[ _captive_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4e>) at his will, even as many as would not [** __f__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4f>)[ _hearken_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note4f>) unto my voice.

**5** And now the serpent was more [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note5a>)[ _subtle_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note5a>) than any beast of the field which I, the Lord God, had made.

**6** And [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6a>)[ _Satan_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6a>) put it into the heart of the serpent, (for he had drawn away [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6b>)[ _many_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6b>) after him,) and he sought also to [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6c>)[ _beguile_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6c>) Eve, for he [** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6d>)[ _knew not_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6d>) the [** __e__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6e>)[ _mind_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note6e>) of God, wherefore he sought to destroy the world.

**7** And he said unto the woman: Yea, hath God said—Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden? (And he spake by the mouth of the serpent.)

**8** And the woman said unto the serpent: We may eat of the fruit of the trees of the garden;

**9** But of the fruit of the tree which thou beholdest in the midst of the garden, God hath said—Ye shall not eat of it, neither shall ye touch it, lest ye die.

**10** And the serpent said unto the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note10a>)[ _woman_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note10a>) : Ye shall not surely die;

**11** For God doth know that in the day ye eat thereof, then your [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note11a>)[ _eyes_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note11a>) shall be opened, and ye shall be as gods, [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note11b>)[ _knowing_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note11b>) good and evil.

**12** And when the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it became pleasant to the eyes, and a tree to be [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note12a>)[ _desired_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note12a>) to make her wise, she took of the [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note12b>)[ _fruit_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note12b>) thereof, and did [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note12c>)[ _eat_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note12c>) , and also gave unto her husband with her, and he did eat.

**13** And the eyes of them both were opened, and they knew that they had been [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note13a>)[ _naked_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note13a>). And they sewed fig leaves together and made themselves [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note13b>)[ _aprons_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note13b>).

**14** And they heard the voice of the Lord God, as they were [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note14a>)[ _walking_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note14a>) in the garden, in the cool of the day; and Adam and his wife went to hide themselves from the [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note14b>)[ _presence_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note14b>) of the Lord God amongst the trees of the garden.

**15** And I, the Lord God, called unto Adam, and said unto him: Where [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note15a>)[ _goest_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note15a>) thou?

**16** And he said: I heard thy voice in the garden, and I was afraid, because I beheld that I was naked, and I hid myself.

**17** And I, the Lord God, said unto Adam: Who told thee thou wast naked? Hast thou eaten of the tree whereof I commanded thee that thou shouldst not eat, if so thou shouldst surely [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note17a>)[ _die_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note17a>)?

**18** And the man said: The woman thou gavest me, and commandest that she should remain with me, she gave me of the fruit of the tree and I did eat.

**19** And I, the Lord God, said unto the woman: What is this thing which thou hast done? And the woman said: The serpent [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note19a>)[ _beguiled_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note19a>) me, and I did eat.

**20** And I, the Lord God, said unto the serpent: Because thou hast done this thou shalt be [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note20a>)[ _cursed_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note20a>) above all cattle, and above every beast of the field; upon thy belly shalt thou go, and dust shalt thou eat all the days of thy life;

**21** And I will put [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note21a>)[ _enmity_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note21a>) between thee and the woman, between thy seed and her seed; and he shall [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note21b>)[ _bruise_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note21b>) thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel. (Book of Moses [_https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng>))

and also a [** __f__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36f>)[ _third_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36f>) part of the [** __g__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36g>)[ _hosts_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36g>) of heaven turned he away from me because of their [** __h__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36h>)[ _agency_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36h>) ;

**37** And they were thrust down, and thus came the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37a>)[ _devil_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37a>) and his [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37b>)[ _angels_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37b>) ;

**38** And, behold, there is a place [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note38a>)[ _prepared_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note38a>) for them from the beginning, which place is [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note38b>)[ _hell_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note38b>).

**39** And it must needs be that the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39a>)[ _devil_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39a>) should [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39b>)[ _tempt_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39b>) the children of men, or they could not be [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39c>)[ _agents_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39c>) unto themselves; for if they never should have [** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39d>)[ _bitter_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note39d>) they could not know the sweet—

**40** Wherefore, it came to pass that the devil tempted Adam, and he partook of the forbidden [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note40a>)[ _fruit_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note40a>) and [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note40b>)[ _transgressed_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note40b>) the commandment, wherein he became [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note40c>)[ _subject_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note40c>) to the will of the devil, because he yielded unto temptation.

**41** Wherefore, I, the Lord God, caused that he should be [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41a>)[ _cast out_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41a>) from the Garden of [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41b>)[ _Eden_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41b>) , from my presence, because of his transgression, wherein he became [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41c>)[ _spiritually_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41c>) [** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41d>)[ _dead_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41d>) , which is the first death, even that same death which is the last [** __e__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41e>)[ _death_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41e>) , which is spiritual, which shall be pronounced upon the wicked when I shall say: Depart, ye [** __f__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41f>)[ _cursed_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&ref=answeringislam.blog#note41f>). (Doctrine and Covenants 29, [_https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng &id=36-39#p36_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29?lang=eng&id=36-39#p36>))

  


**25** And this we saw also, and bear record, that an [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25a>)[ _angel_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25a>) of God who was in authority in the presence of God, who [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25b>)[ _rebelled_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25b>) against the Only Begotten [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25c>)[ _Son_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25c>) whom the Father [** __d__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25d>)[ _loved_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note25d>) and who was in the bosom of the Father, was thrust down from the presence of God and the Son,

**26** And was called [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note26a>)[ _Perdition_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note26a>) , for the heavens [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note26b>)[ _wept_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note26b>) over him—he was [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note26c>)[ _Lucifer_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note26c>) , a son of the morning.

**27** And we beheld, and lo, he is [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note27a>)[ _fallen_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note27a>)! is fallen, even a son of the morning!

**28** And while we were yet in the Spirit, the Lord commanded us that we should write the vision; for we beheld Satan, that old [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note28a>)[ _serpent_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note28a>) , even the [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note28b>)[ _devil_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note28b>) , who rebelled against God, and sought to take the kingdom of our [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note28c>)[ _God_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note28c>) and his Christ—

**29** Wherefore, he maketh [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note29a>)[ _war_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note29a>) with the saints of God, and encompasseth them round about.

**30** And we saw a vision of the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note30a>)[ _sufferings_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note30a>) of those with whom he made war and overcame, for thus came the voice of the Lord unto us:

**31** Thus saith the Lord concerning all those who know my power, and have been made partakers thereof, and [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note31a>)[ _suffered_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note31a>) themselves through the power of the devil to be overcome, and to deny the truth and defy my power—

**32** They are they who are the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note32a>)[ _sons_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note32a>) of [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note32b>)[ _perdition_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note32b>) , of whom I say that it had been better for them never to have been born;

**35** Having [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note35a>)[ _denied_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note35a>) the Holy Spirit after having received it, and having denied the Only Begotten Son of the Father, having [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note35b>)[ _crucified_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note35b>) him unto themselves and put him to an open [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note35c>)[ _shame_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note35c>).

**36** These are they who shall go away into the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36a>)[ _lake_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note36a>) of fire and brimstone, with the devil and his angels—

**37** And the [** __a__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37a>)[ _only_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37a>) ones on whom the [** __b__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37b>)[ _second_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37b>) [** __c__**](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37c>)[ _death_](<https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=25-26&ref=answeringislam.blog#note37c>) shall have any power;
