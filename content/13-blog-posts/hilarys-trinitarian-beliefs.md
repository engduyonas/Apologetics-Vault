# HILARY’S TRINITARIAN BELIEFS

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Nov 18, 2025
  * 16 min read


In this post I will be quoting from the works of another early church father, namely Hilary of Poitiers, in respect to his Trinitarian beliefs.

  


The citations will show that Hilary affirmed that the Son was timelessly begotten, and therefore not a creature, since the Son has been eternally God with the Father. The quotations will further prove that Hilary believed the Son to be subject or subordinate to the Father, yet not as a creature of God, but rather as the Father’s eternally begotten Son from whom he timelessly derives his divine essence and authority. All emphasis will be mine.

  


X. And if any one admits that God became Father of the Only-begotten Son at any point in time and not that the Only-begotten Son came into [ _existence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) without passion **beyond all times and beyond all**[** _human_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**calculation** : for contravening the teaching of the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) **which scorned any interval of times between the being of the Father and the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) and faithfully has instructed us that  _In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and the Word was God_ [ _John 1:1_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh001.htm#verse1>) , let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


24\. It is a [ _pious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>) saying that the Father is not limited by times: for the [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) meaning of the name of Father **which He bore before time** began surpasses comprehension. Although religion teaches us to ascribe to Him this name of Father through which comes the impassible origin of the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , yet He is not bound in time, for the [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) and [ _infinite_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08004a.htm>) God **cannot be understood as having become a Father in time** , and according to the teaching of the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) the Only-begotten [ _God the Word_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) is recognized even in the beginning rather to be with God than to be born.

  


XI. And if any one says that the Father is older in time than His Only-begotten Son, and that the Son is younger than the Father: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


25\. The essential likeness conformed to the Father’s [ _essence_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) in **kind is also taught to be identical in time** : lest He who is the image of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , who is the Word, **who is God with God in the beginning** , who is like the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , by the insertion of times between Himself and the Father should not have in Himself in perfection that which is both image, and Word, and God. For if He be proclaimed to be younger in time, He has lost the [ _truth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) of the image and likeness: for that is no longer likeness which is found to be dissimilar in time. **For that very fact that God is Father prevents there being any time in which He was not Father: consequently there can be no time in the Son’s**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**in which He was not Son. Wherefore we must neither call the Father older than the Son nor the Son younger than the Father: for the**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**meaning of neither name can exist without the other** …

38\. We [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) in one [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father Almighty, the Creator and Maker, from whom every fatherhood in heaven and in earth is named.

  


And in His only Son [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) our Lord, **who was born of the Father before all ages** , God of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , Light of Light, through whom all things were made in heaven and in earth, visible and invisible. Who is the Word and Wisdom and Might and Life and [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) Light: who in the last days for our sake took a body, And was born of the [ _Holy Virgin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>) , And was crucified, And was dead and buried: who also rose from the dead on the third day, And ascended into heaven, And sits on the right hand of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , And shall come at the end of the world to judge the quick and the dead; whose kingdom continues without end and remains for perpetual ages. For He shall be sitting at the right hand of the Father not only in this age, but also in the age to come.

  


And in the [ _Holy Ghost_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , that is, the Paraclete, whom according to His promise He sent to the [ _apostles_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) after He ascended into heaven to teach them and to remind them of all things, through whom also are sanctified the [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) of those who [ _believe_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) sincerely in Him.

I. But those who say that the Son is sprung from things non-existent, or from another substance and not from [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **and that there was a time or age when He was not** , the [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _Catholic_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03449a.htm>) [ _Church_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) regards as aliens.

  


II. If any man says that the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) are two Gods: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


III. And if any man says that God is one, but does not confess that [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , God the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , ministered to the Father in the creation of all things: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>) …

IX. If any man says that the man alone born of Mary is the Son: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>). We cannot declare that the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) is born of Mary **without declaring Him to be both**[** _Man_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>)**and**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). But lest the declaration that He is both [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and [ _Man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) should give occasion to deceit, the Council immediately adds,

  


X. If any man though saying that [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and [ _Man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) was born of [ _Mary_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15464b.htm>) , understands thereby the Unborn God: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


47\. Thus is preserved both the name and power of the divine substance. For since he is [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>) who says that the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) by Mary is man and not [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and he falls under the same condemnation who says that the Unborn God became man: God made Man is not denied to be God but denied to be the Unborn [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the Father being distinguished from the Son not under the head of nature or by diversity of substance, but only by such pre-eminence as His birthless nature gives.

  


XI. If any man hearing  _The Word was made Flesh_ thinks **that the Word was transformed into Flesh** , or says that He suffered change in taking Flesh: let him be [** _anathema_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


48\. This preserves the dignity of the Godhead: so that in the fact that the Word was made Flesh, the Word, in becoming Flesh, **has not lost through being Flesh what constituted the Word** , nor has become transformed into Flesh, so as to cease to be the Word; but the Word was made Flesh in order that the Flesh might begin to be what the Word is. Else whence came to His Flesh miraculous power in working, [ _glory_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) on the Mount, [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of the thoughts of [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) hearts, calmness in His [ _passion_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11530a.htm>) , life in His death? **God**[** _knowing_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**no change, when made Flesh lost nothing of the prerogatives of His substance**.

  


XII. If any man hearing that the only Son of God was crucified, says that His divinity suffered corruption or pain or change or diminution or destruction: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


XIII. If any man says  _Let us make man_ [ _Genesis 1:26_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/gen001.htm#verse26>) **was not spoken by the Father to the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , but by [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) to Himself: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


XIV. **If any man says that the Son did not appear to**[** _Abraham_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01051a.htm>) , but the Unborn [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or a part of Him: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


XV. **If any man says that the Son did not wrestle with Jacob as a man** , but the Unborn [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , or a part of Him: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>).

  


XVI. If any man does not understand  _The Lord rained from the Lord_ to be spoken of the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , but says that the Father rained from Himself: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>). **For the Lord the Son rained from the Lord the Father** …

  


XVII. If any man says that the Lord and the Lord, the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , **are two Gods because of the aforesaid words** : let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>). For we do not make the Son the equal or peer of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , **but understand the Son to be subject**. For He did not come down to [ _Sodom_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14130a.htm>) without the Father’s will, nor rain from Himself but  _from the Lord_ , to wit, by the Father’s authority; nor does He sit at the Father’s right hand by His own authority, but because He hears the Father saying,  _Sit on My right hand_. 

  


51\. The foregoing and the following statements utterly remove any ground for suspecting that this definition asserts a diversity of different deities in the Lord and the Lord. No comparison is made because it was seen to be impious to say that there are two Gods: not that they refrain from making the Son equal and peer of the Father in order to deny that He is [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). For, **since he is**[** _anathema_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>)**who denies that**[** _Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**is**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , it is not on that score that it is profane to speak of two equal Gods. **God is One on account of the**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**character of His natural**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**and because from the Unborn**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, who is the one**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, the Only-begotten God the Son is born, and draws His divine Being only from**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**; and since the**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**of Him who is begotten is exactly similar to the**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**of Him who begot Him, there must be one name for the exactly similar nature**. That the Son is not on a level with the Father and is not equal to Him is chiefly shown in the fact that He was subjected to Him to render [ _obedience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) , **in that the Lord rained from the Lord and that the Father did not** , as Photinus and Sabellius say, **rain from Himself, as the Lord from the Lord** ; in that He then sat down at the right hand of God when it was told Him to seat Himself; in that He is sent, in that He receives, in that He submits in all things to the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of Him who sent Him. **But the subordination of filial**[** _love_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09397a.htm>)**is not a diminution of**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**, nor does**[** _pious_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>)**duty**[** _cause_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>)**a degeneration of nature, since in spite of the fact that both the Unborn Father is God and the Only-begotten**[** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)**is**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, God is nevertheless One, and the subjection and dignity of the Son are both taught in that by being called Son He is made subject to that name which because it implies that God is His Father is yet a name which denotes His nature**. Having a name which belongs to Him whose Son He is, He is subject to the Father both in service and name; **yet in such a way that the subordination of His name bears**[** _witness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>)**to the**[** _true_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**character of His natural and exactly similar**[** _essence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>).

  


XVIII. If any man says that the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) are one Person: let him be [ _anathema_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>) …

XXVII. Once more we strengthen the understanding of [** _Christianity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03712a.htm>) by saying, If any man denies that Christ who is God and [** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , **personally**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**before time began** and aided the Father in the perfecting of all things; but says that only from the time that He was born of Mary did He gain the name of Christ and Son and a beginning of His deity: let him be [** _anathema_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01455e.htm>). ([__On the Councils, or the Faith of the Easterns__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3301.htm>))

  


5\. They think also that they have a compendious refutation of Prophets, Evangelists and Apostles alike, in their assertion that the Son was born **within time**. **They pronounce us illogical for saying that the Son has**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**from everlasting; and, since they reject the possibility of His**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) , they are forced to [** _believe_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02408b.htm>) that He was born **at a point in time**. For if He has not always [** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , there was a time when He was not; and if there be a time when He was not, **time was anterior to Him. He who has not**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**everlastingly began to exist within time** , while He Who is free from the limits of time is necessarily [** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>). The reason they give for their rejection of the [** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) of the Son **is that His everlasting**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)**contradicts the**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)**in His birth; as though by confessing that He has**[** _existed_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>)****[**_eternally_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**, we made His birth impossible**.

  


6\. What foolish and godless fears! What impious anxiety on God’s behalf! The meaning which they profess to detect in the word [** __homoousion__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07449a.htm>) , and in the assertion of the [** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) of the [** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , is detested, rejected, denounced by the [** _Church_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>). She confesses one God from Whom are all things; she confesses one [** _Jesus Christ_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) our Lord, through whom are all things; **One from Whom, One through Whom; One the Source of all, One the Agent through Whom all were created**. In the One from Whom are all things she recognises the Majesty which has no beginning, **and in the One through Whom are all things she recognises a might coequal with His Source** ; for Both are jointly supreme in the work of creation and in rule over [** _created_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm>) things. In the Spirit she recognises God as Spirit, impassible and indivisible, for she has learned from the Lord that Spirit has neither flesh nor bones [** _Luke 24:39_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk024.htm#verse39>) ; a warning to save her from supposing that [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , being Spirit, could be burdened with bodily suffering and loss. She recognises one [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , unborn from everlasting; she recognises also one Only-begotten [** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). She confesses the Father [** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) and without beginning; **she confesses also that the Son’s beginning is from**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>). Not that He has no beginning, but that He is Son of the Father Who has none; not that He is self-originated, but that He is from Him Who is unbegotten from everlasting; **born from**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**, receiving, that is, His birth from the**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**of the Father**. Thus our [** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) is free from the guesswork of [** _heretical_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) perversity; it is expressed in fixed and published terms, though as yet no reasoned defense of our confession has been put forth. Still, lest any suspicion should linger around the sense in which the Fathers have used the word [** __homoousion__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07449a.htm>) **and round our confession of the**[** _eternity_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>)**of the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , I have set down the [** _proofs_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) whereby we may be assured that the Son abides ever in that substance wherein He was begotten from the [** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and that the birth of His Son has not diminished ought of that Substance wherein the Father was abiding; that [** _holy_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) men, inspired by the teaching of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , when they said that the Son is  _homoousios_ with the Father pointed to no such flaws or defects as I have mentioned. My purpose has been to counteract the impression that this  _ousia_ , this assertion that He is  _homoousios_ with the [** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , is a negation of the nativity of the Only-begotten Son…

  


33\. Continue your study of the [** _witness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) borne by [** _Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) ; mark how diligently he seizes every opportunity of proclaiming the Lord and God. You take note of the passage,  _Hear, O_[** __Israel__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, the Lord your God is One_. [** _Deuteronomy 6:4_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu006.htm#verse4>) Note also the words of that Divine song of his;  _See, See, that I am the Lord, and there is no God beside_ Me. While God has been the Speaker throughout the poem, he ends with,  _Rejoice, you heavens, together with Him and let all the sons of God praise Him. Rejoice, O you nations, with His people, and let all the Angels of God do Him_[** __honour__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>). God is to be [** _glorified_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) by the Angels of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and He says,  _For I am the Lord, and there is no God beside Me_. For He is God the Only-begotten, and the title ‘Only-begotten’ excludes all partnership in that character, just as the title ‘Unoriginate’ denies that there is, in that regard, any who shares the character of the Unoriginate Father. The Son is One from One. There is none unoriginate except God the Unoriginate, and so likewise there is none only-begotten except God the Only-begotten. They stand Each single and alone, being respectively the One Unoriginate and the One Only-begotten. And so They Two are One [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for between the One, and the One Who is His offspring there lies no gulf of difference of nature in the [** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) Godhead. Therefore He must be worshipped by the sons of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and [** _glorified_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06585a.htm>) by the [** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Honour and reverence is demanded for God from the sons and from the [** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Notice Who it is that shall receive this [** _honour_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) , and by whom it is to be paid. It is [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and they are the sons and [** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). And lest you should [** _imagine_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07672a.htm>) that [** _honour_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) is not demanded for God Who shares our nature , but that [** _Moses_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) is thinking here of reverence due to [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the [** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) — though, indeed, it is in the Son that the Father must be honoured — examine the words of the blessing bestowed by [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) upon Joseph, at the end of the same book. They are,  _And let the things that are well-pleasing to Him that appeared in the bush come upon the head and crown of Joseph_. [** _Deuteronomy 33:16_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/deu033.htm#verse16>) Thus God is to be worshipped by the sons of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; but God Who is Himself the [** _Son of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). And God is to be reverenced by the [** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; but God Who is Himself the Angel of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). For God appeared from the bush as the Angel of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the [** _prayer_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12345b.htm>) for Joseph is that he may receive such blessings as He shall please. He is none the less God because He is the Angel of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; and none the less the Angel of God because He is [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). A clear indication is given of the Divine Persons; the line is definitely drawn between the Unbegotten and the Begotten. A revelation of the [** _mysteries_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) of heaven is granted, and we are taught not to dream of God as dwelling in solitude, when [** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) and sons of God shall worship Him, Who is God’s Angel and His [** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) …

  


37\. The fact is obvious from His own words. For He says to Hosea the [** _prophet_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) ,  _I will no more have mercy upon the house of_[** __Israel__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08193a.htm>) _, but will altogether be their enemy. But I will have mercy upon the children of Judah, and will save them in the Lord their God_. [** _Hosea 1:6-7_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/hos001.htm#verse6>) Here [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father **gives the name of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, without any ambiguity, to the**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , in Whom also He chose us before countless ages.  _Their God_ , He says, for while the [** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , being Unoriginate, is independent of all, He has given us for an inheritance to His [** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>). In like manner we read,  _Ask of Me, and I will give You the_[** __Gentiles__**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06422a.htm>) _for Your inheritance_. None can be God to Him from Whom are all things , for He is [** _eternal_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) and has no beginning; but the Son has [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , from Whom He was born, for His Father. Yet to us the Father is God and the Son is [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) ; the Father reveals to us that the Son is our [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the Son teaches that the Father is [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) over us. The point for us to remember is that in this passage the Father gives to the Son the name of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the title of His own unoriginate majesty. But I have commented sufficiently on these words of Hosea. (_On the Trinity_ , [_Book IV_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/330204.htm>))

  


11\. We do not [** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) Christ the God unless we [** _know_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) God the Begotten. But to be born God is to belong to the [** _nature_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for the name Begotten signifies indeed the manner of His origin, but does not make Him different in kind from the Begetter. And if so, the Begotten owes indeed to His Author the source of His being, **but is not dispossessed of the**[** _nature_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>)**of that Author** , for the birth of God can arise but from one origin, and have but one nature. If its origin is not from [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , it is not a birth; if it is anything but a birth, Christ is not God. **But He**** _is_****God of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and therefore [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father stands to God the Son as God of His birth and Father of His nature, for the birth of God is from [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and in the specific nature of [** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>).

  


12\. See in all that He said, how carefully the Lord tempers the [ _pious_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12748a.htm>) acknowledgment of His debt, so that neither the confession of the birth could be held to reflect upon His divinity, nor His reverent [ _obedience_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11181c.htm>) to infringe upon His sovereign nature. **He does not withhold the homage due from Him as the Begotten, Who owed to His Author His very**[** _existence_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05543b.htm>) , but He manifests by His confident bearing the consciousness of participation in that nature, which belongs to Him by virtue of the origin whereby He was born as [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). Take, for instance, the words,  _He that has seen Me, has seen the Father also_ [ _John 14:9_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh014.htm#verse9>) , and,  _The words that I say, I speak not from Myself_. He does not speak from Himself: **therefore He receives from His Author that which He says**. But if any have seen Him, they have seen the Father also: they are conscious, by this evidence, given to show that God is in Him, that a nature, one in kind with that of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , was born from God to subsist as [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). **Take again the words,****_That which the Father has given unto Me, is greater than all_****, and,****_I and the Father are one_****. To say that the Father gave, is a confession that He received His origin: but the unity of Himself with the Father is a property of His nature derived from that origin**. Take another instance,  _He has given all judgment unto the_[ __Son__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, that all may_[ __honour__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _the Son even as they_[ __honour__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) _the Father_. He acknowledges that the judgment is given to Him, and therefore He does not put His birth in the background: **but He claims equal**[** _honour_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>)**with the**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and therefore He does not resign His nature**. Yet another example,  _I am in the_[ __Father__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) _, and the Father is in Me_ , and,  _The Father is greater than I_. The One is in the Other: **recognise, then, the divinity of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, the Begotten of God** : the Father is greater than He: perceive, then, His acknowledgment of the Father’s authority. In the same way He says,  _The Son can do nothing of Himself but what He has seen the Father doing: for whatever things He does, these the Son also does in like manner_. He does nothing of Himself: that is, in accordance with His birth the Father prompts His actions: yet whatever things the Father does, these the Son also does in like manner; **that is, He subsists as nothing less than**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, and by the Father’s**[** _omnipotent_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11251c.htm>)**nature residing in Him, can do all that**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**the Father does**. All is uttered in agreement with His unity of Spirit with the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and the properties of that nature, which He possesses by virtue of His birth. That birth, which brought Him into being, constituted Him divine, and His being reveals the consciousness of that divine nature. God the Son confesses God His Father, because He was born of Him; **but also, because He was born, He inherits the whole nature of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). (_On the Trinity_ , [_Book XI_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250925213719/https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/330211.htm>))
