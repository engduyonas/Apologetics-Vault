**AMBROSE OF MILAN**

  


20\. Therefore read that the three witnesses in [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , the water, the blood, and the [ _Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , [ _1 John 5:7_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo005.htm#verse7>) are one, for if you take away one of these, the Sacrament of Baptism does not exist. For what is water without the [ _cross_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04529a.htm>) of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)? A common element, without any sacramental effect. **_Nor, again, is there the Sacrament of Regeneration without water: For except a man be born again of water and of the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, he cannot enter into the_**[** _kingdom of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>)** _._**[**_John 3:5_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse5>) Now, even the catechumen believes in the cross of the [ _Lord Jesus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , wherewith he too is signed; but unless he be [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the Name of the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and of the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and of the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , he cannot receive remission of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) nor gain the gift of spiritual [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>). ([_On the Mysteries_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/3405.htm>) , Chapter 4)

  


Chapter 3.

  


The statement of the Apostle, that all things are of the Father by the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , does not separate the Spirit from Their company, since what is referred to one Person is also attributed to each. So those [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the Name of Christ are held to be [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the Name of the Father and of the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , **_if, that is, there is belief in the Three Persons, otherwise the_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _will be null_**. This also applies to [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the Name of the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). If because of one passage the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) is separated from the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , it will necessarily follow from other passages that the Father will be subordinated to the Son. **The Son is worshipped by**[** _angels_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>)**, not by the**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**, for the latter is His**[** _witness_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>)**, not His servant**. Where the Son is spoken of as being before all, it is to be understood of creatures. **_The great dignity of the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _is_**[** _proved_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>)** _by the absence of forgiveness for the_**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _against Him_**. How it is that such [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) cannot be forgiven, and how the Spirit is one…

  


42\. So they were [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the Name of [ _Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , and [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) was not repeated in their case, but administered differently, for there is but one [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). But where there is not the complete sacrament of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , there is not considered to be a commencement nor any kind of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). **_But_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _is complete if one confess the_**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, the_**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _, and the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _. If you deny One you overthrow the whole. And just as if you mention in words One only, either the_**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, or the_**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _, or the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, and in your belief do not deny either the_**[** _Father_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)** _, the_**[** _Son_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>)** _, or the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, the_**[** _mystery_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>)** _of the_**[** _faith_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>)** _is complete_** , so, too, although you name the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , Son, and [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , and lessen the power of either the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , or the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , the whole [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) is made empty. And, lastly, they who had said: We have not heard if there be any [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , were [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) afterwards in the Name of the [ _Lord Jesus Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>). And this was an additional abundance of [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) , for now through [ _Paul’s_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) preaching they [ _knew_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) …

  


44\. Let us now consider whether as we read that the sacrament of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the Name of [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) was complete, so, too, when the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) alone is named, anything is wanting to the completeness of the [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>). Let us follow out the argument that he who has named One has signified the Trinity. If you name Christ, you imply both [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the Father by Whom the Son was anointed, and the Son Himself Who was anointed, and the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) with Whom He was anointed. For it is written: This [ _Jesus of Nazareth_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , Whom God anointed with the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). [ _Acts 10:38_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/act010.htm#verse38>) And if you name the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , you denote equally His Son and the Spirit of His mouth, if, that is, you apprehend it in your heart. And if you speak of the [ _Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , you name also [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , from Whom the Spirit proceeds, and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , inasmuch as He is also the Spirit of the Son.

  


45\. Wherefore that authority may also be joined to reason Scripture indicates that we can also be rightly [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the [ _Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , when the Lord says: But you shall be [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>). [ _Acts 1:5_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/act001.htm#verse5>) And in another place the Apostle says: For we were all [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in the body itself into one Spirit. [ _1 Corinthians 12:13_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/1co012.htm#verse13>) The work is one, for the [ _mystery_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) is one; the [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) one, for there was one death on behalf of the world; there is, then, a oneness of working, a oneness of setting forth, which cannot be separated…

Chapter 6.

  


Although we are [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) ** _with water and the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , the latter is much superior to the former, and is not therefore to be separated from the Father and the [ _Son_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>).

76\. There are, however, many who, because we are [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) with water and the [ _Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , think that there is no difference in the offices of water and the [ _Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , and therefore think that they do not differ in nature. Nor do they observe **_that we are buried in the element of water that we may rise again renewed by the Spirit_**. For in the water is the representation of death, in the [ _Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) is the pledge of life, **_that the body of_**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _may die through the water_** , which encloses the body as it were in a kind of tomb, **_that we, by the power of the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, may be renewed from the death of_**[** _sin_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _, being born again in_**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>). (_On the Holy Spirit_ , [_Book I_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/34021.htm>))

  


**JOHN CHRYSOSTOM**

  


5\. For if any one will consider how great a thing it is for one, being a [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and compassed with flesh and blood, to be enabled to draw near to that blessed and pure nature, he will then clearly see what great [ _honor_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) the [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of the Spirit has vouchsafed to [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) ; since by their agency these [ _rites_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13064b.htm>) are celebrated, and others nowise inferior to these both in respect of our dignity and our [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>). For they who inhabit the earth and make their abode there are entrusted with the administration of things which are in [ _Heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>) , and have received an authority which [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) has not given to [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) or archangels. For it has not been said to them, Whatsoever you shall bind on earth shall be bound in [ _Heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>) , and whatsoever you shall loose on earth shall be loosed in [ _Heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>). [ _Matthew 18:18_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat018.htm#verse18>) They who rule on earth have indeed authority to bind, but only the body: whereas this binding lays hold of the [ _soul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) and penetrates the heavens; and what [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) do here below God ratifies above, and the Master confirms the sentence of his servants. For indeed what is it but all manner of heavenly authority which He has given them when He says, Whose [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ye remit they are remitted, and whose [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) ye retain they are retained? [ _John 20:23_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh020.htm#verse23>) What authority could be greater than this? The Father has committed all judgment to the Son? [ _John 5:22_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh005.htm#verse22>) But I see it all put into the hands of these men by the Son. For they have been conducted to this dignity as if they were already translated to [ _Heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>) , and had transcended [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) , and were released from the [ _passions_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11534a.htm>) to which we are liable. Moreover, if a king should bestow this [ _honor_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) upon any of his subjects, authorizing him to cast into [ _prison_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12430a.htm>) whom he pleased and to release them again, he becomes an object of [ _envy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08326b.htm>) and respect to all [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) ; but he who has received from God an authority as much greater as heaven is more precious than earth, and [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) more precious than bodies, seems to some to have received so small an [ _honor_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm>) that they are actually able to [ _imagine_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07672a.htm>) that one of those who have been entrusted with these things will despise the gift. Away with such [ _madness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08041a.htm>)! For transparent [ _madness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08041a.htm>) it is to despise so great a dignity, without which it is not possible to obtain either our own [ _salvation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13407a.htm>) , or the [ _good_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06636b.htm>) things which have been promised to us. **_For if no one can enter into the kingdom of_**[** _Heaven_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07170a.htm>)** _except he be regenerate through water and the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _,_** and he who does not eat the flesh of the Lord and drink His blood is excluded from [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) life, and if all these things are accomplished only by means of those [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) hands, I mean the hands of the [ _priest_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>) , how will any one, without these, be able to escape the fire of [ _hell_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07207a.htm>) , or to win those crowns which are reserved for the victorious?

  


6\. These verily are they who are entrusted with the pangs of spiritual travail and the birth which comes through [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) : by their means we put on Christ, and are buried with the [ _Son of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) , and become members of that blessed Head. 8\. He further very appositely expounds the meaning of the term Only-Begotten, and of the term First born, four times used by the Apostle.

  


The mighty [ _Paul_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , [ _knowing_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) that the Only-begotten [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , Who has the pre-eminence in all things , is the author and [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) of all good, bears [ _witness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm>) to Him that not only was the creation of all existent things wrought by Him, but that when the original [ _creation_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04470a.htm>) of [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) had decayed and vanished away , to use his own language, and another new creation was wrought in [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , in this too no other than He took the lead, but He is Himself the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) of all that new creation of men which is effected by the [ _Gospel_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>). And that our view about this may be made clearer let us thus divide our argument. The inspired apostle on four occasions employs this term, once as here, calling Him, [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) of all creation [ _Colossians 1:15_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/col001.htm#verse15>) , another time, the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) among many brethren [ _Romans 8:29_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/rom008.htm#verse29>) , again, [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) from the dead , and on another occasion he employs the term absolutely, without combining it with other words, saying, But when again He brings the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) into the world, He says, And let all the [ _angels_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01476d.htm>) of God worship Him [ _Hebrews 1:6_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/heb001.htm#verse6>) . Accordingly whatever view we entertain concerning this title in the other combinations, the same we shall in consistency apply to the phrase [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) of all creation. For since the title is one and the same it must needs be that the meaning conveyed is also one. In what sense then does He become the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) among many brethren? in what sense does He become the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) from the dead? Assuredly this is plain, that because we are by birth flesh and blood, as the [ _Scripture_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) says, He Who for our sakes was born among us and was partaker of flesh and blood , ** _purposing to change us from corruption to incorruption by the birth from above, the birth by water and the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, Himself led the way in this birth, drawing down upon the water, by His own_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _, the_**[** _Holy Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) ; so that in all things He became the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) of those who are spiritually born again, and gave the name of brethren to those who partook in a birth like to His own by water and the Spirit. But since it was also meet that He should implant in our nature the power of rising again from the dead, He becomes the [ _first-fruits_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06082a.htm>) of them that slept [ _1 Corinthians 15:20_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/1co015.htm#verse20>) and the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) from the dead [ _Colossians 1:18_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/col001.htm#verse18>) , in that He first by His own act loosed the pains of death , so that His new birth from the dead was made a way for us also, since the pains of death, wherein we were held, were loosed by the resurrection of the Lord. Thus, just as by having shared in the washing of regeneration He became the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) among many brethren, and again by having made Himself the [ _first-fruits_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06082a.htm>) of the resurrection, He obtains the name of the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) from the dead, so having in all things the pre-eminence, after that all old things, as the apostle says, have passed away , He becomes the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) of the new creation of men in Christ by the two-fold regeneration, alike that by Holy Baptism and that which is the consequence of the resurrection from the dead, becoming for us in both alike the Prince of Life , the [ _first-fruits_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06082a.htm>) , the [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>). This [ _first-born_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>) , then, has also brethren, concerning whom He speaks to Mary, saying, Go and tell My brethren, I go to My Father and your Father, and to My God and your God. In these words He sums up the whole aim of His dispensation as Man. For men revolted from [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and served them which by nature were no gods , and though being the children of God became attached to an [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) father [ _falsely_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05781a.htm>) so called. For this [ _cause_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) the mediator between [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) and [ _man_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) having assumed the [ _first-fruits_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06082a.htm>) of all [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) [ _nature_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10715a.htm>) , sends to His brethren the announcement of Himself not in His divine character, but in that which He shares with us, saying, I am departing in order to make by My own self that [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) Father, from whom you were separated, to be your Father, and by My own self to make that [ _true_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) God from whom you had revolted to be your [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , for by that [ _first-fruits_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06082a.htm>) which I have assumed, I am in Myself presenting all humanity to its God and Father. (_Against Eunomius_ , [_Book III_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/290103.htm>))

But when he wished to answer with respect, however, to those infants who are prevented by death from being first [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in [ _Christ_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , he was so bold as to promise them not only paradise, but also the [ _kingdom of heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>) — finding no way else of avoiding the [ _necessity_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10733a.htm>) of saying that God condemns to [ _eternal_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05551b.htm>) death innocent [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) which, without any previous desert of [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , He introduces into [ _sinful_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) flesh. He saw, however, to some extent what [ _evil_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) he was giving utterance to, in implying that without any [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of Christ the [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) of infants are redeemed to everlasting life and the [ _kingdom of heaven_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>) , and that in their case original [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) may be cancelled without Christ’s [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , in which is effected the forgiveness of [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) : observing all this, and into what a depth he had plunged in his sea of shipwreck, he says, I am of opinion that for them, indeed, constant oblations and [ _sacrifices_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm>) must be continually offered up by [ _holy_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) [ _priests_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12406a.htm>). 2\. But the possibility of regeneration through the office rendered by the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of another, when the child is presented to receive the sacred rite, is the work exclusively of the Spirit by whom the child thus presented is regenerated. For it is not written, Unless a man be born again by the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of his [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) , or by the [ _faith_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>) of those presenting the child, or of those administering the ordinance, ** _but, Unless a man be born again of water and of the Spirit._**[**_John 3:5_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse5>)** _By the water, therefore, which holds forth the sacrament of_**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)** _in its outward form, and by the Spirit who bestows the benefit of_**[** _grace_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>)** _in its inward power, cancelling the bond of guilt, and restoring natural goodness [reconcilians bonum naturæ], the man deriving his first birth originally from_**[** _Adam_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/01129a.htm>)** _alone, is regenerated in Christ alone_**. Now the regenerating Spirit is possessed in common both by the [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) who present the child, and by the infant that is presented and is born again; wherefore, in virtue of this participation in the same Spirit, the [ _will_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15624a.htm>) of those who present the infant is useful to the child. But when the [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) against the child by presenting him to the false gods of the [ _heathen_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11388a.htm>) , and attempting to bring him under impious bonds unto these false gods, there is not such community of [ _souls_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>) subsisting between the [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) and the child, that the guilt of one party can be common to both alike. For we are not made partakers of guilt along with others through their will, in the same way as we are made partakers of [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) along with others through the unity of the [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) ; because the one [ _Holy Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) can be in two different [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) without their [ _knowing_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) in respect to each other that by Him [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) is the common possession of both, but the [ _human_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) spirit cannot so belong to two individuals as to make the blame common to both in a case in which one of the two [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) , and the other does not [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>). Therefore a child, having once received natural birth through his [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) , can be made partaker of the second (or spiritual) birth by the [ _Spirit of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , so that the bond of guilt which he inherited from his [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) is cancelled; but he that has once received this second birth by the [ _Spirit of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) cannot be made again partaker of natural birth through his [ _parents_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11478c.htm>) , so that the bond once cancelled should again bind him. And thus, when the [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of Christ has been once received, the child does not lose it otherwise than by his own impiety, if, when he becomes older, he turn out so ill. For by that time he will begin to have [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) of his own, which cannot be removed by regeneration, but must be healed by other remedial measures. ([_Letter 98_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/1102098.htm>) (408) From Augustine to Boniface)

  


Chapter 7.— Of the Death Which the Unbaptized Suffer for the Confession of Christ.

  


For whatever unbaptized [ _persons_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/11726a.htm>) die confessing Christ, this confession **_is of the same efficacy for the remission of_**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _as if they were washed in the sacred font of_**[** _baptism_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>)** _. For He who said, Unless a man be born of water and of the_**[** _Spirit_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)** _, he cannot enter into the_**[** _kingdom of God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>)** _,_**[**_John 3:5_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse5>)** _made also an exception in their favor_** , in that other sentence where He no less absolutely said, Whosoever shall confess me before men, him will I confess also before my Father which is in heaven; [ _Matthew 10:32_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat010.htm#verse32>) and in another place, Whosoever will lose his life for my sake, shall find it. [ _Matthew 16:25_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/mat016.htm#verse25>) And this explains the verse, Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His [ _saints_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>). ** _For what is more precious than a death by which a man’s_**[** _sins_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>)** _are all forgiven, and his merits increased an hundredfold?_** For those who have been [ _baptized_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) when they could no longer escape death, and have departed this life with all their [ _sins_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) blotted out have not equal merit with those who did not defer death, though it was in their power to do so, but preferred to end their life by confessing Christ, rather than by denying Him to secure an opportunity of [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>). And even had they denied Him under pressure of the [ _fear_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06021a.htm>) of death, this too would have been forgiven them in that [ _baptism_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) , in which was remitted even the enormous [ _wickedness_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/05649a.htm>) of those who had slain Christ. But how abundant in these men must have been the [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of the [ _Spirit_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>) , who breathes where He lists, seeing that they so dearly loved Christ as to be unable to deny Him even in so sore an emergency, and with so sure a hope of pardon! Precious, therefore, is the death of the [ _saints_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/04171a.htm>) , to whom the [ _grace_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06689a.htm>) of Christ has been applied with such gracious effects, that they do not hesitate to meet death themselves, if so be they might meet Him. And precious is it, also, because it has [ _proved_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm>) that what was originally ordained for the punishment of the sinner, has been used for the production of a richer harvest of righteousness. But not on this account should we look upon death as a good thing, for it is diverted to such useful purposes, not by any [ _virtue_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>) of its own, but by the divine interference. Death was originally proposed as an object of dread, that [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) might not be committed; now it must be undergone that [ _sin_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) may not be committed, or, if committed, be remitted, and the award of righteousness bestowed on him whose victory has earned it. (_City of God_ , [_Book 13_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250818232608/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/120113.htm>) In this book it is taught that death is penal, and had its origin in Adam’s sin.)
