# Eusebius on the New Testament Canon

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Feb 24
  * 8 min read


The following is taken from Eusebius’ [_Church History_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2501.htm>) , [ _Book III_](<https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/250103.htm>). All emphasis will be mine.

Chapter 3. The Epistles of the Apostles.

1\. **One epistle of Peter, that called the**[** _first_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1pe000.htm>)**, is acknowledged as genuine. And this the ancient elders used freely in their own writings as an undisputed work.****_But we have learned that his extant second Epistle does not belong to the canon_****; yet, as it has appeared profitable to many, it has been used with the other Scriptures**.

2\. The so-called Acts of Peter, however, and the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) which bears his name, and the Preaching and the Apocalypse, as they are called, we [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) have not been universally accepted, because no [ _ecclesiastical_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) writer, ancient or modern, has made use of testimonies drawn from them.

3\. But in the course of my history I shall be careful to show, in addition to the official succession, what [ _ecclesiastical_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) writers have from time to time made use of any of the disputed works, and what they have said in regard to the canonical and accepted writings, as well as in regard to those which are not of this class.

4\. **Such are the writings that bear the name of Peter,****_only one of which I_**[** _know_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)** _to be genuine and acknowledged by the ancient elders_**.

5\. [ _Paul's_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) fourteen epistles are well [ _known_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) and undisputed. **_It is not indeed right to overlook the fact that some have rejected the_**[** _Epistle to the Hebrews_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/heb000.htm>)** _, saying that it is disputed by the_**[** _church of Rome_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07424b.htm>)** _, on the ground that it was not written by_**[** _Paul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>). But what has been said concerning this epistle by those who lived before our time I shall quote in the proper place. In regard to the so-called Acts of Paul, I have not found them among the undisputed writings.

6\. But as the same apostle, in the salutations at the end of the [ _Epistle to the Romans_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/rom000.htm>) , has made mention among others of [ _Hermas_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07268b.htm>) , to whom the book called The Shepherd is ascribed, it should be observed that this too has been disputed by some, and on their account cannot be placed among the acknowledged books; **while by others it is considered quite indispensable, especially to those who need instruction in the elements of the**[** _faith_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05752c.htm>). Hence, as we [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , it has been publicly read in [ _churches_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) , and I have found that some of the most ancient writers used it.

7\. This will serve to show the [ _divine writings_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/index.html>) that are undisputed as well as those that are not universally acknowledged…

Chapter 24. The Order of the Gospels.

1\. This extract from Clement I have inserted here for the sake of the history and for the benefit of my readers. Let us now point out the undisputed writings of this apostle.

2\. And in the first place his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , which is [ _known_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) to all the [ _churches_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) under heaven, must be acknowledged as genuine. That it has with good reason been put by the ancients in the fourth place, after the other three [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , may be made evident in the following way.

3\. **Those great and**[** _truly_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**divine men, I mean the**[** _apostles_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>)**of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, were purified in their life, and were adorned with every**[** _virtue_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15472a.htm>)**of the**[** _soul_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14153a.htm>)**, but were uncultivated in speech**. They were confident indeed in their trust in the divine and wonder-working power which was granted unto them by the [ _Saviour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , **but they did not**[** _know_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**how, nor did they attempt to proclaim the doctrines of their teacher in studied and artistic language, but employing only the demonstration of the**[** _divine Spirit_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>)**, which worked with them, and the wonder-working power of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**, which was displayed through them** , they published the [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of the [ _kingdom of heaven_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>) throughout the whole world, paying little attention to the composition of written works.

4\. And this they did because they were assisted in their ministry by one greater than man. [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) , for instance, who surpassed them all in vigor of expression and in richness of thought, committed to writing no more than the briefest epistles, although he had innumerable [ _mysterious_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10662a.htm>) matters to communicate, **for he had attained even unto the sights of the third heaven, had been**[** _carried_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05277a.htm>)**to the very paradise of God** , and had been deemed worthy to hear unspeakable utterances there.

5\. And the rest of the followers of [ _our Saviour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , the twelve [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , the seventy [ _disciples_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) , and countless others besides, were not [ _ignorant_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07648a.htm>) of these things. Nevertheless, of all the [ _disciples_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05029a.htm>) of the [ _Lord_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , only Matthew and John have left us written memorials, and they, tradition says, were led to write only under the pressure of necessity.

6\. For Matthew, who had at first preached to the Hebrews, when he was about to go to other peoples, committed his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) to writing in his native tongue, and thus compensated those whom he was [ _obliged_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11189a.htm>) to leave for the loss of his presence.

7\. And when [ _Mark_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09672c.htm>) and Luke had already published their [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , they say that John, who had employed all his time in proclaiming the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) orally, **finally proceeded to write for the following reason. The three**[** _Gospels_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**already mentioned having come into the hands of all**** _and into his own too, they say that he accepted them and bore witness to their_**[** _truthfulness_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**; but that there was lacking in them an account of the deeds done by**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**at the beginning of his ministry**.

8\. **And this indeed is**[** _true_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>). For it is evident that the three [ _evangelists_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) recorded only the deeds done by the [ _Saviour_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) for one year after the imprisonment of John the Baptist, and indicated this in the beginning of their account.

9\. For Matthew, after the forty days' fast and the [ _temptation_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14504b.htm>) which followed it, indicates the [ _chronology_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08377a.htm>) of his work when he says: Now when he heard that [ _John_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08486b.htm>) was delivered up he withdrew from [ _Judea_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08544a.htm>) into [ _Galilee_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06341c.htm>). [ _Matthew 4:12_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mat004.htm#verse12>)

10\. [ _Mark_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09672c.htm>) likewise says: Now after that John was delivered up [ _Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) came into [ _Galilee_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06341c.htm>). [ _Mark 1:14_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/mar001.htm#verse14>) And Luke, before commencing his account of the deeds of [ _Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , similarly marks the time, when he says that [ _Herod_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07289c.htm>) , adding to all the [ _evil deeds_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14004b.htm>) which he had done, shut up [ _John_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08486b.htm>) in prison. [ _Luke 3:20_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/luk003.htm#verse20>)

11\. They say, therefore, **that the apostle John, being asked to do it for this reason, gave in his**[** _Gospel_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>)**an account of the period which had been omitted by the earlier**[** _evangelists_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>)**, and of the deeds done by the**[** _Saviour_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**during that period; that is, of those which were done before the imprisonment of the Baptist**. And this is indicated by him, they say, in the following words: This beginning of [ _miracles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10338a.htm>) did [ _Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) ; and again when he refers to the Baptist, in the midst of the deeds of [ _Jesus_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , as still [ _baptizing_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02258b.htm>) in Ænon near Salim; [ _John 3:23_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/joh003.htm#verse23>) where he states the matter clearly in the words: For [ _John_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08486b.htm>) was not yet cast into prison.

12\. John accordingly, in his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , **records the deeds of**[** _Christ_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>)**which were performed before the Baptist was cast into prison** , but the other three [ _evangelists_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>) mention the events which happened after that time.

13\. One who understands this can no longer think that the [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) are at variance with one another, inasmuch as the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) according to John contains the first acts of [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) , while the others give an account of the latter part of his [ _life_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08377a.htm>). And the genealogy of our Saviour according to the flesh John quite naturally omitted, because it had been already given by Matthew and Luke, **and began with the doctrine of his divinity, which had, as it were, been reserved for him, as their superior, by the**[** _divine Spirit_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07409a.htm>).

14\. These things may suffice, which we have said concerning the [ _Gospel of John_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08438a.htm>). The [ _cause_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03459a.htm>) which led to the composition of the [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) of Mark has been already stated by us.

15\. But as for Luke, in the beginning of his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , he states himself the reasons which led him to write it. He states that since many others had more rashly undertaken to compose a narrative of the events of which he had acquired perfect [ _knowledge_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , he himself, feeling the necessity of freeing us from their uncertain opinions, delivered in his own [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) an accurate account of those events in regard to which he had learned the full [ _truth_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) , being aided by his intimacy and his stay with [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) and by his acquaintance with the rest of the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>).

16\. So much for our own account of these things. But in a more fitting place we shall attempt to show by quotations from the ancients, what others have said concerning them.

17\. But of the writings of John, not only his [ _Gospel_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) , but also the [ _former of his epistles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo000.htm>) , has been accepted without dispute both now and in ancient times. **_But the other two are disputed_**.

18\. **_In regard to the Apocalypse, the opinions of most men ARE STILL DIVIDED_**. But at the proper time this question likewise shall be decided from the testimony of the ancients.

Chapter 25. The Divine Scriptures that are accepted **and those that are not**.

1\. Since we are dealing with this subject it is proper to sum up the writings of the [ _New Testament_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14530a.htm>) which have been already mentioned. First then must be put the [ _holy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07386a.htm>) quaternion of the [ _Gospels_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06655b.htm>) ; following them the Acts of the Apostles.

2\. After this must be reckoned the epistles of [ _Paul_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm>) ; next in order the extant [ _former epistle of John_](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/1jo000.htm>) , and likewise the epistle of Peter, must be maintained. After them is to be placed, if it really seem proper, the Apocalypse of John, concerning which we shall give the different opinions at the proper time. These then belong among the accepted writings.

3\. **_Among the disputed writings, which are nevertheless recognized by many, are extant the so-called epistle of James and that of Jude, also the_**[** _second epistle of Peter_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2pe000.htm>)** _, and those that are called the_**[** _second_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/2jo000.htm>)** _and_**[** _third of John_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/bible/3jo000.htm>)** _, whether they belong to the_**[** _evangelist_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05645a.htm>)** _or to another person of the same name_**.

4\. Among the rejected writings must be reckoned also the Acts of Paul, and the so-called Shepherd, and the Apocalypse of Peter, and in addition to these the extant [ _epistle of Barnabas_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02299a.htm>) , and the so-called [ _Teachings of the Apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04779a.htm>) ; **_and besides, as I said, the Apocalypse of John, if it seem proper, which some, as I said, reject, but which others class with the accepted books_**.

5\. And among these some have placed also the Gospel according to the Hebrews, with which those of the Hebrews that have accepted [ _Christ_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08374c.htm>) are especially delighted. And all these may be reckoned among the disputed books.

6\. **But we have nevertheless felt compelled to give a catalogue of these also, distinguishing those works which according to**[** _ecclesiastical tradition_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15006b.htm>)**are**[** _true_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>)**and genuine and commonly accepted, from those others which, although not canonical but disputed, are yet at the same time**[** _known_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**to most**[** _ecclesiastical_**](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>)**writers** — we have felt compelled to give this catalogue in order that we might be able to [ _know_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) both these works and those that are cited by the [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>) under the name of the [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , including, for instance, such books as the Gospels of Peter, of Thomas, of Matthias, or of any others besides them, and the Acts of Andrew and John and the other [ _apostles_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01626c.htm>) , which no one belonging to the succession of [ _ecclesiastical_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm>) writers has deemed worthy of mention in his writings.

7\. And further, the character of the style is at variance with apostolic usage, and both the thoughts and the purpose of the things that are related in them are so completely out of accord with [ _true_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm>) [ _orthodoxy_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11330a.htm>) that they clearly show themselves to be the fictions of [ _heretics_](<https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07256b.htm>). Wherefore they are not to be placed even among the rejected writings, but are all of them to be cast aside as absurd and impious.

Let us now proceed with our history.
