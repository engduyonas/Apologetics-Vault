# Origen on God’s Uncreated Firstborn Son

  * [samshmn](<https://www.samshmnthelogy.net/profile/samshmn78303/profile>)
  * Oct 30, 2025
  * 4 min read


In his refutation to the cynical pagan opponent of the Christian faith named Celsus, Origen affirms that Jesus Christ is the uncreated firstborn Son of God:

Chapter 17

  


Since Celsus, moreover, from a desire to depreciate the accounts which our Scriptures give of the [ _kingdom of God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08646a.htm>) , has quoted none of them, as if they were unworthy of being recorded by him (or perhaps because he was unacquainted with them), while, on the other hand, he quotes the sayings of [ _Plato_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12159a.htm>) , both from his  _Epistles_ and the  _Phædrus_ , as if these were divinely inspired, but our Scriptures were not, let us set forth a few points, for the sake of comparison with these plausible declarations of [ _Plato_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12159a.htm>) , which did not however, dispose the [ _philosopher_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12025c.htm>) to worship in a manner worthy of him the Maker of all things. For he ought not to have adulterated or polluted this worship with what we call [ __idolatry__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm>) , but what the many would describe by the term [ __superstition__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14339a.htm>). Now, according to a Hebrew figure of speech, it is said of [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) in the [ _eighteenth Psalm_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/bible/psa017.htm>) , that He made darkness His  _secret place_ , to signify that those notions which should be worthily entertained of God are invisible and unknowable, because God conceals Himself in darkness, as it were, from those who cannot endure the splendours of His [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) , or are incapable of looking at them, partly owing to the pollution of their understanding, which is clothed with the body of mortal lowliness, and partly owing to its feebler power of comprehending God. And in order that it may appear that the [ _knowledge_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) of God has rarely been vouchsafed to [ _men_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09580c.htm>) , and has been found in very few individuals, [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) is related to have entered into the darkness where God was. And again, with regard to [ _Moses_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) it is said: [ __Moses__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/10596a.htm>) _alone shall come near the Lord, but the rest shall not come near_. And again, that the [ _prophet_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/12477a.htm>) may show the depth of the doctrines which relate to [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , and which is unattainable by those who do not possess the  _Spirit which searches all things, even the deep things of_[ __God__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , he added:  _The abyss like a garment is His covering_. **Nay, our Lord and Saviour, the**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**of**[** _God_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>)**, manifesting that the greatness of the**[** _knowledge_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**of the Father is appropriately comprehended and**[** _known_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>)**pre-eminently by Him alone** , and in the second place by those whose minds are enlightened by the [ _Logos_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>) Himself and [ _God_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , declares:  _No man_[ __knows__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _the_[ __Son__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, but the Father; neither_[ __knows__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) _any man the Father but the_[ __Son__](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/14142b.htm>) _, and he to whomsoever the Son will reveal Him_. For no one can worthily [ _know_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) **the UNCREATED and**[** _first-born_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06081a.htm>)**(****_ton ageneton prototokon [“the uncreated firstborn”]_****) of all created nature like the Father who begot Him, nor any one the Father like the living**[** _Logos_**](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/09328a.htm>)**, and His Wisdom and Truth**. By sharing in Him who takes away from the Father what is called darkness, which He made His secret place, and the abyss, which is called His covering, and in this way unveiling the [ _Father_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/06608a.htm>) , every one [ _knows_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) the Father who is capable of [ _knowing_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/cathen/08673a.htm>) Him. ([_Against Celsus_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/0416.htm>) , [ _BOOK VI_](<https://web.archive.org/web/20250928194406/https:/www.newadvent.org/fathers/04166.htm>) ; emphasis mine)

  


Elsewhere, Origen writes:

  


5\.  _Concerning his Son_.83 He who was a son according to the flesh came indeed from the seed of David. Undoubtedly, he became that which previously was not, according to the flesh. **According to the Spirit, however, he existed first, AND THERE WAS NEVER A TIME WHEN HE WAS NOT**.84 It should be noted that [M849] he did not say, “who has been predestined Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness,” but, “who has been destined the Son of God.”85 Let no one think that we are scrutinizing the words used here more carefully than the matter allows. For even though it is customary to find “predestined” in the Latin copies,86 **the correct translation here is “destined” and not “predestined.” For only a person who is in existence can be destined; but to be predestined applies to someone who is not yet in existence, such as those, concerning whom the Apostle says, “Those whom he foreknew these he also predestined.”****87 Accordingly those who do not yet exist can be foreknown and predestined, BUT HE WHO IS AND WHO ALWAYS IS, is not predestined but destined**.88 **We should mention this on account of those who utter blasphemies against the only begotten Son of God** and, ignorant of the distinction between “destined” and “predestined,” **they imagine that he ought to be counted among those who were predestined to exist when they previously did not exist. But he was never predestined to be the Son, BUT HE ALWAYS WAS, JUST AS ALSO THE FATHER. So then, HE WHO ALWAYS IS** , is destined, as we have said, not predestined. But one who is predestined did not yet exist at the time he was predestined, but he began at some point in time.89 The Apostle is therefore making a critical distinction when he refers to him as “made of the seed of David according to the flesh,” but calls him destined “the Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness.” (_Commentary on the Epistle to the Romans, Books 1-5 (Fathers of the Church Patristic Series)_ , translated by Thomas P. Scheck [The Catholic University of America Press Publication, 2009], pp. 69-70; emphasis mine)

  


84\. This formulation also occurs in Fr in Heb 1.8 (= von Balthasar,  _Origen: Spirit and Fire_ , p. 77). The Greek formula is attributed to Origen by Pamphilus,  _Apology_ 1.3. Arius, whose teaching was condemned by the Council of Nicaea, 325, became infamous for his slogan,  _en pote hote ouk en_ , “There was a time when he was not,” referring to the time before the Son was created. Origen’s expression clearly anticipates the Nicene and Athanasian definitions. Cf. Bigg,  _Christian Platonists_ , p. 167, “**There is NO SHADOW OF A DOUBT for Origen the Son IS CO-ETERNAL AND CO-EQUAL WITH THE FATHER**.” Cf. 10.8.5;  _Princ_ 1.1.2; 1.2.9; 4.4.1. (Ibid., p. 69; emphasis mine)
