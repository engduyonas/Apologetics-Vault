**THE DAY OF YHWH JESUS**

  


Another way we know that Paul was not identifying Jesus as an archangel, particularly Michael, but was rather describing the risen Christ is YHWH Almighty Incarnate is by his expressly referring to the time when Christ returns to resurrect the dead, judge the world and usher in the age to come, as the Day of our/the Lord Jesus Christ, or any other similar attributions. Note the following examples:

“For what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting before **our Lord Jesus at his coming**? Is it not you?” 1 Thessalonians 2:19

Paul has deliberately taken the very language of the Greek rendering of the book of Isaiah in its depiction of YHWH manifesting his power and majesty as he comes to destroy the wicked in flames of fire, and attributed to Christ! 

  


**PRAYING TO YHWH JESUS**

  


The final line of evidence from Paul that Christ is none other than the human incarnation of YHWH God comes from the worship, which this blessed servant attributes to the risen Son of God.

  


For instance, the Apostle begins and concludes his epistles with invocations and benedictions for spiritual blessings to be bestowed upon the churches and/or individuals he is writing to. What makes these prayers astounding is that Paul not only invokes God the Father but Christ as well.

  


In fact, there are even places where the Son is invoked all by himself!

  


“Paul, Silva′nus, and Timothy, To the church of the Thessalo′nians **_IN_****God the Father**** _AND_****the Lord Jesus Christ** : Grace to you and peace.” 1 Thessalonians 1:1

“The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.” 1 Thessalonians 5:28

  


“Paul, Silva′nus, and Timothy, To the church of the Thessalo′nians **_IN_****God our Father**** _AND_****the Lord Jesus Christ** : Grace to you and peace **_FROM_****God the Father**** _AND_****the Lord Jesus Christ**.” 2 Thessalonians 1:1-2

  


“To this end we always pray for you, that our God may make you worthy of his call, and may fulfil every good resolve and work of faith by his power,so that **the name of our Lord Jesus** may be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace **of our God**** _AND_****the Lord Jesus Christ**.” 2 Thessalonians 1:11-12

  


Remarkably, Paul not only prays to Christ either along with the Father or all by himself, but this inspired emissary even goes as far as to say that the churches are in union and fellowship with both God and Christ!

  


This presupposes that the Son is omnipresent and omniscient since he is able to have fellowship and communion with, as well as oversee, all believers to the same extent that the Father does! 

  


Even more astonishing are the next two examples:

  


“Now may **our God and Father himself,****_AND_****our Lord Jesus** ,__**_direct (kateuthynai)_** our way to you;” 1 Thessalonians 3:11

  


In the following text, Paul mentions Christ ahead of the Father!

  


“Now **may our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God our Father,****_who loved (ho agapeesas)_** us and **_gave (dous)_** us eternal comfort and good hope through grace,**_comfort (parakalesai)_** your hearts and establish them in every good work and word.” 2 Thessalonians 2:16-17

  


The verbs employed in both of the aforementioned passages are all singular, even though the Apostle ascribes these actions to both God and Christ. I.e., Paul has just prayed to the Father and the Son to direct and give love and comfort to the believers he writes, by invoking them together through the use of singular verbs. 

  


What this shows is that, for this inspired envoy, the actions of God and Christ are one and the same, not because they are the same Person but because they are one and the same God!

Here are more cases of Paul praying to the Lord Jesus directly:

  


“Now **may the Lord of peace himself give you peace** at all times in all ways. **The Lord be with you all**. I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. This is the mark in every letter of mine; it is the way I write.**The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all**.” 2 Thessalonians 3:16-18

  


“If any one has no love for the Lord, let him be accursed. **Our Lord, come (****_Maranatha_****)**! **The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you**.” 1 Corinthians 16:22-23

  


This one is truly astonishing:

  


“To the church of God which is at Corinth, to those sanctified in Christ Jesus, called to be saints together with all those **who in every place**** _call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ_****, both their Lord and ours** : Grace to you and peace **_FROM_****God our Father**** _AND_****the Lord Jesus Christ**.” 1 Corinthians 1:2-3

  


Paul not only again prays to both God and Christ, but he also describes believers as being characterized by their practice of calling upon the name of our Lord Jesus.

The foregoing cases demonstrate that the practice of calling on the name is an act of worship, which true believers were/are to render only to YHWH God Almighty. Therefore, that Christians such as Paul became known for their habitual practice of calling on the name of the risen Christ would have been a blatant act of idolatry on their part if the Son is a mere creature.

  


The only justifiable way that Jewish monotheists steeped in the Hebrew Scriptures could attribute this worship pattern to Jesus is if Christ’s Jewish disciples did not view him as a mere creature. Instead, the first Christians came to believe as a result of Jesus’ physical bodily resurrection and ascension into heaven that Christ is actually YHWH God Almighty Incarnate (even though he is not the Father or the Holy Spirit). There’s simply no way around this point.

  


If this wasn’t amazing enough, we even have a Trinitarian benediction where the holy Apostle invokes the Holy Spirit as well, thereby showing the Spirit’s essential coequality with the Father and the Son!

  


“**The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ** and **the love of God** and **the fellowship of the Holy Spirit** be with you all.” 2 Corinthians 13:14

  


With the foregoing in view, can there be any doubt left that Paul in no way was claiming that the risen Lord is the Archangel Michael? Isn’t it obvious that this blessed and inspired emissary of Christ was emphatically affirming that the Son is none other than YHWH God Almighty that became a Man?
